## The chapter in one minute

The Chapter 1 map is crumbling. In [Jakapo Town](gm_glossary.md#jakapo-town), a [galtzagorri](gm_glossary.md#galtzagorri) village of giant mushrooms, cranky elder [S'matzia](gm_glossary.md#smatzia), a descendant of [Arahosko the Red](gm_glossary.md#arahosko-the-red), seals it after three favors: rescue her gardener from a killer rose, fight the ooze her failed experiment summoned, and fetch silver dye from the herder [Kuaranda](gm_glossary.md#kuaranda).

The sealed map shows six obelisks on the route to [Shivering Mountain](gm_glossary.md#shivering-mountain); the PCs must touch the [sapphire scale](gm_glossary.md#sapphire-scale) to each. Breeding mantis swarms block the second, in the Hissing Chasm. To calm them, the PCs take a stolen magic harmonica from Batarsi, an evil toymaker, then guard Kuaranda as she plays it at Aksyanis Amphitheater against waves of predators. With the chasm calm, they follow the obelisks to [Shiverskull Cave](gm_glossary.md#shiverskull-cave).

**By the end of the chapter the PCs have:**
1. **A sealed, permanent map** (Event 8).
2. **The gentle whistler**, taken from Batarsi (Event 10).
3. **Calmed the Hissing Chasm** by surviving the amphitheater waves.
4. **Touched the scale to five obelisks** and reached the sixth at Shiverskull Cave.

**Party level:** starts at **2**, ends at **3**.

> **🎯 Why this chapter exists:** It turns a cursed scrap into a working quest map, teaches its rule (touch every obelisk), introduces Arahosko's people, and offers Kuaranda as a companion. Structure: a favor chain, each favor unlocking the next.

The book has four parts (p. 48–67):

| **Big Show at Aksyanis Amphitheater** (p. 65–67) | Kuaranda plays the whistler through four waves | After Event 10 |

**Play order:**
1. Jakapo Town: Events 5 → 6 → 7 → 8; Event 9's spy shadows the PCs throughout.

> **💡 GM tip — pacing:** Short favors (Bolfo, S'matzia, Kuaranda, S'matzia), but the map loses HP until Event 8 ends. Keep the clock visible.

### Rituals

A **ritual** is a long group spell, cast without a spell slot. A caster who knows it can lead it if they have the primary check's skill rank and the ritual's level doesn't exceed half theirs, rounded up. So a PC who learns the silver seal (a 2nd-level ritual) can't lead it before level 3.
- The **primary caster** rolls the **primary check**; its degree of success is the result.
- **Secondary casters** each roll a **secondary check** first; successes help the primary, failures hurt (core rulebook ritual rules).
- Each ritual lists **Cast** time, **Cost**, and skills.

**This chapter's example:** the silver seal (Event 8). S'matzia leads with Occultism +12; at least one PC must assist with Crafting, Scribing Lore, or Indigo Isles Geography Lore. See [Event 8](#event-8--spellcasting-with-smatzia-ritual).

> **⚠️ Gotcha:** No DC is printed for the seal's checks. See the Event 8 tip.

> **⚠️ Gotcha — is a helper required?** Event 8 says S'matzia "needs one secondary spellcaster," then calls helping "optional" (p. 59). The ritual block requires at least 1. Require it.

### NPC attitudes, Make an Impression, and Request

Attitudes run **hostile → unfriendly → indifferent → friendly → helpful**.
- **Make an Impression:** about a minute of talk, then Diplomacy vs. the NPC's Will DC. Success: one step better. Critical success: two. Critical failure: one step worse.
- **Request:** Diplomacy to ask a favor. Only friendly or helpful NPCs grant one.
- Story beats shift attitudes too: naming Arahosko raises S'matzia one step; snapping at Kuaranda drops her from indifferent to unfriendly.

**Start indifferent:** S'matzia (Event 6), Kuaranda (Event 7), Krissia (Landmarks 2 and 3), Golomor (E3).

### Elite and weak creatures

Stat lines marked "weak" or "elite" cite **BB 9**.
- **Elite:** +2 to AC, attack rolls, DCs, saves, Perception, and skills; more damage and HP; higher level.
- **Weak:** the same numbers −2; less damage and HP; lower level.

**This chapter's examples:** young sanguine rose (weak sanguine rose, Creature 3), weak blood mice (Creature 0), weak toy spy (Creature 2), Mrs. Sloth (elite springjaw doll, Creature 2). Use the printed adjusted level for XP.

### Concealed, hidden, and undetected

In Event 6, a PC standing in the puddles of eyes can't become any of these to the eyesore.
- **Concealed:** fog or dim light. Attacks need a **DC 5 flat check** or miss.
- **Hidden:** the attacker knows the square but can't see the creature. The attacker is flat-footed to it, and attacks need a **DC 11 flat check**.
- **Undetected:** the attacker doesn't know the square. It guesses one, and the attack also needs a DC 11 flat check.

([Flat checks](gm_chapter_1.md#flat-checks) are in Chapter 1.) Golomor (E3) and the toy spy (Event 9) try to stay out of sight.

### Tiny creatures, reach, and mounts

**Batarsi** is **Tiny** and rides a beetle.
- Tiny creatures can **share a space**. Most have **0-foot reach**, so they must enter a target's space to attack in melee, as the book says Batarsi must to use his galtzagorri hook spear.
- His **longspear** has 5 feet of reach, so he attacks from the next square or from Goosebark's back.
- **Goosebark** has a fly Speed of 25 feet and carries him along. If it flies out of reach, melee PCs need ranged attacks or a way up.

### Swarms

A **swarm** is a mass of Tiny creatures acting as one across several squares. Most take extra damage from **area** and **splash** effects and shrug off much of a single weapon hit; check each stat block.

**This chapter's swarms:** jungle mantis swarm (Landmark 2), toy swarm (E4), swarm assassin (Wave 1). Area spells and splash weapons like alchemist's fire are the fast answer.

### Rounds, minutes, and the wave timer

**1 round = 6 seconds; 1 minute = 10 rounds.** At the amphitheater:
- Song: **5 minutes** (50 rounds), nonstop.
- First wave: after **3 minutes** (30 rounds).
- Defense: **2 minutes** (20 rounds), **exactly 2 rounds** of rest between waves.

Two rounds (12 seconds) rules out Treat Wounds and Refocus (10 minutes each). Heal with spells, potions, and other one-turn effects.

### Identify Magic and Learn a Spell

- **Identify Magic:** a skill use (usually Arcana, Nature, Occultism, or Religion) to learn what magic does. **DC 15 Arcana** reveals what the map's ashes can do; a DC 15 Arcana check examining the map reveals how *mending* works on it.
- **Learn a Spell:** adds a spell or ritual to a caster's repertoire or spellbook. A Sylvan reader can learn the silver seal this way (Event 8).

## The Gilded Treasure Map

> **🎯 Why this section exists:** The chapter's clock and quest structure: decay drives the PCs to Jakapo Town, and the obelisks lay out the rest.

If you didn't hand out **Handout 1: The Gilded Treasure Map** (p. 46–47) at the end of Chapter 1, now is a good time (p. 48).

### Disintegrating magic

Poppy had Arahosko enchant the map to fall apart once assembled, testing its finder's speed (p. 49).

- The assembled map has a maximum of **20 Hit Points** and **loses 1 HP every hour**.
- Below **10 HP**, the DC to decipher the map rises by 2.
- At **0 HP** the map is incomprehensible and soon becomes a handful of ash.

> **⚠️ Gotcha — map HP:** The king's half had 10 HP in Chapter 1 (p. 16). The assembled map has 20 (p. 49). Use 20.

> **⚠️ Gotcha — "the DC to decipher":** This chapter prints no base DC for reading the map. Pick one if a PC studies it.

### Stalling the decay

- The map **can't be Repaired** with Crafting.
- HP-restoring spells like ***mending*** **don't restore HP**; they **pause the decay for hours equal to the HP they'd restore**, magic item or not. A typical *mending*: **5 hours**.
- A **DC 15 Arcana** check examining the map reveals this.

> **⚠️ Gotcha — Chapter 1's mending scroll:** U'oko's *mending* scroll buys 5 hours; it doesn't heal the map or end the curse. Princess Atriella can add two more ([Royal Family Interlude](#royal-family-interlude)).

### Reading the map

- Covers part of Goldcrop Island, from a little west of [Rumplank](gm_glossary.md#rumplank) to a little north of Shivering Mountain.
- No terrain or true distances: landmark icons and **six glowing golden dots** between Jakapo Town and the mountain's base, the scale's glowing color.
- **Copies don't work:** a copy lacks the map's magic link to the scale, which the hunt's end needs (p. 49).

### Follow the dots: the waymarkers

Each landmark has a **5-foot-tall lava-rock obelisk** that glows as the PCs approach. Touching the **sapphire scale** to it pours its golden light into the scale, which pulses or thrums, and that dot on the map goes dark. S'matzia explains this in Event 8 if needed. All six open Poppy's sanctum in Chapter 3.

> **🧩 Sets up:** **Area F27 in Chapter 3**, the secret door to Poppy's hideout, opens only for a fully charged scale (p. 50). A skipped obelisk means a trip back.

### Make Haste, Not Waste (if the map is destroyed)

Three ways forward if the map turns to ash (p. 49):
- **Magical ashes:** they glow dim yellow. A **DC 15 Arcana** check to Identify Magic shows they still hold divination magic. A pinch dropped in the air drifts toward the nearest landmark, even without wind, so the PCs can still reach and charge every waymarker.
- **Unlikely aid:** D'marti Tixima, Prince Kulupi, or Melar Kan has seen obelisks like the one in Kuaranda's yard and can give directions.
- **Help from a stranger:** the [Indigo Stranger](gm_glossary.md#indigo-stranger) finds the PCs and helps, enigmatically: odd requests, aid as riddles. Any of this chapter's encounters can become a task he sets.

> **💡 GM tip:** The ashes keep the game moving with the least railroading. Use the Stranger last, as in Chapter 1.

### Landmark 1 — Jakapo Town

- **Where:** [Jakapo Town](#jakapo-town).
- **What happens:** the map gets sealed (Events 5–8); the PCs learn how to pass the chasm.
- **The waymarker:** in **Kuaranda's fenced thyalodon yard**, the PCs' first scale touch, in [Event 7](#event-7--inking-the-map-no-combat).

If the PCs don't know to bring the map here and head elsewhere, repeat the hint or have the Indigo Stranger tell them (p. 50).

### Landmark 2 — Hissing Chasm

- **Travel:** 2 miles of difficult terrain from Jakapo Town, about **2 hours**.
- **On the map:** a gaping hole with a praying mantis hovering over a glowing dot at its center.

At the overlook, read aloud (p. 50):

> *Thousands of bright green mantises swarm about this deep ravine, which stretches for miles in either direction. At the gulch's lowest point, a five-foot-tall obelisk made of carved lava rock glows softly at your approach.*

**Premature visit (before the whistler is played):** a near-deafening squeal grows as the PCs approach; thousands of angry mantises swarm.
- Normally a pleasant valley. Goldcrop's jungle mantises return to breed every ten years, and a cycle starts this year: for **at least 3 months** they attack anything that enters.
- **DC 20 Nature** or **DC 17 Indigo Isles Geography Lore** (Recall Knowledge) reveals this, as can anyone in Jakapo Town or Rumplank.
- The PCs must get through or wait out the season in Rumplank.

**Going around:** the chasm runs for miles; the off-path jungle is thick and hostile.
- Best routes: Aksyanis Amphitheater north and east to Shiverskull Cave, or the [Writhing Swamp](gm_glossary.md#writhing-swamp) east then north to Never Lonely Hill. Both are many miles of **greater difficult terrain** ([Travel distances](#travel-distances)).
- Several wandering monster attacks (perhaps the legendary **Shugiyar**) and a **DC 25 Survival** check each hour to avoid getting lost.
- Wasted effort: F27 in Chapter 3 needs every obelisk, so they must come back.

**Going through:** walking in triggers a **jungle mantis swarm** (BB 50), which the book calls deadly. Mating mantises **don't chase PCs who flee**.

> **⚠️ Gotcha:** The book gives the swarm no level or threat label. Let the PCs see and hear it first, and let them run.

**Friendly roadster (only if the PCs skipped Jakapo Town):** **Krissia** (LN female [orpok](gm_glossary.md#orpok) roadster 3) rests on a boulder above the chasm.
- She explains the breeding cycle and sends the PCs to Jakapo Town.
- She escorts for **1 sp per hour** to Jakapo Town, Rumplank, or anywhere west of the chasm.
- Guiding, she makes terrain **one step easier** (greater difficult → difficult, difficult → normal), never faster than normal speed.
- **Background Special Effect:** **indigo trailblazer** PCs get a **+2 circumstance bonus** to Diplomacy to Make an Impression on or Request from her, here or at Landmark 3.

**The way is clear (after the amphitheater):** the chasm quiets. A few mantises flutter, but the path to the obelisk is open.

**Waymarker:** touch the scale to the obelisk at the bottom; the dot disappears.

> **⚠️ Gotcha — two names:** The book uses both **Hissing Chasm** and **Hissing Gulch** for the same ravine.

### Royal Family Interlude

If the PCs return to Rumplank to report or ask advice (sidebar, p. 51), the king and queen are busy, so **Prince Kulupi** or **Princess Atriella** hears them out, advises them, and urges them on, thrilled with their progress.
- **If the map is still decaying:** Atriella gives **two scrolls of *mending*** and points them to the Jakapo Town elder, said to descend from Arahosko the Red.

> **🔒 GM-only:** [Kulupi](gm_glossary.md#prince-kulupi) leads the cult, and the PCs are collecting what it wants. Play him as an eager, helpful sponsor; let Atriella hand over the scrolls.

### Landmark 3 — Never Lonely Hill

- **Travel:** 2 miles of difficult terrain from the Hissing Chasm.
- **On the map:** a grassy mound east of the chasm with a shining obelisk. In person: sparse vegetation, gentle slope, a 5-foot glowing lava-rock pillar.

It rises a few dozen feet above the canopy. Old Rumplankers named it because someone was nearly always already up there.

**Waymarker:** touch the scale to the obelisk; the dot fades.

**Krissia is here** whether or not the PCs met her at the chasm, lunching and gazing over the jungle. If they talk to her:

*"This is the farthest I've ever gone. Beyond here, the entire jungle's a mystery."* Nodding toward the mountain, she adds, *"Only the fiercest roadsters have ever been there. Shivering Mountain. No idea why it's called that. Guess it does give me the shivers, though."* Then she shudders, gets up, and moves on.

- **Escort:** **1 gp** to Jakapo Town or Rumplank, by shortcuts taking **half the usual time** from here.
- **Bonus lore:** starts **indifferent**. At **friendly or helpful** ([attitudes](#npc-attitudes-make-an-impression-and-request)), she shares a legend: orpok miners of the **Emerald Earth Mining Company** dug a quarry inside Shivering Mountain and abandoned it. *"The place is supposedly riddled with shale spitters... Spitters love eating gems, you know. Their bodies are even made of gems, which you can take for yourself if you manage to kill one of the things. Either way, no doubt them miners left some interesting things if they fled the place in such a hurry."*

**XP:** **10 XP** each for reaching it, or **30 XP** each if they improved Krissia's attitude and learned of the mining company.

> **🧩 Sets up:** The miners' abandoned camp and shale spitters fill Shiverskull Cave (**Chapter 3**). Krissia's tip is the PCs' first warning.

> **⚠️ Gotcha — dates:** Krissia says "over ten years ago" (p. 52); Chapter 3 says "a few decades ago" (p. 69); the [synopsis timeline](gm_story_synopsis.md#the-real-history) says 5992 to 5995, about 30 years ago. She's repeating legend, so her error fits.

### Landmark 4 — Ruined Tower *(Moderate 2)*

- **Travel:** 2 miles of difficult terrain from Never Lonely Hill.
- **On the map:** a whimsical stone tower among palms (outdated).

Read aloud (p. 52):

> *The crumbled ruins of a cylindrical stone structure lie in a messy heap at the center of this jungle clearing. Though the building was likely once a multi-story stone tower, the nearby palm trees now dwarf the tower's remains, which amount to a mound of rubble no taller than ten feet at its center. Impossibly, a stone obelisk made of lava rock remains intact and juts out from the rubble, glowing softly at your approach.*

The **twin of Batarsi's tower**: the gnome transmuter who first lived in Batarsi's tower moved here shortly before dying. The upper floors have collapsed; the front door opens onto rubble.

**Map:** the Toymaker's Tower exterior (p. 61), interior filled with one layer of impassable rubble that's greater difficult terrain, or theater of the mind.

**Venomous Snakedead (2)** — BB 118, Creature 2. Initiative: Perception +6. Monster Parts 5/12/22 gp. Two mindless [snakedead](gm_glossary.md#venomous-snakedead) wandered in; they attack intruders out of malice toward the living.

**Waymarker:** after the fight, touch the scale; it thrums stronger and the dot disappears.

**Treasure (without Monster Parts only):** a **lensatic compass** in the rubble.

### Landmark 5 — Melon Tree *(Moderate 2)*

- **Travel:** 2 miles of difficult terrain from the Ruined Tower.
- **On the map:** a glowing dot on a squat, gangly tree bearing one big melon unlike any Goldcrop fruit.
- **DC 21 Nature:** the drawing shows a **hook melon**, a monstrous, parasitic plant with a malevolent mind.

Read aloud (p. 52):

> *A softly glowing obelisk of lava rock stands immediately adjacent to a spindly, single-limbed "tree" in the center of this low-lying jungle clearing. Dangling from the tree's sole branch is a torso-sized lump of grotesque, fleshy fruit. Animal bones surround the tree. A small, indiscernible trinket near the base of the tree glints in the sunlight.*

- **The trinket:** **Seek within 15 feet** of the tree and succeed at a **DC 21 Perception** check to spot a silver ring on a mummified hand. More needs a close look.
- **Map:** a featureless clearing with the tree and obelisk, a cropped section of another map like the Writhing Swamp, or theater of the mind.

**Hook Melon** — BB 43, Creature 4. Initiative: Perception +11. Monster Parts 12/27/50 gp.
- Waits for prey to come **within 15 feet**, then attacks.
- Latches onto the **strongest-looking PC** and gallops around using its captive.
- **Against ranged PCs:** no ranged attacks and slow, so the best it can do is detach, move behind cover, and block line of effect.

> **⚠️ Gotcha:** Two levels above the party, it hits often. Alone it's 80 XP (Moderate), but all its pressure lands on whoever it grabs. Watch that PC's HP.

**Treasure:** the ring is a silver wedding band (**1 gp**); the hand is a ***hand of the mage***.

**Waymarker:** touch the scale to the obelisk; the dot disappears.

### Landmark 6 — Shivering Mountain

**Shiverskull Cave**, an entrance shaped like a snake's open jaws. Reaching it after the other five obelisks ends the chapter; its obelisk is touched at the start of Chapter 3 ([Concluding the chapter](#concluding-the-chapter)).

## Jakapo Town

- **Where:** jungle, a few miles east of Rumplank.
- **Who:** S'matzia, Kuaranda, Bolfo, other [galtzagorris](gm_glossary.md#galtzagorri).
- **Goal:** seal the map; learn how to pass the Hissing Chasm.

When the PCs arrive, read aloud (p. 53):

> *A sudden hush falls over the jungle outlands as you arrive at a stand of verdant pine trees interwoven with towering red-and-white mushrooms. Each mushroom is as large as a small g'mayun hut, and sprites with the wings of ladybugs flit between the fleshy fungal structures. You witness a bright blue pond to the north, a group of happily grazing thyalodons in a fenced yard to the south, and a lazy dirt road that winds among the bases of the mushroom huts. This settlement seems like an idyllic pastoral community that lives in harmony with the natural world.*

### Jakapo Town at a glance *(Settlement 2)*

- **CN town.** A galtzagorri enclave with strong magical traditions.
- **Government:** none (unenforced socialism).
- **Population:** 189 (90% galtzagorri, 5% orpoks, 4% wildfire [leshies](gm_glossary.md#leshy), 1% other).
- **Languages:** Common, Sylvan. **Religion:** [the Balance](gm_glossary.md#the-balance).
- **Threats:** crafted creations run amok, encroaching jungle fauna, renegade artisans.
- **Crafter's Paradise:** NPCs up to **6th level** can be hired to Craft or Repair items.
- **Notable NPCs:** S'matzia (N female galtzagorri sage 5), mystical elder and unofficial overseer. Kuaranda (NG female galtzagorri bard 2), beloved bard, rancher, and jane-of-all-trades. Dalynky (LG nonbinary galtzagorri researcher 3), eccentric researcher and artifact collector. Selevi (CN male galtzagorri shopkeep 3), shopkeeper, junk collector, and magic item merchant.

### Recall Knowledge (secret check)

On the way, roll a **secret DC 15 Nature or Society, or DC 13 Indigo Isles Geography Lore** check for each PC ([secret checks](gm_chapter_1.md#secret-checks-and-recall-knowledge)).
- **Critical success:** As success, plus: the enclave is centuries older than Rumplank, and its people are skilled magic-item makers who love tall tales about how the jungle's landmarks came to be.
- **Success:** A small galtzagorri enclave a few miles east of Rumplank, living in giant mushroom huts crafted into comfortable homes.
- **Failure:** Nothing specific.
- **Critical failure:** A confident lie: Jakapo Towners are tricksters and vandals in grotesque living fungus huts who lure travelers off cliffs and into monster dens for fun.

## Jakapo Town events

Anyone can point the PCs to S'matzia's house, the biggest mushroom, if they ask about the map, but first they must handle her problems (p. 54).

> **🎯 Why these events exist:** A favor chain that indebts the PCs to the town and earns S'matzia's grudging respect. Each step hands off: Bolfo → S'matzia → Kuaranda → amphitheater.

### Event 5 — Thorny Situation *(Low 2)*

- **Where:** two small gardener's huts west of S'matzia's (map, p. 54).
- **Who:** **Bolfo** (she/her), galtzagorri gardener.

Read aloud (p. 54):

> *Along the path leading up to the enclave's largest mushroom house are two much smaller mushroom huts surrounded by finely manicured grass and garden beds of pretty flowers. Between the huts, a strikingly drab rose bush shakes and mumbles audibly. The source of the noise quickly becomes clear: trapped within the bush—which is plainly moving of its own accord—is a struggling galtzagorri!*

A **sanguine rose** (plant monster) took root between the huts and caught Bolfo, who underestimated it. She screams for help when she sees the PCs.

**Young Sanguine Rose** — weak sanguine rose (BB 9, 84), Creature 3. Initiative: Perception +9. Monster Parts 7/18/30 gp.
- Young and brash, it eats at Bolfo until the PCs approach, then drops her and turns on them.

**Aftermath:** Bolfo thanks them and points to S'matzia's hut to the east:

*"Please, I'm sure my mistress will reward you for your help," Bolfo says. "Just tell her you saved Bolfo's life. But tread carefully—Mistress S'matzia has quite a temper, and she doesn't care for folks interrupting her studies. Indeed, she's been banging around her hut for quite some time now, no doubt wrestling with a particularly frustrating magical challenge."*

**Treasure (without Monster Parts only):** from Bolfo's shed, a dose each of **oil of mending** and **oil of potency**.

> **🧩 Sets up:** Naming Bolfo's rescue earns 10 gp from S'matzia in Event 6. Make sure the players remember her.

### Event 6 — A Sight for Sore Eyes *(Moderate 2)*

- **Where:** S'matzia's hut, east side of the p. 54 map.
- **Who:** **S'matzia** (N female galtzagorri sage 5), too brilliant to be thrown out of town for her manners.

As the PCs approach, read aloud (p. 56):

> *A massive mushroom stands before you, easily the largest in the grove. A small stone landing leads to a set of double doors, one of which is slightly ajar. You can hear what you believe to be bottles breaking and a woman cursing from within the strange home.*

If they enter or announce themselves, someone yells, *"Whoever you are, get in here and help me!"* Heading toward the noise (east side), read aloud:

> *An elderly galtzagorri in fine robes flits about the hut, half-heartedly thrusting a wooden staff in one hand at an animated green glob of eyeballs. Small orbs of pink light swirl about the elder's free hand, their brightness pulsing as the elder flicks her wrist and fingers about in arcane gestures. Upon noticing you, the elder snaps, "Well, don't just stand there! Take care of this thing while I complete my ritual!"*

**What happened:** S'matzia, a dabbler in dangerous occult and primal magic, tried to summon a spider-shaped being of primordial ooze and got a glob of eyes and sticky ooze. Some became a slippery hazard; one chunk woke up hungry. For the summoning she unraveled ley-line threads under her hut, so she's casting an hours-long sealing ritual while fending off the ooze. If she doesn't finish, more violent oozes may pour into town.

**Puddles of Eyes** *(complex magical trap, Hazard 2)*
- **Stealth** +5 (expert).
- Sticky puddles of green goo full of alien eyeballs cover every inch of the hut. **Each space with a puddle is difficult terrain.**
- A creature in a puddle space **can't become concealed, hidden, or undetected** to the eyesore ([what those mean](#concealed-hidden-and-undetected)).
- **Disable:** three **DC 18 Occultism or Thievery** checks (trained) to scoop the three puddles into the corners. Each success clears the puddles and their difficult terrain in a **5-foot emanation** around the PC.
- **AC** 18; **Fort** +12, **Ref** +5. **Puddle HP** 10 for each of the three puddles. **Immunities:** acid, critical hits, mental, precision, unconscious.
- **Look Upon** (reaction, visual). **Trigger:** a creature enters the hut. **Effect:** the eyeballs fix on the newcomers, and the hazard rolls initiative.
- **Routine (1 action):** eyeballs roll toward and climb anyone in a puddle. Each creature in an affected square makes a **DC 18 Reflex** save.
  - **Success:** unaffected.
  - **Failure:** **clumsy 1** until it uses an Interact action to brush off the eyeballs.
  - **Critical failure:** as failure, but **clumsy 2**.

**Eyesore** — BB 29, Creature 3. Initiative: Perception +12. Monster Parts 7/18/30 gp. Chases S'matzia until the PCs step in, then targets them and anyone stuck in the puddles.

**Ending the encounter:** when the eyesore dies or **6 rounds** pass, whichever comes first, S'matzia finishes her ritual, joins in, and likely ends it with a **3rd-level *fireball*** at the eyesore.

> **💡 GM tip (from the book):** If the PCs are about to win on their own, delay S'matzia a round or two so she doesn't steal the moment (p. 57).

> **📏 Rules refresher — XP math:** Eyesore (one level up, 60 XP) + party-level complex hazard (40 XP) = 100, between Moderate (80) and Severe (120). The book says **Moderate 2**; go with it. The round-6 fireball caps the fight. See [encounter math](gm_chapter_1.md#encounter-difficulty-and-xp).

**Aftermath:** S'matzia sighs at her ruined experiment and turns to the PCs: *"Yes, well,"* she says sharply. *"What is it?"*
- Brief and aloof at any attitude; starts **indifferent**.
- Warms to magic talk; **improves one step** the moment anyone mentions **Arahosko the Red**.
- Studies the map either way, since the PCs handled the eyesore.

After a few minutes she hands it back:

*"Of course," she says, motioning toward her ruined hut, "I'll need you to gather an ingredient for me. I'm afraid I've misplaced a few things. Don't worry," she continues, "it's not far. Go see the thyalodon herder, Kuaranda, at the other end of town. Ask for a vial of silver dye. It's more expensive than it sounds, so hopefully you've got some coin on you. Well, what are you waiting for? Get to it!"*

**Treasure:** if the PCs mention saving Bolfo, S'matzia grudgingly gives them **10 gp**.

> **🔒 GM-only:** Her annoyance is an act: Bolfo is her dearest friend, unadmitted.

**Background Special Effect:** a **Poppy fanatic** PC grew up on Arahosko stories; S'matzia eagerly shares many new ones.

When the PCs are ready to see Kuaranda, go to Event 7.

### Event 7 — Inking the Map *(no combat)*

- **Where:** Kuaranda's hut and ranch, southwest corner of town (D2).
- **Who:** **[Kuaranda](gm_glossary.md#kuaranda)** (NG female galtzagorri bard 2). **Perception +7.**
- **When:** in the favor chain, **or** on arrival if returning her lost thyalodon. Either way, the PCs must already have activated the map with the sapphire scale.

Read aloud (p. 57):

> *A squat mushroom hut abuts a rustic but sturdy fence at what can only be described as a ranch. Thyalodons—slow, furry animals with docile demeanors—range around the yard, tended to by a galtzagorri herder in a cotton shirt and thick trousers. Near the yard's western end stands a five-foot-tall stone obelisk made of roughly carved lava rock. The obelisk begins to glow with golden light upon your approach, and the sapphire scale in your possession starts vibrating softly as well.*

Kuaranda chews sugar cane and drawls slowly: *"Hello there,"* she calls with a smile. *"You here for some of my famous thyaldon wool? Just spun a new batch this morning, softest wool this side of Goldcrop and will keep you warm in the fiercest of rainstorms!"*
- **Wool:** many colors and textures, **2 sp to 5 gp per skein**.

**The waymarker (Landmark 1):** the scale brightens near the obelisk. If the PCs approach it, Kuaranda notices the glow and suggests touching their "fancy blue rock" to it: *"I always thought that thing was just a weird hitchin' post. Looks like it might be important to y'all, though."*
- On contact, its light rushes into the scale, which thrums softly, and the dot disappears. **Five glowing dots remain.**

> **🧩 Pays off from Ch 1:** The scale's first landmark; every obelisk works this way ([Follow the dots](#follow-the-dots-the-waymarkers)).

**Talking to Kuaranda:** afterward she chats, noting to non-galtzagorri that Jakapo Town gets few tall folk. *"Y'all from Rumplank?"* She doesn't wait for an answer. *"I been there once. Stayed at the Rumpled Feather, if you know where that is. Really nice folk—even taught me a shanty or two."* Then she sings to a thyalodon while combing it, chattering for minutes until interrupted.
- Starts **indifferent** and isn't easily hurt, but snapping at her drops her to **unfriendly** until the PCs win her back; no business before then.
- **Winning her back:** show love for animals or offer a new tune. Hard to fake: she sees through most deception (Perception +7) and knows plenty of local songs.

> **💡 GM tip:** The Rumpled Feather is [Clamardinia Wakamarsis](gm_glossary.md#clamardinia-wakamarsis)'s tavern, rival to the Gutted Snake's barkeep. Rumplank-savvy PCs can chat about it.

**Getting the silver dye:** when the PCs finally explain, she stands and dusts off: *"Well why didn't you say so?"*
- It's treated **black thyalodon ink**, from animals fed a strict diet of certain beetles and magic herbs.
- **Price:** **10 gp** a vial, or **8 gp** (her lowest) if the PCs make her **helpful**.
- **Free**, and she's overjoyed, if the PCs returned her missing thyalodon from the Writhing Swamp or can tell her it's safe.

**Kuaranda as a companion:** if the PCs **saved her thyalodon** from the leshy husks, she asks to join.
- With **fewer than two** NPC companions, she joins now; otherwise she waits in Jakapo Town for room.
- **Either way, the PCs must bring her to Aksyanis Amphitheater** once they have the gentle whistler. She plays it.
- **On the road:** music, mending clothes and gear, tending animal companions.
- **With other companions:** likes D'marti, the scroll mold, and [Apraka Ogari](gm_glossary.md#apraka-ogari) (Chapter 3); bristles at [Ignatius Barkley](gm_glossary.md#ignatius-barkley)'s rigid, shortsighted justice.

> **⚠️ Gotcha — dye or ink:** The book's "silver dye" (Events 6–7) and mostly "silver ink" (Event 8) are the same thing.

Dye in hand, back to S'matzia: Event 8.

### Event 8 — Spellcasting with S'matzia *(ritual)*

- **Where:** S'matzia's hut, at her divining table.
- **Goal:** seal the map so it stops decaying.

S'matzia clears her table and has the PCs lay the map flat. She needs **one secondary caster**; others may help. Read aloud (p. 59):

> *The elderly galtzagorri woman flits over to a round table etched with hundreds of curly runes and covered in jars, trays, and papers. In one swift motion, she swipes everything off the table and into a crate on the floor. "Go on, then," S'matzia rasps, "lay out the map." Meanwhile, she collects occult appurtenances from around the room—many-faceted crystals, an enormous black quill, candles of all colors and sizes—and explains the ritual process. "Using the silver ink," she says, "I'll ink over every line and symbol on the map, binding them to the parchment. This will seal the map and stop its deterioration. You may help if you like: pull up a stool and hold the sheet in place, sharpen my nib, help me make sense of these runes—or just stay out of the way.*
>
> *"Now, are you ready?" S'matzia dips her quill into the well of silver ink and waggles her thick eyebrows in anticipation. "Once I touch this quill to the page, I won't be able to stop until the ritual is complete."*

**S'matzia's Silver Seal** — Ritual 2. Rare, divination.
- **Cast** 4 hours. **Cost** rare silver ink worth at least 5 gp. **Secondary casters** at least 1, up to 4.
- **Primary check:** Arcana or Occultism (trained). S'matzia uses **Occultism +12**.
- **Secondary checks:** one of Crafting, Scribing Lore, or a Lore tied to the document. For the map, that's **Indigo Isles Geography Lore**.
- **Range** 10 feet. **Target** 1 scroll, map, tome, or other portable written material.
- You permanently seal parchment or vellum with magic silver ink. It becomes nearly indestructible and stays legible forever. With **at least two** secondary casters, the secondary check gains a **+1 circumstance bonus**. With **four**, **+2**.
- **Critical success:** as success, and the item can roll saving throws against effects like *disintegrate*, using the ritual's spell DC − 10 as its save modifier.
- **Success:** the target regains HP up to its maximum and gains **Hardness 5** if its Hardness was lower. It's also protected against mundane environmental harm like severe heat, damp, or cold. Spells like *disintegrate* still affect it normally.
- **Failure:** no effect; the ritual fails.
- **Critical failure:** the ritual fails, and the target takes damage equal to half its maximum HP and becomes **broken**.

**How it plays out:**
- Uses **half the silver ink**. S'matzia casts ***mending*** on the map to protect it meanwhile.
- **On any failure** she retries, taking more time and the rest of the ink. If that fails too, the PCs buy more from Kuaranda at **10 gp per inkwell**.
- **On a critical failure** the map loses **half its maximum HP** (10).
- The decay stops if the map has **at least 1 HP** when a successful casting ends.

> **⚠️ Gotcha — how long does it take?** Narration: **1 hour**. Ritual block: **Cast 4 hours** (p. 59). The text also says the map erodes during the ritual, then has her protect it with *mending*, which usually pauses decay 5 hours and covers either. Run 1 hour of story with no HP lost, unless the map was already near 0.

> **⚠️ Gotcha — a critical failure can destroy the map:** At 10 HP or less, a critical failure's 10 HP loss leaves the map at 0, and it turns to ash. Use [Make Haste, Not Waste](#make-haste-not-waste-if-the-map-is-destroyed).

> **💡 GM tip — the DC:** None is printed. The level-2 DC is DC 16; a 2nd-level spell's is DC 18. With Occultism +12, S'matzia succeeds on a 4 or better (DC 16) or a 6 or better (DC 18). Raise it if you want the PCs' help to matter more.

When the map is safe, read aloud (p. 59):

> *As S'matzia lays the final strokes of silver ink on the map, the symbols, lines, and words on the parchment begin to glow a faint gold color. With a slight flourish, S'matzia pulls back the quill and watches the map's crumbling edges glow brighter, as if they were limned by magical flame. Yet the flames don't engulf or destroy the page, but rather wash over it like a sheen of oil, encasing the map in a translucent, shiny film. When the glowing stops after a moment, the map remains shiny and smooth, and its edges are lined in protective silver. S'matzia lifts the map from the table and, in an instant, unceremoniously crumples it into a ball in her fist. With a knowing grin, the sage untangles the wad of parchment and hands the flat sheet to you—there's not even a single crease on it. The map's self-destruction has plainly stopped.*

> **🧩 Pays off from Ch 1:** The cure the Chapter 1 cliffhanger was building to.

**Background Special Effect:** **indigo trailblazer** and **indigo historian** PCs have reason to want this ritual for maps and old documents. Given a good reason, S'matzia shoves a thick vellum book at one: *"It's all in this tome. You can read Sylvan, can't you? I don't have time to teach you myself."*
- A Sylvan reader can **Learn a Spell** to learn the silver seal ([Learn a Spell](#identify-magic-and-learn-a-spell)), but can't be **primary caster** before **level 3**.

**Next steps:** while inking, S'matzia worked out the map. She tells the PCs:
- Charge the scale at each landmark, if they didn't learn that at Kuaranda's. *"At each landmark is a magical waystone. It's clear that you must touch the sapphire scale to these waystones to gain their power. As for what the purpose of empowering the scale—I imagine that will become clear once you find the Gilded Pirate's hideout."*
- **The next landmark is the Hissing Gulch.** *"It's a ravine known for jungle mantises, which, like cicadas, return to the same place every ten years to breed en masse. You won't be able to reach the waypoint in the gulch unless you calm the angry insects first."*
- **The fix:** a particular song on a sacred harmonica, the **gentle whistler**, played at nearby **Aksyanis Amphitheater**, calms every bug in the gulch. **Kuaranda** can play it and knows the song.
- **The catch:** *"Unfortunately, an evil Jakapo outcast named Batarsi stole the gentle whistler when he fled town in rageful shame. Finding him will be easy—he's set up a vile workshop in an old wizard's tower not far from here. Actually defeating the wicked toymaker, however, will be a different matter."*

### Event 9 — Batarsi's Spy *(Low 2)*

- **What:** one of the covert minions Batarsi sends to report on his hometown, under orders to be back at the workshop **by sundown**.

A wooden figurine of a young galtzagorri in an oversized cloak and floppy hat: fine from a distance, but no galtzagorri is fooled up close.

**Toy Spy** — BB 112, Creature 3. Initiative: Stealth +11. Monster Parts 7/18/30 gp. Its parts can imbue arcane spells: *color spray*, *daze*, *ghost sound*, *glitterdust*, and *sleep*.

**Studying the spy:** a **DC 18 Crafting** check to Recall Knowledge on its remains finds evenly spaced notches by the winding gear on its wooden programming module: it's rewound daily at **sunset**, so Batarsi returns then.

> **💡 GM tip:** Let a PC notice a "galtzagorri child" tailing them around town. Deal with it now or pay later.

## Batarsi's Workshop

> **🎯 Why this scene exists:** The chapter's dungeon: explore an empty tower, then ambush the boss at sunset. Golomor (E3) adds a moral choice.

**Batarsi**, an outcast who fled Jakapo Town in shame, runs his "toy shop" from an abandoned wizard's tower deep in the woods. Many years ago a gnome transmuter made harmless experiments and wooden constructs (long since wandered off) here; Batarsi builds terrifying dolls, twisted wooden spies, and other malicious constructs (p. 61).

**How it runs:**
- S'matzia, Kuaranda, and many others know the way. With the map sealed, the PCs can **rest first**.
- **Batarsi and the whistler are out** until **sunset** (learnable from the toy spy or E5).
- The PCs can explore, then hide and ambush him. The book puts his fight after exploration.

**Golomor** — shadow thief (BB 90), Creature 2. Initiative: Perception +7 or Stealth +10. Monster Parts 5/12/22 gp.

### E4 — Workshop *(Trivial 2)*

> *Dolls, stuffed animals, wooden figurines, and other tiny toys fill every nook and cranny of this crowded workshop.*

**Weak Toy Spy** — BB 9, 112, Creature 2. Initiative: Perception +7. Monster Parts 5/12/22 gp. With it here, E4 is a **Moderate 2** fight.

**Treasure (without Monster Parts only):** an **onyx dog** wondrous figurine, a **levered crowbar**, and a **hat of the magi**.

### E5 — Quarters *(Trivial 2)*

> *A hay-filled bed sits against the northwestern window. South of that, a blackened wood stove fills most of the western wall. In the southeast section, two dressers turned into worktables flank a stained-glass door to a balcony.*

**Mrs. Sloth** — elite springjaw doll (BB 9, 108), Creature 2. Initiative: Perception +9. **Speed** 20 feet, **fly** 20 feet. Monster Parts 5/12/22 gp.
- Batarsi's most cherished construct: a slightly oversized galtzagorri matron with gray curls, a pudgy flushed face, soft edges, and custom palm-leaf wings.

> **🔒 GM-only:** Batarsi never knew his mother, who died when he was small; Mrs. Sloth is his guess at her. A PC who works this out gains a lever on him, or a reason for pity.

### E6 — Balcony

> *This rickety wooden balcony juts out from the rest of the tower about 30 feet above the ground below.*

Batarsi lands his beetle here every night at sundown unless he has reason not to. Event 10 likely happens here.

### Event 10 — Sunset Showdown *(Moderate 2)*

Batarsi roams the jungle by day on **Goosebark**, his silk-tailed beetle, gathering crafting materials, nobody knows where. At **sundown** he comes home to craft and rest.

**Creatures:** at sunset they flutter over the treetops and land on the **balcony (E6)** unless he suspects the tower is compromised. A winged sprite, he needs no mount; he rides one out of vanity.

**Batarsi** — Creature 2, unique CE Tiny fey sprite. Male galtzagorri hunter (BB 35).
- **Initiative** Perception +7
- **Items:** basic crafter's book, galtzagorri hook spear, **gentle whistler**, longspear
- **Melee** longspear +8, 1d8+4 piercing
- **Tiny:** he must enter a target's space to use his hook spear, but his longspear's 5 feet of reach lets him attack from an adjacent square or from Goosebark's back ([Tiny creatures and reach](#tiny-creatures-reach-and-mounts)).

**Goosebark** — silk-tailed beetle (BB 98), Creature 2. Initiative: Perception +7. **Speed** 35 feet, **fly** 25 feet. Monster Parts 5/12/22 gp.

**Tactics:** a coward. At **15 HP or less** he fakes begging for mercy, then makes a surprise attack the moment the PCs relax or turn their backs. He'd rather die than submit to "weaklings" like them.

> **⚠️ Gotcha:** Tell the players he's begging; let them choose. Accept, and he strikes when they're distracted. Stay watchful, and he gets no opening.

**Aftermath:** the PCs take the whistler from Batarsi, dead or unconscious. Almost nobody in Jakapo Town will mourn him. Next: collect Kuaranda in Jakapo Town and head to the amphitheater.

### Gentle Whistler *(Item 4)*

Uncommon, enchantment, magical. **Price** 90 gp. **Usage** held in 2 hands. **Bulk** negligible.
- A sleek golden harmonica decorated with swirling patterns like insect wings, legs, and bodies.
- **Activate** (2 actions; envision, Interact). **Frequency** once per day. You play a short, quiet melody that soothes the minds of nearby insects and other bugs. It casts ***calm emotions*** at **DC 18**. Affected animals with an Intelligence modifier of −5 take a **−2 circumstance penalty** to the save.

Per the item appendix, a Jakapo Town folk hero supposedly played the original, not knowing its maker (p. 379).

> **⚠️ Gotcha — the item versus the song:** The daily activation is a short melody. The amphitheater song is a story event (5 minutes, whole chasm); use the amphitheater rules.

## Big Show at Aksyanis Amphitheater

- **Where:** a historic amphitheater overlooking the Hissing Chasm. Map on p. 65.
- **Who:** Kuaranda, or whoever plays the whistler.
- **Goal:** keep the player safe for 5 minutes of music.

Named for **Aksyanis**, a famed orpok conductor and polymath whose performances were said to be unmatched for order and mathematical precision. No living musician could meet her demands, so, weary of the Isles, she left (supposedly Alacar too) to find otherworldly musicians who could play her songs (p. 65).

**Getting there:** any local can point the way ([Travel distances](#travel-distances)).

**The song:**
- Calming the mantises takes **5 minutes** of **nonstop** whistler playing.
- The music does the **opposite to other wildlife**, enraging some creatures against its source, the party. Kuaranda **warns the PCs** en route.
- Predators know placid mantises mean easy food, so they come in **waves**.

**Timing** ([rounds and minutes](#rounds-minutes-and-the-wave-timer)): first wave at **3 minutes**; then **2 minutes** defending Kuaranda nonstop, with **exactly 2 rounds** to regroup between waves.

**If the song is interrupted:** waves stop and attackers calm; depending on the moment, the current creature may fight on or retreat. The PCs must **restart the song**, resetting the 5-minute timer and bringing more waves (the same ones or similar substitutes).

> **⚠️ Gotcha (from the book):** Don't let restarts farm extra XP or treasure (p. 66). Track awards.

| Wave | Threat | Creatures | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Angry Assassin** | Low 2 | Swarm assassin | Enraged by the music |
| **2. Blood Suckers** | Low 2 | 3 weak blood mice | Come from every direction |
| **3. Grotesque Nest** | Moderate 2 | Rottskal | Sneaks up disguised as a bird's nest |
| **4. Beak and Fur** | Moderate 2 (book awards 140 XP) | Parrotbear | Survive 4 rounds |

### Wave 1 — Angry Assassin *(Low 2)*

The music soothes mantises but enrages, even pains, some swarms. A **swarm assassin** bound for Rumplank and its next client and target hears it and beelines for the stage to destroy the source.

**Swarm Assassin** — BB 109, Creature 3. Initiative: Perception +10. Monster Parts 7/18/30 gp.

### Wave 2 — Blood Suckers *(Low 2)*

[Blood mice](gm_glossary.md#blood-mouse), furious at the music's effect on mantises (their favorite food), pour in from every direction.

**Weak Blood Mice (3)** — BB 9, 14, Creature 0. Initiative: Perception +6. Monster Parts 2.25/5/9 gp each.

> **🧩 Pays off from Ch 1:** The jungle blood mice Pekipak warned about, stirred up by music as he said.

### Wave 3 — Grotesque Nest *(Moderate 2)*

A **rottskal**, a carnivorous egg-loving aberration, has grown fat on stray mantises, which draw the birds its food chain depends on. Sensing a threat to its meals, it investigates.
- **Sneaks up**, Striding closer over several rounds, then sits still to **Impersonate a bird's nest**.
- Hopes curious PCs come close, giving it the first-round edge.
- Attacks all-out if it thinks it's spotted.

**Rottskal** — p. 393, Creature 4. Initiative: Perception +14 or Deception +14. Monster Parts 12/27/50 gp.

> **💡 GM tip:** With only 2 rounds between waves, a "bird's nest" appearing at the stage's edge is suspicious. Reward a PC who checks it from a distance.

### Wave 4 — Beak and Fur *(Moderate 2)*

The last attacker, a bloodthirsty **parrotbear** (she/her), is furious at the chaos in her jungle. The book warns it's likely hard for 2nd-level PCs unrested since the waves began.

**Parrotbear** — BB 72, Creature 4. Initiative: Perception +13. Monster Parts 12/27/50 gp.

**Survive, don't win:**
- Kuaranda is nearly done: protect her for **4 rounds**. If your tracked time leaves more than 4 rounds, she finishes early.
- The PCs can tell she's close. Roll a **secret DC 15 Performance** check for each PC trained in Performance; success reveals exactly how many rounds remain.
- When she stops, the parrotbear calms, licks her paws, and lumbers off.
- **Even if most or all PCs fall,** they succeed if Kuaranda goes unattacked for 4 rounds, and she stabilizes survivors.
- **XP:** the full **140 XP** for lasting until she finishes (the book: halfway between Severe and Extreme).
- **Monster Parts:** the full award, even if she walks away.

> **⚠️ Gotcha — the label and the award disagree:** **Moderate 2** matches one level-4 creature (80 XP). The text awards **140 XP**, Severe-to-Extreme, since the PCs fight unrested after three waves. Use 140 XP.

### Melody's End

When all waves fall or **5 minutes** pass, whichever comes first, the chasm's mantises calm. The PCs can descend to the Landmark 2 obelisk and follow the map ([The six landmarks](#the-six-landmarks)).

**XP:** **30 XP** each for finishing the melody and clearing out the mantises.
