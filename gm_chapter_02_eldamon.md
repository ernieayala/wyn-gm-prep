## Encounter 2.1 — Squishberry in Bolfo's garden

**Insert after:** [Event 5 — Thorny Situation](gm_chapter_02.md#event-5--thorny-situation-low-2), once the rose is dead and Bolfo is free · **Where/when:** the beds between [Bolfo's](gm_glossary.md#bolfo) two huts, west of S'matzia's house. Run it right after the fight or after the map is sealed. Level 2 (Eldamon p. 264).

> **🎯** Home ground and a shared enemy. Wood eldamon live where plants thrive (Eldamon p. 30). The rose wrecked this one's garden, so it already likes the PCs when it meets them.

> *Something stirs in the wreck of the flower bed. A snail no bigger than a coconut slides out from under the torn stems, its shell a pale strawberry dotted with red seeds. It glides up to the gardener, considers her bleeding arm, and begins earnestly pressing a fistful of damp soil onto the cut.*

- **Setup:** For years it ate the strawberries Bolfo (she/her) leaves out "for the garden luck."
- **Find:** no check. It comes out on its own. Seeking the beds (Perception DC 16) finds no others.
- **Recognize:** secret Nature, Farming Lore, or Eldamon Lore, DC 18.
  - Success: a wood eldamon. Its garden is its whole world. It nurtures people the same way, with less skill.
  - Crit success adds: helping it tend a diverse garden, or a few rare plants, evolves it.
  - Crit fail lie: "Strawberry snails are garden pests. They hollow out fruit and lay eggs in it. Squash it before it spreads."
- **Befriend:** Request (Diplomacy) DC 16. A PC who recognized it can roll connection DC 11 instead:
  - Replant the beds (Nature, Survival, or Farming Lore): spend 10 minutes resetting torn stems with Bolfo.
  - Bring a rare plant (Nature): the magical herbs Kuaranda's thyalodons eat (p. 58, after [Event 7](gm_chapter_02.md#event-7--inking-the-map-no-combat)). Any odd plant from Ch 1's swamp also works.
  - Let it fuss, then teach it (Medicine): a wounded PC lets it pack soil on a cut. Then the PC shows it how a bandage goes on.
- **Leaves if:** someone tramples the beds, uproots plants, or brings fire near. It's weak to fire. Harvesting the rose is fine.
- **Outcomes:**
  - Crit success: befriends and climbs onto the pack at once, tucking leaves into gear.
  - Success: befriends after saying goodbye to the beds.
  - Failure: goes back to the beds. Retry after a connection approach or once the map is sealed.
  - Crit failure: hides in its shell while that PC is near. Another PC can try.
- **Rolls (Eldamon p. 32–35):** 5 31–80 Typical (Common, Muan) · 6 01–50 No Variation · 7 59–60 Different Body or Hair Color (white shell) · 8 63 Meddlesome · 9a 91–99, 9b 82–84 hoards seeds in its friend's gear.
- **XP:** 20 each. This is a suggestion. The book gives none.
- **⚠️** The map loses 1 HP an hour until Event 8 ends ([map rules](gm_chapter_02.md#disintegrating-magic)). The greeting takes a minute. The PCs may want to replant for an hour. If so, the snail can wait with Bolfo. It's still in the garden after the map is sealed, and the PCs can come back then. Use level-3 DCs if they return at level 3.
- **🔒** The seeds are harmless. Let the player find them one pocket at a time.
- **🧩** It's a defender. It has Retreat Into Strawberry (+2 AC while immobilized) and Green Thumb to sow extra squares (Eldamon p. 264). Any PC can befriend it. Others can help with a connection approach.
- **💡** If the PCs mention the snail to S'matzia in Event 6, she sniffs that Bolfo "always did have more luck than sense." Use it or skip it.

## Encounter 2.2 — Cephalittle at Aksyanis Amphitheater

**Insert after:** [Melody's End](gm_chapter_02.md#melodys-end), once the last wave is over · **Where/when:** the stage of [Aksyanis Amphitheater](gm_glossary.md#aksyanis-amphitheater), in the hush after the song. Kuaranda (NG female galtzagorri bard 2) is still on stage. Level 2 (Eldamon p. 214).

> **🎯** Melding explains the strange detail: the stage's carved lines are the eldamon. Cephalittles seek geometric places (Eldamon p. 214). Aksyanis performed with mathematical precision (p. 65). Music bouncing off those curves draws it out.

> *The last note fades over the chasm. In the hush, the carved lines on the stage floor begin to glow faintly, then peel up off the stone and gather into a small creature: a land-going octopus traced all over with a fine grid of shimmering lines. It sets the tips of three arms at three points on the stage, cocks its head toward the top row of seats, and starts scratching figures into the dust with a fourth.*

- **Setup:** It ignores the PCs at first.
- **Find:** no check once it unmelds. While melded, it can't be detected (Eldamon p. 12). Perception DC 16: its figures are arcs from stage to seats.
- **Recognize:** secret Nature, Arcana, or Eldamon Lore, DC 18.
  - Success: a force eldamon obsessed with geometry. It does the math even when shown an easier way.
  - Crit success adds: a great geometric breakthrough evolves it.
  - Crit fail lie: "That's a deep-sea octopus washed up by the storm. Get it to water or it'll dry out." (It lives on land.)
- **Befriend:** Request (Diplomacy) DC 16. A PC who recognized it can roll connection DC 11 instead:
  - Measure the bowl (Crafting or Engineering Lore): find the spot where a whisper carries to the top seat.
  - Test the echo (Performance): sound a note from its marked spot while it times the echo. Kuaranda can play it if no PC wants to.
  - Draw it a circle (Arcana or Occultism): sketch a magic circle or rune diagram beside its figures. It corrects the angles, delighted.
- **Leaves if:** someone scuffs its figures or smashes or defaces the carvings. Saying "There's an easier way" only makes it ignore that PC.
- **Outcomes:**
  - Crit success: befriends and proudly shows where the sound focuses.
  - Success: befriends after finishing its figure.
  - Failure: retry with a connection approach before the party leaves.
  - Crit failure: melds into the floor for the day. Another PC can try tomorrow, if the party returns.
- **Rolls (Eldamon p. 32–35):** 5 31–80 Typical (Common, Aetheran) · 6 01–50 No Variation · 7 77–78 Elemental Lines (force grid) · 8 71 Pedantic · 9a 91–99, 9b 73–75 obsessed with the mathematics in Aksyanis's compositions (p. 65).
- **XP:** 20 each (suggestion), on top of the book's 30 for the melody.
- **⚠️** Don't run it during the waves. They're only 2 rounds apart ([wave timer](gm_chapter_02.md#rounds-minutes-and-the-wave-timer)). It stays melded until the song ends. It doesn't help or hinder the fight.
- **🔒** The obsession is flavor. It knows no lost scores and doesn't know where Aksyanis went. Eldamon can't succeed at Recall Knowledge for the PCs ([What eldamon won't do](gm_chapter_01_eldamon.md#what-eldamon-wont-do)).
- **🧩** Locus of Force: +20 feet force power range, +5 feet to bursts, cones, and emanations. Locus Arms: +2 vs Shove and Trip (Eldamon p. 214). Any PC can befriend it. Others can help with a connection approach. Puzzle rooms and Poppy's trials can pay off its evolution. The Chapter 4 doc's cephalittle sighting is a different cephalittle.

## Encounter 2.3 — Bubows on Never Lonely Hill

**Insert after:** [Landmark 3 — Never Lonely Hill](gm_chapter_02.md#landmark-3--never-lonely-hill), after the PCs touch the scale to the obelisk and meet [Krissia](gm_glossary.md#krissia) (LN female orpok roadster 3) · **Where/when:** the top of [Never Lonely Hill](gm_glossary.md#never-lonely-hill), midday after a rain squall, while Krissia eats lunch. Two bubows, level 2 (Eldamon p. 164).

> **🎯** The hill's name sets the scene. Nobody climbs Never Lonely Hill without finding someone there. Bubows chase rainbows after rain (Eldamon p. 164).

> *The rain has moved off to the west. Mist rises from the treetops, and in it hangs a rainbow so close you could almost walk out onto it. Two shapes dart through the colors like kites with no strings, ribbon tails snapping behind them. One dives through the red band, hooting. The other hangs at the edge of the violet, watching you.*

- **Find, Recognize:** they perch on the obelisk when the rainbow fades. Perception DC 16 sees owls with ribbon tails.
  - Recall (Nature or Eldamon Lore) DC 18: air eldamon who love colors. Colors stand for emotions to them. Never grab a tail uninvited.
  - Crit success adds: a magnificent rainbow evolves one.
  - Crit fail lie: "Kite owls want you to catch their tails. Grab one and run." (That gets you pecked.)
- **Befriend:** Request (Diplomacy) DC 16 per PC. Connection DC 11 approaches:
  - Spray a waterskin into a rainbow with the sun at your back (Nature, or Arcana with *prestidigitation*).
  - Stream Kuaranda's dyed thyalodon wool (Event 7) in the wind (Crafting or Performance).
  - Hold out a hand until it offers its ribbon (Nature).
- **Leaves if:** someone grabs a tail or turns cruel nearby.
- **Outcomes:**
  - Crit success: offers its ribbon.
  - Success: lands on a shoulder.
  - Failure: retry in an hour.
  - Crit failure: avoids that PC today.
- **Rolls:**
  - Bold one: 5 31–80 Typical (Sussuran, Common) · 6 01–50 No Variation · 7 63–64 Vibrant Coloration · 8 68 Outgoing · 9a 01–90 no secret.
  - Quiet one: 5 11–30 Young (Sussuran only) · 6 01–50 No Variation · 7 65–66 Soft Coloration · 8 42 Emotive (feathers fluff and flatten with nearby moods) · 9a 91–99, 9b 52–54 wants a trainer to phase it in so it can taste Krissia's lunch. Only a trainer can phase it in.
- **XP:** 10 each (suggestion), on top of the landmark XP.
- **⚠️** A touched ribbon shares emotions (Eldamon p. 164). Ask the player what the bubow feels from their PC. Don't decide it for them.
- **💡** A suggested Krissia line: *"Those two? They're always up here when the rain stops."* When she shudders at the mountain (p. 51), the quiet one's feathers flatten in sympathy. It knows nothing about the mountain.

## Flavor sighting — Tairopoof under the balcony

**Insert after:** [E6 — Balcony](gm_chapter_02.md#e6--balcony), while the PCs explore or set the ambush before sunset. One tairopoof, level 2 (Eldamon p. 160), under the eaves 30 feet up.

- Perception DC 16 from the balcony or stairs spots a bat with wings of moving air. Once seen, it hides its face and squeaks an apology.
- 🎯 Tairopoofs are anxious about their looks (Eldamon p. 160). This one lives among Batarsi's dolls, so it's sure it's as scary as they are.
- It doesn't help with Batarsi or the ambush. It knows nothing about the tower. It flees when Goosebark's wings sound at sunset. **Rolls:** 5 11–30 Young (Sussuran only) · 7 01–50 Standard Appearance · 8 21 Apologetic · 9a 01–90 no secret.
- **If a player falls for it:** Recall DC 16, Request (Diplomacy, a kind word) DC 16, or connection DC 11 (Performance or Nature: show it the dolls are the scary ones). It evolves by overcoming its anxiety about its body and about scaring others. No XP.
