## Encounter 9.1 — The bakindle at the Show of Skill

**Insert after:** [2C. Show of Skill (p. 225)](gm_chapter_09.md#2c-show-of-skill-p-225) · **Where/when:** beside the stage, close to Ash's juggling, during the Show of Skill.

> **🎯** A memory-keeper meets a festival full of new acts. Its condition is "Helping bakindle make new memories and mnemonics" (Eldamon p. 204). This festival gives it plenty of both. Its Firepower gives +2 fire damage once it's expert in power DCs (Eldamon p. 204).

> *Ash's torches spin higher, and so does something beside the stage: a small elephant made of embers, trunk swaying with every throw. Around its neck hangs a necklace of glowing beads. Each time the crowd gasps, one bead flares brighter.*

- **Find:** no check.
- **Recognize:** DC 26. Success: bakindle, fire. It seeks new experiences and stores its best memories in its necklace. It will stop at nothing to get the necklace back. Crit success adds the condition and weaknesses (cold, earth, water). Failure: some kind of fire eldamon. Crit fail lie: "Bakindles never forget a slight. Don't let it see you fumble."
- **Befriend:** Request DC 24. A PC who recognized it can roll connection DC 19 instead:
  - Give it a moment worth a bead (Performance). A PC performing in the Show of Skill can roll once against two DCs: DC 25 for the act and DC 19 for the bakindle (suggestion).
  - Teach it a mnemonic (Arcana or Occultism).
  - Tell it your best adventure memory (Society).
- **Leaves if:** anyone touches its necklace.
- **Outcomes:** crit success joins and gives the act a bead / success joins / failure retries after the next event / crit failure won't join that PC today.
- **Rolls:** Table 5 Typical (Pyric, Common) · 6 No Variation · 7 Elemental Gems (the beads) · 8 Energetic · 9a no secret.
- **XP:** 20 per PC if anyone befriends it.
- 🔒 It has no link to today's fires and gives no tell about Korog. Ash's act is only a juggling show.

## Festival contest — The Barrel Square bout

**Insert after:** [2E. Poppy's Prize (p. 226–227)](gm_chapter_09.md#2e-poppys-prize-p-226227), before the Rum Run, or any gap before Event 16 · **Where:** [Barrel Square](gm_glossary.md#barrel-square), beside the Gilded Pirate.

> **🎯** The matchups force teamwork. Puff shrugs off earth and wood, and it is weak to cold and electricity. Quizzle resists light and mental. So the PCs' two picks must cover each other.

> *A chalk ring has been scuffed onto the cobbles beside the Gilded Pirate, and the crowd stands three deep around it. In the middle, a broad orpok in a fish-scale apron grins at you. A moth made of drifting fluff bobs over their shoulder, and a small pink brain on stubby legs stands at their feet, muttering facts about Poppy von Barnacle to nobody in particular. "Seven siblings, and not one of them will battle me!" the orpok shouts, to loud protest from seven orpoks in matching shirts. "Heroes of Rumplank! I hear you've got eldamon friends. Pair match? Two of yours against my two?"*

- **Setup:** This bout is an addition to the book's festival. Most towns have rough arenas, and some have only chalk marks on grass (Eldamon p. 16–17). **Okko Bramblesnout** (they/them) is an [orpok](gm_glossary.md#orpok) fishmonger and level 8 trainer from Seaview. Okko fields **Puff** (larvapor, air) and **Quizzle** (cerebella, mind). Okko has just lost Poppy's Prize.

### Step 1: venue, stakes, and ruleset (Eldamon p. 17–19)

**Venue:** chalk ring, 60-foot radius, flat cobbles, no terrain or elemental field.

**Stakes:** friendly, for bragging rights. Suggested prize: a mystery pie for each winning trainer, and a painted coral eldamon charm (5 gp) from Okko's family.

| Menu | Choice |
|---|---|
| A: Starting position | 30 feet apart (60 if both agree, otherwise coin toss). Leaving the ring on purpose eliminates |
| B: Fielded | Pair format: two per side at once |
| C: Team size | Two per side, no substitutions |
| D: Defeat | Out at 0 HP |
| E: Victory | Defeat both foes, or a trainer forfeits |
| F: Evolution | Allowed. It won't come up before 11th level (p. 40) |
| Arena rules | None |

**Push-outs (optional):** the PCs can propose that forcing a foe out of the ring eliminates it. Water and force eldamon like this rule (Eldamon p. 18). Okko agrees to anything.

### Steps 2 to 5: running it

Both sides choose eldamon in secret, then reveal them together. Run normal combat. Each eldamon rolls its own initiative. Expect two or three rounds. For scale, Gelid Touch deals 10d8 cold at level 8, against about 60 HP. Afterward, a minute phased out restores all HP to any eldamon not dropped by a death effect (Eldamon p. 40, 158). Neither of Okko's eldamon is a death eldamon, so everyone is fresh for area S.

### Okko's eldamon (stat guidance)

Level 8, Advanced Training row (Eldamon p. 158–159), 7th-level power list. Check these against the entries before play. If the PCs' eldamon get their friend's runes and *handwraps* bonuses (Eldamon p. 157–158), give Okko's eldamon the same (suggestion).

| | Puff (larvapor, air, Eldamon p. 162) | Quizzle (cerebella, mind, rare, Eldamon p. 250) |
|---|---|---|
| Attributes | Str +0, Dex +3, Con +3, Int +2, Wis +4, Cha +1 | Str +1, Dex +2, Con +3, Int +3, Wis +4, Cha +0 |
| HP / AC | 60 / 25 | 60 / 24 |
| Fort / Ref / Will | +13 / **+15** / +14 | +13 / +12 / **+16** |
| Perception | +14, low-light vision, scent (imprecise) 60 feet | +14, low-light vision |
| Power attack / DC | +16 / 26 | +16 / 26 |
| Strike | wing +13 (agile, finesse), 1d4 slashing | ridge +12 (agile, finesse), 1d4+1 slashing |
| Eldamon skills (expert) | Acrobatics +15, Stealth +15, Survival +16 | Arcana +15, Nature +16, Occultism +15 |
| Speed | 10 feet, fly 25 feet | 25 feet |
| Resistances 8 | air, body, earth, plant, wood | electricity, light, mental, teleportation |
| Weaknesses 8 | cold, electricity, metal, teleportation | darkness, sonic, spirit, time |
| Abilities | **Enveloping Air:** air lines can be +10 feet; cones, bursts, emanations +5 feet | Magical Disruption and Brainpower do nothing here. Nobody casts spells, and Brainpower works only phased in |
| Powers | Disrupting Gust (p. 72), Whirlwind (p. 73), Wind Cutter (p. 73), Dispersing Retreat (p. 76), Rushing Refresh (p. 76) | Contagious Folly (p. 131), Psychic Thrust (p. 130), Stupefying Inception (p. 130), Psychic Scream (p. 134), Rush of Insight (p. 134) |

**Matchups:**
- **Puff** resists earth and wood eldamon. Its air slashing also hits their weakness, since both elements are weak to air. Ice and electricity eldamon hit its weaknesses.
- **Quizzle** resists electricity, light, mental, and teleportation. It is weak to darkness, sonic, spirit, and time. Light eldamon are weak to its mental damage.
- Life powers deal vitality, which harms neither foe (Eldamon p. 150). So life eldamon play support. Water and force eldamon push foes farther if push-outs are on (Eldamon p. 18). If the PCs have only one eldamon fit to field, Okko's family can talk a sibling into a one-on-one match.
- **XP:** 20 per PC, win or lose.

> **💡 House rule:** If an eldamon turned a PC down earlier today, it watches the bout. If that PC's side wins, or loses with good grace, lower that PC's retry DC by 2. The book has no such rule.

> **⚠️ Gotcha:** The bout can't delay the cult. Run it before Event 16, which starts when the PCs wrap up the festival (p. 220). If it drags, end it when the first eldamon drops.

> **⚠️ Gotcha:** Puff flies. Before the bout, decide how flying works using the flying-eldamon sidebar (Eldamon p. 160).

> **🧩 Sets up:** Puff dreams of something soft and fluffy (Eldamon p. 162). If Okko is at [Event 16](gm_chapter_09.md#scene-3--event-16-divine-visitors), Puff dives into Amon's snow and comes out crushed. Quizzle wants to quiz every PC on Poppy trivia. It evolves on "Helping cerebella master trivia to an excessive degree" (Eldamon p. 250).

## Encounter 9.2 — The lemiure chorus

**Insert after:** [2A. Peerless Ruby Tall Tale Trial (p. 220–221)](gm_chapter_09.md#2a-peerless-ruby-tall-tale-trial-p-220221), before 2B · **Where/when:** the trial, in the noon sun.

> **🎯** The event itself is what they want. Lemiures learn "from numerous people with different viewpoints" (Eldamon p. 234). At this trial, each teller takes up the last tale.

> *Between tales, three lemur-like creatures with glass prisms in their brows hop along the benches, each settling beside whoever is talking. When the story changes hands, they change seats. The noon sun runs through their prisms and scatters tiny rainbows across the teller's face. One of them turns and looks at you, as if waiting to hear what you think.*

- **Find:** Perception DC 19. A teller needs no roll.
- **Recognize:** DC 24. They weigh other viewpoints, avoid snap judgments, and want friends to weigh in. Crit success adds the condition and weaknesses (darkness, mental, shadow, sonic). Crit fail lie: "Out-talk everyone and one will follow you."
- **Befriend:** Request DC 24, or DC 19 with one of these:
  - Performance: retell the last tale from another view.
  - Society: introduce it to people from three islands.
- **Leaves if:** someone makes snap calls for the group or mocks a teller.
- **Outcomes:** (crit) success joins / failure retries after the next event / crit failure won't join that PC today.
- **XP:** 20 per PC if anyone befriends one.

**Rolls:**
- *Eldest:* Elemental Envoy (Mind), Luxan and Astral · No Variation · Shifting Colors · Patient · once friends with a long-gone Trailblazer mapmaker.
- *Middle:* Typical, Luxan and Common · No Variation · Heterochromia · Curious · no secret.
- *Youngest:* Young, Luxan only · Glowing (it doesn't glow) · Standard · Indecisive · no secret.

> **🧩 Sets up:** At Event 16, Amon and Castilli look like the same ancestry as whoever is watching (p. 233). A lemiure will pester every PC to hear what they saw.

## Encounter 9.3 — The soleo at Monbak's bedside

**Insert after:** [T3. Monbak's Bedroom](gm_chapter_09.md#t3-monbaks-bedroom), before [What happens if the PCs lose?](gm_chapter_09.md#what-happens-if-the-pcs-lose) · **Where/when:** after T2, guarding [Monbak](gm_glossary.md#monbak).

> **🎯** This is one clean win in the losing half of the chapter. The soleo wants to "understand the true meaning of courage or perform a heroic act of true bravery" (Eldamon p. 238). So it joins whoever is brave for Monbak. Phased out, it can't carry him or fight (Eldamon p. 12). It stays anyway.

> *Behind the curtain, a steady gold light holds back the smoke. A lion cub no bigger than a house cat stands on the narrow bed, its mane bright as noon. Its legs are braced, and they're shaking. It looks at the flames beyond the doorway, then down at the battered g'mayun on the bed, and it doesn't move.*

- **Find:** during T2, Perception DC 19 sees the glow. Seeing it changes nothing in T2: Monbak is still in the fire zone. After T2, no check is needed.
- **Recognize:** DC 26. It feels fear and holds firm for a worthy cause. Crit success adds the condition and the legend of a soleonidas holding a pass against an army almost 1,000 times larger. Crit fail lie: "Soleos love daredevils."
- **Befriend:** it won't leave until Monbak is safe. Request DC 24, or DC 19 with one of these:
  - Athletics: carry him out.
  - Medicine: tend him.
  - Survival: show the exit you scouted.
- **Leaves if:** someone grandstands or abandons Monbak, or the PCs lose and Monbak dies (p. 241).
- **Outcomes:** (crit) success joins once Monbak is safe / failure retries in [Scene 6](gm_chapter_09.md#scene-6--into-thin-air) if the party takes the king's plea / crit failure won't join that PC.
- **Rolls:** Typical (Luxan, Common) · No Variation · Sparkly · Dependable · no secret.
- **XP:** 30 per PC if someone befriends it. Anyone can help by carrying Monbak out.

> **🔒** It knows nothing of what Falarni wanted destroyed. It never looked at Monbak's books (Eldamon p. 13).

## Encounter 9.4 — A luciola in the gloom

**Insert after:** [Concluding the chapter](gm_chapter_09.md#concluding-the-chapter) · **Where/when:** the PCs' rest after the nobbler fight, in a dark cave tunnel or a Rumplank alley.

> **🎯** The luciola is an optimist who needs realism (Eldamon p. 236). It gives the players a reason to say the loss out loud.

> *A pale blue glow crawls along the tunnel wall: a fat little glow worm, its tail shining like a lantern. It stops at the nearest of you, looks up, and chirps something that sounds a lot like "Cheer up! You found the trail, didn't you?"*

- Aim it at whoever takes the loss hardest. It offers oversized hope: they'll catch up tomorrow, and everyone will be fine.
- A PC who answers with the truth gives it pause. It then offers something smaller and truer.
- **Rolls:** Typical (Luxan, Common) · No Variation · Elemental Lines · Wishful · no secret.
- It doesn't join on its own, but anyone can befriend it (suggestion): Recall 26, Request 24, or DC 19 Performance (a hopeful, honest story).
- **XP:** 10 per PC if it joins.

> **🔒** It knows nothing about the cult or the "abduction." Don't let it hint at Kulupi.
