## Encounter 8.1 — The kuddle by the dead teakni (kuddle)

**Insert after:** [R5 — Library](gm_chapter_8.md#r5--library-moderate-7), once the living library falls · **Where/when:** the west wall's soggy book mound, by the dead teakni sneak. In the usual order (R5 before R11) the flood clock hasn't started, so there's time for a long hug.

> **🎯** Melding explains the odd detail: the kuddle lived inside the teakni, so the corpse gets a friend who misses it, set against the R11 hydrojinxer's shrug.

> *Beside the dead elemental, the puddle of book pulp is moving. Something clear as glass, all little arms, is wrapped around the water as if it were holding a friend. Where its arms pass, the gray pulp settles and the water runs clear.*

- **Setup:** Kuddles want long hugs, so they share phase with a trainer or meld into a creature to get one (Eldamon p. 258). The meld ended when the teakni died, so it hugs the leftover puddle clean. Any PC can help with the puddle or the remains.
- **Find:** DC 23 (24) Perception to Seek. Its clear body hides in the water.
- **Recognize:** secret DC 28 (29) Nature, Arcana, or a relevant Lore such as Eldamon Lore (DC 25 at level 5). Success: rare cuttlefish eldamon that wants long hugs, melds into creatures to get them, and cleans water by hugging. Failure: a little water eldamon. Crit success adds that it's grieving, and sharing phase gives it the real thing. Crit fail lie: kuddles hollow out what they hug, and that killed the teakni.
- **Befriend:** Request DC 23 (24), connection DC 18 (19). Or set it to the teakni's level, Creature 5 (p. 401), and the trainer trains it up later (Eldamon p. 28): Request DC 20, connection DC 15. If missing a rare friend would sting, [skip the Request](gm_chapter_1_eldamon.md#variant-skipping-the-request).
  - Hug (Diplomacy): hold still in a hug for 10 minutes. A trainer offering to share phase rolls the same check.
  - Help it clean (Nature or Survival): scoop out the pulp so its arms can work.
  - Honor the dead (Medicine or Religion): lay out the teakni's remains with care.
- **Leaves if:** a quick pat and a hurry, or treating the body as trash.
- **Outcomes:** crit success befriends and it wraps the PC's arm all day / success befriends / failure back to the puddle, another approach can try / crit failure it won't come.
- **Rolls:** Table 5 Typical (Thalassic, Common) · 6 none · 7 Translucent Body · 8 Loving · 9b 16–18 an unkept promise to its last friend.
- **XP:** 20 per PC if anyone befriends it (suggestion).
- 🔒 It promised the teakni it would be there when the sea came back into the temple, and can't explain the teaknis' plan (Eldamon p. 156). The flooding after R17 thrills it, as flavor only; it never pushes the PCs to help the teaknis.
- ⚠️ If the kuddle comes along to R11, the hydrojinxer still shrugs about the dead sneak, as the book says. The kuddle doesn't change her attitude.
- 🧩 Evolves on "Helping kuddle perfect the art of hugging." (Eldamon p. 258).

## Encounter 8.2 — The keeper of the empty vault (pangeolil)

**Insert after:** [R15 — Empty Vault](gm_chapter_8.md#r15--empty-vault) · **Where/when:** the mountain pedestal, past the [R13](gm_chapter_8.md#r13--vault-landing-trivial-7) vault ward. Keep it short if the flood clock runs. One pangeolil, a rare earth eldamon (Eldamon p. 192).

> **🎯** Repurpose a dead room: R15's harmless illusion becomes the eldamon's cover story. A secret-keeper (Eldamon p. 192) guarding an empty vault is a joke the players get and it doesn't.

> *Illusory lava bubbles over the mountain pedestal, though nothing sits in its bowl. Then the bowl's "nothing" shifts. Curled in it, high above the murky water, is a stone the size of a loaf, made of overlapping plates. One plate lifts, an eye looks at you, and the plate snaps shut.*

- **Setup:** it melded into the pedestal long ago, which keeps the lava illusion bubbling (Eldamon p. 15), and the bowl is the only dry spot in the room. It's weak to water and hates the flooded temple. It believes it guards the vault's treasure. The vault was meant for a volcanic artifact the cult never got ([R15](gm_chapter_8.md#r15--empty-vault)); the pangeolil knows this and has kept the secret that the vault is empty for centuries.
- **Find:** DC 23 (24) Perception to Seek; automatic if a PC looks into the bowl.
- **Recognize:** secret DC 28 (29) Nature, Arcana, or a relevant Lore such as Eldamon or Mining Lore. Success: a shy secret-keeper and self-styled guardian that can Curl Up. Crit success adds: one that gives away a secret may flee in guilt; a new important secret can win it back; evolution: "Helping pangeolil learn and keep an especially deep or important secret." Failure: some earth eldamon. Crit fail lie: "Pangeolils curl up on treasure. Lift it and the hoard is underneath." The bowl is empty.
- **Befriend:** Request DC 23 (24), connection DC 18 (19):
  - **Trade a secret** (Diplomacy): the player tells you a real character secret, by note if they like, and the PC whispers it.
  - **Keep its secret** (Deception): play along, admire the "treasure," and never say aloud that the vault is empty.
  - **A dry way out** (Athletics or Survival): plan a route through the temple that keeps it out of the water.
- **Leaves if:** someone asks what it guards (it Curls Up for 10 minutes), announces that the vault is empty in front of it, or digs it out of the pedestal. A PC who repeats its confidence loses it for good.
- **Outcomes:** crit success: befriends and whispers the "secret" to that PC alone / success: befriends / failure: stays curled; another approach can try / crit failure: sinks into the pedestal for the visit.
- **Rolls:** Table 5 Ancient Slumberer (Petran and Draconic) · 6 No Variation · 7 Elemental Gems (obsidian chips) · 8 Stubborn · 9a one secret.
- **XP:** 20 per PC (suggestion).
- **Abilities:** **Earth's Vengeance** adds damage to its earth powers against anything that hurt it (Eldamon p. 192). Weak to air, plant, sonic, water, and wood. If the party already has a pangeolil, the two are secret-keepers who can't tell each other anything.
- 💡 Rare: if missing it would sting, use [Variant: Skipping the Request](gm_chapter_1_eldamon.md#variant-skipping-the-request).
- ⚠️ It can't find the shard, open vaults, or lead anywhere (Eldamon p. 156). Its secret is only that this vault is empty.
- 🧩 The Chapter 12 defense plan is a secret worth keeping.

## Encounter 8.3 — Riding the squall (tawhirtle)

**Insert after:** [On the way](gm_chapter_8.md#on-the-way), Scene 1, before "Reaching Razorback Isle" · **Where/when:** the [*Chromatic Queen*](gm_glossary.md#chromatic-queen)'s rigging, night 2 of 3 from Moonshadow Island (p. 193).

> **🎯** Fill travel time: two navigation rolls become a squall, a mast to climb, and two thrill-seekers looking for a friend.

> *Rain hammers the deck sideways. From the crow's nest, Slate Hodda shouts and points up. Something the size of a cat clings to the top of the mainmast: a turtle whose shell should crackle with light, and doesn't. Off the bow, a second turtle rides the crest of a wave, blazing blue-white, and whoops every time lightning hits the sea.*

- **Setup:** Tawhirtles chase storms and join only a friend who promises more excitement (Eldamon p. 194). A drained one gets its spark back by finding its joy again quickly, through other tawhirtles' ritual dance, or by waiting on a high point for lightning (Eldamon p. 194). This one waits atop the mainmast, and phased out it moves with the ship (Eldamon p. 43); a friend surfs the bow wave. Lookout **Slate Hodda** (she/her) points out the mast one. DC 23 (24) Perception spots the surfer.
- **Recognize:** secret DC 23 (24) Nature or Arcana. Success: thrill-seeking lightning turtles, one sparkless. Crit fail lie: storm omens calling lightning onto the ship, so chase them off.
- **Befriend:** Request DC 23 (24), selling danger; the drained one listens only after a connection thrill. Connection DC 18 (19): climb the slick mast and sit with it (Athletics, covers the climb) · ride the tallest swells with the helm, a few minutes clear of the reef by Garnikor's leave (Sailing Lore or Survival) · dance on deck beside the surfer (Performance).
- **Leaves if:** a PC plays it safe or chases it. Crit failure: it ignores that PC tonight. No connection before the squall passes (about an hour): both ride away. Anyone can climb or dance to help, and a PC who speaks Thalassic can talk to the surfer for a friend.
- **Rolls:** *Drained:* Table 5 Typical (Voltan, Common) · 6 none · 7 Soft Coloration · 8 Determined · 9a none. *Surfer:* Table 5 Elemental Envoy (Table 3a 78–84, water: Voltan and Thalassic, no Common) · 6 Glowing · 7 Sparkly · 8 Flashy · 9a none.
- **XP:** 10 per PC if anyone befriends a tawhirtle (suggestion). ⚠️ The squall is flavor: no marine resource cost, ship damage, or navigation bonus. 💡 House rule: a PC's electricity at the masthead can stand in for lightning and count as a successful connection.
