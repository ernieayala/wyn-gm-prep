## Encounter 5.1 — Glacé (chillot)

**Insert after:** [K4 — Storage Closet](gm_chapter_05.md#k4--storage-closet) to meet, and [K6 — Private Room](gm_chapter_05.md#k6--private-room-moderate-4) to befriend · **Where/when:** the [Wavewatcher Inn](gm_glossary.md#wavewatcher-inn) closet with [Mupto Burgin](gm_glossary.md#mupto-burgin). Then the quiet common room, once Pelborii is talked down or knocked out.

> **🎯** Want-driven NPC. Her evolution condition is "Helping chillot learn truly spine-tingling ghost stories" (Eldamon p. 218). Seaview's haunting is the best story in the Isles.

- **Setup:** Glacé (she/her) melded into the stone of Caleb's Colds (p. 371) and chills its ice (this doc's choice). The inn's ghost-dog legend (p. 370) drew her. She keeps Mupto's food cold. She has told him every story she knows, so he thinks all Seaview is haunted.
- **Find:** DC 19 Perception, Seeking before the door opens, spots frost on the barrel hoops. Otherwise, a cold tail brushes the first PC's neck: *"Boo."* No damage, no condition. Read this after the book's K4 read-aloud:

> *Frost has crept along the iron hoops of the ale barrels. On the highest barrel sits a small white otter, her body trailing off into curls of cold mist like the hem of a ghost's sheet. She holds a mug of ale between her paws, frozen to slush. Mupto stops sobbing long enough to whisper, "Don't look at her. She'll start the one about the drowned baker again."*

- **Ghost sniffer** (30 feet). Her flavor clues point only where the book already points. At [K5](gm_chapter_05.md#k5--inn-kitchen), she sniffs the pantry wall: *"Ghosts went in and out through there all week. And something alive is behind it. It doesn't smell like a ghost at all. That's the scariest part."* In [K6](gm_chapter_05.md#k6--private-room-moderate-4) she watches from the doorway, out of phase. She won't fight and can't be hurt.
- **After K6,** when Pelborii collapses in grief or goes down, read:

> *The otter crosses the chalk circle, sniffs the empty air where the spirits used to gather, and looks up at you with shining eyes. "That's it? A councilor did it? Tell it again from the beginning. Slower. And make the dogs scarier this time."*

- **Story swap:** that night, once the hounds are calm. Hold it far from the [hearth hounds](gm_chapter_05.md#k1--common-room-moderate-4)' fire, or in her pantry. She's weak to fire, though out of combat this is flavor. She tells a twisted **Grimhock's Light** (p. 371), with her tail on the listener's neck at the end. Then she wants a story back.
- **Recognize:** [secret check](gm_chapter_01.md#secret-checks-and-recall-knowledge), DC 19 Nature, Arcana, or a relevant Lore like Eldamon Lore (20). Success: a chillot (ice) that loves ghost stories. Crit success: she scares only to bond. She likes old classics and twisted retellings alike. She wants listeners who stay alert, and she smells ghosts. The PC also learns the approaches. Failure: some kind of ice eldamon. Crit fail lie: "Chillots want silence." In truth, silence bores her.
- **Befriend:** Request DC 19 (20), connection DC 14 (15):
  - Tell a ghost story (Performance, or Deception for a fake-out ending). Use Seaview's own: H1 ghostwriter, H3 killing intent, I2 yomhibdis, J4 "Goodly Granny," K1 ghost dogs, the councilor.
  - Explain the ghosts (Occultism, Religion, or Spirit Lore). The ghost dogs are hearth hounds, shy protector spirits of the inn's fireplace (p. 136).
  - Scare her back (Intimidation, or Stealth to sneak up). Chillots bond through a shared fright.
- **Leaves if:** the story is dull, someone tries to trap her, or a scare upsets a PC a second time. The first time, she apologizes and backs off.
- **Outcomes** (suggestions, since the book leaves them to the GM): crit success: befriends, and the story is her first true spine-tingler / success: befriends / failure: *"Not scary enough yet."* She tags along and asks again after the next spooky night, at Briargrove or the feast / crit failure: back to Caleb's Colds, where a new story earns a retry.
- **Rolls:** Table 5 Typical (Common, Cryan) · 6 No Variation · 7 Wispy Body · 8 Spooky · 9b favorite snack: Caleb's buttermilk ice cream with frozen berries.
- **XP:** 30 per PC (suggestion).
- **Abilities:** she speaks Cryan and can teach it. A trainer with a +4 key attribute prepares a fourth eldamon from 5th level (Advanced Training, Eldamon p. 45). A new friend swaps in at daily preparations (Eldamon p. 40).
- **Helpers:** any PC can Aid the scare-back or bring a story.
- **💡** If missing her would sting, use [Variant: Skipping the Request](gm_chapter_01_eldamon.md#variant-skipping-the-request). The book offers it for a rare find (Eldamon p. 29). She's common, but you can use it anyway. Then roll only for how much she loves the story.
- **🧩** Her stories feed Ch 6 [Tale Telling](gm_chapter_06.md#tale-telling), haunted Moonshadow, and her evolution condition. Once that condition is met, a trainer's eldamon can evolve at 11th level (Eldamon p. 40, 45). At the Scene 8 [feast](gm_chapter_05.md#the-feast), Caleb's Colds is a dish (p. 147). She's phased in and eyes every bowl (flavor only).

## Encounter 5.2 — The farm's new healer (phidi)

**Insert after:** [Event 14 — Hags Return](gm_chapter_05.md#event-14--hags-return-severe-4), end of Scene 5 · **Where/when:** the [Dardus Farm](gm_glossary.md#dardus-farm) farmhouse that evening. By then the hayloft captives and the children are safe, and the family is tending its hurt. One phidi, a rare life snake eldamon (Eldamon p. 232).

> **🎯** The place is the want. Life eldamon go where vitality and healing are (Eldamon p. 30). The farm's own eldamon vanished days ago (p. 131–132). Now a farmhouse full of fresh wounds draws a new one in. The hag wounds are a medical puzzle, and a phidi lives for those.

> *The kitchen table has become a sickbed. A Dardus cousin lies on it, arm wrapped in a dish towel, while the others boil water and argue about what to do. Coiled on the edge of the table, unnoticed by any of them, is a slim golden snake that glows like a lantern. It leans close to the cousin's arm, flicks its tongue, and recoils, the way a doctor does at a smell they don't recognize.*

- **Setup:** it arrived after the hags, out of phase. It has been going from patient to patient. The feld hags' birch-whip wounds smell of ozone ([J1](gm_chapter_05.md#j1--farmhouse-living-room), DC 15 Medicine). It can't work out why.
- **Find:** DC 19 Perception (20) spots the glow. It's automatic for a PC who Treats Wounds on a Dardus.
- **Recognize:** secret, DC 24 Nature or Religion (25). Success: a phidi, a rare life eldamon that follows healers and loves medicine. Crit success adds: healers treat phidis as sacred. Its evolution condition is "Helping phidi discover a true medical breakthrough." (Eldamon p. 232). Failure: some life eldamon. Crit fail lie: "A gold snake at a sickbed is a hag's familiar, come to finish the job." Shooing it off because of the lie ends tonight's chance.
- **Befriend:** Request DC 19 (20), connection DC 14 (15):
  - **Let it assist** (Medicine): Treat Wounds on a Dardus with it watching and talk it through each step.
  - **Solve the ozone** (Arcana, Nature, or Occultism): explain that the smell is the hag's magic in the whip, not rot. It's delighted.
  - **Calm the patient** (Diplomacy or Performance): keep a frightened Dardus, or one of the children, still while it studies the wound.
- **Leaves if:** someone refuses care to a Dardus, or treats the hurt family as an afterthought to the reward.
- **Outcomes** (suggestions): crit success: befriends, and coils around that PC's wrist through dinner / success: befriends / failure: stays with the patients. Retry at the family's thank-you meal "in a day or so" (p. 135) / crit failure: adopts that PC as a patient and won't join this chapter.
- **Rolls:** Table 5 Typical (Jyoti, Common) · 6 No Variation · 7 Bigger · 8 Serious · 9a no secret.
- **XP:** 20 per PC (suggestion).
- **Abilities:** **Caduceus Flow** protects it when it helps others (Eldamon p. 232). It's weak to darkness, death, and shadow.
- **Helpers:** any PC with Medicine can Aid.
- 💡 Rare: if missing it would sting, use [Variant: Skipping the Request](gm_chapter_01_eldamon.md#variant-skipping-the-request).
- **🔒** It knows nothing of Pelborii or why the farm's spirits vanished (Eldamon p. 156). It came after the hags, not before.
- **🧩** If the party already has a phidi, the two compare notes. Later, any NPC healer, or a cure for something strange, can be the breakthrough.

## Encounter 5.3 — The Breath Thief (grima)

**Insert after:** [An explanation](gm_chapter_05.md#an-explanation), end of Scene 2 · **Where/when:** the Dean de Cuisine's kitchen at the [Academy of Tastes](gm_glossary.md#academy-of-tastes), where he was murdered (p. 124). It happens the evening after [H3](gm_chapter_05.md#h3--academy-plaza-moderate-4).

> **🎯** Misread monster. Seaview blames a strange spirit for Pelborii's damage. Clearing its name turns the murder into a quiet goodbye.

- **Setup:** students say a glowing cat fled the murder. They also say cats steal breath, a rumor born from grimas (Eldamon p. 180). Guards swing saps at it, but it's out of phase. It took the dying dean's last breath as a memorial. Grimas don't kill, and they don't stop souls from moving on. The scene runs with [Sous Scholar Limbin Gumnammar](gm_glossary.md#sous-scholar-limbin-gumnammar) (he/him), or a guard if he died.

> *On the stove's cold iron sits a lean black cat. Its ears and tail glow the orange of banked coals, and a string of glowing beads hangs at its throat. One bead burns brighter than the rest.*

- **Find:** DC 19 Perception spots it behind the cold stove, or it appears when someone touches the dean's half-finished recipe card.
- **Recognize:** secret, DC 21 Nature, Religion, or Occultism (22). Success: a grima (death) that keeps the last breaths of those already dying. It doesn't kill. Failure: some death eldamon. Crit success: learn the approaches. Crit fail lie: it steals breath from the living and killed the dean.
- **Befriend:** Request DC 19 (20), connection DC 14 (15):
  - Hold a rite to the Balance or the Eld (Religion, p. 363).
  - Finish the dean's dish (Cooking Lore or Crafting). Seaview marks funerals with a feast.
  - Explain grimas to the guards (Society or Occultism) and end the chase.
- **Leaves if:** someone accuses it of the murder, demands the breath, or flares a bright light. It's weak to light. The H1 ***scroll of searing light*** drives it off.
- **Outcomes** (suggestions): crit success: befriends, and the accused student glimpses the dean's last memory / success: befriends / failure: it waits at the dean's empty chair at the Sous Scholar's feast for a retry / crit failure: gone until the next important death.
- **Last breath:** a friend who touches the bright bead sees the student over the dean with his filleting knife. Someone else is looking out of her eyes. His last thought: *"This isn't you."* It confirms H3, nothing more.
- **Rolls:** Table 5 Typical (Common, Requian) · 6 No Variation · 7 Elemental Aura: banked-coal glow from the dean's breath · 8 Patient · 9a no secret.
- **XP:** 20 per PC for befriending it or clearing its name, whichever comes first (suggestion).
- **Note:** Religion or Cooking Lore are the strongest approaches. With a non-trainer, it never evolves but keeps collecting breaths.
- **🔒** The memory shows the possession only. Pelborii isn't in it, so her secret holds until Scene 6.
