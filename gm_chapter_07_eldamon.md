## Encounter 7.1 — The Frost on the Cell Door (skolld)

**Insert after:** [P6 — Cell](gm_chapter_07.md#p6--cell) · **Where/when:** meet it at the cell door. Befriend it once [Goaflom](gm_chapter_07.md#p6--cell) is out of the cell, or at the next rest.

> **🎯** The fortress is warm and close, so frost on the cell grill is strange. Melding explains it. The frost is the skolld, melded into the door to guard its chosen ward (Eldamon p. 10, 15). It wants a ward, so every approach offers one.

> *Frost furs the metal grill of the cell door, though the air in this hall is warm and close. Your breath doesn't fog. The frost is thickest along the bottom of the door, where it gathers into the shape of a crouching animal with its head low and its eyes on you.*

- **Setup:** a rare ice eldamon (Eldamon p. 224). It chose Goaflom, a wildfire leshy, as its ward. That was the night the cult's villagers locked him up for speaking out. It's out of phase, so it can't open the door or stop anything (Eldamon p. 12). It stays anyway. It's weak to fire and guards a fire leshy. It doesn't care. The meld doesn't change the door or its latch.
- **Find:** Seek, **DC 22 Perception** (23). It's automatic once the PCs talk with Goaflom. Suggested line, once he trusts them: *"The frost dog? It came the night they locked me in. Sits by the door. Won't let me be cold alone, it says, which is a joke, because it's freezing."*
- **Recognize:** **DC 27 Nature, Arcana, or Eldamon Lore** (28), secret.
  - Success: it guards one chosen ward with everything it has.
  - Crit success: it drops plans at the last minute to check on its ward. That looks flaky, but it's the opposite. Most skollds change wards only when they have no other choice. Examples: the ward refuses them, or the ward is destroyed. Evolution: "Helping skolld guard what matters to them most."
  - Crit fail lie: "Skollds hunt in packs. This one is scouting for the rest." There's no pack.
- **Free to choose when:** Goaflom is out of the fortress and tells it he's safe now, on the ship or on Moonshadow ([P6](gm_chapter_07.md#p6--cell)). If he stays aboard the [*Chromatic Queen*](gm_glossary.md#chromatic-queen), it's happy to guard the whole ship. A PC can still befriend it. If the PCs leave him locked up, it stays.
- **Befriend:** Request **DC 22** (23) Diplomacy, connection **DC 17** (18):
  - **Offer a ward** (Diplomacy, Society, or Lore): name what it would guard and why it matters.
  - **Stand watch with it** (Athletics or Stealth): hold the cell door with it while Goaflom eats and recovers. Or stay still and hidden beside it.
  - **Honor the old ward** (Diplomacy): only if Goaflom was freed kindly. Speak well of him.
- **Leaves if:** the PCs mistreat Goaflom or leave him locked in. It also leaves if they mock it as flaky when it slips off to check on him.
- **Outcomes:** crit success: befriends, takes that PC or what they named as its ward / success: befriends, glances back toward Goaflom for a day / failure: follows, one more try before the party sails / crit failure: stays with Goaflom, gone this chapter.
- **Rolls:** Table 5 31–80 Typical (Cryan, Common) · 6 01–50 No Variation · 7 79–80 Elemental Aura (frost haze) · 8 90 Stubborn · 9a 01–90 no secret
- **XP:** 20 per PC for befriending it (suggestion)
- **Abilities:** **Interposing Guardian** puts it in the way of attacks on those it protects (Eldamon p. 224). Weak to body, earth, fire, and metal.
- 💡 It's rare. If missing it would sting, use [Variant: Skipping the Request](gm_chapter_01_eldamon.md#variant-skipping-the-request).
- 🔒 It knows nothing of the inner fortress. Goaflom's story and what he knows don't change. It never learned his past. He'd hide it from the skolld too.
- 🧩 Evolution: watch for its ward in danger in the [Chapter 12 siege](gm_chapter_12_eldamon.md#eldamon-in-the-siege). That's the moment.

## Encounter 7.2 — The Tree That Wasn't There (arbush)

**Insert after:** [Pristine Clearing *(defense special resource)*](gm_chapter_07.md#pristine-clearing-defense-special-resource) · **Where/when:** the clearing, on the way in or on the way back (p. 191). It shows up whenever the PCs stop to look at or harvest the bark. One arbush, a rare wood trickster (Eldamon p. 266).

> **🎯** A creature with a clear want, in a grim place. It wants the perfect joke, "just outside of expectations" (Eldamon p. 266). Nobody expects a laugh in the last clean trees on a blighted island. Wood eldamon live in lush forest (Eldamon p. 30). This is the only lush forest left.

> *The clearing is the first place on this island that smells green. You pick a tree to work on, set a blade to its bark, and look down to brace your feet. When you look up, the tree is three feet to the left. Your blade is touching air. Somewhere in its branches, something snorts.*

- **Setup:** it fled the resin when the sklaggans blight-bombed the forest. It has hidden among the pristine trees since then. It's bored and desperate for an audience.
- **Find:** **DC 22 Perception** (23) spots a toothy grin in the bark and no roots. Otherwise it gives itself away with the moving-tree joke above.
- **Recognize:** Nature **DC 27** (28), [secret](gm_chapter_01.md#secret-checks-and-recall-knowledge).
  - Success: a rare wood trickster. It travels only with people who share its humor.
  - Failure: a tree-shaped wood eldamon.
  - Crit success adds "Helping arbush pull off the perfect joke, or a series of excellent jokes."
  - Crit fail lie: "That's a resin-tree. The blight is in it. Burn it before it spreads." Fire can't touch it while it's out of phase. If the PCs try, it leaves for the day, offended.
- **Befriend:** Request Diplomacy **DC 22** (23), connection **DC 17** (18):
  - **Play the straight man** (Deception or Performance): set up the moving-tree joke on another PC and sell it.
  - **Ambush it back** (Stealth): a harmless joke of the PC's own.
  - **Beat it to its spot** (Survival): be standing first where it plants itself next, grinning.
- **Leaves if:** someone explains the joke, scolds it, or makes a cruel joke about the villagers. It avoids fire.
- **Outcomes:** crit success: laughs its leaves loose, joins / success: joins, plots a joke on the PC / failure: retry with another approach on the way back / crit failure: tree-still until the party leaves the island.
- **Rolls:** Table 5 Young (Muan only) · 6 No Variation · 7 Soft Coloration · 8 Playful · 9a none
- **XP:** 20 per PC (suggestion).
- **Abilities:** **Wild Growth** sows extra plants when it sows (Eldamon p. 266). Weak to acid, air, cold, fire, and poison. If the party already has an arbush, the two can run a two-tree joke.
- 💡 It's rare. If missing it would sting, use [Variant: Skipping the Request](gm_chapter_01_eldamon.md#variant-skipping-the-request).
- ⚠️ The prank doesn't change the bark harvest. It's still one character, once, with the book's secret DC 22 check (Harvest Purifying Tree Bark). The arbush isn't a bark source.
- 🔒 It knows nothing about the fortress or the villagers (Eldamon p. 156). It only knows the blight came and it ran.
- 🧩 Evolution: a joke on the Wave 4 husks in Chapter 12 (p. 322) is a strong candidate.

## Encounter 7.3 — The Murk Hand in the Rear Hall (manight)

**Insert after:** [P13 — Rear Hall *(Hazard 8)*](gm_chapter_07.md#p13--rear-hall-hazard-8) · **Where/when:** the bend beside the dead kragrak. Use it after the haunt there (p. 183) triggers, is disabled, or stops resetting.

> **🎯** A creature with a clear want. It's a perfectionist stuck on its first task. So every approach helps it get that task right. Its motions hint at the haunt's DC 27 Medicine disable. They never replace that check.

Before the PCs reach the bend, ask who holds the light. Read the box below to PCs who see in darkness. If they carry a light, use the sneeze (**Find**) first.

> *In the dark at the bend of the hall, something darker moves. It's a hand, four fingers and a thumb, made of shadow. It smooths the air above the dead kragrak's wounds, pats, stops, wrings its fingers, and starts over from the top.*

- **Setup:** a young **manight** (rare darkness hand, Eldamon p. 176). It settled in this unnaturally dark hall (Eldamon p. 30). Then it saw the sklaggans execute the kragrak. It's out of phase. It has tried to soothe his wounds ever since.
- **Abilities:** cold damage, concealment, and darkness areas.
- **Find:** in the dark, Seek **DC 22 Perception**. With a light, no check is needed. It sneezes, then hides behind the corpse.
- **Recognize:** **DC 27 Nature or Occultism** (28) or Lore, secret.
  - Success: a darkness perfectionist. It loves praise and sticks to its first task.
  - Crit success adds its evolution condition: keep trying until it's perfect. It also reveals that most manights keep a "top five" list.
  - Crit fail lie: "A shadow spy. The sklaggans use them to watch for intruders, and it will report you."
- **Befriend:** Request **DC 22** (23); connection **DC 17** (18):
  - Show it the right way (Medicine): clean, press, bind. It takes about 10 minutes, until it copies cleanly. This is separate from the haunt's disable.
  - The perfect high five (Acrobatics or Performance): out of phase, timing is everything. Keep trying until it lands right.
  - Praise the finished job (Performance): once it gets something right, give applause, a toast, or a little ceremony.
- **Leaves if:**
  - someone calls it "good enough." It ignores that PC for the rest of the scene.
  - a light stays on it. It hides while it's lit.
  - the corpse is moved or destroyed first. It leaves the hall.
- **Outcomes:** crit success joins with a flawless high five / success joins, wants to finish first / failure returns to the corpse, retry with a new approach or after the haunt is laid to rest / crit failure melts into the dark, gone for the chapter.
- **Rolls:** Table 5 Young (Shadowtongue only; talks with its fingers) · 6 No Variation · 7 Fastidious · 8 Apologetic · 9b Sneezes at Its Weaknesses (body, light, sonic; light above all).
- **Top five (suggested):** make the hurting stop the right way · a perfect high five · sit in lantern light without sneezing · count the P14 tally marks without losing its place · slip through a resin door before it snaps shut.
- **XP:** 20 per PC for befriending it. Only one exists. If missing it would sting, use [Skipping the Request](gm_chapter_01_eldamon.md#variant-skipping-the-request).
- ⚠️ The haunt resets after 1 hour unless the three P20 sklaggans are dead (p. 183). A PC who comes back after that triggers it again. Befriend the manight while the haunt is disabled or after it stops resetting.
- 🧩 Option (not a book rule): two things can count as a successful connection approach. One is a successful Medicine disable that it watches. The other is the spirit's grim nod once the P20 sklaggans die. The nod also crosses off item 1 on its top five.
- **Softened fortress** (p. 173): if you use the softened version, it smooths a still, covered shape instead.

## Encounter 7.4 — The White Hart on the Path (hydgwyn)

**Insert after:** [Pristine Clearing *(defense special resource)*](gm_chapter_07.md#pristine-clearing-defense-special-resource) going in; [Concluding the chapter](gm_chapter_07.md#concluding-the-chapter) coming out · **Where:** the resin-blighted path to the fortress.

> **🎯** A bookend. The PCs meet the same creature going in and coming out. The change in it shows what the PCs changed. A death eldamon fits a land the sklaggans emptied (p. 170-171; Eldamon p. 30).

> *Off the path, a boar lies tangled in black resin, breathing hard. A small white deer steps out of the trees and kneels beside it. Its eyes are full of tiny stars. The boar's breathing slows, eases, and stops. The deer stands, and its starry eyes turn up the path, toward the fortress.*

- **Setup:** a **hydgwyn** (uncommon death deer, Eldamon p. 182). It eases creatures that are already dying. The stars in its eyes show where it's needed, until its mission ends.
- **Find:** no check on the walk in. Following it takes **DC 22 Survival**. It doesn't flee, but it keeps its distance.
- **Recognize:** **DC 24 Nature or Religion** (25) or Lore, secret.
  - Success: a death eldamon of mercy.
  - Crit success adds its evolution condition: a great quest or many small ones. It also reveals that hydgwyns sometimes travel with people who set out to end unnaturally long-lived creatures.
  - Crit fail lie: "A death omen. Whoever it looks at won't come back out of that fortress."
- 🔒 Its stars point at the fortress because the Skinloom keeps taking villagers (p. 171). Its mission stays unfinished while the Skinloom works. Don't confirm why it watches the path.
- **The return,** after the Flesh Stitcher falls and the villagers surrender (p. 191):

> *The white deer stands at the path's edge where you first saw it. Its eyes are plain and dark now. The stars are gone. It lowers its head to you, once, and walks into the trees.*

- **Befriend (optional, walk out only):** Request **DC 22** (23); connection **DC 17** (18):
  - Read its stars at night or before dawn (Nature or Astronomy Lore).
  - Help ease a dying animal (Medicine).
  - Tell it about the Jewel hunt (Society or a fitting Lore).
- **Leaves if:**
  - a dying animal is harmed or rushed in front of it. It leaves for good.
  - someone pulls it toward the fortress. It won't go.
- **Outcomes:** crit success joins, a new star points to the next port / success joins / failure walks off, may wait at the beach for one more try / crit failure gone.
- **Rolls:** Table 5 Typical (Requian and Common) · 6 No Variation · 7 Soft Coloration · 8 Serious · 9a no secret.
- **XP:** 10 per PC for easing an animal or befriending it. Any PC can befriend it.
