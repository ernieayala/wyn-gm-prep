## Encounter 6.1 — Psyrano in Marni's hut

**Insert after:** [O4 — Leader's Hut (Severe 5)](gm_chapter_6.md#o4--leaders-hut-severe-5), before O5 · **Where/when:** by the blast shadow, once the ghost pirate and sword are dealt with. Psyrano (rare, force, L5).

> **🎯** Want-driven NPC: it wants ever more abstruse conversation, so every approach is a way to talk with it.

> *In the corner by the blast shadow sits a little lizard with a huge head and tiny arms, its scales striped in soft dream-violet. It studies the wall the way a scholar studies a manuscript. "Ah. Visitors," it says in fussy, perfect Common. "Do forgive the disarray. I was perusing the psychic palimpsest of this unfortunate domicile."*

- **Setup:** Force eldamon posing as mind behind long words (Eldamon p. 216).
- **Find:** visible. Watch a minute: **DC 20 Perception** (22) shows dust moving in force shoves.
- **Recognize:** Nature or Arcana **DC 25** (27), [secret](gm_chapter_1.md#secret-checks-and-recall-knowledge). Success: rare force posing as mind; its coloring is part of the act. Crit success adds "Helping psyrano ascend to new heights of abstruse conversation." Failure: it's the mind eldamon it claims. Crit fail lie: "A rare mind eldamon. It can read this hut's memories. Ask it what happened here." It invents the answer.
- **Befriend:** Request Diplomacy **DC 20** (22), connection **DC 15** (17):
  - Out-talk it (Society, Arcana, Occultism, scholarly Lore): longer words than its own.
  - Play along (Deception): admire its "mind powers," straight-faced.
- **Leaves if:** mocked or cut off mid-sentence (a kind, long-worded correction is fine). Shies from bright light.
- **Outcomes:** crit success: "an interlocutor of rare perspicacity," joins / success: joins after a speech / failure: retry another approach after 10 minutes of conversation / crit failure: gone tonight; shuns that PC on Moonshadow.
- **Rolls:** Table 5 Typical (Aetheran, Common) · 6 No Variation · 7 Vibrant Coloration · 8 Pretentious (the hut is "the atelier") · 9a none
- **XP:** 30 per PC for befriending it (suggestion). Abilities include Telekinetic Storm and Force Hands. Only one exists: [skip the Request](gm_chapter_1_eldamon.md#variant-skipping-the-request) if a bad roll would sting.
- ⚠️ Cut O4's gory sentence (p. 161)? Then the psyrano discusses only the blast shadow. It adds nothing to O4's curse checks.

## Encounter 6.2 — Arbush in Rotsnout's yard

**Insert after:** [Concluding the chapter](gm_chapter_6.md#concluding-the-chapter), last scene before the fortress · **Where/when:** [Rotsnout](gm_glossary.md#rotsnout)'s yard, the morning after the rest (L6; arrival evening, L5 DCs). Arbush (rare, wood, L6).

> **🎯** Want-driven NPC: it wants the perfect joke, "just outside of expectations" (Eldamon p. 266), so each approach joins the joke.

> *The cottage looks the same as last night, except for the young tree by the door. You'd swear it stood ten feet farther off yesterday. Rotsnout steps out, glares at it, and swears it's moved again. As he stomps off toward the dock, a low branch on the tree shakes, as if something inside it is trying not to laugh.*

- **Setup:** A trickster tree creeping nightly toward Rotsnout's door. If the PCs pull the joke on him, he roars, then cackles (this doc's invention).
- **Find:** **DC 22 Perception** (20): a toothy grin in the bark, no roots.
- **Recognize:** Nature **DC 27** (25), [secret](gm_chapter_1.md#secret-checks-and-recall-knowledge). Success: rare wood trickster; travels only with people who share its humor. Failure: a tree-shaped wood eldamon. Crit success adds "Helping arbush pull off the perfect joke, or a series of excellent jokes." Crit fail lie: "Arbushes are guardian trees. It's protecting the witch. Keep clear of the door."
- **Befriend:** Request Diplomacy **DC 22** (20), connection **DC 17** (15):
  - Help it set up the punchline (Deception or Performance): surprises Rotsnout, harms nobody.
  - Ambush it back (Stealth): a harmless joke of the PC's own.
  - Beat it to its spot (Survival): be standing first where it plants itself next, grinning.
- **Leaves if:** someone explains the joke, scolds it, or pranks Rotsnout cruelly. Avoids fire.
- **Outcomes:** crit success: laughs its leaves loose, joins / success: joins, plots a joke on the PC / failure: retry another approach before departure / crit failure: tree-still until the party leaves.
- **Rolls:** Table 5 Typical (Muan, and Common learned from Rotsnout's swearing) · 6 No Variation · 7 Cousin (a young ironwood) · 8 Jocular (a pun first) · 9a none
- **XP:** 30 per PC for befriending it (suggestion). Plantwalker and Wild Growth suit the swamps ahead. Only one exists: [skip the Request](gm_chapter_1_eldamon.md#variant-skipping-the-request) if a bad roll would sting.

## Encounter 6.3 — Kagekko in Gullet's belly

**Insert after:** [N7 — Lower Pit](gm_chapter_6.md#n7--lower-pit), before N8 · **Where/when:** wherever in Gullstone Cave [Gullet](gm_glossary.md#gullet) is destroyed and expels everything (p. 159). Kagekko (rare, darkness, L5).

> **🎯** Melding hides a reward in a fight they already have: a melded eldamon can't be detected until the meld ends (Eldamon p. 12).

> *Gullet splits like a rotten sack and spills its dinner across the mud: stone tiles, a rusted buckle, fish bones, half a boot. Something else tumbles out with them. It's a gecko made of shadow, with a head too big for its body. It freezes, eyes wide, then scuttles under the nearest scrap of burlap and holds still.*

- **Setup:** Kagekkos run from a problem or eat it (Eldamon p. 174). At the blight bombs, this one melded into Gullet, which does both. The meld gives Gullet nothing. It deals cold damage and resists mental and spirit.
- **Find:** Seek the pile, **DC 20 Perception**. Failure: it hides until someone handles the pile.
- **Recognize:** Nature or Occultism **DC 25**, [secret](gm_chapter_1.md#secret-checks-and-recall-knowledge). Success: rare darkness; comes out only for someone it trusts to be as careful and competent as itself. Failure: a gecko-shaped darkness eldamon. Crit success adds its evolution condition: build thorough contingencies, then learn to let go. Crit fail lie: "Kagekkos bond with whoever is boldest. Scoop it up before it bolts."
- **Befriend:** Request Diplomacy **DC 20** (22), connection **DC 15** (17):
  - Move like it (Stealth): slow, low, from cover.
  - Careful hands (Thievery): check the junk for traps and edges as it watches, before touching anything.
  - Backup plans (Survival, or Lore: Warfare or Sailing): the player plans the next room aloud, with two backups.
- **Leaves if:** grabbed, shouted at, or lit brightly.
- **Outcomes:** crit success: burrows under the PC's collar, joins / success: joins at a distance, hides behind the PC in every room / failure: retry another approach after 10 quiet minutes / crit failure: flees into N5's tunnels (p. 158), lost to that PC; another can try after N8.
- **Rolls:** Table 5 Typical (Shadowtongue, Common) · 6 No Variation · 7 Smaller · 8 Anxious (counts exits aloud in every room) · 9a/9b lost-treasure secret (🔒 below)
- **XP:** 20 per PC for befriending it (suggestion). Only one exists: [skip the Request](gm_chapter_1_eldamon.md#variant-skipping-the-request) if a bad roll would sting, and it picks the PC who handled it most carefully.
- 💡 A PC Gullet swallowed shared its hiding place: you could let them roll Diplomacy at the connection DC (not a book rule).
- ⚠️ Gullet's crafty mind is the book's (p. 159) and predates the kagekko. Keep them unlinked.
- 🔒 It lived in the cave long before Poppy came and remembers the old pirate's treasure behind the iron door, unaware Poppy took it (p. 155). When the door opens, it searches N8 in a panic and finds nothing. It can't solve the tile puzzle.
- 🔒 Optional tell, only if you want one nobody can confirm: it ducks behind its friend when Prince Kulupi speaks. It hides from everyone, so this proves nothing.

## Encounter 6.4 — Duskoyotls at Rotsnout's fire

**Insert after:** [After the attack](gm_chapter_6.md#after-the-attack) · **Where/when:** the dock's end, evening after [Event 15](gm_chapter_6.md#event-15--swamp-raiders-severe-5), stew cooking. Three duskoyotls (uncommon, darkness, L5).

> **🎯** Rarity sets the hook: they watch fire-cast shadows (Eldamon p. 170), and ironwood won't burn (p. 150). Make tonight a half moon; they love one.

> *Past the end of the dock, where the mist thins, three pairs of eyes catch the firelight. When the flames under the cauldron jump, the eyes jump with them. Somewhere out on the water, something gives a short, happy yip.*

- **Find / Recognize:** **DC 18 Perception**: shadow coyotes, no threat. Nature or Occultism **DC 22** ([secret](gm_chapter_1.md#secret-checks-and-recall-knowledge)): they love any balance of light and dark. Crit success adds its evolution condition (understanding its fire obsession). Crit fail lie: "They hate firelight. Put the fire out and they'll come to you."
- **Befriend:** Request Diplomacy **DC 20**, one try per duskoyotl. Connection **DC 15**: Survival (a second fire), Performance (shadow puppets, howling), Nature (moon phases). Leave if someone puts out the fire, or at magical darkness or bright light. Success: it joins and spends the night at the firelight's edge.
- **Rolls:** Elder: Typical, Half and Half Body Color, Patient. Pup: Young, Glowing, Energetic. Ember-eye: Elemental Envoy (fire), Heterochromia, Courageous, 🔒 secret: fire finds it; it drifts toward any fire-element creature or PC all night.
- **XP:** 10 per PC if anyone befriends one (suggestion). ⚠️ Right after Event 15, glowing eyes look like round two; let the players react. Out of phase, nothing can hurt them; suggestion: they scatter at the first attack and creep back an hour later.

## Flavor sighting — Shadoppy in the Upper Pit

**Insert after:** [N6 — Upper Pit (Severe 5)](gm_chapter_6.md#n6--upper-pit-severe-5), once the mogadbs are down and while Gullet is still on the run, among the rat-bitten corpses (p. 159). Shadoppy (common, darkness). It never approaches, so nobody befriends it. If a player chases it, it's already gone.

> **🎯** Want as worldbuilding: it collects natural-death bones, so its disgust tells the island's history.

> *A shadow the size of a cat noses along the corpses on the slope. Bones poke through its smoky fur like a cage. It sniffs one body, then another, then rears back and hisses at nothing. A moment later it's gone into the dark.*

- **Recognize:** Nature or Occultism **DC 20**, [secret](gm_chapter_1.md#secret-checks-and-recall-knowledge). Success: violent-death bones are cursed to it. Failure: a bony, opossum-shaped darkness eldamon. Crit success: it believes the most cursed bones belong to those who died of fright, and their collectors end up nibbled to death by rats (Eldamon p. 168). These bodies are rat-bitten. Crit fail lie: "Shadoppies eat the dead. Burn these bodies before it comes back."
- 🧩 Echoes the voyage's Religion crit success: violent deaths, angry spirits (p. 148–149). Marni is the proof.
