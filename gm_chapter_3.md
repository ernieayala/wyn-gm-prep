## The chapter in one minute

The map's last dot marks the snake-jawed mouth of [Shiverskull Cave](gm_glossary.md#shiverskull-cave) below [Shivering Mountain](gm_glossary.md#shivering-mountain). Undead treasure hunters and a killer fern guard it. A "survivor" begs to tag along; she's a devil wearing a dead bard's face. Inside: an abandoned mining camp, cult sacrificial chambers, and gem-eating shale spitters. An elemental rift jams Poppy's sealed door. The PCs close the rift with a kragrak wizard or a friendly metal-eating elemental, press the [sapphire scale](gm_glossary.md#sapphire-scale) to a golden "P," and ride a floating platform down to Poppy's proving grounds.

**By the end of the chapter the PCs have:**
1. **Charged the sapphire scale** at the sixth and last waymarker, the F1 obelisk.
2. **Closed the elemental rift** at F25, one of three ways.
3. **Opened the secret door** at F27 and reached the F29 **elevator** to Chapter 4's G1.

**Party level:** **3** throughout. No level-up; the next milestone, Escaping from Shivering Mountain, ends Chapter 4 (p. 11).

> **🎯 Why this chapter exists:** It turns the overland hunt into a dungeon crawl and hides the campaign's secret in plain sight: [Cult of the Shivering Behemoth](gm_glossary.md#cult-of-the-shivering-behemoth) ruins, including an isopod statue of the finale's behemoth. The PCs aren't meant to understand them yet (p. 79).

   - Kill the F11 rottskal to recruit **Apraka** (F9); bring them to F25 and they walk in, closing it.

### Threat math at 3rd level

Every encounter here assumes a **3rd-level party**. Same budgets as Chapter 1; each creature costs less because the party is two levels higher:

| Creature level | −1 | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| vs. party | −4 | −3 | −2 | −1 | same | +1 | +2 |
| XP | 10 | 15 | 20 | 30 | 40 | 60 | 80 |

**Worked examples:**
- **Event 12:** Pargomun (level 4, 60) = 60 = **Low**.
- **F11:** elite rottskal (level 5, 80) = 80 = **Moderate**.
- **F4:** yomhibdi (level 4, 60) + Screaming Poet, complex level-2 haunt (30) = 90. Book: **Moderate**.
- **F15:** Dugari (level 4, 60) + eight unholy stalagmites (simple level-2 hazards, 6 each) = 108. Book: **Moderate**.

> **⚠️ Gotcha:** Event 11 (70 for Low), F4 (90), F15 (108), and F25 (90, all Moderate) total over their labels; F21's spitter (30) + swarm (40) = 70 runs under Moderate. The book's label wins. F15's stalagmites hurt only when broken, and runes can be disabled first.

### Elite and weak creatures

- **Elite and weak creatures:** see [Chapter 2](gm_chapter_2.md#elite-and-weak-creatures). Per **BB 9**, **young shale spitters** are weak shale spitters (Creature 1); the **snake-infested rottskal** is elite (Creature 5).

### Afflictions and poison

A poison is an **affliction**, a ladder of stages climbed or descended by repeated saves.
- Exposure save: **failure** stage 1, **critical failure** stage 2.
- At each stage's end, save again: **success** −1 stage, **critical success** −2, **failure** +1, **critical failure** +2. Below stage 1 ends it.
- **Maximum duration** ends it regardless.

**Example, goldkiss snake venom (F11):** DC 13 Fortitude, maximum duration 3 rounds, stage 1 is 1d4 poison damage (1 round). One stage: a PC who fails takes 1d4 a round until a save succeeds or 3 rounds pass.

### Haunts

A **haunt** is a spirit-made [hazard](gm_chapter_1.md#hazards), with two twists:
- **Often disabled socially or spiritually.** Beat the F4 Screaming Poet in a poetry contest (Performance) or hold it off with Religion; Thievery won't help.
- **Disabling can be temporary.** It returns on its **reset** condition and ends for good only when its spirit rests (named in the stat block; here, slaying Pargomun).

A **complex** haunt rolls initiative and runs a **routine** each round, like the Chapter 1 snake pit. Its initiative is **Stealth +15 (expert)**; 10 + that (DC 25) is the Perception DC to notice it, and only a PC with **expert** Perception can try.

### Counteracting

*Dispel magic* and similar spells roll a **counteract** check against the effect's DC, then compare **counteract levels** (a spell's equals its spell level).
- **Critical success:** up to 3 levels above yours.
- **Success:** up to 1 level above.
- **Failure:** only a lower level.
- **Critical failure:** nothing.

**Examples:** a stalagmite rune (F15) needs 2nd-level *dispel magic* vs. **DC 18**. The F16 door is counteract level 4, DC 23: 3rd-level *dispel magic* needs a success, and by book exception 2nd-level fails even on a critical success.

### Weakness, resistance, and immunity

- **Weakness X:** X extra damage of that type. Earth zuggles' **weakness bludgeoning 5** adds 5 to a hammer hit.
- **Resistance X:** subtract X from damage of that type. Zuggles resist piercing 5 and slashing 5.
- **Immunity:** no effect. **Object immunities** (mental effects, conditions like sickened, and so on) cover this chapter's hazards and walls.

### Seek, Search, and secret doors

- **Seek** (1 action): Perception vs. hidden things in an area; most secret doors here list a DC "to Seek."
- **Search:** the exploration version at half speed; you roll in secret.
- Without either, PCs walk past secret doors. "We look for hidden doors" earns a roll.

### Breaking and forcing objects

- **Hardness** comes off every hit. At **BT** (Broken Threshold) HP it breaks; a broken wall here is passable.
- **Force Open:** Athletics vs. the DC. Success opens it; critical failure jams it (−2 on later tries).
- **Here:** F8 door (DC 17), F10 wall (DC 25, or Hardness 10, HP 40, BT 20), F26 wall (DC 20), F27 wall (DC 50, out of reach).

### Conditions new this chapter

| Condition | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Deafened** | Critically fails hearing-based Perception; −2 Perception for initiative. **Auditory** actions need a **DC 5 flat check** or are lost. Immune to auditory effects. |
| **Confused** | Flat-footed (off-guard in the Remaster). Treats no one as an ally; no Delay, Ready, or reactions. Attacks random targets you pick. Each time attack or spell damage hits it, it rolls a **DC 11 flat check** to end it. |
| **Immobilized** | No move-trait actions. F20's cave-in: **Escape DC 21**. |
| **Fatigued** | −1 status to AC and saves; no exploration activity while traveling. A full night's rest ends it. |

The book's **negative** damage (F15) is **void** in the PF2 Remaster.

## Arriving at Shivering Mountain

> **🔒 GM-only — the name:** Goldcroppers mostly think "Shivering" corrupts "Slithering," for the snakedead and the snake head carved in the flank. Poppy's cadre used the cult's name; the cult once planned a powerful ritual here (p. 68). Poppy said it before some Rumplankers, and it stuck.

**Background the PCs might know:** adventurers have spelunked Shiverskull Cave for ages; few return with treasure. Decades ago the orpok **Emerald Earth Mining Company** mined it profitably until monsters, ruins, ghostly phenomena, and territorial **shale spitters** drove them back to Rumplank, leaving gear and base camp still inside (p. 68–69).

### Recall Knowledge (secret check)

At the mountain's base, roll secret **DC 17 Society**, **DC 15 Indigo Isles History Lore**, or **DC 15 Mining Lore** per PC (p. 69). Reuse the results if asked about the mountain earlier in the campaign.
- **Critical success:** The success result, plus: decades ago the Emerald Earth Mining Company reportedly found Shiverskull Cave and ran a lucrative gemstone operation, until the caves' most terrifying denizens, the shale spitters, supposedly drove the miners out.
- **Success:** Shivering Mountain is Goldcrop Island's tallest peak, better known for its caverns and past than its summit. Many believe Poppy's treasure-filled hideout is in these caves, where she supposedly trained recruits in the ways of the Gilded Pirate. The most infamous way in is Shiverskull Cave.
- **Failure:** Nothing specific.
- **Critical failure:** "Shivering Mountain" is what people say when they mean "Slithering Mountain," a jungle peak crawling with snakedead, giant snakes, and serpentine aberrations, where the Snake King plots to conquer Rumplank.

## Event 11 — Welcome Party (Low 3)

- **Where:** the clearing at the cave mouth, F1 (p. 71).
- **Who:** four skeletal treasure hunters and the iron fern that killed them.
- **Goal:** survive, then notice the dead party had a fifth member.

As the party leaves the jungle with the cave mouth ahead, read aloud:

> *The grass around the cave is overgrown and thick with weeds. What appears to be g'mayun bones are scattered within the silver-colored grass, several blades of which drip with fresh blood. The bones begin to rattle, then magically animate and reconstruct themselves into four g'mayun skeletons, each bearing a dagger and a grim, hateful gaze.*

> **🔒 GM-only — what happened here:** Years ago an **iron fern** killed five g'mayun and hardriggan treasure hunters feet from the entrance. Four rose as skeletons, unwilling to be outdone. The fifth, hardriggan bard **Viekaska**, was mortally wounded but met a different end (Event 12, F4).

**Creatures:**
- **Undead Explorers (4):** skeletal iron eels (p. 38), Creature −1. Initiative: Perception +5. Monster Parts 1.5/3.5/6.5 gp. Stat block in [Chapter 1, B2](gm_chapter_1.md#b2--upper-deck-trivial-1).
- **Iron Fern:** BB 48, Creature 2. Initiative: Stealth +7. Monster Parts 5/12/22 gp. A skink's remains drip from its razor leaves. Once the skeletons engage, it sneaks up and attacks the nearest PC.
- **Spotting the fern:** examining the bloody leaves, **DC 10 Perception**, shows it rustle with excitement.

> **📏 Rules refresher:** Skeletons resist piercing and slashing 5 and carry bone ticks; use bludgeoning, as in Chapter 1.

> **⚠️ Gotcha — skeleton Perception:** p. 38 says +4; Event 11's initiative says +5. Use +5 for initiative.

**Investigating the skeletons** (after the fight):
- **DC 15 Medicine or Survival:** dead for years. **Critical success:** blunt trauma, likely a wild animal or monster.
- **DC 15 Perception** to Search: the gear points to a **fifth** member, hidden under bushes by the entrance ([F1](#f1--maw-of-shiverskull)).
- Cries for help cut them off. Go to Event 12.

> **⚠️ Gotcha — the critical success clue:** The book's crit says "blunt trauma," but its text names the iron fern as the killer. Read it as written; players will blame the fern anyway.

## Event 12 — Once Charted, Twice Dead (Low 3)

- **Where:** atop the ruined F2 platform (p. 71–72).
- **Who:** **Pargomun**, a siluvain or "memento devil" (they/them), disguised as Viekaska (she/her).
- **When:** after Event 11 and a short rest, or mid-Event 11 if it fits.

Read aloud:

> *From around the corner comes a shout. "Help!" yells a gruff but panicked voice. "I'm over here! Please save me from those evil skeletons!"*

A quivering young hardriggan in tattered gear stands on the ruins. Once the skeletons fall: *"Thank the gods you're here,"* she says. *"I thought I'd be trapped here forever."* She calls herself Viekaska, sole survivor of the iron fern, and begs to come along: *"I can't be alone any longer. Please don't leave me here."*

> **🔒 GM-only — the devil:** Dying on the platform, Viekaska prayed to any god. An evil one sent **Pargomun**, who killed her and uses her beloved **golden quill** to wear her guise. Years of waiting, nothing to a devil, for a party to infiltrate and feed on.

**How Pargomun plays it:**
- Steers the PCs away from the skeletons' bones.
- Asks for an escort to Rumplank, or else to join them in the cave.
- Keeps lying until the PCs **attack** or **sleep and set a watch**.
- **Escorted to Rumplank:** splits or distracts the party and attacks a lone PC well before the city.
- **In the cave:** feigns injury or invents an excuse to split the party, then attacks.

**Pargomun (as Viekaska)** — Siluvain (BB 99), Creature 4. Initiative: Deception +15. Monster Parts 12/27/50 gp.

> **💡 GM tip — let the players catch on:** Event 11's Search shows a missing fifth adventurer; **DC 16 Perception** at F1 finds a hardriggan skeleton; Viekaska was the hardriggan. Give players who connect it a chance to strike first.

> **🧩 Sets up:** Killing Pargomun rests Viekaska's spirit at [F4](#f4--secret-entrance-moderate-3); do it before visiting F4 and the haunt thanks the PCs instead of fighting.

## Outside the cave (F1–F4)

### F1 — Maw of Shiverskull

Read aloud:

> *The entrance to Shiverskull Cave, fifteen feet wide and easily as tall, is carved into the shape of a serpent's face, its powerful jaws agape as if ready to swallow anyone who dares enter the legendary caves. A faintly glowing obelisk made of lava rock stands just next to the cave's opening.*

- **Viekaska's remains:** Searching the mountainside's eastern edges, **DC 16 Perception**, finds a hardriggan skeleton Pargomun hid under bushes.
- **The F3 path:** bushes hide a southern cliff path; Seek or Search finds it, no check.
- **Treasure (without Monster Parts only):** on the remains, a climbing bolt, a stone fist elixir, a fear gem, and 25 gp.

**Magical Waymarker:** the final one. Touching the scale to the obelisk draws its light into the scale; the map's dot vanishes.
- **All six done:** the scale is **fully charged**: heavier, teeming with arcane energy, thrumming every few seconds like a glowing heartbeat.
- Near F27 it glows brightly, revealing the door.

> **⚠️ Gotcha — where the body is:** Event 11 says a nook **south** of the entrance; F1 says bushes to the **east**. Pick one beforehand.

### F2 — Ceremonial Ruins

Read aloud:

> *Just east of the entrance, nestled in a natural grotto between mountain walls, stands a forgotten stone platform flanked by two hexagonal columns.*

The cult's outdoor ceremony and sacrifice site; only bloodstained stones remain. Its stairs have crumbled: **DC 15 Athletics** to Climb the 5-foot platform.

### F3 — Crumbling Cliff Path

Read aloud:

> *South of the entrance to Shiverskull Cave, the forest floor drops steeply into a ravine at least thirty feet below. Palm trees and scrubby overgrowth all around crush against the mountain's steep sides, making it difficult to discern the edge between the mountain's side and the ravine below.*

The eroded path wraps around to F4. **DC 20 Perception** or **DC 18 Survival** shows it could collapse any moment.

**Crumbling Path** *(Hazard 2, simple, environmental)*
- **Stealth DC 20 (expert).**
- **Description:** a 15-foot span of the path gives way and drops anyone on it 40 feet to the jungle floor.
- **Disable:** **DC 21 Survival** (trained) to collapse the path on purpose from 5 feet away.
- **AC** 15; **Fort** +5, **Ref** +5; **HP** 30; object immunities.
- **Dirt Slide (reaction).** **Trigger:** two or more Small or larger creatures stand on the 15-foot section. **Effect:** the path crumbles down the cliff. Each creature on it can attempt a **DC 17 Reflex** save to Grab an Edge. On a failure, it tumbles down, takes **4d8 bludgeoning**, and lands **prone** 40 feet below.

> **⚠️ Gotcha — two triggers:** Area text: **a Medium or larger creature** on the marked spaces. Stat block: **two or more Small or larger creatures**. Choose before play. Per the book, it can complicate fights here, including the F4 yomhibdi fight.

### F4 — Secret Entrance *(Moderate 3)*

Cult priests' hidden cliffside door. Poppy erased the path and markings; the doorway remains.

> **🔒 GM-only — the haunt and its parasite:** Viekaska's spirit is now an angry haunt that attacks anyone near. It drew a **yomhibdi**, an aberration drawn to the anguish of floundering or frustrated artists, which feeds on the haunt's ever-renewing emotions, can't be hurt by it, and defends it.

**When the PCs arrive:**
- **Pargomun alive:** the haunt manifests; the yomhibdi, maybe dozing in the bushes, leaps up to fight.
- **Pargomun dead:** the haunt only thanks the PCs and sinks into the ground. The yomhibdi attacks anyway, furious at losing its free meal.

**Yomhibdi** — BB 122, Creature 4. Initiative: Perception +7 or Stealth +11. Monster Parts 12/27/50 gp.

**Screaming Poet** *(Haunt 2, unique, complex)* — p. 74
- **Stealth** +15 (expert).
- **Description:** spectral pieces of a dead poet's unfinished works float through the air, deafening and befuddling living creatures in F4.
- **Disable:** **two DC 20 Performance** checks (expert) to beat the poet in a contest of wit and verse, or **DC 22 Religion** (trained) to exorcise the spirit for a while.
- **First Stanza (reaction).** **Trigger:** a creature ends its turn in F4, between the crumbling path to the west and the secret door to the north. **Effect:** overlapping verses fill the air, starting as whispers and swelling fast. The triggering creature must make a **DC 20 Fortitude** save or be **deafened** for 1 round. The haunt rolls initiative.
- **Routine (1 action):** a cacophony fills the whole outdoor area between the cliff path (F3) and the cave interior past the secret door. Each non-ethereal creature there attempts a **DC 18 Will** save.
  - **Critical success:** unaffected.
  - **Success:** 1d4 sonic damage.
  - **Failure:** 2d4 sonic damage, and deafened for 1 round.
  - **Critical failure:** 4d4 sonic damage, deafened for 1d4 rounds, and **confused** as long as it's deafened.
- **Reset:** once no living, non-ethereal creatures remain in the area, the spirit sinks back into the Ethereal Plane until something disturbs it again. Slaying Pargomun puts it to rest for good.

> **📏 Rules refresher:** See [haunts](#haunts) and [conditions new this chapter](#conditions-new-this-chapter). The contest disables it; only Pargomun's death ends it.

> **💡 GM tip — the poetry contest:** The book's best way to stall the haunt while Pargomun lives. The yomhibdi may watch, amused, then attack mid-contest, sooner if a PC shows talent, since it feeds on frustrated, talentless performers. Bad verse can buy the party a round.

**Viekaska's Gift:** with Pargomun **and** the yomhibdi dead, Viekaska's spirit appears once more, beckons north, and points at the flat rock hiding the entrance, then moves on. No Seek needed.

**Secret Door:** otherwise, Seek along the wall, **DC 30 Perception**. The "mountain face" is a flat oval rock rolled against the cliff.
- Moving it takes **two people** pushing together; Medium creatures slip through.
- The tunnel winds over **300 feet** to **F12**.
- **XP:** **30 XP** per PC for finding it.

> **⚠️ Gotcha — DC 30:** Out of reach for most 3rd-level PCs. The intended routes are Viekaska's spirit or the F12 side, where light through cracks makes it obvious.

## The mining camp (F5–F11)

> **🔒 GM-only — Inglomeonite was here:** After the miners fled, the ferropaceon **Inglomeonite** (F28), a magnetic earth elemental, stripped every valuable metal from these halls, machinery to door hinges, onto his carapace, and scraped off a piece in F7. Clues to him: F6, F7, F8.

### F5 — Mineshaft

The tunnel from the Maw runs 100 feet to this shaft, shored decades ago with wooden beams by the Emerald Earth outfit. It continues east to the base camp and quarries; north and west walls are untouched cavern.

### F6 — Ore Processing Room

Read aloud:

> *A few scraps of rotten wood lie scattered about the corners of this unfinished mining chamber. The floor is marked here and there with pockmarks, grooves, and gouges. The cave's gray walls are slightly reddened in spots, as if scuffed with orange-red dust.*

- **DC 15 Crafting or Mining Lore:** rust flakes and floor marks are all that's left of the ore machines.

### F7 — Base Camp Ruins

Read aloud:

> *The remains of some semi-permanent subterranean labor operation linger in the dark, dusty corners of this expertly reinforced base camp. Rusty picks, chipped shovels, cooking pots, tattered linens, broken crates, and other detritus signal an industrious but hastily abandoned mining endeavor. A battered wooden sign is nailed to one of the vertical beams nearest the mineshaft, and one of the beams in the western alcove is broken.*

- At most a dozen workers lived here, run efficiently by tireless **Boss Ozgold**.
- **The sign** (northern beam): the company name carved in Orpok, "Permanently Closed" painted over in faded black ink.
- The mine continues east; a southeast door leads to F8.

**Monster Part — the carapace chunk:** while pillaging what the miners left, Inglomeonite bumped the **western beam** and scraped off a fist-sized piece of magical carapace.
- **50 gp** as a monster part, with a ferropaceon's refinements and imbued properties.
- **Returned** to Inglomeonite (F28), it lets him close the F25 rift.
- The book says to **tell the players** it's special and may have another use, since players spend parts fast.

> **🧩 Sets up:** One of three ways to open Poppy's door; spent on gear, it's gone.

### F8 — Boss's Quarters

Inglomeonite took the door's latch, hinges, and fittings and wedged it back. It's jammed: **Force Open DC 17 Athletics.**

Read aloud:

> *This simple office is outfitted with several pieces of partially rotted wooden furniture that, miraculously, seem to have withstood the test of time. A thick book lies closed on a writing desk in the northwestern corner, next to a barrel. A tattered silk hammock hangs from the wooden beams to the south.*

**Ozgold's Journal:** left behind when the company fled (he took the ledger), under thick dust. A PC who **reads Orpok** learns:
- how the miners found the mines (by chance, exploring the jungle outlands);
- the riches they found;
- their first spitter run-ins, and driving most out of **F17**;
- that they fled after finding **the horrible beast east of the northern quarry (F15)**.

> **⚠️ Gotcha — did the miners find F15?** The journal says yes; F10 says they quit **before** finding the cult ruins; F11 blames the spitters. Pick one. Perhaps they glimpsed Dugari through the F10 cracks and never broke through.

### F9 — Auxiliary Room

Read aloud:

> *Dusty wooden bookshelves line the walls of this ten-foot-by-twenty-foot storage area. Arranged on the dirt floor in the center of this room is a bowl-shaped pile of incredibly smooth stones.*

Spare tools, geology and mining books, and worthless archaeological finds.

**[Apraka Ogari](gm_glossary.md#apraka-ogari)** (LN agender kragrak wizard 3, they/them) sits cross-legged on the stones.
- An old kragrak from **Kraken**, alone here a little over a year.
- A loner devoted to inborn earth magic, drawn by a powerful elemental aura to this quiet room.
- Meditating, they miss the PCs at first. If the PCs make themselves known, Apraka slowly opens their eyes: *"Are you real? Or did I fall asleep again?"* They pinch themselves, leaving a small trail of pebbles. *"No, I believe I am awake. Well, hello there. Are you here to contemplate the aura of the rocks as well? Or perhaps you are lost?"*
- They seek the aura's source but fear the monsters, above all the **F11 rottskal**, and would welcome its death.

**Apraka as a companion:**
- **Trigger:** the PCs kill the F11 rottskal and bring proof.
- With **fewer than two** companions, Apraka offers to join to learn the cave's elemental mysteries.
- Knows nothing of Poppy but much of the mountain's geology. Explanations run long, slowed by a gravelly, plodding dialect.
- **Leaves at F25** through the portal, unless the PCs close the rift another way first.

> **🎯 Why Apraka exists:** The simplest rift key: kill the rottskal, bring the wizard. The cost is a one-chapter companion.

### F10 — Northern Quarry

Read aloud:

> *A steep ten-foot slope separates the five tunnels along the northern section of this room from the quarry's southern half. The quarry tunnels each end after just a dozen or so feet, with the exception of the eastern tunnel, which continues for some distance.*

**Secret Door:** **DC 20 Perception** to Seek spots cracks between the easternmost tunnel and F15.
- Thin stone: **Hardness 10, HP 40 (BT 20)**.
- Smash it, or **Force Open** at **DC 25 Athletics**.

### F11 — Southern Quarry *(Moderate 3)*

Miners dug emeralds and sapphires here until the spitters drove them off and ate every visible gem.

**Snake-Infested Rottskal** — elite rottskal (BB 9, p. 393), Creature 5. Initiative: Perception +16. Monster Parts 18/45/80 gp.
- Same kind as the Aksyanis Amphitheater rottskal (p. 65), but tangled with venomous vipers that mistook its back for a nest of bird eggs.
- It skitters at the PCs gleefully on entry.
- **Snake-Infested:** four **goldkiss snakes**, Tiny venomous Goldcrop serpents without stat blocks, cling to it.
  - **Removing a snake:** a single action with the attack trait. The PC rolls Athletics against the rottskal's Reflex DC. On a success the snake slithers off in a panic and leaves the fight.
  - While at least one snake is attached, the rottskal's **claw**, **needles**, and **Swarming Bites** inflict **goldkiss snake venom**.
- **Goldkiss Snake Venom** (poison): **DC 13 Fortitude**; maximum duration 3 rounds; stage 1: 1d4 poison damage (1 round). See [afflictions and poison](#afflictions-and-poison).
- **Treasure (without Monster Parts only):** in the southwesternmost tunnel's rubble, a **sapphire worth 100 gp**.

> **📏 Rules refresher:** Base rottskal p. 393 plus [elite adjustments](gm_chapter_2.md#elite-and-weak-creatures). **Snatch** (reaction) tries to Disarm a melee weapon that hits it. Resists bludgeoning 5, weak to slashing 5.

> **⚠️ Gotcha — Perception:** Base +11, elite +13, book +16 for initiative. Use +16.

> **💡 GM tip:** At Creature 5, the chapter's hardest single monster. Pulling snakes strips the venom; describe them writhing on its back so players think to grab them.

### F14 — Chamber of Final Rest

Read aloud:

> **🔒 GM-only:** A sect founder lies here, dead of old age weeks before Poppy wiped out the rest. Poppy left the grave to show her successor someday what they faced. That day never came.

- Dust dulls the granite. Its symbols are foreign to those who don't know the cult, but perceptive PCs match them to F16's door and statues.
- **Opening it:** a **20 Bulk** lid, a hard drag.
- **Inside:** a black-robed corpse with profane burial goods, nearly untouched by time.
  - **Two gold coins** on the eyes, stamped with a shale spitter no one alive would know; cultists traded them among themselves.
  - A wicked ***+1 dagger (2)*** across the chest.
  - Fine, impossibly black **robes**, worth **10 gp**.

### F15 — Stalagmite Corridor *(Moderate 3)*

Read aloud:

> *Stalagmites jut from the flat stone floor of this long, broad cavern corridor. Ten-foot-high carved ledges line the northern and southern ends of the corridor. Other exits include a hall winding northeast and two stone doors on the southern ledge.*

Priests threw each victim from the southern ledge to a cult-enchanted beast. Few survived.

**Dugari, Hope Eater** — NE Large animal, runebranded aurochs (BB 79), Creature 4. Initiative: Perception +9. Monster Parts 12/27/50 gp.
- An ancient cursed aurochs at the **southwest end** that passes for a fiendish bovine statue.
- Cult runes hold it in **stasis**: it can't act and is **immune to all effects**.
- **Stasis ends** when anyone steps onto the floor. It attacks with abandon, obeying old orders to destroy intruders.

**Unholy Stalagmites (8)** *(Hazard 2, simple; environmental, necromancy, negative, magical)*
- **Stealth DC 20** (trained) to notice the runes carved on them.
- **Description:** rune-covered stalagmites explode with negative energy when shattered.
- **Disable:** **DC 20 Occultism** (trained) to obscure the rune, or *dispel magic* (2nd level; counteract **DC 18**) to counteract it. See [counteracting](#counteracting).
- **AC** 15; **Fort** +5, **Ref** +10; **Hardness** 5, **HP** 30 (BT 15); object immunities.
- **Unholy Blast (reaction).** **Trigger:** the stalagmite is broken or destroyed, or Dugari Tramples it (one stalagmite per Trample). **Effect:** tendrils of negative energy deal **3d6 negative** damage to the triggering creature, **DC 18 basic Reflex**. If Dugari or another immune creature broke it, the blast hits the nearest creature that would take the damage. On a critical failure, the target is also **frightened 1**.
- Dugari is warded and immune to the stalagmites. Each Trample can break one, no roll needed.

**Clues of the Cult:** **DC 20 Religion** to Recall Knowledge: unholy necromancy. Nothing more yet.

> **⚠️ Gotcha — no sniping from the ledge:** Stasis makes Dugari immune, so ledge attacks do nothing until someone touches the floor. Jumping the 10-foot ledge means [falling damage](gm_chapter_1.md#movement-and-terrain) unless the PC climbs.

> **💡 GM tip:** Dugari Tramples stalagmites to blast PCs, so the room worsens as the fight goes on. PCs who spot the runes first (Stealth DC 20) can disable some with Occultism before anyone drops down.

### F16 — Cult Ruins Entrance

Read aloud:

> *A flattened stone and dirt mound to the southeast looks down on the rest of this natural cavern. A stone door is set into the southern wall of the mound. Two statues made from black obsidian flank the door. Each statue depicts a monstrous shale spitter rearing up and hissing. One of the statues has fallen over partially and obscures the door, which is inscribed with fiendish runes and similar shale spitter iconography.*

- The cult's route from F15 to the rest of the caves.
- A few miners saw the door behind the fallen statue; it looked too dangerous to touch.

**The sealed door:**
- **From F16:** opens only to **3rd-level or higher *dispel magic*** (counteract level 4, counteract DC 23); 2nd-level fails even on a critical success. Likely beyond the PCs now, per the book.
- **From F15:** pushes open easily, then swings shut and reseals unless propped with a sturdy object of **at least 100 pounds**.

> **💡 GM tip:** PCs who pass from F15 to F16 without propping it can't come back that way. Describe it swinging shut behind the last one through.

Read aloud:

Two cocky, testy juveniles starting a nest (only nibbled stone so far). They attack on sight.

**Young Shale Spitters (2)** — weak shale spitters (BB 9, BB 92), Creature 1. Initiative: Perception +6. Monster Parts 3.5/7/13 gp.

### F20 — Collapsing Tunnel *(Trivial 3)*

Read aloud:

> *This cramped, winding tunnel is congested with loose dirt and crumbling rocks, as though excavated by a burrowing animal little concerned by the potential for cave-ins. The tunnel inclines steeply as it winds upward toward the northeast.*

**Cave-In** *(Hazard 4, simple, environmental)*
- **Stealth DC 22** (trained).
- **Description:** the tunnel collapses and fills the passage with rubble.
- **Disable:** **DC 25 Survival** (trained) to reinforce the ceiling without triggering a collapse.
- **AC** 21; **Fort** +14, **Ref** +14; **Hardness** 13, **HP** 46 (BT 23); object immunities.
- **Collapse (reaction).** **Trigger:** a Medium or larger creature Strides at full Speed, or moves at full speed while exploring, into the section of tunnel that loops under itself. **Effect:** the upper tunnel falls onto the lower one. **4d8+10 bludgeoning** to the triggering creature and everything within 10 feet, **DC 21 basic Reflex**. On a failed save, the triggering creature is also **immobilized** (Escape DC 21). The tunnel is blocked for good.
- **Safe movement:** the hazard doesn't trigger for a creature moving carefully (exploring or Striding at half Speed) or Stepping.

> **💡 GM tip:** The book suggests turning the collapse on enemies like the F19 spitters. Clever players who spot it can bait one into the loop.

> **⚠️ Gotcha — trigger wording:** Area text: "any Medium creature" Striding at full Speed. Stat block: "a Medium or larger creature," plus full-speed exploring. Use the stat block.

> **⚠️ Gotcha:** A collapse seals F20. Know the other routes on the p. 70 map before it happens.

## The way to Poppy's door (F24–F29)

### F24 — False Hideout

Read aloud:

> *Standing on a rocky mound at the center of this roughly rounded chamber is a stone statue of a g'mayun woman. Though the stone woman is posed gloriously in classic pirate attire and with a shiny cutlass in one outstretched hand, the details of the sculpture are muddled at best and downright vague for the most part. A short staircase ascends the mound to the statue. A smattering of sturdy wooden chests, barrels, and boxes dot the cavern's periphery.*

Statues, a platform, a rope challenge, an open chest: it looks like Poppy's hideout and underwhelms, because it's a **decoy** to turn back unworthy hunters. The way on is the F29 elevator.

- **Treasure:** a **superb repair kit**, **three lesser healing potions**, **two vine arrows**, and a **potion of water breathing**; without Monster Parts, **50 gp** in a chest.

> **💡 GM tip:** Let "we found it!" land before the letdown. It's Poppy's humor, and Chapter 4 is full of it.

### F25 — Elemental Rift *(Moderate 3)*

Read aloud:

> *The rock walls of this cavern are unnaturally hewn at extreme angles, as though a large, oval-shaped swath of rock had simply vanished into thin air. The idea is not so far-fetched, considering that a twenty-foot-wide circular portal floats vertically, like a magical mirror swirling in on itself, in the center of the cave. The portal glows with a bright green light that crackles with harmless sparks of green electricity. Nebulous dustings of brown soil and chunks of gray rock drift lazily around the portal's swirling center, like water down a drain, all of it funneling toward the yawning green center of the extradimensional gateway. The portal makes a quiet grinding noise, like gravel endlessly tumbling down a distant hillside.*

- Inglomeonite's freak elemental energy opened this rift to the **Elemental Plane of Earth** years ago.
- **Earth zuggles**, funny crystal-studded clay caterpillars, pop out now and then; spitters eat most, but a few always linger by the portal.
- Only elementals and elemental-magic masters can pass. PCs go through like vapor, with a mild, uncomfortable vibration.

**Earth Zuggles (3)** — CN Small earth elemental. Use the ice zuggle stats (BB 123) with these changes (p. 82). They see the portal as their parent and attack anyone who comes near it.
- **Initiative** Perception +10; **HP** 30.
- **Immunities** bleed, paralyzed, poison, sleep. **Weaknesses** bludgeoning 5. **Resistances** piercing 5, slashing 5.
- **Speed** 20 feet, burrow 20 feet.
- **Ranged** zuggle orb +9 (earth, primal), **1d8+3 bludgeoning**.
- **Elemental Absorption (reaction).** **Trigger:** the zuggle is hit by an effect with the **earth** trait that deals physical damage, before damage is rolled. **Effect:** it regains HP equal to the physical damage instead. Healing past its maximum becomes temporary HP. If that would give it **more than 15 temporary HP**, it explodes: the zuggle is destroyed, and everything in a 10-foot burst takes **4d4 bludgeoning**, **DC 18 basic Reflex**.
- **Monster Parts** 5/12/22 gp.

> **⚠️ Gotcha:** Earth-trait spells and attacks heal zuggles. Overfeed one and it explodes on everyone nearby, PCs included.

**Disrupted Scale:** within **100 feet** of the rift, the scale crackles with arcane lightning. **DC 20 Arcana**: the portal blocks the scale's purpose while open.

**Closing the rift (pick one):**
1. **Apraka.** If Apraka is with the party, they gasp: *"This is the source of unearthly power that drew me to these caves."* After a year of meditation, they're attuned to the portal. *"It is as though I am gazing at a great mountain, and the rocks themselves are calling to me."* They thank the PCs and explain that they must go through: *"I see it now, my destiny. By walking through this gateway, I can close it, as a key when turned can close a lock."* They'll wait if the PCs want to study it, but won't leave its side: *"Now that I have found my muse, I scarcely shall leave it from my sight."* Apraka steps through and vanishes in a brilliant flash; the portal collapses with a deafening thunderclap.
2. **Inglomeonite's carapace.** Return the F7 chunk to him at F28; he closes it.
3. **Gemstones.** His other suggestion: throw in **50 gp worth of gemstones**. Sated, it closes at once.

> **💡 GM tip — gem sources:** Without Monster Parts there are enough: sapphire (F11, 100 gp), tourmaline (F13, 30 gp), flawed emerald (F17, 50 gp), F21 tourmalines (30 gp). With Monster Parts, most don't exist; decide ahead whether a mined F21 gemstone counts toward the 50 gp.

### F26 — Glowing Fissure *(Low 3)*

Southeast of the portal, a short tunnel ends at a narrow crack leaking gravelly animal noises and a faint green glow. A wayward **shale spitter** and an adventurous **earth zuggle** nest inside. Spitters usually eat zuggles; these two nuzzle, growl softly, and wrestle in a dim green glow of affection.

- **Earth Zuggle** — p. 82, Creature 2. Initiative: Perception +10. Monster Parts 5/12/22 gp.
- **Young Shale Spitter** — weak shale spitter (BB 9, BB 92), Creature 1. Initiative: Perception +6. Monster Parts 3.5/7/13 gp.
- Far gentler than other monsters here. Attacked, they fight **1 round**, then burrow away.

**Treasure — the gift:** a gentle approach and **DC 18 Nature or Elemental Lore** moves them to fuse their primal essences into a **green-flecked brown gemstone**, a **2nd-level scroll of *summon elemental***. They hand it over and burrow deeper.

**Weak Wall:** the thin south wall ("S" on the p. 70 map). **Force Open DC 20 Athletics** from either side opens it to the tunnel north of F21.

> **💡 GM tip:** The one fight kindness can skip. Describe the cuddling so players think twice before rolling initiative.

### F27 — Secret Door

Read aloud:

> *This square-shaped alcove is obviously artificial, though its purpose is unclear. The five-foot-deep depression in the cavern wall dead-ends at a flat, featureless stone wall made of the same rock as the rest of the cave network.*

Unused for over two centuries; at first glance an odd squared-off nook with an unusually flat northeast face.

**Finding the "P":**
- A fully charged scale thrums and glows brightly along this corridor.
- **DC 15 Perception** while Seeking along the wall spots a faded, hand-sized golden **"P"**.
- The "P" brightens near the scale; searching with the scale finds it **with no check**.

**Elemental Interference:** while the F25 rift is open, scale and "P" crackle with harmless green lightning. The scale is still drawn to the wall, but pressing them together feels like pushing the wrong ends of two magnets.
- Inglomeonite can explain the door waits on the rift.
- **DC 20 Arcana** to Identify Magic deduces the same.

**Opening the Door:** once the rift is closed, press the fully charged scale to the gilded "P." Both glow brighter, then flash golden, leaving a **perfect archway** edged in gold leaf to the F29 elevator.

**If the scale isn't fully charged:** the "P" glows, plainly attuned, but the scale thrums weakly, on and off. The PCs must revisit every missed waymarker.

**Brute force won't work:** magically reinforced stone, **Hardness 50, HP 1,000 (BT 500)**, immune to critical hits and precision damage; Force Open **DC 50 Athletics**.

> **💡 GM tip (book's note):** If waymarkers are missing, keep the trip back quick and simple to hold momentum.

### F28 — Rusted Hoard

Read aloud:

> *Twenty-foot-tall mounds of rust-pocked metal treasures, both mundane and precious, fill the edges of this expansive cave chamber. Standing amid the rusted treasure hoard is a massive crustaceous creature with a shell made of shiny black minerals.*

**Inglomeonite** (LN male ferropaceon merchant 10, he/him). A ferropaceon (BB 31) far outclasses 3rd-level PCs, but this one is cheery and keener on trade than fighting. He insists on **Inglo**.

**What Inglo tells them:**
- He claims centuries here, keeping a low profile and leaving native creatures alone.
- For the past **thirteen quake-cycles**, "about two hundred years in the parlance of your people," almost no mortals have come this far.
- About Poppy, in his words:
  - > *"Long ago, there was a bird-woman—a pirate who held herself in high regard. She would occasionally come through this way, leading young disciples behind her. They would go through a passage over there."*
- He points to the north alcove (F27) and offers to walk them over. *"I believe there's a secret door here. I have no idea how it opens, but I fear that it's inaccessible right now anyway."* About two quake-cycles ago a rift opened in a cave to the west, interfering with the door's magic.
- He could close it easily, but he's **missing a piece of his magnetic carapace**; whenever he nears it, the rift spews dozens of zuggles and tries to suck him in like a vacuum.
- The piece has been gone under one quake-cycle, since he poked around the south caves right after the "two-legged inhabitants" (the miners) left. It's in **F7**.

**Returning the carapace:** overjoyed, Inglo fastens it on magnetically, walks to the rift, and closes it instantly, clearing the F27 door.
- **Treasure (without Monster Parts only):** he adds **50 gp** in assorted metal gear.

**Trading:**
- Buys and sells **gemstones** and **common magic items up to level 4** of **metal or stone**.
- Starting stock: ***+1 striking weapon (2)***, ***minor sturdy shield (4)***, ***onyx dog* wondrous figurine**.
- Made **helpful** ([attitudes](gm_chapter_2.md#npc-attitudes-make-an-impression-and-request)), he buys non-metal, non-stone items too.

> **⚠️ Gotcha — Inglo's clock:** "Under one quake-cycle" is about 15 years by his math, yet he lost it right after the miners left, which the book puts decades ago. His sense of time is "far beyond the characters' ken" (p. 85); play the fuzziness as character.

> **🧩 Sets up:** In Chapter 11 the PCs meet **Sarganx**, a ferropaceon and Inglo's bitter old rival. Disparaging Inglo there gives +2 circumstance to negotiate with Sarganx; claiming his friendship, −2 (p. 284). Note how the PCs felt about him.

### F29 — Elevator to Poppy's Sanctum

Read aloud:

> *Three alcoves dot the perimeter of this grand circular chamber, whose finished features stand in stark contrast to the rest of Shiverskull Cave. The floor here is made of flagstone tile, and the walls are made of thousands of sturdy bricks. A statue at the southwestern end of the chamber overlooks a circular platform just below, which somehow floats effortlessly in mid-air. The edges of the circle are separated by a few inches of thin air from the rest of the chamber's floor. Staircases around the circle descend to the platform.*

- Each statue shows **Poppy** in a different outfit and weapon: anyone could earn the name of the Gilded Pirate.
- **Using the elevator:** stand on the golden platform and imagine it descending. It drifts slowly **200 feet** to **G1**.
- **Safety:** gravity magic fills the 200-foot shaft; a creature falling off while the platform is down drops a safe **10 feet per round**.
