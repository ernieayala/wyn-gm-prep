## Encounter 7.1 — The Frost on the Cell Door (skolld)

**Insert after:** [P6 — Cell](gm_chapter_7.md#p6--cell) · **Where/when:** meet it at the cell door. Befriend it once [Goaflom](gm_chapter_7.md#p6--cell) is out of the cell, or at the next rest.

> **🎯** Melding explains the weird detail: the fortress is warm and close, so the frost on the cell grill is the skolld, melded into the door to guard its chosen ward (Eldamon p. 10, 15). It wants a ward, so every approach offers one.

> *Frost furs the metal grill of the cell door, though the air in this hall is warm and close. Your breath doesn't fog. The frost is thickest along the bottom of the door, where it gathers into the shape of a crouching animal with its head low and its eyes on you.*

- **Setup:** a rare ice eldamon (Eldamon p. 224) that chose Goaflom, a wildfire leshy, as its ward the night the cult's villagers locked him up for speaking out. Out of phase, it can't open the door or stop anything (Eldamon p. 12); it stays anyway. It's weak to fire and guards a fire leshy, and it doesn't care. The meld doesn't change the door or its latch.
- **Find:** Seek, **DC 22 Perception** (23), or automatic once the PCs talk with Goaflom. Suggested line, once he trusts them: *"The frost dog? It came the night they locked me in. Sits by the door. Won't let me be cold alone, it says, which is a joke, because it's freezing."*
- **Recognize:** **DC 27 Nature, Arcana, or Eldamon Lore** (28), secret. Success: it guards one chosen ward with everything it has. Crit success: it drops plans at the last minute to check on its ward, which looks flaky but is the opposite, and most change wards only when they have no other choice, such as when the ward refuses them or is destroyed. Evolution: "Helping skolld guard what matters to them most." Crit fail lie: "Skollds hunt in packs. This one is scouting for the rest." There's no pack.
- **Free to choose when:** Goaflom is out of the fortress and tells it he's safe now, on the ship or on Moonshadow ([P6](gm_chapter_7.md#p6--cell)). If he stays aboard the [*Chromatic Queen*](gm_glossary.md#chromatic-queen), it's happy to guard the whole ship, and a PC can still befriend it. If the PCs leave him locked up, it stays.
- **Befriend:** Request **DC 22** (23) Diplomacy, connection **DC 17** (18):
  - **Offer a ward** (Diplomacy, Society, or Lore): name what it would guard and why it matters.
  - **Stand watch with it** (Athletics or Stealth): hold the cell door with it while Goaflom eats and recovers, or stay still and hidden beside it.
  - **Honor the old ward** (Diplomacy): only if Goaflom was freed kindly. Speak well of him.
- **Leaves if:** the PCs mistreat Goaflom, leave him locked in, or mock it as flaky when it slips off to check on him.
- **Outcomes:** crit success: befriends, takes that PC or what they named as its ward / success: befriends, glances back toward Goaflom for a day / failure: follows, one more try before the party sails / crit failure: stays with Goaflom, gone this chapter.
- **Rolls:** Table 5 31–80 Typical (Cryan, Common) · 6 01–50 No Variation · 7 79–80 Elemental Aura (frost haze) · 8 90 Stubborn · 9a 01–90 no secret
- **XP:** 20 per PC for befriending it (suggestion)
- **Abilities:** **Interposing Guardian** puts it in the way of attacks on those it protects (Eldamon p. 224). Weak to body, earth, fire, and metal.
- 💡 Rare: if missing it would sting, use [Variant: Skipping the Request](gm_chapter_1_eldamon.md#variant-skipping-the-request).
- 🔒 It knows nothing of the inner fortress, and Goaflom's story and what he knows don't change. It never learned his past; he'd hide it from the skolld too.
- 🧩 Evolution: if its ward is in danger in the [Chapter 12 siege](gm_chapter_12_eldamon.md#eldamon-in-the-siege), that's the moment.

## Encounter 7.2 — The Tree That Wasn't There (arbush)

**Insert after:** [Pristine Clearing *(defense special resource)*](gm_chapter_7.md#pristine-clearing-defense-special-resource) · **Where/when:** the clearing, on the way in or on the way back (p. 191), whenever the PCs stop to look at or harvest the bark. One arbush, a rare wood trickster (Eldamon p. 266).

> **🎯** Want-driven NPC in a grim place: it wants the perfect joke, "just outside of expectations" (Eldamon p. 266), and the last clean trees on a blighted island are where nobody expects a laugh. Wood eldamon live in lush forest (Eldamon p. 30), and this is the only lush forest left.

> *The clearing is the first place on this island that smells green. You pick a tree to work on, set a blade to its bark, and look down to brace your feet. When you look up, the tree is three feet to the left. Your blade is touching air. Somewhere in its branches, something snorts.*

- **Setup:** it fled the resin when the sklaggans blight-bombed the forest and has hidden among the pristine trees since, bored and desperate for an audience.
- **Find:** **DC 22 Perception** (23): a toothy grin in the bark, no roots. Otherwise it gives itself away with the moving-tree joke above.
- **Recognize:** Nature **DC 27** (28), [secret](gm_chapter_1.md#secret-checks-and-recall-knowledge). Success: rare wood trickster; travels only with people who share its humor. Failure: a tree-shaped wood eldamon. Crit success adds "Helping arbush pull off the perfect joke, or a series of excellent jokes." Crit fail lie: "That's a resin-tree. The blight is in it. Burn it before it spreads." Fire can't touch it out of phase; it leaves for the day, offended.
- **Befriend:** Request Diplomacy **DC 22** (23), connection **DC 17** (18):
  - **Play the straight man** (Deception or Performance): set up the moving-tree joke on another PC and sell it.
  - **Ambush it back** (Stealth): a harmless joke of the PC's own.
  - **Beat it to its spot** (Survival): be standing first where it plants itself next, grinning.
- **Leaves if:** someone explains the joke, scolds it, or makes a cruel joke about the villagers. Avoids fire.
- **Outcomes:** crit success: laughs its leaves loose, joins / success: joins, plots a joke on the PC / failure: retry with another approach on the way back / crit failure: tree-still until the party leaves the island.
- **Rolls:** Table 5 Young (Muan only) · 6 No Variation · 7 Soft Coloration · 8 Playful · 9a none
- **XP:** 20 per PC (suggestion).
- **Abilities:** **Wild Growth** sows extra plants when it sows (Eldamon p. 266). Weak to acid, air, cold, fire, and poison. If the party already has an arbush, the two can run a two-tree joke.
- 💡 Rare: if missing it would sting, use [Variant: Skipping the Request](gm_chapter_1_eldamon.md#variant-skipping-the-request).
- ⚠️ The prank doesn't change the bark harvest: one character, once, the book's secret DC 22 check (Harvest Purifying Tree Bark). The arbush isn't a bark source.
- 🔒 It knows nothing about the fortress or the villagers (Eldamon p. 156); it only knows the blight came and it ran.
- 🧩 Evolution: a joke on the Wave 4 husks in Chapter 12 (p. 322) is a strong candidate.

## Encounter 7.3 — The Murk Hand in the Rear Hall (manight)

**Insert after:** [P13 — Rear Hall *(Hazard 8)*](gm_chapter_7.md#p13--rear-hall-hazard-8) · **Where/when:** the bend beside the dead kragrak, after the haunt there (p. 183) triggers, is disabled, or stops resetting.

> **🎯** Want-driven NPC: a perfectionist fixed on its first task, so every approach helps it get that task right. Its motions hint at the haunt's DC 27 Medicine disable but never replace that check.

Ask who holds the light before the PCs reach the bend. Read to PCs who see in darkness. With a light, use the sneeze (**Find**) first.

> *In the dark at the bend of the hall, something darker moves. It's a hand, four fingers and a thumb, made of shadow. It smooths the air above the dead kragrak's wounds, pats, stops, wrings its fingers, and starts over from the top.*

- **Setup:** A young **manight** (rare darkness hand, Eldamon p. 176) settled in this unnaturally dark hall (Eldamon p. 30), then saw the sklaggans execute the kragrak. Out of phase, it has tried to soothe his wounds ever since.
- **Abilities:** cold damage, concealment, and darkness areas.
- **Find:** in the dark, Seek **DC 22 Perception**. With a light, no check: it sneezes, then hides behind the corpse.
- **Recognize:** **DC 27 Nature or Occultism** (28) or Lore, secret. Success: a darkness perfectionist that loves praise and sticks to its first task. Crit success adds its evolution condition (keep trying until it's perfect) and that most manights keep a "top five" list. Crit fail lie: "A shadow spy. The sklaggans use them to watch for intruders, and it will report you."
- **Befriend:** Request **DC 22** (23); connection **DC 17** (18):
  - Show it the right way (Medicine): clean, press, bind, about 10 minutes, until it copies cleanly. Separate from the haunt's disable.
  - The perfect high five (Acrobatics or Performance): out of phase, timing is everything; keep trying until it lands right.
  - Praise the finished job (Performance): once it gets something right, applause, a toast, a little ceremony.
- **Leaves if:** called "good enough" (ignores that PC for the rest of the scene), a light stays on it (hides while it's lit), or the corpse is moved or destroyed first (leaves the hall).
- **Outcomes:** crit success joins with a flawless high five / success joins, wants to finish first / failure returns to the corpse, retry with a new approach or after the haunt is laid to rest / crit failure melts into the dark, gone for the chapter.
- **Rolls:** Table 5 Young (Shadowtongue only; talks with its fingers) · 6 No Variation · 7 Fastidious · 8 Apologetic · 9b Sneezes at Its Weaknesses (body, light, sonic; light above all).
- **Top five (suggested):** make the hurting stop the right way · a perfect high five · sit in lantern light without sneezing · count the P14 tally marks without losing its place · slip through a resin door before it snaps shut.
- **XP:** 20 per PC for befriending it. Only one exists; if missing it would sting, use [Skipping the Request](gm_chapter_1_eldamon.md#variant-skipping-the-request).
- ⚠️ The haunt resets after 1 hour unless the three P20 sklaggans are dead (p. 183), so a PC who comes back after that triggers it again. Befriend while it's disabled or after it stops resetting.
- 🧩 Option (not a book rule): a successful Medicine disable it watches, or the spirit's grim nod once the P20 sklaggans die, counts as a successful connection approach. The nod also crosses off item 1.
- **Softened fortress** (p. 173): it smooths a still, covered shape.

## Encounter 7.4 — The White Hart on the Path (hydgwyn)

**Insert after:** [Pristine Clearing *(defense special resource)*](gm_chapter_7.md#pristine-clearing-defense-special-resource) going in; [Concluding the chapter](gm_chapter_7.md#concluding-the-chapter) coming out · **Where:** the resin-blighted path to the fortress.

> **🎯** Bookend: the same creature going in and coming out shows what the PCs changed. Death fits a land the sklaggans emptied (p. 170-171; Eldamon p. 30).

> *Off the path, a boar lies tangled in black resin, breathing hard. A small white deer steps out of the trees and kneels beside it. Its eyes are full of tiny stars. The boar's breathing slows, eases, and stops. The deer stands, and its starry eyes turn up the path, toward the fortress.*

- **Setup:** A **hydgwyn** (uncommon death deer, Eldamon p. 182) eases the already dying; the stars in its eyes show where it's needed until the mission ends. Find: no check on the walk in; follow it with **DC 22 Survival**. It doesn't flee but keeps its distance.
- **Recognize:** **DC 24 Nature or Religion** (25) or Lore, secret. Success: a death eldamon of mercy. Crit success adds its evolution condition (a great quest or many small ones) and that hydgwyns sometimes travel with people out to end unnaturally long-lived creatures. Crit fail lie: "A death omen. Whoever it looks at won't come back out of that fortress."
- 🔒 Its stars point at the fortress because the Skinloom keeps taking villagers (p. 171). Its mission stays unfinished while the Skinloom works. Don't confirm why it watches the path.
- **The return,** after the Flesh Stitcher falls and the villagers surrender (p. 191):

> *The white deer stands at the path's edge where you first saw it. Its eyes are plain and dark now. The stars are gone. It lowers its head to you, once, and walks into the trees.*

- **Befriend (optional, walk out only):** Request **DC 22** (23); connection **DC 17** (18): read its stars at night or before dawn (Nature or Astronomy Lore), help ease a dying animal (Medicine), or tell it about the Jewel hunt (Society or a fitting Lore). **Leaves if** a dying animal is harmed or rushed in front of it (for good), or it's pulled toward the fortress (it won't go). **Outcomes:** crit success joins, a new star points to the next port / success joins / failure walks off, may wait at the beach for one more try / crit failure gone.
- **Rolls:** Table 5 Typical (Requian and Common) · 6 No Variation · 7 Soft Coloration · 8 Serious · 9a no secret. **XP:** 10 per PC for easing an animal or befriending it. Any PC can befriend it.
