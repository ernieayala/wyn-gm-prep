## Befriending rules

Every chapter's eldamon doc uses these rules. Each ends with why it matters at the table.

### "Capture" means befriend

Eldamon are out of phase: mortal effects never touch them, and the reverse holds too (Eldamon p. 14). Nobody traps or beats one into joining. It chooses a friend.

**At the table:** steer "capture" toward talking.

### Method 2: GM Decides

You place each eldamon where it fits the story (Eldamon p. 29). The book's Standard rate is about two per level across the party. These docs place a few eldamon in each chapter, of mixed elements and rarities. Where elements turn up (Eldamon p. 30):
- **Air:** high winds, high altitude
- **Darkness:** unnaturally dark places, shadow creatures
- **Death:** deserts, badlands, unnaturally dead places, death-trait creatures
- **Earth:** mountains, caves, crystals
- **Electricity:** storms, electric currents
- **Fire:** volcanoes, wildfires
- **Force:** places with strange forces at work
- **Ice:** frigid places
- **Life:** intense life force, vitality and healing
- **Light:** intense natural light
- **Mind:** academies, puzzle rooms, mental creatures
- **Water:** large bodies of water
- **Wood:** lush jungle, ancient forest, plant creatures

**At the table:** these docs did the placing. The list is how you place your own.

### Who can befriend an eldamon

Any PC can befriend any eldamon. An eldamon trainer can phase it in to fight, using one of their daily preparations (Eldamon p. 40). With anyone else it follows phased out and talks; it doesn't fight, though it can battle (Eldamon p. 18). Some eldamon in these docs are sightings that don't approach and aren't expected to be befriended.

**At the table:** let the players decide who befriends what. Any PC can Aid another's attempt.

### The eldamon's level

It equals the befriending PC's level. One melded into a lower-level creature or item can start at that level, and the PC trains it up later (Eldamon p. 28). Trainer eldamon rise with the trainer (Eldamon p. 40). Others level at your call, case by case; the book suggests leveling ones that battle and skipping statistics for ones that don't (Eldamon p. 157).

**At the table:** for this party in Chapter 1, every new eldamon is level 2.

### Finding and recognizing an eldamon

**Find:** Perception against the level-based DC to spot it or work out where it went. Most eldamon are melded at any given moment, and a melded eldamon can't be detected until it unmelds (Eldamon p. 12); unmelded, anyone can see it. **Recognize:** secret Recall Knowledge ([secret checks](gm_chapter_1.md#secret-checks-and-recall-knowledge)) against the level-based DC, **+2 uncommon, +5 rare**. These docs suggest **Nature** for any eldamon (the researcher's focus, Eldamon p. 43), or Occultism for mind, Religion for death or life, Arcana for elemental energy, or a Lore such as Eldamon Lore. The book names no skill.

**At the table:** recognizing it unlocks the cheaper connection check.

### The Request and the connection check

An eldamon that comes to the PCs is already inclined to like them, so one roll befriends it (Eldamon p. 28): a **Diplomacy** Request at the plain **level-based DC**, or, for a PC who recognized it, a skill that shows a connection with what it loves at a "very easy DC" (−5), the **connection DC**. Each PC rolls their own. Degrees follow the core PF2 Request action.

> **📏 Callings (Eldamon p. 43):** whisperer, +2 circumstance to Diplomacy to Make an Impression on or Request of an eldamon and to relationship checks. Researcher, +2 circumstance to Nature to Recall about eldamon, and an immediate Make an Impression (−5) in a non-hostile meeting. Ask each trainer which they took.

**At the table:** the connection roll pays off the player who learned what it loves.

Recall adds **+2 uncommon, +5 rare**. Other PF2 adjustments: **easy −2**, **hard +2**.

### Multiple trainers

When more than one PC wants an eldamon, the book recommends a **group of the same kind** big enough for each to try (Eldamon p. 28–29).

**At the table:** one rare eldamon between two trainers can leave somebody hurt.

### Variant: Skipping the Request

If missing a rare eldamon would sting, you can drop the roll and have it befriend the PC automatically (Eldamon p. 29). Even with a Hero Point, a player can roll two 1s. The book also suggests narrating the ones who didn't come along, such as shy ones scurrying off, so the world still feels full. Later docs flag each rare eldamon.

**At the table:** decide before the roll.

### Trainer limits

- **Daily preparations:** a trainer picks **3 or their key attribute modifier, whichever is less**. Only those phase in that day (Eldamon p. 40).
- **New friends** join the stable at once, but only prepared eldamon phase in, so a new friend can swap into the lineup at the next daily preparations (Eldamon p. 40).
- **Level cap:** no sharing phase with a higher-level eldamon (Eldamon p. 40).

> **⚠️ Gotcha:** an eldamon befriended mid-dungeon can't fight in that dungeon. It follows along phased out until the trainer's next daily preparations.

### Eldamon don't die

At 0 HP an eldamon phases out and goes unresponsive (Eldamon p. 40). Damaged above 0: full HP once it phases out at the end of the encounter. Dropped to 0 by a non-death source: full HP after 1 minute phased out after the encounter. By a death effect: unresponsive and out of phase for 1 hour, then it recovers.

**At the table:** a lost fight costs tempo, never a friend, and no befriending needs a fight against the eldamon.

### Melding

Eldamon meld into fitting creatures, objects, or places, and a long meld can empower the host (Eldamon p. 10, 15). **Creature:** one elemental power of its element, once per minute, or **Eldamon Infused Creature** (+1 level, +1 to AC, attacks, DCs, saves, Perception, and skills, more damage and HP, one power). **Item:** its resistances, weaknesses, quirks, or, in a major case, a relic aspect or imbued property up to its level. **Place:** difficult or hazardous terrain, ley lines, pervasive magic, or an elemental hazard. All optional (Eldamon p. 12).

**At the table:** a meld explains the strange detail. Free the eldamon, then befriend it. Infused Creature's +1 level changes XP; later docs flag it.

### Eldamon battles

Friendly play; the loser is de-energized and unhurt (Eldamon p. 16–19). Agree on venue, stakes, and ruleset; field one, two at once, or a team of six (one or two at a time), the common formats; use normal PF2 combat with each eldamon's statistics for its level; replace a defeated or forfeiting eldamon from the team; usually win when the other side is all at 0 HP. Battles usually run phased out, so a non-trainer's eldamon can battle (Eldamon p. 18).

**At the table (suggestion):** a wild eldamon or an NPC trainer can challenge a trainer. A trainer who wins, or loses as a good sport, earns the eldamon's respect: its Request and connection DCs −2. Use once or twice a campaign; Chapter 9 runs one.

### Customization tables

Under Method 2 you can pick results instead of rolling, and these docs do (Eldamon p. 29, 31–35): **Table 5** age, background, and languages · **6** ability variation · **7** appearance · **8** quirk · **9a/9b** secret, which the book suggests the GM handle even when players roll the rest, so it fits the campaign (Eldamon p. 31). Young speaks only its element's language; Typical adds the local common tongue. Any eldamon has a strong instinctive understanding with its friend (Eldamon p. 157).

**At the table:** the quirk is a roleplay hook; the 🔒 secret is a future scene.

### What eldamon won't do

- **Scout or recall facts.** They see time and memory through a broader lens and can't succeed at Recall Knowledge for an adventure (Eldamon p. 156). A phased-out friend follows its own whims (Eldamon p. 14).
- **Solve the chapter.** In these docs, no skipping a fight the plot needs or exposing a secret villain.
- **Touch the mortal world** alone, beyond small things tied to its passion, and those only over time, such as a squirrelash gathering tiny objects (Eldamon p. 14).

**At the table:** an eldamon sent to scout wanders off after its whims.

### Build your own eldamon encounter

1. **Pick an element that fits the place** ([list](#method-2-gm-decides)). For a trainer, use their pool.
2. **Give it a want:** its evolution condition.
3. **Hide it** in a meld with whatever is strange in the scene, or give it a tell (glow, sound, sneeze). Set a Perception DC.
4. **Set the DCs** from the [table](#level-based-dc-table) at the befriending PC's level.
5. **Write two connection approaches** that give it what it wants.
6. **Add a leave trigger:** whatever insults that want.
7. **Pick Tables 5–9** for voice, quirk, and 🔒 secret.

## Encounter 1.1 — The main deck (Etmoist)

**Insert after:** [B6 — Main Deck](gm_chapter_1.md#b6--main-deck-moderate-1), once both springjaw dolls are down · **Where/when:** the flooded half of the [Gasping Gipper](gm_glossary.md#gasping-gipper)'s main deck, right after. One etmoist (Eldamon p. 256).

> **🎯** Want-driven NPC: etmoists long to understand family (Eldamon p. 256), so this one adopted the two matching dolls, and every approach shows it what kin means.

Read aloud after the book's B6 fight:

> *The second doll clatters into the water and lies still beside the first. The flooded half of the deck goes quiet. Then the water by the hull stirs, and a small shark noses up between the two sailor suits. Its belly glows a faint crimson through the murk. It nudges one doll, then the other, and looks up at the five of you standing together.*

- **Setup:** It's curious. The dolls never moved in two hundred years; the PCs came as a group and check on each other. It wants to know what that is.
- **Find:** Seek, **DC 16 Perception**, spots the crimson glow. Failure: it bumps a PC's leg underwater, so they find it anyway.
- **Recognize:** Nature or a relevant Lore (Sailing, Eldamon), **DC 18**. Success: a water eldamon fascinated by kinship; leaping an evolved one's spikes on a dare is a frowned-upon custom, the source of "jumping the shark" (Eldamon p. 256). Crit success adds its evolution condition (🧩). Crit fail lie: "Shark eldamon circle whoever is bleeding. Keep your wounds out of the water or it'll bite."
- **Befriend:** it came to the PCs on its own. Request **DC 16**, connection **DC 11**:
  - Explain kin (Society): a family custom from the PC's people.
  - Tell a family story (Performance): the PC's family, or the party as one.
  - Introduce the family (Nature or Eldamon Lore): a trainer presents their eldamon one by one.
- **Leaves if:** someone says eldamon can't have family, leaps it on a dare, or smashes the dolls for fun after the fight.
- **Outcomes:** crit success, befriends and counts the PC as kin / success, befriends / failure, stays by the dolls, retry before leaving the Gipper / crit failure, hides from that PC today.
- **Rolls:** Table 5 Typical (Common, Thalassic) · 6 No Variation · 7 Heterochromia · 8 Loving · 9b searching for an old friend
- **XP:** 20 each for befriending it (suggestion), on top of the book's B6 XP.
- **🔒** The old friend is the Gipper's cook, who swam ashore at the wreck two centuries ago. It doesn't know the cook must be long dead. It may perk up at an old g'mayun cook in Rumplank. A record of the Gipper's survivors found in a later chapter could give it an answer, your call.
- **🧩** "Helping etmoist truly come to see the meaning of family." (Eldamon p. 256) Note moments the party acts as kin. Evolution is an 11th-level trainer feature (Eldamon p. 40).

## Encounter 1.2 — The thyalodon (Skepi)

**Insert after:** [C6 — Sacrificial Animal](gm_chapter_1.md#c6--sacrificial-animal-severe-1--the-sapphire-scale-is-here), once the scale is off and the fight is over · **Where/when:** the C6 platform, [Writhing Swamp](gm_glossary.md#writhing-swamp), right after. One skepi (Eldamon p. 228), melded into the thyalodon, Kuaranda's runt.

> **🎯** Melding explains the weird detail: a skepi shielded the thyalodon from the husks' paste, which is why it kept going. Skepis rush into danger for others (Eldamon p. 228).

Read aloud once the thyalodon is calm:

> *The thyalodon's sides heave, and its eyes clear. Then a small goat steps out of its flank as if out of a doorway. It has tufts of red hair, short horns, and a faint golden glow that leaves the herbal muck sliding off the thyalodon's hide where it passes. It plants itself between the beast and the five of you, lowers its horns, and does not move.*

- **Setup:** It guards the thyalodon until it knows the PCs mean well.
- **Find:** no check while melded. Reading its stance: **DC 16 Perception**. Success: it's guarding. Failure: it looks ready to charge.
- **Recognize:** Nature, Religion, or a Lore such as Eldamon Lore, **DC 18**. Success: a life eldamon that throws itself into danger; show it others could be hurt longer than it would to talk it down. Crit success adds its evolution condition (🧩). Crit fail lie: "Life goats feed on wounded beasts. It's been draining the thyalodon."
- **Befriend:** Request **DC 16** (promising to take the beast home is the natural one), connection **DC 11**:
  - Tend the wounds (Medicine): scrape off the paste, treat the cuts while the skepi watches.
  - Take it home (Survival): plot a safe route to Kuaranda in [Jakapo Town](gm_glossary.md#jakapo-town).
  - Calm the beast (Nature): soothe it onto its feet.
- **Leaves if:** someone harms the thyalodon after it's calm, talks about harvesting it, or leaves it in the swamp.
- **Outcomes:** crit success, befriends and guards PC and beast all the way out / success, befriends, stays with the thyalodon until it's home / failure, it follows the thyalodon; retry on the trip to Jakapo Town / crit failure, horns down at that PC all day; another PC can try.
- **Rolls:** Table 5 Typical (Common, Jyoti) · 6 No Variation · 7 Bigger (same size category) · 8 Courageous · 9a no secret
- **XP:** 20 each for befriending it (suggestion), on top of the book's C6 XP.
- **🔒** The meld has no rules effect: the thyalodon's stats, confusion, and the scale stay as written. The skepi knows nothing about Pyksara or the scale.
- **⚠️** C6 is Severe. If the thyalodon dies, the skepi unmelds as it falls, stays with the body, and won't come along today. Suggestion: it reappears in Chapter 2 in Kuaranda's herd in Jakapo Town, guarding another runt, same DCs.
- **🧩** "Helping skepi save others and protect the helpless." (Eldamon p. 228) Note rescues. Bringing the thyalodon home makes Kuaranda a friend for life ([Ch 2](gm_chapter_2.md), Event 7).

## Encounter 1.3 — The puzzle cube (Meese)

**Insert after:** [2F. Quill & Scroll Academy](gm_chapter_1.md#2f-quill--scroll-academy), when a PC opens [Headmistress U'oko](gm_glossary.md#headmistress-uoko)'s puzzle cube (book DC 15 Thievery, p. 24) · **Where:** her library at [Quill & Scroll Academy](gm_glossary.md#quill--scroll-academy). Two meeses (Eldamon p. 244).

> **🎯** The place draws the element: academies and puzzles draw mind eldamon (Eldamon p. 30), and meeses hoard gems. The cube's obsidian drew a pair; their meld is flavor for why U'oko can't solve it.

- Two tiny mice tumble out of the split cube on violet crystal sleds, hide behind the books, then stare at the obsidian. **Find** Seek, DC 16 Perception, finds them on a shelf; failure, they peek out after a minute anyway. Cube unopened: the same check spots a violet glint, and gentle talk draws them out. **Recognize** Nature or Occultism DC 16: gem pack rats who catalog every stone. Crit success: an amazing gem or a complete collection evolves them. Crit fail lie: "Gem mice are a thieves' trick. Someone here trains them to rob the Headmistress."
- **Befriend** Request 16, connection 11: appraise a stone (Crafting), admire the sled (Occultism), or play "which hand" with a gem (Thievery). Each PC rolls for their own. **Hides for the day from** anyone who grabs at them or calls them thieves in anger. **Outcomes:** crit, joins with a worthless gem chip / success, joins / failure, it hides and watches; retry later in the visit / crit failure, hides from that PC today.
- **Rolls:** sparkly one, Typical (Astral, Common, so it translates) · No Variation · Sparkly · Pedantic ("volcanic glass, technically") · no secret. Small one, Young (Astral) · Thievery replaces Stealth · Smaller · Curious · lost item.
- **🔒** The small one lost a rose quartz chip in Barrel Square at a Glitterfest parade. If you run [Event 2](gm_chapter_1.md#event-2--glitterfest-parade-trivial-1) after the Academy, a PC who picks through the glitter with DC 16 Perception finds a rose quartz chip the small one is sure is "it."
- **⚠️** No Academy visit: put them in A8 of the Iron Eels' hideout ([map](gm_chapter_1.md#map-quick-reference)). A befriended meese also wants to catalog the [sapphire scale](gm_chapter_1.md#sapphire-scale-item-3) but can't carry it, since eldamon gather only tiny objects (Eldamon p. 14).
- **XP** 10 each (suggestion), on top of the book's Academy XP.

## Encounter 1.4 — The hold (Morpup)

**Insert after:** [B7 — Haunted Hold](gm_chapter_1.md#b7--haunted-hold-moderate-1), once the [wispy wayfarer](gm_glossary.md#wispy-wayfarer) is destroyed · **Where:** the [Gasping Gipper](gm_glossary.md#gasping-gipper)'s hold, by the prow. One morpup (Eldamon p. 178).

> **🎯** Flip the omen: morpups come when a soul is about to move on and bring hope (Eldamon p. 178). This one came for the captain two centuries ago; he chose his treasure.

- A scarfed, butterfly-winged gray puppy pulls itself out of the timbers, droops at the empty sheet, and drifts toward the PCs for company. **Find** Seek, DC 16 Perception, in the dim hold (low-light vision, darkvision, or a light, as in [B3](gm_chapter_1.md#b3--captains-quarters)); skip B7 and it follows them to the [B1](gm_chapter_1.md#b1--rocky-shoreline) shoreline. **Recognize** Nature or Religion DC 16: a death eldamon feared as an omen, but it comes when a soul is moving on, brings hope, loves company, and often follows the grieving. Crit success: "Helping morpup return safely from a dangerous situation, long journey, or the like." Crit fail lie: "A morpup picks the next to die."
- **Befriend** Request 16, connection 11: a rite over the dead (Religion), bury the skeletal Iron Eels above the tideline (Athletics), or a homecoming shanty (Performance or Sailing Lore). **Leaves if** shooed as an omen, the dead are mocked, or someone jokes about a death to lure more, which the book warns against (Eldamon p. 178). **Outcomes:** crit, joins and wraps its scarf around the PC's wrist / success, joins / failure, it follows at a distance; retry on the walk back / crit failure, sinks into the timbers for that PC today.
- **Rolls:** Typical (Requian, old sailors' Common) · No Variation · Soft Coloration · Patient · tired, melds into nearby objects to rest (expect it in a bedroll or a boot after long rests).
- **🔒** It napped in the timbers until B7 woke it. It didn't cause the haunting and knows nothing about the ship.
- **⚠️** Players and Rumplank sailors may treat it as a curse. A successful Recall sets the record straight.
- **XP** 10 each (suggestion).

## Encounter 1.5 — The snake pit (Squirrelash)

**Insert after:** [C1 — Snake Pit](gm_chapter_1.md#c1--snake-pit-moderate-1), once [Ignatius Barkley](gm_glossary.md#ignatius-barkley) (he/him) is out · **Where:** the branches above, [Writhing Swamp](gm_glossary.md#writhing-swamp). One squirrelash (Eldamon p. 202).

> **🎯** An eldamon that puts a face on loss: some squirrelashes collect friends (Eldamon p. 202). This one collected the [Embersand](gm_glossary.md#embersand) clan before they became husks. Barkley is the last.

- During C1 it watches from the branches, out of phase and unable to help. Once Barkley is out, an ash-gray squirrel with a smoldering tail drops onto his shoulder, scolds him, sneezes, and counts the PCs. He calls it "Smudge," which it hates. **Find** no glow, but Seek, DC 16 Perception, hears a sneeze. Skip C1 and it waits at Barkley's pack in [C2](gm_chapter_1.md#c2--ruined-columns); it won't leave the swamp without him. **Recognize** Nature or Arcana DC 16: a fire eldamon that collects one theme, sometimes friends. Crit success: "Helping squirrelash perfect their collection." Harmless crit fail lie: "A fire squirrel this far from flame is starving. Feed it a fire."
- **Befriend** Request 16, connection 11: introduce every party member and companion by full name (Society), or recite or sing the Embersand names (Performance). **Leaves if** the PCs abandon Barkley, mock collecting people, or flick water at it. **Outcomes:** crit, joins and recounts the party from the PC's head / success, joins, visits Barkley / failure, it stays on Barkley; retry after C6 / crit failure, crosses that PC off its list.
- **Rolls:** Typical (Pyric, Common) · Glowing, so for fire it **doesn't glow** · ash-gray · Determined · sneezes near cold, earth, or water, for laughs only.
- **🔒** It knows nothing about [Pyksara](gm_glossary.md#pyksara) or the [C4](gm_chapter_1.md#c4--gnarled-tree) tree (Eldamon p. 156).
- **XP** 10 each (suggestion), on top of the book's 30 for Barkley. Suggestion: eldamon don't count against the [two-companion limit](gm_chapter_1.md#npc-companions).
