## Encounter 12.1 — The mirror in the prince's rooms (speculil)

**Insert after:** [Scene 1 — The Heroes Return](gm_chapter_12.md#scene-1--the-heroes-return), once talk turns to logistics · **Where/when:** Prince Kulupi's rooms (the book doesn't describe them, so they're yours), [Castle Rumplank](gm_glossary.md#castle-rumplank), the first evening or any of the seven days

> **🎯** Grief through a witness: the mirror lets the royals mourn [Prince Kulupi](gm_glossary.md#prince-kulupi) without revealing anything new, and echoes [Varagix](gm_glossary.md#varagix-the-glimmering)'s vanity (p. 297).

> *The prince's rooms smell of polish and nothing else. Every chair is set square to the wall. Only the mirror looks lived with: a tall oval of silver, its frame rubbed bright where a hand rested on it each morning. When you step in front of it, your reflection arrives a half-beat late, and it doesn't look pleased to see you.*

- **Abilities:** light damage and dazzling; **Mirrored Rebound** turns a foe's critically failed spell or power attack back on it (Eldamon p. 242).
- **Setup:** [Queen Maxelle](gm_glossary.md#queen-maxelle-redfeather) can't search her son's rooms, so she asks the PCs to look for anything that explains him, and leaves.
- **Find:** Perception DC 27: the lag, the warm glass. If nobody succeeds, it shows itself when a PC speaks the prince's name.
- **Recognize:** Nature or Arcana DC 32. Success: a light eldamon that reflects on its choices; on a crit, the book says villains and cunning heroes seek them as oracles, and it wants help seeing its inner self. Crit fail lie: "That's a vanity charm. It shows you what you want to see."
- **Befriend:** Request DC 27, connection DC 22:
  - Palace life (Society): Kulupi's questions were about his image.
  - Varagix (Religion): its flattery fed a vanity god.
  - Play the prince (Deception): ask his morning question and let it give the true answer.
- **Leaves if:** asked to predict the siege or spy, offered a wall as an oracle, or covered or cracked.
- **Outcomes:** crit success befriends, showing the PC in a hero's sash / success befriends after asking, "When you choose, do you look at yourself first, or at the people around you?" / failure retry tomorrow / crit failure it shows only Kulupi and won't speak to that PC again; another PC can try.
- **Rolls:** Table 5 Typical (Luxan, Common) · 6 No Variation · 7 Sparkly · 8 Apologetic · 9B 04–06 (once friends with a villain)
- **XP:** 30 per PC (suggestion)
- ⚠️ "Eldamon are typically incapable of succeeding at Recall Knowledge checks when called for in an adventure" (Eldamon p. 156). It can't tell the PCs about the cult, the waves, or who else serves Varagix, and never names Colvi or Zellura; the players learn about Colvi in [Colvi's True Colors](gm_chapter_12.md#colvis-true-colors-moderate-10) or from his map in Ch 11. It can share that Kulupi asked each morning, "Am I the hero of this story?" and it said yes.
- 🔒 The villain is Kulupi, which the players know by now; it hides how close they were. It doesn't know if he chose the cult; the book says he seemed to act of his own will (p. 306), so leave that open.
- 💡 In [Treachery's End](gm_chapter_12.md#treacherys-end-severe-10), describe the illusory princes' reflections in the speculil's mirror as late. Flavor only, no roll and no rule; Illusory Transposition still swaps him with a clone when he's targeted, and the fight plays out as written.
- 💡 Rare: if missing it would sting, [skip the Request](gm_chapter_1_eldamon.md#variant-skipping-the-request).
- 🧩 Evolution: "Helping speculil truly reflect on their inner self and discover the secret truths hidden within" (Eldamon p. 242). Suggestion: if its trainer talks it through Kulupi's death in the epilogue, after the level-up to 11, call the condition met.

## Encounter 12.2 — The ophini over the crater

**Insert after:** [Celebration](gm_chapter_12.md#celebration) · **Where/when:** above the behemoth's crater, feast night, likely at **level 11** (p. 338)

> **🎯** Motive fits moment: ophinis travel to see sights (Eldamon p. 208), and the behemoth's fall is the best in the Isles.

> *Above the crater a ring of fire turns slowly in the dark, a wheel inside a wheel, covered in eyes. Every eye is fixed on the behemoth's broken body, and the wheel's colors shift with the last glow of the heart gem. Then all the eyes turn to you at once. "Be not afraid!" it calls, and half the crowd screams.*

- **Abilities:** fire damage; not off-guard to flankers its level or lower.
- **Setup:** Let the crowd scream, then laugh.
- **Find:** no check.
- **Recognize:** Nature or Arcana DC 33 (32 at level 10). Success: a wandering fire eldamon; on a crit, stories of new places win it. Crit fail lie: "It's a herald of Varagix, come to see what we did to its master's beast."
- **Befriend:** Request DC 28, connection DC 23 (27 / 22 at level 10):
  - New place (Performance or Lore): describe one the PC has seen and offer to show it (the book's technique), such as Moonshadow Island.
  - Next voyage (Society): port by port.
  - Meet every eye (Intimidation): it respects the unafraid.
- **Leaves if:** told of a place it's seen (Rumplank and the crater count), or asked to stay put.
- **Outcomes:** crit success befriends with sparks over the feast / success befriends / failure it drifts off; another PC can try before dawn / crit failure it rolls away.
- **Rolls:** Table 5 Typical (Pyric, Common) · 6 No Variation · 7 Shifting Colors · 8 Curious · 9A none
- **XP:** 20 per PC (suggestion)
- 💡 Rare: if missing it would sting, [skip the Request](gm_chapter_1_eldamon.md#variant-skipping-the-request).
- 🧩 Evolution: "Helping ophini see truly magnificent sights" (Eldamon p. 208).

## Encounter 12.3 — The storm-tower test (iognus)

**Insert after:** [The districts and their generals](gm_chapter_12.md#the-districts-and-their-generals) · **Where/when:** the first storm tower (or a seawall prototype if the PCs bought none), day 3 or 4. Two storm-chasing iognus.

> *The tower's crown splits the sky. Lightning forks five ways and slams into the practice targets on the tidal flats. Through the afterglow, two shaggy beasts with horns like iron rods come running down the ridge, their coats lit from inside.*

- **Find:** Perception DC 25 warns the crew; failure scatters them harmlessly on the next firing. **Recognize:** Nature DC 29. Success: storm-chasers that feel at home in big storms. Lie: "Thunder-shy beasts. Shut the tower down before they stampede."
- **Befriend:** Request DC 27 each, connection DC 22: Crafting (talk the crew into firing again), Athletics or Acrobatics (run the flats), Survival (promise the eighth morning's barrage).
- **Leaves if:** the tower shuts down for good while they're there, or anyone pens them. Failure retries after the next firing; a crit failure makes that one bolt, and the other can still be befriended.
- **Rolls:** bold: Young · No Variation · Elemental Aura · Energetic · 9A none. Gray: Typical · No Variation · Grayscale · Patient · 9A none. **XP:** 10 per PC (suggestion).

## Eldamon in the siege

**Insert after:** [Heroic actions](gm_chapter_12.md#heroic-actions)

> **🎯** Swap one input, keep the math: the book gives eldamon no role in the [siege subsystem](gm_chapter_12.md#the-siege-subsystem), so this homebrew changes a skill and nothing else.

- **Default:** a trainer can describe a prepared eldamon fighting beside their squadron in any heroic action. It changes nothing; the trainer rolls normally (p. 317–318).
- **Partner stratagem (💡 homebrew):** a trainer with an eldamon prepared that day can make **Inspired Stratagem** with one of its eldamon skills at **DC 27**, in place of Diplomacy, Performance, or Warfare Lore, if the trainer describes how that skill turns the battle. Examples:
  - **Deception** (psyrano, tsurikabe, boteau): bluster or bait that draws an army into the line of fire.
  - **Stealth** (arbush, skolld, kagekko): an ambush that pins an army for a tower's second volley.
  - **Scoring:** normal Inspired Stratagem results and once-per-round cap; a quartermaster's bonus applies.
  - **Cost:** the trainer's heroic action that round and one prepared eldamon slot (Eldamon p. 40). No RP.
  - **Replaces:** only the skill.
- **Water eldamon (💡 homebrew):** a trainer with one prepared gets the lower [Calm](#encounter-124--calming-the-ancient-water-eldamon-not-befriendable) DC.
- ⚠️ No eldamon damage, Guard, or extra actions. The math assumes one heroic action per PC per round.

## Encounter 12.4 — Calming the Ancient Water Eldamon (not befriendable)

**Insert after:** [Wave 3 — Vanguard](gm_chapter_12.md#wave-3--vanguard) · **Where/when:** [Rosewater](gm_glossary.md#rosewater), Wave 3, beside the Myzoform Broods

> **🎯** A win with a price: calming spares the eldamon but costs actions, and the wave still has to be won.

- **The book:** army, Aquatic, **Damage 7, HP 70** (p. 321), frenzied by the cult (the book doesn't say how). It can be hurt because "certain ancient eldamon can phase themselves in" (Eldamon p. 15).
- **Recognize:** a PC in Rosewater can try once when the wave begins, using no heroic action: Nature or Arcana DC 32 (💡). Crit success: in pain, means no harm, soothable. Success: a phased-in eldamon driven wild. Failure: it looks like a water elemental gone mad. Crit fail lie: "It's a cult summoning. Hurting it is the only way to break the spell."
- ⚠️ It's an army: never befriended. Calming sends it home; towers phase it out still frenzied.
- ⚠️ Peerless Ruby's tale of a rain-bringing water eldamon ([Ch 10](gm_chapter_10.md#tales-and-refreshments)) is a jungle rumor the book doesn't link to this one. Keep them apart unless you choose otherwise.

### The Calm heroic action

> **💡 Calm (homebrew):** only in Rosewater, only in Wave 3, only by a PC in Rosewater.
> - **Check:** Diplomacy or Nature, **DC 27**. **DC 25** for a trainer with a water eldamon prepared that day, or a PC who succeeded at Recognizing it.
> - **Critical success:** reduce its HP by **20**, and it deals no damage in step 4 this round.
> - **Success:** reduce its HP by **10**.
> - **Failure:** reduce its HP by **5**.
> - **Critical failure:** no effect.
> - It takes step 2 of the [wave round](gm_chapter_12.md#wave-rounds). Quartermaster bonus and hero points work as usual.

**Scoring:** all damage reduces the same 70 HP. Keep a separate **Calm tally** of HP removed by Calm. At 0 HP: **35+** means **calmed**; under 35 means driven off, as in the book.

**Cost:** each Calm replaces an assault, Repair, or stratagem, and tower damage doesn't count toward the tally, so players who want it calmed aim Rosewater's towers at the Broods. Meanwhile it deals 7 a round and ignores non-aquatic Guard (p. 313), so nearly all of it hits Rosewater.

**Reward if calmed (💡):** Rosewater regains **10 HP** (up to its maximum) at wave's end. 30 XP per PC.

Read or paraphrase when it's calmed:

> *The river stops climbing the walls. The shape at its center settles through a dozen forms, eel and turtle and something with too many fins, and holds still for a moment, looking at you. Then the water slides back into its banks and flows upstream, against the current, and the mist goes with it.*

- 🔒 The Broods still attack, and the wave [ends normally](gm_chapter_12.md#ending-a-wave).
