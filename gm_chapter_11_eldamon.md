## Encounter 11.1 — The unfinished gadget (centeslas)

**Insert after:** [Z7 — Malabria's Workshop](gm_chapter_11.md#z7--malabrias-workshop-severe-9) · **Where/when:** on Falarni's nearly finished **etheric essence disruptor** (p. 294), once she's beaten, captured, or gone.

Two are here, so two PCs can each befriend one.

> **🎯** Want-driven NPC with a trap: centeslas finish what they start (Eldamon p. 200), and Falarni gave them a job that needs hands. The book's disruptor check (DC 30 Crafting, 4 hours, that evening) becomes the payoff.

> *Among the half-built projects, one gadget looks closest to done: a squat casing of brass and glass with a single panel left open. Two lines of blue light crawl along its wiring, back and forth, back and forth. One is a bright, steady crackle. The other barely flickers. Each light has a great many tiny legs.*

- **Setup:** two rare electricity eldamon (Eldamon p. 200) drawn to Falarni's sparks. She couldn't cage them, so she told them the disruptor needed them to finish it. A centesla stuck on an impossible task loses hope and withdraws, and, made to choose between the task and its friends, may switch tasks to keep them. Their meld doesn't change the disruptor.
- **Find:** automatic if a PC picks up or examines the disruptor, else Search DC 24 Perception (25). Mid-fight, a look at the bench shows only faint sparks; hold the scene until the fight ends.
- **Recognize:** DC 31 Nature, Arcana, or Eldamon Lore (32). Success: ambitious completionists, some hoarding odd treasures. Crit success: the hope and friends notes above. Crit fail lie: "Those are capacipod larvae. Leave them alone and they'll cocoon into storm moths." Centeslas never cocoon.
- **Befriend:** Request DC 26 Diplomacy (27) per centesla, connection DC 21 (22):
  - **Promise to finish it** (Crafting): study it for a minute, talk through the last steps and set one part right. It doesn't replace the DC 30 check.
  - **A piece for the hoard** (Society, Crafting, or Lore): offer an odd object and its story. A spare part works if the PC can tell its story.
  - **Friends over the task** (Diplomacy): for the second, once the first says yes. Ask it to come with its partner.
- **Leaves if:** the disruptor is scrapped, smashed, or sold; unfinished work is mocked; or they're told to quit with nothing finished or offered instead.
- **Outcomes:** crit success: befriends and shows the last loose connection (homebrew: +1 circumstance to the DC 30 check) / success: befriends if the disruptor gets finished, and checks on it until then / failure: stays with it; carried out, another try that evening / crit failure: goes dark until the disruptor is finished, then approaches on its own for another try.
- **Rolls, bigger:** Table 5 31–80 Typical (Voltan, Common from Falarni's monologues) · 6 01–50 No Variation · 7 51–52 Bigger · 8 37 Determined · 9a 01–90 no secret
- **Rolls, Specimen 2:** Table 5 31–80 Typical (Voltan, Common) · 6 76–80 Glowing (a despairing flicker, full once befriended) · 7 53–54 Smaller · 8 99 Vulnerable · 9a 91–99, 9b 70–72 an evil organization hunts it: the cult, since Falarni marked it her best specimen
- **Prop:** Falarni's schematics: *"Specimens 1 and 2. Phase-shifted electrical arthropods. Cannot be caged. CAN be assigned."* Specimen 2 is circled.
- **XP:** 30 per PC for one or both (suggestion)
- **⚠️ Gotcha:** left behind, they stay with the disruptor and leave the campaign unless you bring them back. Say aloud that the sparks cling to the gadget, so the choice is the players'.
- **🔒 Questioning Falarni:** if she's captured and asked about them, she's dismissive, per p. 293: *"The bugs? Fascinating. Can't be caged, can't be dissected, but give them a job and they never leave. Better than staff."* Nothing about the cult's leaders.
- **🔒 Near Z19:** they crackle and press close to their friends. It's the ritual's energy and reveals nothing about who leads it.
- **🧩 Evolution:** "Helping centesla satisfy their completionism." (Eldamon p. 200). Finishing the disruptor fits, as does any big Chapter 12 job it takes on. In Chapter 12 a Barrel Square mannequin can grab at Specimen 2 as flavor (p. 319); leave the army's stats alone.
- **Abilities:** Segmented Capacitor (Eldamon p. 200). Whoever makes the Crafting check gets the moment.

## Encounter 11.2 — The kitchen myrmind

**Insert after:** [Z11 — Kitchen](gm_chapter_11.md#z11--kitchen) · **Where/when:** the kitchen, whenever the PCs enter. Optional: use it if the party is having fun and the pacing allows.

> **🎯** Bait for a skippable room: a lonely eldamon pulls players toward the culinary supply kit.

> *A thin line of light marches along the spice shelf, jar by jar. At the end of the line sits an ant-shaped creature, striped yellow and black, polishing a spoon that's already clean. It looks up at you, then past you at the door, as if counting how many more of you are coming.*

- **Setup:** an uncommon mind eldamon (Eldamon p. 248), out of its element's places because it followed the cult's cooks (Eldamon p. 29). Nobody gave it a ritual role. **Find:** no check; it comes to them. **Recognize:** DC 28 Nature, Occultism, or Eldamon Lore (29); success: it needs a group role. Crit fail lie: "It's a scout for the ant swarms in the workshop. It'll call them." It won't.
- **Befriend:** Request DC 26 Diplomacy (27), or **Give it a job** (Crafting, Cooking Lore, or Society) DC 21 (22): pack the supply kit while the PCs gather it, or join the marching order. **Leaves if** left alone or told it isn't needed. Failure: it follows; try later in the complex. Crit failure: it waits for the cooks.
- **Rolls:** Table 5 31–80 Typical (Astral, Common) · 6 01–50 No Variation · 7 73–74 Cousin (bee stripes, stinger, same Strike) · 8 78 Responsible · 9a 01–90 no secret. **XP:** 10 per PC (suggestion).
- **🔒** It knew only the cooks and doesn't know what the ritual is; asked where everyone went, it points at the floor, which tells no more than the Z8 trail. **🧩 Evolution:** "Helping myrmind truly become part of a group and fully embody their role in it." (Eldamon p. 248). Give it a real Chapter 12 defense job.
