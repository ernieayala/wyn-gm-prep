## A

### Academy of Tastes
**Type:** Place (Seaview, Ch 5). Top Isles university, founded 200 years ago by [Gustavus Gollihew](#gustavus-gollihew), whose archived maps locate the three missing Jewel fragments. Clocktower rings at each of the seven orpok mealtimes. The [Sous Scholar](#sous-scholar-limbin-gumnammar) runs it since the Dean de Cuisine's murder.
**At the table:** Archive bans water, fire, and eating.
**Eldamon:** [the Breath Thief](#the-breath-thief) sits in the dean's kitchen ([Ch 5 eldamon](gm_chapter_5_eldamon.md)).

### Acolyte Bituminous and Acolyte Igneous
**Type:** Creatures (automatic acolytes, BB 19, Creature 8; Ch 8, R7 and R8). The cult's rival construct assistants in the sunken temple, each with two animated incense holders.
- **Bituminous** — pitch-black robe; earth and water magic. Suspicious, can be calmed.
- **Igneous** — lava-colored robe; summoner. Holds "court," then attacks without warning.

### Aeternal dragon
**Type:** Ancestry (dragon). Dragons shrunk to about orc size by the **lux aeterna** ritual to live among mortals; many die young, possibly from the ritual. Common among wild and indigo dragons, so the Isles have many. About 2% of Rumplank.

### Akirin
**Type:** NPC (dead, Ch 4, G14). One of Poppy's last students, from Flank Street. His diary (Handout 3) suspects Poppy had secret passages and tells [Zakatari](#zakatari)'s fate. He watched Poppy burn his pendant in her hearth the night before his final test.

### Aksyanis Amphitheater
**Type:** Place (Ch 2). Historic amphitheater over the [Hissing Chasm](#hissing-chasm), named for an orpok conductor who left the Isles seeking otherworldly musicians. Kuaranda plays the [gentle whistler](#gentle-whistler) here amid waves of enraged wildlife.
**Eldamon:** a cephalittle is melded into the stage's carved lines ([Ch 2 eldamon](gm_chapter_2_eldamon.md)).

### Allasha the Surveyor
**Type:** NPC (Ch 5). LG female orpok cartographer 5 (Clean Lines Surveying), cousin of Councilor [Gunlu Pongo](#gunlu-pongo). Her surveys pass for official maps. Two yomhibdis are altering her plat maps, spiraling in on the [Wavewatcher Inn](#wavewatcher-inn). Gives a geomantic stone (Ch 12 resource).

### Amber dragon
**Type:** Dragon. Friendly, nosy gossips who spread rumors, true or not, and favor crowded cities; local ones spread many Poppy's-map rumors. Poppy reputedly dueled one, [Helexes](#helexes).

### Amon
**Type:** Deity. God of revelry, Rumplank's beloved patron of leisure and parties; sibling of [Castilli](#castilli). **Ch 9:** both crash Founder's Day. *See: [The Balance](#the-balance).*
**At the table:** Balance gods walk among mortals: "Throw a party, and Amon might crash it."

### Ancient Water Eldamon
**Type:** Creature (army, Ch 12, Wave 3). Aquatic army, **Damage 7, HP 70** (p. 321): a shifting water [eldamon](#eldamon) from the Misty River's headwaters, frenzied by the cult, attacking [Rosewater](#rosewater). It can be hurt because certain ancient eldamon can phase themselves in (Eldamon p. 15). Never befriended.
**At the table:** A homebrew **Calm** heroic action can send it home instead of driving it off ([Ch 12 eldamon](gm_chapter_12_eldamon.md#encounter-124--calming-the-ancient-water-eldamon-not-befriendable)).

### Aoz
**Type:** Deity. Twin of Life, paired with [Zoa](#zoa); their temples always stand together. Fishing and farming make Aoz big on the Isles. Rules nature spirits ([vitae](#vitae)). *See: [The Balance](#the-balance).*

### Apraka Ogari
**Type:** NPC, potential companion (Ch 3). LN agender kragrak wizard 3. Old geomancer from Kraken, in Shiverskull Cave for over a year. Joins only after the PCs kill the F11 rottskal; leaves through the F25 portal (p. 76).

### Aquamarine Wedge
**Type:** Artifact (item 7). 🔒 Second [Jewel](#jewel-of-the-indigo-isles) fragment: blue-green crystal star edged dark blue and pale green. **Ch 5:** on a pedestal in Poppy's [Briargrove](#briargrove) cave (L7). Invested at the neck, it grants water breathing.

### Arahosko the Red
**Type:** NPC (dead). "Shellfire," galtzagorri evoker of the [Fortunate Four](#fortunate-four), Poppy's chief cartographer, and the crew's only Goldcrop native ([Jakapo Town](#jakapo-town)). She **made and cursed the treasure map**, created the [sapphire scale](#sapphire-scale), and oversaw the building of [Castle Rumplank](#castle-rumplank) and [Quill & Scroll Academy](#quill--scroll-academy). Her ritual unlocked every Razorback temple door before the attack (Ch 8).
**At the table:** U'oko jokes she could only be beaten by **dressing her in blue** (her least favorite color): drape her Ch 4 G25 statue in blue. Descendant [S'matzia](#smatzia) lifts the map's curse (Ch 2).

### Aranelle
**Type:** NPC/creature (Ch 10, W14). Unique living blade, Creature 10: a Jirigati warrior's spirit guarding the abandoned desert. Tests the PCs in battle, then offers herself to the best fighter; her wielder is bound by geas to slay powerful monsters and protect Goldcrop's people.

### Aylushrixa the Azure Inferno
**Type:** Creature (Ch 11, Y4). Unique NE elder sapphire drake, Creature 11 (p. 288), ruler of the [Embattled Caverns](#embattled-caverns). The cult brings tribute and respects her rule; she passes them and kills other intruders. Greedy and cruel, but keeps her word.
**At the table:** Kulupi has described the PCs to her: expect a fight. Flees below 40 HP. Captured, she reluctantly switches sides and stays loyal.

## B

### Bacoo
**Type:** Ancestry. Fey shapeshifters descended from amphibious mangrove fey, staying on the harsh Material Plane anyway. Party-loving tricksters, slow to trust, then loyal for life. Rarer in Rumplank than g'mayun, orpoks, or chochori.
**At the table:** Can shrink into a bonded bottle. Bacoo mercenaries charge less paid in rum, milk, or bananas.

### The Balance
**Type:** Religion. The Isles' main faith: parents **Tova the Father** (Law) and **Lilin the Mother** (Chaos), and paired children like [Aoz](#aoz)/[Zoa](#zoa) (life/death) and [Amon](#amon)/[Castilli](#castilli) (revelry/restraint). [Prismatic Colvi](#prismatic-colvi)'s temple serves it.

### Bannog
**Type:** Ancestry. Dream-realm rabbit-folk who value dreaming as much as waking. They can feed on souls but choose not to; on the Isles they needn't.

### Barnacle Bay
**Type:** Place (Goldcrop Island). [Rumplank](#rumplank)'s calm harbor of blue water, coral, and sandy beaches. Unrelated to Poppy: early explorers chose an ugly name to deter others. **Ch 12** climax.

### Barrel Square
**Type:** Place (Rumplank district). "New Rumplank": market, food stalls, party space, tourist hub. Holds Pinkwater Well and the [Gilded Pirate](#gilded-pirate) statue (plaque: *"May the party never end, Poppy von Barnacle!"*).
**Ch 9:** [Okko Bramblesnout](#okko-bramblesnout)'s eldamon bout, in a chalk ring beside the statue ([Ch 9 eldamon](gm_chapter_9_eldamon.md)).

### Batarsi
**Type:** NPC (Ch 2 boss). Unique CE male galtzagorri hunter (BB 35), Creature 2, Tiny. Shamed outcast from Jakapo Town who stole the [gentle whistler](#gentle-whistler). Makes terrifying dolls and wooden spies in an abandoned wizard's tower; vainly rides a silk-tailed beetle, Goosebark.
**At the table:** At 15 HP or less he begs for mercy, then attacks once the PCs relax.

### Beakbluster Hills
**Type:** Place (Rumplank district). Old-money hillside of manors and imitation [tarukis](#taruki), named for stiff bay winds.
**At the table:** Residents hire adventurers for treasure and pay well for security; they resist evacuating in Ch 12.

### Blood mouse
**Type:** Creature (BB 14). Nasty Goldcrop jungle rodents. Many g'mayuns eat them ("find a mouse, eat a mouse"); the Claw Quarter hosts illegal blood-mouse fights. **Ch 1:** Riupito's pet [Squints](#riupito--squints).

### Bluebell Island
**Type:** Place (Indigo Isles). Known for its bluebell harvest. Main town: [Seaview](#seaview), home of the **Academy of Tastes**. **Ch 5.**

### Bolfo
**Type:** NPC (Ch 2, Event 5). Galtzagorri gardener (she/her) trapped by a sanguine rose between her huts.
**At the table:** 🔒 [S'matzia](#smatzia)'s unadmitted dearest friend: telling S'matzia the PCs saved her earns 10 gp.
**Eldamon:** a squishberry lives in her garden ([Ch 2 eldamon](gm_chapter_2_eldamon.md)).

### Boss Ozgold
**Type:** NPC (offscreen, Ch 3). Tireless orpok boss of the [Emerald Earth Mining Company](#emerald-earth-mining-company). Took his ledger when the company fled; his journal at F8 (in Orpok) covers the mine's discovery, the shale spitters, and the beast that drove the miners out.

### The Breath Thief
**Type:** Eldamon (grima, death; Ch 5). A lean black cat with ears and tail glowing like banked coals, found on the cold stove in the Dean de Cuisine's kitchen at the [Academy of Tastes](#academy-of-tastes). Students blame it for the murder; grimas only keep the last breaths of the already dying. Touching its brightest bead shows the dean's last memory ([Ch 5 eldamon](gm_chapter_5_eldamon.md#encounter-53--the-breath-thief-grima)).
**At the table:** Accusing it of the murder, or flaring bright light, drives it off.

### Briargrove
**Type:** Place (Bluebell Island, Ch 5). Thorny forest over a mile wide, 6 miles from Seaview; briars are greater difficult, hazardous terrain. Hides the [spongeberry](#spongeberry) bush and Poppy's rope-golem-guarded [Aquamarine Wedge](#aquamarine-wedge) cave.

### Bruised Crystal
**Type:** Artifact (item 7). 🔒 Third [Jewel](#jewel-of-the-indigo-isles) fragment: reddish-purple crystal wedge; held, it grants resistance 3 to bludgeoning. Hidden by Poppy in [Gullstone Cave](#gullstone-cave), stolen by [Umbrocious](#umbrocious) to power the [Skinloom](#skinloom). **Ch 7:** the PCs pull it out.

### Buoyant heart
**Type:** Concept. Rumplank's carefree, wholehearted spirit of generosity and good cheer, paired with ["the never-ending gala."](#the-never-ending-gala)

## C

### Captain Bloodtail
**Type:** NPC (Ch 9). NE male g'mayun, tall and blood-red-feathered; head of [Garardi's School for Scamps & Scallywags](#garardis-school-for-scamps--scallywags). 5th dan in the dueling tournament (Creature 7 in the duel block). 🔒 [Prismatic Colvi](#prismatic-colvi) converted him; the school is the cult's recruiting ground.
**At the table:** A braggart, sure he'll win.

### Captain Nevergo
**Type:** NPC (Ch 1, Scene 2B). Surly [Claw Quarter](#claw-quarter) g'mayun apothecary who sells poisons out the back at night. Real name Nellard; the habitual liar never sailed, and his fake pirate drawl earned the joke nickname. One of [D'marti](#dmarti-tixima)'s few friends.
**At the table:** Lies so much he's lost track of the truth; shoos away paying customers.

### Castilli
**Type:** Deity. **God of restraint**, "straight man" to [Amon](#amon)'s joker, invoked to warn lazy children. The setting book makes them brothers, Castilli often nursing Amon after parties; Alacar's gods have no fixed gender. **Ch 9:** at Founder's Day, two men matching each watcher's ancestry. **Ch 12:** women calling each other "sister." *See: [The Balance](#the-balance).*

### Castle Rumplank
**Type:** Place. Redfeather royal seat of native lava stone on the Isle of Flowers, north Barnacle Bay. Built under [Arahosko](#arahosko-the-red) in the [Rumplank Renaissance](#rumplank-renaissance) from dismantled sacred [tarukis](#taruki). **Ch 1** opens here.
**At the table:** Guards have orders not to hurt trespassers.

### Chakisa
**Type:** Deity. God of summer, worshipped year-round in the Isles' steady climate; many homes keep his small shrine.

### Chochori
**Type:** Ancestry. Living coral in loose humanoid form, mostly sea-dwelling; about 4% of Rumplank. Each belongs to one of seven [chori](#chori-chochori-chora): personality types and social roles, **not genders**.
**At the table:** Don't compare chori to genders around a chochori; they're tired of correcting outsiders.

### Chori (chochori chora)
**Type:** Concept. Seven chochori personality types, each tied to an element. Two you'll hear:
- **Hypisk** (water): gentle, thoughtful, considered wise.
- **Farose** (fire): brave, confident, impatient.

### Chromatic Queen
**Type:** Ship. [Poppy](#poppy-von-barnacle)'s legendary flagship. **End of Ch 4:** found in a secret cove under Shivering Mountain; the [Obsidian Star](#obsidian-star) unlocks its helm so the PCs can escape. **Ch 5:** the royals fund its 2,000 gp restoration if the PCs first sail the royal route with the royal crew. A royal crew means Prince Kulupi commands; if the PCs won't share authority, he sails as envoy. After that voyage the PCs own it outright.

### Clamardinia Wakamarsis
**Type:** NPC. "Dini," nonbinary g'mayun ex-pirate who owns the [Rumpled Feather](#rumpled-feather); rival of [Molo](#molo-make-sick-dogodor). **Ch 1:** only in a critical-failure Iron Eels rumor. **Ch 9:** the Rumpled Feather hosts the Poppy's Prize tournament.

### Clancy
**Type:** NPC (Ch 1, A5). LE male orpok olfamancer (p. 390, Creature 1). Iron Eels chef, as good with a knife on a gangplank as in the kitchen; Melar Kan's lifelong friend and, since Malardy left, favorite. Sniffs out rare spell ingredients. Drew the A9 Redfeather caricatures.
**At the table:** Caught in the galley, he plays simple cook, then throws magical gumbo that can turn fists to stone.

### Claw Quarter
**Type:** Place (Rumplank district). Gray-skied rough district of pirates, con artists, and blood-mouse rings, plus artisans, street performers, and a kindly orphanage. [Iron Eels](#iron-eels) turf, near [Ironsalt Cove](#ironsalt-cove).

### Crackbeak Salt Desert
**Type:** Place (Goldcrop Island). Inhospitable salt flat in Goldcrop's dry leeward zone, beside the Okapi Hills. **Ch 10** chase.
**Eldamon:** a klorse, a pale horse of death that looks like an omen and isn't, trots beside the party; a squamatch suns itself at the Cliffside Ruin ([Ch 10 eldamon](gm_chapter_10_eldamon.md)).

### Crackmaw & Litzi
**Type:** NPCs (Ch 1, Event 1). Two lowest-ranking Iron Eels, g'mayun goons (BB 34, Creature 0) Melar Kan sends to shake down the PCs. Flee when hurt.
**At the table:** Captured, Crackmaw mocks the PCs and dares them to visit the hideout near Ironsalt Cove.

### Crawling Caves
**Type:** Place (near Rumplank, Chs 9–10). Deadly cave labyrinth; the royals have offered 1,000 gp for a complete map for generations. [Pinomeri](#pinomeri-elemarte) was last seen at the entrance. 🔒 The cult's Ch 9 escape route.

### Crooked Terrace
**Type:** Place. Claw Quarter main street of tenements, meeting halls, fortune tellers, and buskers, under Melar Kan's balcony (A12).

### Crustyard
**Type:** Place (Rumplank district). Industrial shipping hub (shipwrights, dockhands, warehouses), distinct from the [Claw Quarter](#claw-quarter). Nearby Egger's Ends & Oddities posts weekly bounties.

### Cult of the Shivering Behemoth
**Type:** Organization. 🔒 The hidden enemy: a demonic cult **worshiping [Varagix](#varagix-the-glimmering)**, named for the **shale behemoth** [Jakorvo](#jakorvo) controlled with the [Jewel](#jewel-of-the-indigo-isles). Destroyed by Poppy in 5822; quietly revived under **[Zellura](#zellura)**, **[Prismatic Colvi](#prismatic-colvi)**, and **[Prince Kulupi](#prince-kulupi)**, advised by the demon [Zaroz](#zaroz). Recruits via [Garardi's School for Scamps & Scallywags](#garardis-school-for-scamps--scallywags); armed by [Falarni Malabria](#falarni-malabria), whose research it funds. Goal: reanimate the behemoth and conquer Rumplank.
**At the table:** Initiates hear of "an ancient dragon god who brings wealth"; only upper ranks know Varagix is a demon. Members carry hidden gems instead of symbols. *Full summary: [Synopsis — The cult](gm_story_synopsis.md#the-cult).*

## D

### Dalynky
**Type:** NPC (Ch 2, Jakapo Town). LG nonbinary galtzagorri researcher 3 (they/them), assisted by Bopa. Shows off "dinosaur bones" that are 🔒 ruins of an unknown alien culture.

### Dardus Farm
**Type:** Place (Bluebell Island, Ch 5). Humble orpok family's bluebell farm, 2 miles outside Seaview. 🔒 [Pelborii](#pelborii-karnihar)'s ritual drove off its protective spirits, letting in a coven of three feld hags, one posing as "Goodly Granny." Rescuing the family earns thunder lilies (Ch 12 resource).
**Eldamon:** after the rescue, a phidi tends the wounded ([Ch 5 eldamon](gm_chapter_5_eldamon.md)).

### Distant Star
**Type:** Ship (wreck, Ch 8, Q3). Ran aground in Runaway Reef a few weeks before the PCs arrive; g'mayun-with-spyglass figurehead. [Phantom stardust](#phantom-stardust) in the hold.

### D'marti Tixima
**Type:** NPC, potential companion (Ch 1, Scene 2A). LN genderfluid g'mayun ranger 1 (they/them). Chatty, hopelessly scattered jungle explorer with a huge overstuffed backpack; knows Goldcrop's plants and animals. Found the second map half in a cherry-wood scroll case, then lost it at the [Gutted Snake](#gutted-snake).
**At the table:** Best friend Og'matu. Paces outside Greensand Corral, working up the nerve to talk to Lutilu; feuding with Jalarpi Quick-Cutter. Gives guided tours in the setting book.

### Drowned Jungle
**Type:** Place (Goldcrop Island, Ch 10). Rainy, not wholly natural jungle beside the salt desert; home to [Pliticori Village](#pliticori-village).
**At the table:** Peerless Ruby half-believes "a powerful water eldamon" brings the rain; three rumored versions, none confirmed ([Ch 10 eldamon](gm_chapter_10_eldamon.md#the-jungles-water-eldamon-not-befriendable)).

### Drunken Wall
**Type:** Place (Old Rumplank, Ch 12). Built during a drunken bender; a free Guard 10 wall in the siege.

### Dugari, Hope Eater
**Type:** Creature (Ch 3, F15). NE Large runebranded aurochs (BB 79), Creature 4. 🔒 The cult's sacrificial beast, in centuries-long magical stasis (immune to all effects) until anyone steps onto the cavern floor.

### Duko
**Type:** NPC (Ch 1, A9). Illiterate Iron Eel who scrawled his name across Malardy's old room and painted its stunning murals of Melar Kan versus a giant squid and a skeleton horde. **DC 20 Perception** or **DC 15 Art Lore**: same paint.

## E

### Earth zuggle
**Type:** Creature (Ch 3, F25). Caterpillar-like elementals of crystal-studded clay (based on the ice zuggle, BB 123) that pop out of an elemental rift now and then. Earth-trait damage heals them; too much makes them explode.

### Ebrugeses
**Type:** Deity. God of feasts and good food (she/her), blessing festival meals. Friendly with Amon; feuds with Castilli and her brother Havath, her greatest enemy. **Ch 1:** the orpok statue in the Temple of Many Colors.

### The Eld
**Type:** Religion. Ancient elemental faith predating [the Balance](#the-balance), honoring elemental power, [eldamon](#eldamon), and [vitae](#vitae); source of [Summer Moon Festival](#summer-moon-festival) rites. [Varagix](#varagix-the-glimmering) was once an Eld earth spirit.
**At the table:** Eld followers bristle when Balance clergy credit Aoz and Zoa with the cycle of life and death.

### Eldadex
**Type:** Reference. The species list of all 54 eldamon by element and rarity, with the chapters each appears in: [Eldadex quick reference](gm_chapter_1_eldamon.md#eldadex-quick-reference).

### Eldamon
**Type:** Creature type (*Battlezoo Eldamon*). Elemental monsters slightly out of step with reality, dwelling in creatures, places, and things. Commonest in the Isles, as are their [trainers](#eldamon-trainer) and [elemental avatars](#elemental-avatar). Thirteen elements: air, darkness, death, earth, electricity, fire, force, ice, life, light, mind, water, wood. Each has a pet form, a battle form, and a rarely seen evolved form. They're [phased](#phase) out of the mortal world, often [melded](#melding) into something, and never die: at 0 HP they phase out and recover. Rumors speak of ancient eldamon that can phase into reality unaided (see [Ancient Water Eldamon](#ancient-water-eldamon)).
**At the table:** They're befriended by talking, never captured; any PC can befriend one. Every chapter has an eldamon doc ([Ch 1](gm_chapter_1_eldamon.md) holds the [befriending rules](gm_chapter_1_eldamon.md#befriending-rules)). Eldamon can't succeed at Recall Knowledge for the party.

### Eldamon trainer
**Type:** Class (*Battlezoo Eldamon*). Shares phase with befriended eldamon so they can fight in the mortal world. Prepares 3 eldamon each day, or their key attribute modifier if lower (Eldamon p. 40); from 5th level, a trainer with a +4 key attribute prepares 4 (Advanced Training, Eldamon p. 45). New friends swap in at the next daily preparations. Gains Eldamon Evolution at 11th level, letting an eldamon whose evolution condition is met take its evolved form (Eldamon p. 40).

### Elemental avatar
**Type:** Class (*Battlezoo Eldamon*). Houses an eldamon within themself and gains its elemental ability, though not its form or evolution abilities (Eldamon p. 54, 158).

### Embattled Caverns
**Type:** Place (Ch 11, area Y). Mined-out caves leading to cult headquarters, ruled by the sapphire drake [Aylushrixa](#aylushrixa-the-azure-inferno); leftover corundum makes the walls twinkle. Cultists' passphrase earns the drake's escort.

### Embersand
**Type:** Clan. [Ignatius Barkley](#ignatius-barkley)'s wildfire leshy clan in the [Writhing Swamp](#writhing-swamp), all corrupted into [leshy husks](#leshy-husk) by the dryad husk [Pyksara](#pyksara) while he was away.

### Emerald Earth Mining Company
**Type:** Organization (Chs 2–3). Up to a dozen orpok gem miners under [Boss Ozgold](#boss-ozgold) who worked Shiverskull Cave a few decades ago, then fled shale spitters and worse. Their camp (F5–F11) remains, signed "Permanently Closed."
**Eldamon:** a pangeolil still keeps one miner's secret in the camp ([Ch 3 eldamon](gm_chapter_3_eldamon.md)).

## F

### Falarni Malabria
**Type:** NPC (Ch 11, Z7). 🔒 Unique g'mayun inventor, Creature 10, she/her. Amoral genius Kulupi flattered into the cult with unlimited research funds; no Varagix worshiper. Hired the Ch 9 arsonists and supplied their explosives; builds bootcutter ant swarms and trap rooms. Stat block says NE, cult article CE.
**At the table:** Surrenders below 40 HP once her potions or bodyguard are gone, answering questions freely and with contempt. Her mannequins attack Rumplank in Ch 12.
**Eldamon:** two centeslas she couldn't cage crawl through her unfinished disruptor, told it needs them ([Ch 11 eldamon](gm_chapter_11_eldamon.md)).

### Fippin and Mippin Humcakes
**Type:** NPCs (Chromatic Queen crew, Act 2). NG orpok twin cooks of tasty, unhealthy meals: chatty Fippin (he/him) slips off to gossip; Mippin (they/them) is diligent. They befriend orpok PCs and PCs who cook. **Ch 6:** they tell [Shackled Marni](#shackled-marni)'s story.

### Flesh Stitcher
**Type:** Creature (Ch 7 boss). Unique NE Huge shadow aberration, Creature 8: a variant like three [sklaggans](#sklaggan) fused back to back. Artistic prima donna leading Moonshadow's Shadow Plane invaders from the old cult fortress; "the Master" in Umbrocious's notes. 🔒 Its superiors want the [Skinloom](#skinloom) finished as an invasion gateway.
**At the table:** Lavish Skinloom praise buys a round or two before it attacks. Never leaves the Skinloom room.

### Fortunate Four
**Type:** Group. [Poppy von Barnacle](#poppy-von-barnacle)'s legendary companions:
- [Arahosko the Red](#arahosko-the-red): galtzagorri evoker and mapmaker.
- [Jesea Swiftarrow](#jesea-swiftarrow): hardriggan ranger.
- [Pinomeri Elemarte](#pinomeri-elemarte): indigo dragon cleric of Ebrugeses.
- [Undayo of Stonegulch](#undayo-of-stonegulch): kragrak barbarian.

**At the table:** Each has a Ch 4 G25 statue with a breaking riddle; [Headmistress U'oko](#headmistress-uoko)'s Ch 1 facts answer them. (Ch 4 spells the barbarian "Undoyo" and "Undayo".)

### Founder's Day
**Type:** Festival. Yearly celebration ("Founding Day") of Poppy's supposed founding of Rumplank, normally one early-summer day; in Ch 9 the royals delayed it for the PCs' return. Contests, the **Rum Run** (a drunken obstacle course), mystery pies, and the biggest parade outside [Nevala](#nevala). **Ch 9:** the cult strikes during it.
**Eldamon:** a bakindle beside the Show of Skill, three lemiures at the Tall Tale Trial, and [Okko Bramblesnout](#okko-bramblesnout)'s bout ([Ch 9 eldamon](gm_chapter_9_eldamon.md)).

## G

### Galtzagorri
**Type:** Ancestry. Tiny, anxious, ladybug-winged sprites with hook spears: hardworking builders who fret when idle and care little for money. Most of Rumplank's 5% "sprites." [Jakapo Town](#jakapo-town) is their giant-mushroom village.

### Garardi's School for Scamps & Scallywags
**Type:** Organization (Rumplank). Jazper Garardi's overpriced, shoddy school claiming to train Poppy-style pirates, run by [Captain Bloodtail](#captain-bloodtail). 🔒 "Special classes" indoctrinate students into the cult; graduates become Ch 10's cult scallywags.

### Garnikor
**Type:** NPC (Chromatic Queen crew, Act 2 on). LN female hardriggan bosun 7. Ex-teamster, ex-smuggler, convict with a commuted sentence. Terse, fair mentor whose cat-o'-nine-tails rarely comes out; Kulupi leaves the sailing to her. Sells *potions of water breathing*; owns the blood mouse [Leafling](#leafling).

**At the table:** Petty crime ashore, then months at sea until the heat dies. The bored crew wants to sail soon.

**Type:** Place (Claw Quarter waterfront). Site of the [Iron Eels' hideout](#iron-eels). More than one body has washed up here.

**Type:** Concept (Chs 2–3). Six 5-foot lava-rock obelisks along Poppy's map route; touching the sapphire scale to each charges it. The Ch 3 F27 secret door opens only for a fully charged scale.

### Wisp
**Type:** NPC (Ch 10). CG nonbinary galtzagorri scout (they/them), [Sovala](#sovala)'s partner. Tries to sell gullible PCs a "sacred" stick for 100 gp. Not [Wisps](#wisps), the Ch 1 husk.

### Wisps
**Type:** NPC (Ch 1, C5). [Leshy husk](#leshy-husk) (BB 44, Creature 0) praying in Sylvan to the snakedead; once [Barkley](#ignatius-barkley)'s best friend: *"my main mate, Wisps."* Spotting the PCs within 20 feet, it spends 4 actions untying the snakedead.

### Wispy Wayfarer
**Type:** Creature (BB 100, Creature 2). Ghost of the [Gasping Gipper](#gasping-gipper)'s captain, who stayed with his stolen treasure through the crash. **Ch 1, B7:** disguised as a ragged sheet on the footlocker; spotting it takes a **Seek** action, **DC 22 Perception**, and a way to see in dim light. Raised the dead Iron Eels as [skeletons](#skeletal-iron-eel).

### Wo'a wood
**Type:** Material and defense special resource (Ch 4, G7). Gold-colored hardwood from a rare Goldcrop tree; the Pillar of Grace in Poppy's proving grounds is a mast from a destroyed cult warship. Ch 12: upgrades a Wall to a Fortress Wall, or trades for 10 Resource Points.
**At the table:** The book never says how to extract the pillar.

### Writhing Swamp
**Type:** Place (Goldcrop Island, Ch 1 Scene 6). Miserable bog southeast of [Rumplank](#rumplank): miles of briny muck and snakes, crossed only by Jakapo Town's flying galtzagorris. Its ruins hold the [Embersand](#embersand) leshy husks and, at C6, the [sapphire scale](#sapphire-scale).
**Eldamon:** a squirrelash that collected the Embersand clan rides [Barkley](#ignatius-barkley)'s shoulder, and a skepi is melded into the C6 [thyalodon](#thyalodon) ([Ch 1 eldamon](gm_chapter_1_eldamon.md)).

## Z

### Zakatari
**Type:** NPC (Ch 4, G11). Parasite husk (BB 71), Creature 3. Disliked student who tried to cheat the Testing Hall with a box of magic worms, which consumed him; Poppy and the students bricked him into the well room. While he lives, dormitory sleepers get *nightmare*.

### Zanaya
**Type:** NPC alias. [X'na'zed](#xnazed)'s name in his g'mayun-woman disguise. A g'mayun Zanaya also narrates the book's gazetteer, "Zanaya's Guide to Rumplank," never confirmed as X'na'zed.

### Zaroz
**Type:** Demon. 🔒 Varagix's envoy, summoned by [Zellura](#zellura) in 6021; watches and advises the cult's three leaders for his own ends.

### Zellura
**Type:** NPC. 🔒 The [cult](#cult-of-the-shivering-behemoth)'s Grand Oracle, "Lady of Bone and Bounty": bone-adorned priestess of Varagix, half alive and half dead, with bone-white feathers and blood-red eyes. Pirates killed her family and set her adrift; Varagix's visions kept her alive. Hates Rumplank's romantic Poppy pirate legends. Revived the cult from 5992; made Kulupi official leader in 6022. **Ch 12:** fights alongside [Shaleborn Poppy](#shaleborn-poppy).

### Zoa
**Type:** Deity. Twin of Death, paired with [Aoz](#aoz); respected on the Isles as part of the natural cycle. Zoa's temples offer undead-hunting work. *See: [The Balance](#the-balance).*
