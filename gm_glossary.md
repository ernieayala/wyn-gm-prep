## A

### Academy of Tastes
**Type:** Place (Seaview, Ch 5). The best university in the Isles. [Gustavus Gollihew](#gustavus-gollihew) founded it 200 years ago. His maps sit in its archive and show where the three missing Jewel fragments are. The clocktower rings at each of the seven orpok mealtimes. The Dean de Cuisine was murdered, so the [Sous Scholar](#sous-scholar-limbin-gumnammar) runs it now.
**At the table:** No water, fire, or eating in the archive.
**Eldamon:** [the Breath Thief](#the-breath-thief) sits in the dean's kitchen ([Ch 5 eldamon](gm_chapter_05_eldamon.md)).

### Acolyte Bituminous and Acolyte Igneous
**Type:** Creatures (automatic acolytes, BB 19, Creature 8; Ch 8, R7 and R8). Two construct helpers of the cult in the sunken temple. They're rivals. Each has two animated incense holders.
- **Bituminous:** pitch-black robe. Uses earth and water magic. Suspicious, but the PCs can calm it.
- **Igneous:** lava-colored robe. A summoner. Holds "court," then attacks without warning.

### Aeternal dragon
**Type:** Ancestry (dragon). Dragons shrunk to about orc size so they can live among mortals. The **lux aeterna** ritual shrinks them. Many die young, maybe because of the ritual. Wild and indigo dragons often become aeternal, so the Isles have many. About 2% of Rumplank.

### Akirin
**Type:** NPC (dead, Ch 4, G14). One of Poppy's last students, from Flank Street. His diary is Handout 3. In it, he suspects Poppy had secret passages. It also tells what happened to [Zakatari](#zakatari). The night before his final test, he watched Poppy burn his pendant in her hearth.

### Aksyanis Amphitheater
**Type:** Place (Ch 2). An old, famous amphitheater over the [Hissing Chasm](#hissing-chasm). It's named for an orpok conductor who left the Isles to look for musicians from other worlds. Kuaranda plays the [gentle whistler](#gentle-whistler) here while waves of angry wildlife attack.
**Eldamon:** a cephalittle is melded into the stage's carved lines ([Ch 2 eldamon](gm_chapter_02_eldamon.md)).

### Allasha the Surveyor
**Type:** NPC (Ch 5). LG female orpok cartographer 5 (Clean Lines Surveying). Cousin of Councilor [Gunlu Pongo](#gunlu-pongo). People treat her surveys as official maps. Two yomhibdis are changing her property maps. The changes spiral in toward the [Wavewatcher Inn](#wavewatcher-inn). She gives a geomantic stone (Ch 12 resource).

### Amber dragon
**Type:** Dragon. Friendly, nosy gossips. They spread rumors, true or not, and like crowded cities. Local amber dragons spread many rumors about Poppy's map. People say Poppy dueled one, [Helexes](#helexes).

### Amon
**Type:** Deity. God of revelry. Rumplank loves him as its patron of leisure and parties. Sibling of [Castilli](#castilli). **Ch 9:** both gods crash Founder's Day. *See: [The Balance](#the-balance).*
**At the table:** Balance gods walk among mortals. Locals say, "Throw a party, and Amon might crash it."

### Ancient Water Eldamon
**Type:** Creature (army, Ch 12, Wave 3). An aquatic army, **Damage 7, HP 70** (p. 321). It's a shifting water [eldamon](#eldamon) from the headwaters of the Misty River. The cult drove it into a frenzy, and it attacks [Rosewater](#rosewater). The PCs can hurt it because some ancient eldamon can phase themselves into the world (Eldamon p. 15). The PCs can never befriend it.
**At the table:** A homebrew **Calm** heroic action can send it home. Otherwise the PCs drive it off ([Ch 12 eldamon](gm_chapter_12_eldamon.md#encounter-124--calming-the-ancient-water-eldamon-not-befriendable)).

### Aoz
**Type:** Deity. Twin of Life, paired with [Zoa](#zoa). Their temples always stand side by side. Aoz is big on the Isles because of fishing and farming. Rules nature spirits ([vitae](#vitae)). *See: [The Balance](#the-balance).*

### Apraka Ogari
**Type:** NPC, potential companion (Ch 3). LN agender kragrak wizard 3. An old geomancer from Kraken. Has been in Shiverskull Cave for over a year. Joins only after the PCs kill the F11 rottskal. Leaves through the F25 portal (p. 76).

### Aquamarine Wedge
**Type:** Artifact (item 7). 🔒 The second [Jewel](#jewel-of-the-indigo-isles) fragment. A blue-green crystal star with dark blue and pale green edges. **Ch 5:** it sits on a pedestal in Poppy's [Briargrove](#briargrove) cave (L7). Invested at the neck, it grants water breathing.

### Arahosko the Red
**Type:** NPC (dead). Nickname "Shellfire." The galtzagorri evoker of the [Fortunate Four](#fortunate-four) and Poppy's chief mapmaker. The only crew member born on Goldcrop ([Jakapo Town](#jakapo-town)).
- She **made and cursed the treasure map**.
- She made the [sapphire scale](#sapphire-scale).
- She oversaw the building of [Castle Rumplank](#castle-rumplank) and [Quill & Scroll Academy](#quill--scroll-academy).
- Before the attack, her ritual unlocked every door of the Razorback temple (Ch 8).

**At the table:** U'oko jokes that the only way to beat Arahosko was **dressing her in blue**, her least favorite color. So the PCs drape her Ch 4 G25 statue in blue. Her descendant [S'matzia](#smatzia) lifts the map's curse (Ch 2).

### Aranelle
**Type:** NPC/creature (Ch 10, W14). Unique living blade, Creature 10. She's the spirit of a Jirigati warrior, guarding the empty desert. She tests the PCs in battle. Then she offers herself to the best fighter. A geas binds her wielder to kill powerful monsters and protect the people of Goldcrop.

### Aylushrixa the Azure Inferno
**Type:** Creature (Ch 11, Y4). Unique NE elder sapphire drake, Creature 11 (p. 288). She rules the [Embattled Caverns](#embattled-caverns). The cult brings her tribute and respects her rule. She lets cultists pass and kills other intruders. Greedy and cruel, but she keeps her word.
**At the table:** Kulupi has described the PCs to her, so expect a fight. She flees below 40 HP. If captured, she switches sides against her will and stays loyal.

## B

### Bacoo
**Type:** Ancestry. Fey shapeshifters. Their ancestors were amphibious fey of the mangroves. They stay on the harsh Material Plane anyway. They're tricksters who love parties. They're slow to trust, then loyal for life. Rumplank has fewer of them than g'mayun, orpoks, or chochori.
**At the table:** A bacoo can shrink into a bottle it's bonded to. Bacoo mercenaries charge less if you pay in rum, milk, or bananas.

### The Balance
**Type:** Religion. The main faith of the Isles.
- The parents: **Tova the Father** (Law) and **Lilin the Mother** (Chaos).
- Their children come in pairs, like [Aoz](#aoz)/[Zoa](#zoa) (life/death) and [Amon](#amon)/[Castilli](#castilli) (revelry/restraint).

[Prismatic Colvi](#prismatic-colvi)'s temple serves it.

### Bannog
**Type:** Ancestry. Rabbit-folk from a dream realm. They value dreaming as much as being awake. They can feed on souls but choose not to. On the Isles they don't need to.

### Barnacle Bay
**Type:** Place (Goldcrop Island). [Rumplank](#rumplank)'s calm harbor, with blue water, coral, and sandy beaches. The name has nothing to do with Poppy. Early explorers picked an ugly name to keep others away. Site of the **Ch 12** climax.

### Barrel Square
**Type:** Place (Rumplank district). Called "New Rumplank." It has a market, food stalls, party space, and lots of tourists. Pinkwater Well is here, and so is the [Gilded Pirate](#gilded-pirate) statue. The plaque reads: *"May the party never end, Poppy von Barnacle!"*
**Ch 9:** [Okko Bramblesnout](#okko-bramblesnout)'s eldamon bout, in a chalk ring beside the statue ([Ch 9 eldamon](gm_chapter_09_eldamon.md)).

### Batarsi
**Type:** NPC (Ch 2 boss). Unique CE male galtzagorri hunter (BB 35), Creature 2, Tiny. Jakapo Town shamed him and cast him out. He stole the [gentle whistler](#gentle-whistler). In an abandoned wizard's tower, he makes scary dolls and wooden spies. He's vain and rides a silk-tailed beetle named Goosebark.
**At the table:** At 15 HP or less he begs for mercy. Once the PCs relax, he attacks.

### Beakbluster Hills
**Type:** Place (Rumplank district). A hillside of old-money manors and fake [tarukis](#taruki). Named for the strong winds off the bay.
**At the table:** Residents hire adventurers to find treasure. They pay well for security. They don't want to evacuate in Ch 12.

### Blood mouse
**Type:** Creature (BB 14). Nasty rodents from the Goldcrop jungle. Many g'mayuns eat them ("find a mouse, eat a mouse"). The Claw Quarter runs illegal blood-mouse fights. **Ch 1:** Riupito's pet [Squints](#riupito--squints).

### Bluebell Island
**Type:** Place (Indigo Isles). Known for its bluebell harvest. Main town: [Seaview](#seaview), home of the **Academy of Tastes**. **Ch 5.**

### Bolfo
**Type:** NPC (Ch 2, Event 5). Galtzagorri gardener (she/her). A sanguine rose has her trapped between her huts.
**At the table:** 🔒 She's [S'matzia](#smatzia)'s dearest friend, but S'matzia won't admit it. If the PCs tell S'matzia they saved Bolfo, they earn 10 gp.
**Eldamon:** a squishberry lives in her garden ([Ch 2 eldamon](gm_chapter_02_eldamon.md)).

### Boss Ozgold
**Type:** NPC (offscreen, Ch 3). The tireless orpok boss of the [Emerald Earth Mining Company](#emerald-earth-mining-company). He took his ledger when the company fled. His journal is at F8, written in Orpok. It covers how they found the mine, the shale spitters, and the beast that drove the miners out.

### The Breath Thief
**Type:** Eldamon (grima, death; Ch 5). A lean black cat whose ears and tail glow like coals in a banked fire. It sits on the cold stove in the Dean de Cuisine's kitchen at the [Academy of Tastes](#academy-of-tastes). Students blame it for the murder. Grimas only keep the last breaths of people already dying. Touching its brightest bead shows the dean's last memory ([Ch 5 eldamon](gm_chapter_05_eldamon.md#encounter-53--the-breath-thief-grima)).
**At the table:** It leaves if someone accuses it of the murder or flashes a bright light.

### Briargrove
**Type:** Place (Bluebell Island, Ch 5). A thorny forest over a mile wide, 6 miles from Seaview. The briars are greater difficult terrain and hazardous terrain. The [spongeberry](#spongeberry) bush is hidden here. So is Poppy's cave with the [Aquamarine Wedge](#aquamarine-wedge), guarded by a rope golem.

### Bruised Crystal
**Type:** Artifact (item 7). 🔒 The third [Jewel](#jewel-of-the-indigo-isles) fragment. A reddish-purple crystal wedge. When held, it grants resistance 3 to bludgeoning. Poppy hid it in [Gullstone Cave](#gullstone-cave). [Umbrocious](#umbrocious) stole it to power the [Skinloom](#skinloom). **Ch 7:** the PCs pull it out.

### Buoyant heart
**Type:** Concept. Rumplank's carefree spirit of giving freely and good cheer. It goes hand in hand with ["the never-ending gala."](#the-never-ending-gala)

## C

### Captain Bloodtail
**Type:** NPC (Ch 9). NE male g'mayun, tall, with blood-red feathers. Head of [Garardi's School for Scamps & Scallywags](#garardis-school-for-scamps--scallywags). 5th dan in the dueling tournament (Creature 7 in the duel block). 🔒 [Prismatic Colvi](#prismatic-colvi) converted him. The cult uses the school to recruit.
**At the table:** A braggart. He's sure he'll win.

### Captain Nevergo
**Type:** NPC (Ch 1, Scene 2B). A grumpy g'mayun apothecary in the [Claw Quarter](#claw-quarter). At night he sells poisons out the back. His real name is Nellard. He lies all the time and never sailed. People call him "Captain Nevergo" as a joke because of his fake pirate accent. One of [D'marti](#dmarti-tixima)'s few friends.
**At the table:** He lies so much he's lost track of the truth. He shoos away paying customers.

### Castilli
**Type:** Deity. **God of restraint.** Castilli plays the "straight man" to [Amon](#amon)'s joker. Parents call on Castilli to warn lazy children. The setting book makes them brothers, and Castilli often nurses Amon after parties. The gods of Alacar have no fixed gender.
- **Ch 9:** at Founder's Day, they appear as two men. Each watcher sees them as their own ancestry.
- **Ch 12:** they appear as women who call each other "sister."

*See: [The Balance](#the-balance).*

### Castle Rumplank
**Type:** Place. Home of the Redfeather royals, built of local lava stone. It stands on the Isle of Flowers in north Barnacle Bay. [Arahosko](#arahosko-the-red) oversaw the building during the [Rumplank Renaissance](#rumplank-renaissance). The builders took apart sacred [tarukis](#taruki) for the stone. **Ch 1** opens here.
**At the table:** Guards have orders not to hurt trespassers.

### Chakisa
**Type:** Deity. God of summer. The Isles' weather stays the same all year, so people worship him all year. Many homes keep a small shrine to him.

### Chochori
**Type:** Ancestry. Living coral in a loose humanoid shape. Most live in the sea. About 4% of Rumplank. Each one belongs to one of seven [chori](#chori-chochori-chora). Chori are personality types and social roles, **not genders**.
**At the table:** Don't compare chori to genders around a chochori. They're tired of correcting outsiders.

### Chori (chochori chora)
**Type:** Concept. Seven chochori personality types. Each one is tied to an element. Two you'll hear:
- **Hypisk** (water): gentle, thoughtful, considered wise.
- **Farose** (fire): brave, confident, impatient.

### Chromatic Queen
**Type:** Ship. [Poppy](#poppy-von-barnacle)'s legendary flagship.
- **End of Ch 4:** the PCs find it in a secret cove under Shivering Mountain. The [Obsidian Star](#obsidian-star) unlocks its helm, so the PCs can escape on it.
- **Ch 5:** fixing it up costs 2,000 gp. The royals pay if the PCs first sail the royal route with the royal crew.
- A royal crew means Prince Kulupi commands. If the PCs won't share command, he sails along as an envoy.
- After that voyage, the PCs own the ship outright.

### Clamardinia Wakamarsis
**Type:** NPC. Goes by "Dini." A nonbinary g'mayun ex-pirate who owns the [Rumpled Feather](#rumpled-feather). Rival of [Molo](#molo-make-sick-dogodor). **Ch 1:** shows up only in an Iron Eels rumor on a critical failure. **Ch 9:** the Rumpled Feather hosts the Poppy's Prize tournament.

### Clancy
**Type:** NPC (Ch 1, A5). LE male orpok olfamancer (p. 390, Creature 1). The Iron Eels' chef. He's as good with a knife on a gangplank as in the kitchen. He's been Melar Kan's friend all his life. Since Malardy left, he's Melar Kan's favorite. He sniffs out rare spell ingredients. He drew the cartoons of the Redfeathers in A9.
**At the table:** If the PCs catch him in the galley, he acts like a simple cook. Then he throws magic gumbo that can turn fists to stone.

### Claw Quarter
**Type:** Place (Rumplank district). A rough district under gray skies. It has pirates, con artists, and blood-mouse fights. It also has artisans, street performers, and a kind orphanage. [Iron Eels](#iron-eels) turf, near [Ironsalt Cove](#ironsalt-cove).

### Crackbeak Salt Desert
**Type:** Place (Goldcrop Island). A harsh salt flat in the dry leeward side of Goldcrop, beside the Okapi Hills. Site of the **Ch 10** chase.
**Eldamon:** a klorse trots beside the party. It's a pale horse of death that looks like an omen but isn't one. A squamatch suns itself at the Cliffside Ruin ([Ch 10 eldamon](gm_chapter_10_eldamon.md)).

### Crackmaw & Litzi
**Type:** NPCs (Ch 1, Event 1). The two lowest-ranking Iron Eels, g'mayun goons (BB 34, Creature 0). Melar Kan sends them to shake down the PCs. They flee when hurt.
**At the table:** If captured, Crackmaw mocks the PCs. He dares them to visit the hideout near Ironsalt Cove.

### Crawling Caves
**Type:** Place (near Rumplank, Chs 9–10). A deadly maze of caves. For generations, the royals have offered 1,000 gp for a complete map. [Pinomeri](#pinomeri-elemarte) was last seen at the entrance. 🔒 The cult escapes through here in Ch 9.

### Crooked Terrace
**Type:** Place. The main street of the Claw Quarter. It has run-down apartments, meeting halls, fortune tellers, and street musicians. Melar Kan's balcony (A12) looks over it.

### Crustyard
**Type:** Place (Rumplank district). The shipping and industry hub, with shipwrights, dockhands, and warehouses. It's separate from the [Claw Quarter](#claw-quarter). Egger's Ends & Oddities, nearby, posts bounties each week.

### Cult of the Shivering Behemoth
**Type:** Organization. 🔒 The hidden enemy. A demon cult that **worships [Varagix](#varagix-the-glimmering)**. It's named for the **shale behemoth** that [Jakorvo](#jakorvo) controlled with the [Jewel](#jewel-of-the-indigo-isles).
- Poppy destroyed it in 5822.
- It came back quietly under **[Zellura](#zellura)**, **[Prismatic Colvi](#prismatic-colvi)**, and **[Prince Kulupi](#prince-kulupi)**. The demon [Zaroz](#zaroz) advises them.
- It recruits through [Garardi's School for Scamps & Scallywags](#garardis-school-for-scamps--scallywags).
- [Falarni Malabria](#falarni-malabria) makes its weapons, and the cult pays for her research.
- Goal: bring the behemoth back to life and conquer Rumplank.

**At the table:** New members hear about "an ancient dragon god who brings wealth." Only the upper ranks know Varagix is a demon. Members carry hidden gems instead of holy symbols. *Full summary: [Synopsis — The cult](gm_story_synopsis.md#the-cult).*

## D

### Dalynky
**Type:** NPC (Ch 2, Jakapo Town). LG nonbinary galtzagorri researcher 3 (they/them). Bopa is their assistant. They show off "dinosaur bones." 🔒 The bones are really ruins of an unknown alien culture.

### Dardus Farm
**Type:** Place (Bluebell Island, Ch 5). A humble bluebell farm run by an orpok family, 2 miles outside Seaview. 🔒 [Pelborii](#pelborii-karnihar)'s ritual drove off the farm's guardian spirits. That let in a coven of three feld hags. One of them pretends to be "Goodly Granny." Rescuing the family earns thunder lilies (Ch 12 resource).
**Eldamon:** after the rescue, a phidi tends the wounded ([Ch 5 eldamon](gm_chapter_05_eldamon.md)).

### Distant Star
**Type:** Ship (wreck, Ch 8, Q3). It ran aground in Runaway Reef a few weeks before the PCs arrive. The figurehead is a g'mayun with a spyglass. [Phantom stardust](#phantom-stardust) is in the hold.

### D'marti Tixima
**Type:** NPC, potential companion (Ch 1, Scene 2A). LN genderfluid g'mayun ranger 1 (they/them). A chatty jungle explorer who can't stay organized. Carries a huge, stuffed backpack. Knows Goldcrop's plants and animals. Found the second map half in a cherry-wood scroll case. Then lost it at the [Gutted Snake](#gutted-snake).
**At the table:** Their best friend is Og'matu. They pace outside Greensand Corral, working up the nerve to talk to Lutilu. They're feuding with Jalarpi Quick-Cutter. In the setting book, they give guided tours.

### Drowned Jungle
**Type:** Place (Goldcrop Island, Ch 10). A rainy jungle beside the salt desert. It isn't fully natural. [Pliticori Village](#pliticori-village) is here.
**At the table:** Peerless Ruby half-believes that "a powerful water eldamon" brings the rain. There are three rumored versions, and none is confirmed ([Ch 10 eldamon](gm_chapter_10_eldamon.md#the-jungles-water-eldamon-not-befriendable)).

### Drunken Wall
**Type:** Place (Old Rumplank, Ch 12). Someone built it while on a long drunk. In the siege, it's a free Guard 10 wall.

### Dugari, Hope Eater
**Type:** Creature (Ch 3, F15). NE Large runebranded aurochs (BB 79), Creature 4. 🔒 The cult's sacrificial beast. It has been frozen by magic for centuries and is immune to all effects. It wakes when anyone steps onto the cavern floor.

### Duko
**Type:** NPC (Ch 1, A9). An Iron Eel who can't read. He scrawled his name across Malardy's old room. He also painted the room's amazing murals of Melar Kan fighting a giant squid and a skeleton horde. **DC 20 Perception** or **DC 15 Art Lore** shows it's the same paint.

## E

### Earth zuggle
**Type:** Creature (Ch 3, F25). Elementals shaped like caterpillars, made of clay studded with crystals. They're based on the ice zuggle (BB 123). Now and then they pop out of an elemental rift. Damage with the earth trait heals them. Too much of it makes them explode.

### Ebrugeses
**Type:** Deity. God of feasts and good food (she/her). She blesses festival meals. She's friendly with Amon. She feuds with Castilli and with her brother Havath, her greatest enemy. **Ch 1:** the orpok statue in the Temple of Many Colors.

### The Eld
**Type:** Religion. An ancient elemental faith, older than [the Balance](#the-balance). It honors elemental power, [eldamon](#eldamon), and [vitae](#vitae). The [Summer Moon Festival](#summer-moon-festival) rites come from it. [Varagix](#varagix-the-glimmering) was once an earth spirit of the Eld.
**At the table:** Eld followers get prickly when Balance clergy give Aoz and Zoa credit for the cycle of life and death.

### Eldadex
**Type:** Reference. The list of all 54 eldamon species, by element and rarity. It gives the chapters each one appears in: [Eldadex quick reference](gm_chapter_01_eldamon.md#eldadex-quick-reference).

### Eldamon
**Type:** Creature type (*Battlezoo Eldamon*). Elemental monsters a little out of step with reality. They live in creatures, places, and things. The Isles have more of them than anywhere else. The same goes for their [trainers](#eldamon-trainer) and [elemental avatars](#elemental-avatar).
- **Thirteen elements:** air, darkness, death, earth, electricity, fire, force, ice, life, light, mind, water, wood.
- **Forms:** each has a pet form, a battle form, and an evolved form that people rarely see.
- **Phase:** they're [phased](#phase) out of the mortal world. They're often [melded](#melding) into something.
- **No death:** they never die. At 0 HP they phase out and recover.
- **Rumors:** some ancient eldamon can phase into reality on their own (see [Ancient Water Eldamon](#ancient-water-eldamon)).

**At the table:** The PCs befriend eldamon by talking and never capture them. Any PC can befriend one. Every chapter has an eldamon doc. The [Ch 1](gm_chapter_01_eldamon.md) doc has the [befriending rules](gm_chapter_01_eldamon.md#befriending-rules). Eldamon can't succeed at Recall Knowledge for the party.

### Eldamon trainer
**Type:** Class (*Battlezoo Eldamon*). Shares phase with befriended eldamon so they can fight in the mortal world.
- Prepares 3 eldamon each day, or a number equal to their key attribute modifier if that's lower (Eldamon p. 40).
- From 5th level, a trainer with a +4 key attribute prepares 4 (Advanced Training, Eldamon p. 45).
- New eldamon friends swap in at the next daily preparations.
- Gains Eldamon Evolution at 11th level. Then an eldamon whose evolution condition is met can take its evolved form (Eldamon p. 40).

### Elemental avatar
**Type:** Class (*Battlezoo Eldamon*). Holds an eldamon inside themself and gains its elemental ability. They don't gain its form or its evolution abilities (Eldamon p. 54, 158).

### Embattled Caverns
**Type:** Place (Ch 11, area Y). Mined-out caves that lead to cult headquarters. The sapphire drake [Aylushrixa](#aylushrixa-the-azure-inferno) rules them. Leftover corundum makes the walls twinkle. If someone says the cultists' passphrase, the drake escorts them.

### Embersand
**Type:** Clan. [Ignatius Barkley](#ignatius-barkley)'s clan of wildfire leshies in the [Writhing Swamp](#writhing-swamp). While he was away, the dryad husk [Pyksara](#pyksara) corrupted all of them into [leshy husks](#leshy-husk).

### Emerald Earth Mining Company
**Type:** Organization (Chs 2–3). Up to a dozen orpok gem miners led by [Boss Ozgold](#boss-ozgold). A few decades ago, they worked Shiverskull Cave. Then they fled from shale spitters and worse. Their camp (F5–F11) is still there, with a "Permanently Closed" sign.
**Eldamon:** a pangeolil still keeps one miner's secret in the camp ([Ch 3 eldamon](gm_chapter_03_eldamon.md)).

## F

### Falarni Malabria
**Type:** NPC (Ch 11, Z7). 🔒 Unique g'mayun inventor, Creature 10, she/her. A genius with no morals. Kulupi flattered her into the cult with unlimited research money. She doesn't worship Varagix. She hired the Ch 9 arsonists and gave them their explosives. She builds bootcutter ant swarms and trap rooms. Her stat block says NE, but the cult article says CE.
**At the table:** Once her potions or her bodyguard are gone, she surrenders below 40 HP. She answers questions freely, with contempt. Her mannequins attack Rumplank in Ch 12.
**Eldamon:** she couldn't cage two centeslas. They crawl through her unfinished disruptor because someone told them it needs them ([Ch 11 eldamon](gm_chapter_11_eldamon.md)).

### Fippin and Mippin Humcakes
**Type:** NPCs (Chromatic Queen crew, Act 2). NG orpok twins who cook tasty, unhealthy meals. Fippin (he/him) is chatty and slips off to gossip. Mippin (they/them) works hard. They make friends with orpok PCs and PCs who cook. **Ch 6:** they tell [Shackled Marni](#shackled-marni)'s story.

### Flesh Stitcher
**Type:** Creature (Ch 7 boss). Unique NE Huge shadow aberration, Creature 8. It's a variant that looks like three [sklaggans](#sklaggan) joined back to back. A vain artist who leads Moonshadow's Shadow Plane invaders from the old cult fortress. Umbrocious's notes call it "the Master." 🔒 Its bosses want the [Skinloom](#skinloom) finished as a gateway for an invasion.
**At the table:** Lots of praise for the Skinloom buys a round or two before it attacks. It never leaves the Skinloom room.

### Fortunate Four
**Type:** Group. [Poppy von Barnacle](#poppy-von-barnacle)'s legendary companions:
- [Arahosko the Red](#arahosko-the-red): galtzagorri evoker and mapmaker.

- [Pinomeri Elemarte](#pinomeri-elemarte): indigo dragon cleric of Ebrugeses.

**At the table:** Each one has a Ch 4 G25 statue. Each statue has a riddle that tells how to break it. [Headmistress U'oko](#headmistress-uoko)'s Ch 1 facts answer the riddles. Ch 4 spells the barbarian two ways, "Undoyo" and "Undayo."

### Founder's Day
**Type:** Festival. Also called "Founding Day." A yearly party for the day Poppy is said to have founded Rumplank. It's usually one day in early summer. In Ch 9 the royals put it off until the PCs came back. It has contests, mystery pies, and the **Rum Run**, a drunken obstacle course. Its parade is the biggest one outside [Nevala](#nevala). **Ch 9:** the cult strikes during it.
**Eldamon:** a bakindle beside the Show of Skill, three lemiures at the Tall Tale Trial, and [Okko Bramblesnout](#okko-bramblesnout)'s bout ([Ch 9 eldamon](gm_chapter_09_eldamon.md)).

## G

### Galtzagorri
**Type:** Ancestry. Tiny, nervous sprites with ladybug wings and hook spears. They're hard-working builders. They fret when they have nothing to do, and they care little for money. They make up most of the 5% of Rumplank counted as "sprites." [Jakapo Town](#jakapo-town) is their village of giant mushrooms.

### Garardi's School for Scamps & Scallywags
**Type:** Organization (Rumplank). Jazper Garardi's school. It costs too much, teaches badly, and claims to train pirates like Poppy. [Captain Bloodtail](#captain-bloodtail) runs it. 🔒 Its "special classes" pull students into the cult. Its graduates become the cult scallywags of Ch 10.

### Garnikor
**Type:** NPC (Chromatic Queen crew, Act 2 on). LN female hardriggan bosun 7. She used to be a teamster and a smuggler. She's a convict whose sentence was reduced. She's a fair mentor of few words. She rarely brings out her cat-o'-nine-tails. Kulupi leaves the sailing to her. She sells *potions of water breathing*. She owns the blood mouse [Leafling](#leafling).
- **Ch 9:** 4th dan in the dueling tournament.
- **Ch 12:** default general of Barnacle Bay.

### Gasping Gipper
**Type:** Place (shipwreck, Ch 1 Scene 5). A g'mayun pirate caravel. A freak storm wrecked it off Goldcrop's southern coast about 200 years ago. Poppy shipped goods on it, including the [sapphire scale](#sapphire-scale). Its captain haunts it as a [wispy wayfarer](#wispy-wayfarer). Skeletal Iron Eels and two springjaw dolls are there too. **Ch 1:** the second map half ends up here, inside a [scroll mold](#scroll-mold).
**Eldamon:** an etmoist on the flooded main deck and a morpup in the hold ([Ch 1 eldamon](gm_chapter_01_eldamon.md)).

### Gentle Whistler
**Type:** Item 4 (uncommon, enchantment, magical, 90 gp). A golden harmonica with insect patterns. Once a day it casts *calm emotions* (DC 18) on bugs. **Ch 2:** [Batarsi](#batarsi) stole it. Kuaranda plays it at [Aksyanis Amphitheater](#aksyanis-amphitheater) to calm the Hissing Chasm's mantises.
**At the table:** The music makes other wildlife angry. That's why waves of animals attack the amphitheater.

### Gilded Blade
**Type:** Item (Ch 4, G25). A ceremonial copy of Poppy's sword. It's split into four pieces, hidden inside the Fortunate Four statues in the [Hall of Heroes](#hall-of-heroes). Plunging it into Poppy's golden statue opens the way to the Obsidian Star. After that, it's a *+1 striking scimitar (4)*.

### Gilded Pirate
**Type:** Title. The identity [Poppy von Barnacle](#poppy-von-barnacle) made up for herself. Abidale Ollensworth made a gilded porcelain statue of her. It stands in [Barrel Square](#barrel-square).

### Glacé
**Type:** Eldamon (chillot, ice; Ch 5). A small white otter (she/her). Her body fades into curls of cold mist. She's melded into the stone of Caleb's Colds at the [Wavewatcher Inn](#wavewatcher-inn), and she keeps its ice cold. She loves ghost stories. She scares people only to bond with them. She can smell ghosts. She has told junior cook [Mupto Burgin](#mupto-burgin) every story she knows ([Ch 5 eldamon](gm_chapter_05_eldamon.md#encounter-51--glacé-chillot)).
**At the table:** Silence bores her. Trade her a scary story. Seaview's own haunting is the best story in the Isles.

### Glitterfest
**Type:** Festival. Rumplank's yearly party for glitter and all things sparkly. **Ch 1, Event 2:** the parade and the toogtoog spill.

### G'mayun
**Type:** Ancestry. **Bird-like humanoids.** They look like tropical parrots standing upright, about as tall as halflings. They hatch from eggs. Their eye color shifts with [the Great Song](#the-great-song). They're native to the Isles and make up 67% of Rumplank. *King Okeki, D'marti, and Melar Kan are g'mayun.*
**At the table:** Eye color is a poor guide to mood. They dislike being called "birdfolk."

### Goaflom
**Type:** NPC, possible crew member (Ch 7, P6). CN male wildfire leshy 2. A forger who escaped a prison galley. His own villagers jailed him for speaking out. He knows the layout of the outer fortress. He also knows that viscera opens the resin dome.
**At the table:** He's eager to join the ship. He wants to keep his criminal past hidden, most of all from Prince Kulupi.
**Eldamon:** a skolld chose him as the one it guards. It frosts his cell door ([Ch 7 eldamon](gm_chapter_07_eldamon.md)).

### Goldcrop Island
**Type:** Place. The largest of the Isles.
- **Settlements:** [Rumplank](#rumplank) (south), Kraken (east), and Molmist village inland past the jungle.
- **Land:** jungle, beaches, the [Writhing Swamp](#writhing-swamp), and [Shivering Mountain](#shivering-mountain).
- **Dry leeward side:** the Okapi Hills and the [Crackbeak Salt Desert](#crackbeak-salt-desert).

Setting for Act 1 and most of Act 3.

### Golomor
**Type:** NPC (Ch 2, E3). Shadow thief (BB 90), Creature 2, they/them. Not evil. Batarsi killed their sibling, Holipok. Golomor hides in Batarsi's back room to get revenge.
**At the table:** Starts indifferent. Attacks anyone in the way. If befriended, they give infused shadow worth their full Monster Parts value.

### The Great Song
**Type:** Concept. A mystical link that ties all [g'mayun](#gmayun) together. They feel it as emotion, instinct, or music. It shifts their eye color. Long ago, the [Iris Refrain](#iris-refrain) saved it from being corrupted by demons.
**At the table:** If someone brings up the corruption history, a g'mayun might lecture them or give them the cold shoulder. Be careful with it.

### Grupple
**Type:** NPC (Ch 5, Briargrove). A poor orpok forager. He's pinned under a branch, and a reaver beaver threatens him. His family is sick. They need the 100 gp the [spongeberries](#spongeberry) would sell for. If the PCs give him the berries, he gives them briarthorn (Ch 12 resource).

### Gullet
**Type:** Creature (Ch 6, N6). A unique, crafty nobbler variant (BB 67). It was made from a smuggler's magic bag. It hides among the mogadbs. It swallowed the pirate-ship tile that the Gullstone Cave vault door needs.

### Gullstone Cave
**Type:** Place (Moonshadow Island, Ch 6). A smugglers' cave near the southern beach. Its walls are covered in thousands of carved stone tiles. Three tiles open the iron door to a pirate captain's vault. Poppy cracked the vault and took the treasure. Then she hid the [Bruised Crystal](#bruised-crystal) there behind new traps.
**At the table:** Tile order, top to bottom: tricorn hat, cutlass, pirate ship. The fragment is already gone when the PCs arrive.
**Eldamon:** a kagekko is melded inside [Gullet](#gullet), and a shadoppy noses among the Upper Pit's dead ([Ch 6 eldamon](gm_chapter_06_eldamon.md)).

### Gunlu Pongo
**Type:** NPC (Ch 5). NG female orpok aristocrat 5, Seaview councilor for education and taxation. Quick and logical. She graduated from the Academy of Tastes. Now she fights to open it up to more people. Cousin of [Allasha](#allasha-the-surveyor).

### Gustavus Gollihew
**Type:** NPC (dead). A famous orpok explorer, mapmaker, and food lover. He founded the [Academy of Tastes](#academy-of-tastes). His maps show exactly where the Jewel fragments are. Ancestor of Councilor [Monnino Gollihew](#monnino-gollihew). The book also spells his name "Gustavius."

### Gutted Snake
**Type:** Place (Rumplank tavern, Ch 1 Scene 2A). The worst bar in the [Claw Quarter](#claw-quarter), with sticky floors and sour grog. It also has the best gossip. [Molo "Make-Sick" Dogodor](#molo-make-sick-dogodor) runs it. [D'marti](#dmarti-tixima) lost the map here.
**At the table:** Regulars:
- Belpa the Snake Swinger, a dancer.
- The Red-Bellied Three, card sharps from Jakapo Town. One of them owns a living divination deck.
- Chema, a bodyguard.

### Hall of Heroes

## I

### Ignatius Barkley
**Type:** NPC, potential companion (Ch 1, Scene 6). LG male wildfire leshy fighter 1. A cheerful wandering knight. He's the last [Embersand](#embersand) who isn't corrupted. The PCs find him in the C1 snake pit.
**At the table:** He leaves at the first sign of evil. His life goal is to destroy [Pyksara](#pyksara), who corrupted his clan. The C5 husk Wisps was his best friend.

### Illusory princes
**Type:** Creature (Ch 12, Creature 6). 🔒 Illusion copies of Kulupi. Each gets three actions. His Create Clone makes up to 9 of them. He can swap places with them.

### Indigo dragon
**Type:** Dragon. Charming, kind, lively, and dramatic. They love parties. They want parties to be long, safe, and welcoming. They think pressuring anyone at a party is tacky. *[X'na'zed](#xnazed) is one. So was [Pinomeri Elemarte](#pinomeri-elemarte).*

### Indigo Isles
**Type:** Place. The setting: a chain of islands far from any continent. The most common ancestries, in order: [g'mayun](#gmayun), [orpok](#orpok), [hardriggan](#hardriggan), [kragrak](#kragrak), [chochori](#chochori). Humans are about as rare as monstrous ancestries. The tropical weather stays steady. Only the rainfall changes with the seasons.

### Indigo Stranger
**Type:** NPC role (Ch 1, Event 3). [X'na'zed](#xnazed) uses this disguise to nudge the story along. He shows up as a friendly stranger, always with **brilliant indigo eyes**. As a g'mayun woman, he goes by "[Zanaya](#zanaya)."
**At the table:** Use him rarely, or players feel pushed along a fixed path. Fighting him at low level means certain death. **Ch 12:** he shows his true form and helps organize the city's defenses.

### Inglomeonite
**Type:** NPC (Ch 3, F28). LN male ferropaceon merchant 10, a magnetic earth elemental. People call him "Inglo." He's cheerful. He says he's centuries old. He counts time in "quake-cycles" of about 15 years. He watched Poppy lead her followers through the cave. He trades gemstones and metal or stone magic items up to level 4.
**At the table:** If the PCs return the F7 chunk of shell, he closes the elemental rift. In Ch 11, how the PCs treated him matters to his old rival [Sarganx](#sarganx).

### Iris Refrain
**Type:** Historical event. Demons once tried to corrupt [the Great Song](#the-great-song). They wanted to make g'mayuns a cruel race of servants. The goddess **Chira** sent a rainbow of emotion into the song and saved them. That ended the Age of Monsters and started the Age of Thorns. The song never fully healed.
**At the table:** This history hurts g'mayuns. Treat it seriously.

### Iron Eels
**Type:** Organization (Ch 1). [Melar Kan](#melar-kan)'s pirate gang. Their base is an abandoned rum distillery in the [Claw Quarter](#claw-quarter). They sail the white sloop [*Sharpened Bone*](#sharpened-bone) under an eel-skull flag. They stole D'marti's map half, then lost it on the Gasping Gipper.
**At the table:** They do petty crime on shore, then spend months at sea until people stop looking for them. The crew is bored and wants to sail soon.

### Ironsalt Cove
**Type:** Place (Claw Quarter waterfront). Site of the [Iron Eels' hideout](#iron-eels). More than one body has washed up here.

## J

### Jakapo Town
**Type:** Place (Goldcrop Island, Ch 2). A galtzagorri village in a thicket of giant mushrooms, 4 miles from [Rumplank](#rumplank). [Arahosko](#arahosko-the-red) was born here. Its elder, [S'matzia](#smatzia), lifts the map's curse.
**At the table:** If the PCs visit during Ch 1, hold off on Events 5–8. S'matzia is away until the PCs put the map together.

### Jakorvo
**Type:** NPC (dead). 🔒 CE male g'mayun high priest of the original [cult](#cult-of-the-shivering-behemoth), according to the cult article. He controlled the shale behemoth with the [Jewel](#jewel-of-the-indigo-isles). Poppy's team killed him in his temple on Razorback Isle.
**At the table:** The book contradicts itself here. In Ch 8, the high priest Poppy stabbed beside the Jewel is an unnamed woman. She's now the [skeletal cult dragon](#skeletal-cult-dragon). Pick one version.

### Jesea Swiftarrow
**Type:** NPC (dead). "The Rugged Ranger." The hardriggan archer of the [Fortunate Four](#fortunate-four), and no one could beat her. She built the first hanging bridge from [Beakbluster Hills](#beakbluster-hills) to the Western Talon. She never explained the **X-shaped scar on her jawline, below her left ear**.
**At the table:** Her Ch 4 G25 statue breaks when a projectile hits the X scar.

### Jewel of the Indigo Isles
**Type:** Artifact. 🔒 The cult's unholy relic. Jakorvo used it to control the shale behemoth. Poppy shattered it into four pieces:
- the [Obsidian Star](#obsidian-star), kept on Goldcrop (Ch 4).
- the [Aquamarine Wedge](#aquamarine-wedge), hidden on Bluebell Island (Ch 5).
- the [Bruised Crystal](#bruised-crystal), hidden on Moonshadow Island (Chs 6–7).
- the [Tangerine Shard](#tangerine-shard), left on its pedestal in the sunken temple (Ch 8). Poppy thought taking the whole Jewel would collapse the temple.

In Ch 9 the pieces join again. The Jewel becomes an item-20 artifact, cursed through Kulupi, and the cult takes it. If the PCs keep it, the book has two backup plans: Kelgrid's ambush, or Kulupi steals it in Ch 11. **The "treasure" the PCs collect is the cult's relic.**

### Jirigati
**Type:** People (historical). Magical inventors who moved around part of the year. They came from many ancestries and elected their chiefs. They lived in the Crackbeak region until a few centuries ago. They built the stormspire, the Cliffside Ruin, and the desalination device in the Salt Den (Ch 10).

## K

### Kalacho
**Type:** Place (Runaway Reef, Ch 8). A neutral chochori trading town under the sea, with 861 people. It's about 6 miles from where Razorback Isle used to be. A council runs it, with one member for each chora. People from the surface can get by only in the Airdweller's Annex. That's an air dome they reach by diving bell.
**At the table:** Kulupi and the crew grumble if the PCs stay too long.

### Kelgrid
**Type:** NPC (Ch 9, optional). 🔒 Kragrak cultist (he/him), Creature 8, a variant Shivering Behemoth guard. If the PCs keep the Jewel, he ambushes them with a *scroll of dominate*. He knows the prayer that holds back the Jewel's curse.

### King Okeki Redfeather
**Type:** NPC. Rumplank's king. He's good-natured and dignified, and a little distracted. He's happy to leave hard choices to his queen. *"I was a bit of a rogue back then, you know."* He gives the Ch 1 quest. His great-great-grandmother was Lady Kili, the first monarch.

### Korog
**Type:** NPC (Ch 9, T2). NE kragrak saboteur firebringer, Creature 8 (she/her). She performs with fireworks and has set many fires. She was first hired for a show in Rumplank. Falarni Malabria gave her the explosives. Not a cultist.
**At the table:** She names Falarni in exchange for a lighter punishment.

### Kragrak
**Type:** Ancestry. **Earthen folk** made of rock, sand, or volcanic stone.
- As children, their bodies are even on both sides.
- Uneven features mark an adult, like a rocky shoulder or fingers that don't match. These come from coming-of-age rituals near ages 12 and 24.
- They have big appetites. Each one has a second element.

About 2% of Rumplank, but kragraks have been on the Isles since ancient times. *[Undayo](#undayo-of-stonegulch) was kragrak.*
**At the table:** Skip the debate about uneven bodies and "earth transcends the elements." It annoys them.

### Krissia
**Type:** NPC (Ch 2). LN female orpok roadster 3. A wandering jungle guide. She charges 1 sp an hour and makes terrain one step easier. The PCs find her eating lunch at [Never Lonely Hill](#never-lonely-hill).
**At the table:** If the PCs make her friendly, she tells the legend of the [Emerald Earth Mining Company](#emerald-earth-mining-company) and the shale spitters.

### Kuaranda
**Type:** NPC, potential companion (Ch 2). NG female galtzagorri bard 2. A kind herder and musician from Jakapo Town, with many hobbies. She bought the magical [sapphire scale](#sapphire-scale) from a traveling g'mayun. She didn't know it was stolen. She tied it to the runt of her [thyalodon](#thyalodon) herd, and the runt wandered off.
**At the table:** Bring the thyalodon home alive, and she's a friend for life (Ch 2, Event 7, p. 57).

## M

### Master Olo
**Type:** NPC (Ch 1, Scene 2E). A short, stocky old orpok. He's a martial-arts master and used to fight in tournaments. He runs a fighting camp on the beach from dawn to dusk, with three chochori students. He saw [Squotan Malardy](#squotan-malardy) run across the Ironsalt Cove beach holding a map. Olo hit him as he went by.
**At the table:** If the PCs beat two of his students and no one gets knocked out, he teaches the **Flying Pig Stance** and **Flying Pig Rush** feats (p. 378). **Ch 9:** he comes out of retirement for the tournament (LN male orpok martial artist 8, 7th dan). **Ch 12:** possible district general.

### Melar Kan
**Type:** NPC (Ch 1 boss). Unique LE male g'mayun pirate captain, Creature 1. Leader of the [Iron Eels](#iron-eels). Cruel and calculating. He has read a lot of philosophy and tactics, and kept only the brutal parts. His weapon gives the gang its name: a giant iron eel skeleton on a chain.
**At the table:**
- He likes bloodshed. He loves gambling and making deals even more. He uses every loophole.
- He secretly takes the dagger hidden under the crow's nest, unless a PC grabs it first.
- Don't let him die by accident. The book suggests using him as a villain who keeps coming back.
- **Ch 12:** possible defense adjutant for the Claw Quarter.

### Melding
**Type:** Eldamon ability. An eldamon can merge into a creature, object, or place that suits it. While melded, no one can detect it. A long meld can give its host power (Eldamon p. 10–15):
- a creature gets an elemental power.
- an item gets resistances or quirks.
- a place gets special terrain or an elemental hazard.

**At the table:** A meld explains a strange detail, like ice that never melts or frost in a warm room. Free the eldamon, then befriend it ([rules](gm_chapter_01_eldamon.md#melding)).

### Molo "Make-Sick" Dogodor
**Type:** NPC (Ch 1, Scene 2A). A grumpy, stingy g'mayun who runs the bar at [the Gutted Snake](#gutted-snake). On a **DC 17 Intimidation** check, he admits he saw [Squotan Malardy](#squotan-malardy) take the map. He claims the tavern of his rival, [Clamardinia Wakamarsis](#clamardinia-wakamarsis), is haunted.

### Monbak
**Type:** NPC (Ch 9). LN male g'mayun historian, one of Rumplank's top scholars. The cult's arsonists target his archive.
**At the table:** He can sponsor a PC with the indigo historian background into the Royal Society of History.
**Eldamon:** a soleo stands guard on his bed, legs shaking, during the fire ([Ch 9 eldamon](gm_chapter_09_eldamon.md)).

### Monnino Gollihew
**Type:** NPC (Ch 5). NG male orpok aristocrat 7, Seaview councilor for the arts and restaurants. A pompous descendant of [Gustavus Gollihew](#gustavus-gollihew). He loves the Wavewatcher Inn. Its owner is his ex-lover.

### Monster Parts
**Type:** System. The treasure system from the *Battlezoo Bestiary*. The PCs harvest parts from creatures. Then they spend the parts to refine and imbue magic items. The book is built for the **hybrid** variant. The normal magic-item economy also works, and the book lists replacement treasure. [How to read the numbers →](gm_chapter_01.md#monster-parts-treasure-system)

### Mrs. Sloth

### Mului

**Type:** NPC (Ch 5, K4). NG male orpok cook 1, the Wavewatcher Inn's junior cook. He's hiding in a closet. He remembers that Pelborii rented the private room for a week or so before the haunting.

- She trained people to take her place in secret proving grounds under Shivering Mountain.
- In 5862, after her last students died, she vanished. People assume she's dead.
- **Ch 12:** Zellura raises her body as [Shaleborn Poppy](#shaleborn-poppy).

## T

### Tangerine Shard
**Type:** Artifact (item 7; Ch 8). 🔒 The fourth [Jewel](#jewel-of-the-indigo-isles) fragment. Poppy left it on its pedestal in the sunken temple. She feared that taking the whole Jewel would collapse the temple. It glows like a torch. Once a day it can wrap its holder in a haze of glitter that hides them. Taking it ends the temple's protection. After that, the flood stage rises by 1 each minute.

### Taruki
**Type:** Architecture. Ancient buildings of lava rock from before Poppy's time. A few are left in [Old Rumplank](#old-rumplank). [Beakbluster Hills](#beakbluster-hills) is full of copies. The [Temple of Many Colors](#temple-of-many-colors) is a famous one.

### Teakni
**Type:** Creature (water elementals, p. 401). Moody water elementals with a loose humanoid shape. They heat or cool water that passes through them. Locked doors and blocked passages make them furious. **Ch 8:** a [hydrojinxer](#teakni-hydrojinxer)'s gang is flooding the sunken temple.

### Teakni hydrojinxer
**Type:** NPC (Ch 8). Unique CN female teakni arcane saboteur, Creature 8. She has no name, by choice. She explains the sabotage openly. If anyone asks the teaknis to stop, she turns hostile. She surrenders below 30 HP.

### Temple of Many Colors
**Type:** Place (Rumplank, Ch 1 Scene 2D). A large stone temple west of [Barrel Square](#barrel-square). It serves [the Balance](#the-balance) and some older faiths. [Prismatic Colvi](#prismatic-colvi) leads it. Undayo of Stonegulch's ashes lie in the Rumplank Catacombs beneath it.

### Testing Hall
**Type:** Place (Ch 4, G10). A corridor in Poppy's proving grounds, under a sign that says "NO TURNING BACK." It has three complex traps. The doors lock two rounds after anyone enters.

### Thyalodon
**Type:** Creature (p. 402 in this book's Adventure Toolbox, Creature 3). An animal native to Goldcrop. People ride it, tame it, and sometimes eat it. **Ch 1, C6:** [Kuaranda](#kuaranda)'s runt. The leshy husks are about to sacrifice it. The sapphire scale confuses it, so it attacks friend and foe alike.

### Toogtoog
**Type:** Creature (p. 403). Clever, chubby **frog-like creatures** native to the Isles. They have inkwells in their heads, and the ink is usable. That makes them popular pets. Large groups are called "spills," and they gobble up ingredients. **Ch 1, Event 2:** the Glitterfest spill.

### Treasure Beach
**Type:** Place (Goldcrop Island). The sandy shore where [Poppy](#poppy-von-barnacle) first landed. She named it. [Master Olo](#master-olo) often camps here.

### Twin Talons
**Type:** Place (Rumplank district). Two huge rock pillars shaped like claws, with flat tops. Bridges tie them to [Beakbluster Hills](#beakbluster-hills). They're full of odd people, alchemists, engineers, and strange criminals. The old dueling grounds have so much blood in them that weeds won't grow. [Pekipak](#sagoya-pekipak)'s wagon is here.

## U

To earn his trust, you had to beat him one-on-one. His ashes lie in the catacombs under the [Temple of Many Colors](#temple-of-many-colors).

## V

### Varagix the Glimmering
**Type:** Demon lord. 🔒 Patron of the [cult](#cult-of-the-shivering-behemoth). He was once an earth spirit of the [Eld](#the-eld) who loved gems and resented the Balance gods. He ate a clutch of dragon eggs and left to rule a demon realm. He looks like a huge dragon with gem scales, behind a jeweled mask. His cult wants to raise the shale behemoth and conquer the Isles. He never forgets a debt or an insult.

### Velluris
**Type:** NPC (Ch 11, Y3). Unique CN othruni ranger (a fey fungus), Creature 10, he/him. A stubborn loner who is hunting the sapphire drake. He's walled into a tunnel behind a living barricade. Bright light weakens him.
**At the table:** He might become an uneasy ally against Aylushrixa. He leaves before the ritual stairs.

### Venomous snakedead
**Type:** Creature (BB 118, Creature 2). An undead snake. **Ch 1, C5:** the leshy husks' idol. It wandered into the Writhing Swamp. Now the husks keep it on a 10-foot leash tied to a statue and feed it sacrifices.

### Viekaska
**Type:** NPC (dead, Ch 3). A hardriggan bard (she/her). Her treasure-hunting party died outside Shiverskull Cave. [Pargomun](#pargomun) killed her and hid the body. Her spirit is the F4 haunt.
**At the table:** Once the PCs destroy both Pargomun and the F4 yomhibdi, she shows them the F4 secret door.

### Vitae
**Type:** Concept. Nature spirits that answer druids. When druids place them in plant bodies, they become [leshies](#leshy). Common in the Isles. [Aoz](#aoz) rules them.

## W

### Wallopula
**Type:** Creature (myzoform, BB 65, Creature 8; Ch 8). They/them. A myzoform that eats memories. They have a pet giant glaucus slug. They ate the two survivors of the *Distant Star*. They can take either form. They carve muddled portraits into the coral.
**At the table:** They're willing to talk. Befriending them earns full XP plus Monster Parts.

### War of the Shivering Behemoth
**Type:** Historical event. The war the Isles fought against the cult, led by Poppy. It ended two centuries ago on Razorback Isle. The cult's elemental defense rituals sank the island.

### Wavewatcher Inn
**Type:** Place (Seaview, Ch 5). The best restaurant on the docks. 🔒 [Pelborii](#pelborii-karnihar) worked her ritual from here. Hearth hounds and a dishrag dervish haunt it.
**Eldamon:** home of [Glacé](#glacé). She keeps the ice cold and collects stories about the haunting.

### Waymarkers
**Type:** Concept (Chs 2–3). Six lava-rock pillars, each 5 feet tall, along the route on Poppy's map. Touching the sapphire scale to each one charges it. The F27 secret door in Ch 3 opens only for a fully charged scale.

### Wisp
**Type:** NPC (Ch 10). CG nonbinary galtzagorri scout (they/them), [Sovala](#sovala)'s partner. Tries to sell a "sacred" stick for 100 gp to any PC who'll believe it. Not the same as [Wisps](#wisps), the Ch 1 husk.

### Wisps
**Type:** NPC (Ch 1, C5). A [leshy husk](#leshy-husk) (BB 44, Creature 0) who prays to the snakedead in Sylvan. Once [Barkley](#ignatius-barkley)'s best friend: *"my main mate, Wisps."* If it spots the PCs within 20 feet, it spends 4 actions untying the snakedead.

### Wispy Wayfarer
**Type:** Creature (BB 100, Creature 2). The ghost of the [Gasping Gipper](#gasping-gipper)'s captain. He stayed with his stolen treasure through the crash. **Ch 1, B7:** it looks like a ragged sheet on the footlocker. Spotting it takes three things: a **Seek** action, **DC 22 Perception**, and a way to see in dim light. It raised the dead Iron Eels as [skeletons](#skeletal-iron-eel).

### Wo'a wood
**Type:** Material and defense special resource (Ch 4, G7). A gold-colored hardwood from a rare Goldcrop tree. The Pillar of Grace in Poppy's proving grounds is a mast from a cult warship that was destroyed. Ch 12: it upgrades a Wall to a Fortress Wall, or trades for 10 Resource Points.
**At the table:** The book never says how to get the pillar out.

### Writhing Swamp
**Type:** Place (Goldcrop Island, Ch 1 Scene 6). A miserable bog southeast of [Rumplank](#rumplank). It's miles of salty muck and snakes. Only Jakapo Town's flying galtzagorris cross it. In its ruins are the [Embersand](#embersand) leshy husks. The [sapphire scale](#sapphire-scale) is at C6.
**Eldamon:** a squirrelash that collected the Embersand clan rides on [Barkley](#ignatius-barkley)'s shoulder. A skepi is melded into the C6 [thyalodon](#thyalodon) ([Ch 1 eldamon](gm_chapter_01_eldamon.md)).

## Z

### Zakatari
**Type:** NPC (Ch 4, G11). Parasite husk (BB 71), Creature 3. A student nobody liked. He tried to cheat the Testing Hall with a box of magic worms, and the worms ate him. Poppy and the students bricked him into the well room. While he lives, anyone who sleeps in the dormitory gets *nightmare*.

### Zanaya
**Type:** NPC alias. The name [X'na'zed](#xnazed) uses in his g'mayun-woman disguise. A g'mayun named Zanaya also narrates the book's gazetteer, "Zanaya's Guide to Rumplank." The book never confirms she's X'na'zed.

### Zaroz
**Type:** Demon. 🔒 Varagix's envoy. [Zellura](#zellura) summoned him in 6021. He watches and advises the cult's three leaders, for his own goals.

### Zellura
**Type:** NPC. 🔒 The [cult](#cult-of-the-shivering-behemoth)'s Grand Oracle, the "Lady of Bone and Bounty."
- A priestess of Varagix, covered in bones. She's half alive and half dead, with bone-white feathers and blood-red eyes.
- Pirates killed her family and set her adrift at sea. Varagix's visions kept her alive.
- She hates Rumplank's romantic pirate legends about Poppy.
- She began reviving the cult in 5992. She made Kulupi the official leader in 6022.
- **Ch 12:** she fights alongside [Shaleborn Poppy](#shaleborn-poppy).

### Zoa
**Type:** Deity. Twin of Death, paired with [Aoz](#aoz). The Isles respect Zoa as part of the natural cycle. Zoa's temples offer work hunting undead. *See: [The Balance](#the-balance).*
