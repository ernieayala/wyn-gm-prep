## Encounter 4.1 — The patients in the shrine (two phidis)

**Insert after:** [G12. Shrine of Aoz](gm_chapter_04.md#g12-shrine-of-aoz-severe-3) · **Where/when:** the Shrine of Aoz, once the chamber ooze and ruin bruiser are dead and the party rests. The best time is the 8-hour rest that restores twice the normal HP. Two phidis, rare life snake eldamon (Eldamon p. 232).

> **🎯 The place is the want:** a phidi wants a medical breakthrough. A room that heals double is its unsolved case. So each connection approach makes the PC its research partner. Two of one kind lets two PCs each try (Eldamon p. 28–29).

> *As the party settles in among the splintered pews, two things slide out from behind the masked statue: slim golden snakes, glowing like lanterns, with tongues that flicker toward each of you in turn. The larger one stops beside whoever is bleeding the most, coils up, and watches the wound with the fixed attention of a surgeon. The smaller one watches the larger one, and copies it.*

- **Setup:** Life eldamon live where life force runs strong (Eldamon p. 30). This shrine heals sleepers twice over (p. 98–99).
  - The elder phidi studied the regenerating ooze for years from out of phase (Eldamon p. 10). It stayed out of the fight.
  - The younger phidi is its student.
  - During the rest, they visit each sleeper and watch them heal.
- **Find:** Perception DC 18 before the rest spots a glint behind the statue. By the rest's end they have visited everyone, with no check. Anyone on watch sees one curled beside a sleeper.
- **Recognize:** Nature or Religion DC 23 (24), secret.
  - Success: a phidi, a rare life eldamon that follows healers and loves medicine.
  - Crit success adds: healers treat phidis as sacred. Its evolution condition is "Helping phidi discover a true medical breakthrough." (Eldamon p. 232).
  - Crit fail lie: "Those are death adders wearing a life eldamon's glow. They're sizing up who to bite." The Request DC stays the same. But a PC who shoos them away on that lie gets no second chance tonight.
- **Befriend:** Request DC 18 (19) Diplomacy, one roll per phidi. Connection DC 13 (14):
  - Let it assist (Medicine): Treat Wounds with the phidi watching, and talk it through each step. Two PCs can share this approach. One treats while the other talks.
  - The puzzle of the shrine (Religion): wonder aloud why this shrine heals double. The elder has worked on that question for years and wants a partner.
  - The herbalist's case (Nature or Crafting): explain a remedy, such as the lesser healing potions from [G14](gm_chapter_04.md#g14-dormitory).
- **Leaves if:** someone harms a helpless creature, or refuses care to someone who needs it. Both phidis hide behind the statue for the rest of this rest.
- **Outcomes:**
  - Crit success: it joins and wakes coiled around that PC's wrist.
  - Success: it joins.
  - Failure: it goes back to the sleepers. You can allow a retry in the morning after a connection approach.
  - Crit failure: it adopts that PC as its patient until the party leaves the shrine. It won't join this chapter.
- **Rolls** (Eldamon pp. 32–35): *Elder:* Table 5 Typical (Jyoti, Common) · 6 Glowing · 7 Sparkly · 8 Dependable · 9b 73–75, obsessed with obscure lore: the shrine's double healing. *Younger:* Table 5 Young (Jyoti only) · 6 No Variation · 7 Smaller · 8 Curious · 9a no secret.
- **XP:** 20 per PC if at least one joins (suggestion).
- 💡 Phidis are rare. If a missed roll would sting, use [Variant: Skipping the Request](gm_chapter_01_eldamon.md#variant-skipping-the-request) (Eldamon p. 29).
- 🔒 The double healing comes from the Obsidian Star (p. 99).
  - The elder senses a source deeper in the mountain. You can show this as a faint tell. It can't name the source or lead the PCs there.
  - When the PCs lift the Star in G26, the healing ends.
  - Any phidi with the party then stares at the Star's holder for a long while. It never knows why.
- 🧩 Any later NPC healer can supply a medical breakthrough.

## Encounter 4.2 — The empty fourth cushion (simiens)

**Insert after:** [G6. Space of Wisdom](gm_chapter_04.md#g6-space-of-wisdom) · **Where/when:** the walking-meditation hallway and golden sandpit, whenever the PCs enter G6. The best time is while a PC spends the hour for the Wisdom blessing ([Poppy's Blessings](gm_chapter_04.md#poppys-blessings)). Three simiens, mind monkey eldamon (Eldamon p. 246).

> **🎯 An absence is a hook:** players will ask about the empty seat. The answer is "think no evil," the step beyond all but the wisest (Eldamon p. 246). That step is what the troop wants.

> *Someone has worn an oval track into the flagstones of the hallway, and the golden sand beyond the arch is combed into slow curves. At the edge of the pit sit three small monkeys with a faint shimmer around their heads. One has its hands over its eyes. One has its hands over its ears. One has its hands over its mouth. Beside them, a fourth hollow is pressed into the sand, and no one sits in it.*

- **Checks:**
  - **Find:** Perception DC 18. It's automatic for a PC spending the blessing hour.
  - **Recall:** Nature or Occultism DC 20 (21). Crit success adds the four-part path. It also adds that simiens don't scold themselves for falling short. They try a little better next time.
  - **Crit fail lie:** "Simiens are mute and take speech as an insult. Talk only in mime." Miming earns curious looks and nothing more.
  - **Request:** DC 18 (19).
  - **Connection:** DC 13 (14). Pick one:
    - Ten minutes of silent meditation that fits inside the blessing hour (Religion or Occultism).
    - A real question, such as "Is a test fair if it can kill the student?" (Society or a relevant Lore).
    - Owning a failure without self-pity, and naming the next try (the skill the PC failed).
- **Leaves if:** someone makes an insult or a cruel joke, or mocks someone's failure. That simiens covers its eyes, ears, or mouth toward the speaker for the day.
- **Outcomes:**
  - Crit success: it joins and asks "Can you honor a hero without worshipping her?"
  - Success: it joins.
  - Failure: it bows and returns to the oval.
  - Crit failure: it covers its ears toward that PC for the day.
- **Rolls** (Eldamon pp. 32–35): all Table 5 Typical (Astral, Common). *Eyes:* Sagacious. *Ears:* Alternate Skill (Performance replaces Intimidation), Smaller, Curious. *Mouth:* Soft coloration, Enigmatic, 9b 01–03 once friends with a hero.
- **XP:** 10 per PC if at least one joins (suggestion).
- 🔒 The Mouth's old friend was [Pinomeri Elemarte](gm_glossary.md#pinomeri-elemarte), who laid Poppy's Blessings (p. 90). The Mouth may be along when the PCs melt Pinomeri's statue at [G25](gm_chapter_04.md#g25-hall-of-heroes). If so, let it uncover its mouth for the first time and say one thing about its friend.

## Flavor sighting — The cove's mathematician (cephalittle)

**Insert after:** [G27. Secret Cove](gm_chapter_04.md#g27-secret-cove) · One cephalittle sits on the western boardwalk. It's a force cephalopod eldamon drawn to geometric patterns (Eldamon p. 214). For years it has worked out the angle the *Queen* needs to clear the south tunnel (p. 107–108).

> *At the end of the boardwalk, something like a small octopus stands on its arms, which shimmer with force. It sweeps two of them through the air in long arcs and angles, sighting along them from the ship's bow to the pinprick of light at the tunnel's end. It mutters numbers to itself in a buzzing, humming tongue.*

It solves nothing. The party sails out as in [Event 13](gm_chapter_04.md#event-13--escape-from-shivering-mountain). In the climactic version, it clings to the bowsprit as the ceiling falls. It keeps doing math, phased out and safe (Eldamon p. 10). It throws its arms up when the ship clears. Rolls: Typical, Pedantic.

If a player asks:
- **Recall:** Nature or Arcana DC 20 (21).
- **Request:** DC 18 (19).
- **Connection:** DC 13 (14). Check its math (Crafting or Sailing Lore), or offer it a new geometry problem (Arcana or Society).
- No XP.
