## Encounter 3.1 — The keeper of the camp (pangeolil)

**Insert after:** [F7 — Base Camp Ruins](gm_chapter_03.md#f7--base-camp-ruins) · **Where/when:** the first time the PCs linger in camp. Or when a PC examines the old beams of the F5 mineshaft on the way through.

> **🎯** This is a want-driven NPC. It lives to keep secrets (Eldamon p. 192). So each approach either gives it a secret or protects the one it holds. Earth eldamon gather where the rock is notable (Eldamon p. 30). This camp sat on an emerald and sapphire mine (F11).

> *Among the rusty picks and broken crates, one stone sits wrong. It's the size of a loaf of bread, and its surface is made of overlapping plates, like roof tiles, flecked with green and blue. When your light passes over it, the plates tighten.*

- **Setup:** a rare earth eldamon (Eldamon p. 192). Its last friend was an Emerald Earth miner. When the company fled, it curled up in camp to keep her secret. It has stayed curled up through everything since.
- **Find:** Seek, **DC 18 Perception** (19). A success at F6's DC 15 Crafting or Mining Lore check also spots it, since it doesn't match the rubble. During a rest in camp, the watch rolls a secret DC 16 Perception. On a success, they see it uncurl to look at them.
- **Recognize:** secret **DC 23** (24) **Nature**, **Mining Lore**, or Eldamon Lore. Success: a shy secret-keeper and self-styled guardian that can Curl Up. Crit success adds: one that gives away a secret may flee in guilt. Logic won't bring it back, but a new important secret might. Evolution: "Helping pangeolil learn and keep an especially deep or important secret." Crit fail lie: "Pangeolils hoard gems. Offer it the shiniest one you've got." A gem does nothing.
- **Befriend:** Diplomacy to Request **DC 18** (19). A PC who recognized it can roll connection **DC 13** (14) instead:
  - **Trade a secret** (Diplomacy): the player tells you a real character secret, by note if they like. The PC whispers it.
  - **Hide the camp** (Stealth or Survival): wipe out the party's tracks and rebuild a screen of crates around its corner.
  - **Shore it up** (Crafting, Athletics, or Mining Lore): set a proper prop under the soft F5 beams. This does right by the miners.
- **Leaves if:** someone asks what it knows (it Curls Up for 10 minutes), or someone digs it out uninvited. A PC who repeats its secret loses it for good.
- **Outcomes:** crit success: befriends, tells that friend alone its secret, and asks them to keep it / success: befriends, keeps the secret / failure: stays curled, one more try after a connection approach / crit failure: burrows into the wall, gone while the PCs are in the cave.
- **Rolls** (Eldamon pp. 32–35): 5 Typical, speaks Petran and Orpok · 6 No Variation · 7 Elemental Gems (emerald and sapphire chips) · 8 Shy · 9a one secret, 9b 16–18 an unfinished last task.
- **XP:** 20 per PC (suggestion). No level-up this chapter, so the XP carries into Chapter 4.
- **Abilities:** **Earth's Vengeance** adds damage to its earth powers against anything that hurt it. Weak to air, plant, sonic, water, and wood. Any PC can Aid a Shore it up check.
- 💡 Rare: if missing it would sting, use [Variant: Skipping the Request](gm_chapter_01_eldamon.md#variant-skipping-the-request).
- ⚠️ Inglomeonite's carapace chunk stays in the western beam (p. 75). The pangeolil hid deeper while the camp was stripped. It can't point to the chunk or name what took the metal (Eldamon p. 156).
- 🔒 **Its secret (this doc's invention):** its friend was an orpok miner. She warned [Boss Ozgold](gm_chapter_03.md#f7--base-camp-ruins) that the F5 props were going soft, and he ignored her. Give her a name. The pangeolil promised to keep the warning until someone put things right. So Shore it up finishes that task. It makes the pangeolil fond of that PC, even if someone else befriends it.
- 🧩 Poppy's proving grounds ([Chapter 4](gm_chapter_04.md)) are full of secrets worth keeping. A big secret later, such as the Chapter 12 defense plan, is a strong candidate. Your call.

## Encounter 3.2 — The porcute who guards the decoy

**Insert after:** [F24 — False Hideout](gm_chapter_03.md#f24--false-hideout) · **Where/when:** at the feet of Poppy's statue, when the PCs first enter F24. Electricity, Eldamon p. 198. The F25 rift's green sparks drew it here.

> **🎯** Porcutes take legends at face value. So Poppy's decoy for unworthy treasure hunters (p. 82) fooled this one completely.

> *At the statue's feet, a porcupine the size of a cat stands at attention. Each quill crackles with a spark of green lightning. It lowers its quills toward you like a row of lances and speaks in a voice like a small, brave bell. "Halt! Who approacheth the hoard of the Gilded Pirate? I have sworn to guard it until a worthy pirate comes to claim it."*

- **Find:** no check; it announces itself.
- **Run it:** any honest proof of worth (an oath, a Poppy legend, a daring feat) makes it step aside from the treasure. If ignored, it can't stop them because it's out of phase. But it follows them to the door, demanding an oath. Calling the hideout fake fails. The book warns that people who dismiss its beliefs get swept along in its story (Eldamon p. 198). One option, not a book rule: the next thing that happens to a mocker fits its story, like a rope snapping.
- **Recognize:** secret **DC 20** (21) **Nature** or **Arcana**: an oath-bound storybook hero. Crit success adds that reality bends to its story, so its companions do best to see the story through. It also adds the evolution: "Helping porcute keep their word and hold fast to their code against harsh odds." Crit fail lie: "Porcutes are tricksters. They swear false oaths for fun, and nothing it says counts."
- **Befriend:** Request **DC 18** (19), or connection **DC 13** (14). Use Performance for a storybook oath, or Society or Indigo Isles History Lore for a flawless Gilded Pirate tale. It refuses anyone who laughed. On a success it swears to follow its new friend until Poppy's true treasure is found. At the proving grounds in [Chapter 4](gm_chapter_04.md), its story comes true. Your call what that means for it.
- **Rolls:** 5 Typical, speaks Voltan and Common (from campfire legends) · 6 No Variation · 7 green Elemental Aura · 8 Archaic · 9 no secret. **XP:** 10 per PC if won over, whether or not anyone befriends it (suggestion).

## Encounter 3.3 — The lepismini in the black robes

**Insert after:** [F14 — Chamber of Final Rest](gm_chapter_03.md#f14--chamber-of-final-rest) · **Where/when:** the F14 sarcophagus, when the PCs drag the lid off. Darkness, Eldamon p. 172.

> **🎯** Melding explains an odd detail in the book. The eldamon is why the "impossibly black" robes stay "untouched by time" (p. 78).

> *The black robes on the corpse don't reflect your light. They drink it. A fold of that perfect black shifts on its own, two small violet eyes open in the cloth, take in the wide chamber above, and squeeze shut again.*

- **Find:** no check once the lid is open. With the lid shut, a PC who touches it and succeeds at **DC 18 Perception** (19) sees darkness seep from the seam.
- **Recognize:** **DC 20** (21) **Nature** or **Occultism**: fears open spaces, hooks into hiding spots, lowers its purple ruff for petting. Crit success adds that a bag, pocket, or hat is its ideal retreat. Crit fail lie: "Lepisminis are pests that chew cloth."
- **Befriend:** Request **DC 18** (19), or connection **DC 13** (14). Options: Crafting a hood or sack den, Stealth to dim the room and sit still, or Nature to stroke the ruff the way it lies.
- **Leaves if** pulled out, lit brightly, or startled by a loud noise (weak to light and sonic). On a crit failure it melds into the granite. It stays there while the PCs are in the cave.
- **Rolls:** 5 Ancient Slumberer, speaks Shadowtongue and Draconic · 6 No Variation · 7 Grayscale · 8 Anxious · 9 often tired, melds into nearby objects to rest. **XP:** 10 per PC (suggestion).
- ⚠️ The robes keep their **10 gp** value. If the PCs take the robes first, the lepismini rides inside, and they get another try.
- 🔒 It recalls only "the robed ones" closing the lid. It reveals nothing of the cult (Eldamon p. 156). It hides at the [F13 isopod statue](gm_chapter_03.md#f13--chamber-of-final-prayers). It also hides when Pargomun comes close, if the devil still travels with the party as "Viekaska" ([Event 12](gm_chapter_03.md#event-12--once-charted-twice-dead-low-3)). It does both in any wide room or near any stranger, so never confirm either.
