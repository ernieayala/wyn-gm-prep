## The campaign in one minute

Rumplank's royals hire the PCs to find the treasure of Poppy von Barnacle, a pirate legend.
- **Act 1:** Poppy's map leads to her proving grounds and a gem fragment, the **Obsidian Star**.
- **Act 2:** The PCs sail the Isles on her ship. They gather the other three fragments of the **[Jewel of the Indigo Isles](gm_glossary.md#jewel-of-the-indigo-isles)**.
- **Act 3:** The PCs come home as heroes. Then a demon cult steals the rebuilt Jewel. The cult raises a giant undead behemoth and attacks Rumplank. The PCs defend the city.

**Levels:** 1 to 11 over 12 chapters. **Length:** long for its levels. The book is full of optional setting detail (p. 7).

## How the twist works

For two acts, the PCs do heroic things. Those same things are what the villains want. The players learn this late, and that's the twist.

| What the players believe | What's true | When it's revealed |
|---|---|---|
| Poppy was a swashbuckling pirate | She was a war hero who destroyed a demon cult. Then she made herself over as a fun-loving pirate | Ch 4 (proving grounds, journal) |
| The hunt is for gold and glory | They're putting the cult's relic back together | Ch 4 on, fully clear in Ch 9 |
| Prince Kulupi is a sponsor and ally | He's one of the cult's three leaders, and its official head | **Ch 11** |
| Prismatic Colvi is a helpful priest | He's another cult leader | Act 3, fought in Ch 12 |
| The Indigo Stranger is a mysterious helper | He's X'na'zed, an indigo dragon who loves Rumplank. He doesn't know he's helping the cult | Ch 12 (true form) |

> **🎯 Why this matters:** A reveal hits hard only if players liked the villain and saw him often. In Acts 1–2, make Kulupi and Colvi useful, friendly, and easy to remember. Give no hints and no sneers. Don't make them helpful in a way that seems suspicious.

> **💡 GM tip:** The book plants a few clues. Play them straight, so players can look back later and spot them.
> - Ch 1: Kulupi sends the PCs to the Gutted Snake, the best lead in town.
> - Ch 1, Scene 2D: Colvi has the Pazuzu statue taken away once a PC names it.
> - Act 2: Kulupi insists on commanding Poppy's ship on its first voyage.
> - Act 3: Kulupi goes "missing" with the kidnapping victims.

> **⚠️ Gotcha:** Colvi's Ch 12 dialogue depends on how the PCs treat him, starting in Ch 1. Write it down in [Chapter 1's tracking table](gm_chapter_01.md#things-to-track-for-later-chapters).

## The real history

The years use the setting's calendar. The present year is **6023**. The book's timelines are on p. 10 (intro) and p. 374 (cult).

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 5802 | The **[Cult of the Shivering Behemoth](gm_glossary.md#cult-of-the-shivering-behemoth)** starts gathering followers across the Isles |
| 5807 | The cult builds its main temple on **Razorback Isle** |
| 5811 | The cult builds a fortress on **Moonshadow Isle** |
| 5821 | A few cultists settle in caves under Goldcrop's tallest peak. They name it **Shivering Mountain** for their god |
| 5822 | **Poppy and her allies defeat the cult at Razorback Isle.** They kill the high priest and shatter the Jewel into four pieces. They also kill the shale behemoth the Jewel controlled. The temple sinks. Poppy then wipes out the Goldcrop group of cultists, but some of their ruins still stand |
| 5823 | Poppy builds her **proving grounds** under Shivering Mountain. She starts training people who might take her place |
| — | Poppy makes herself over as the **Gilded Pirate**. She turns sleepy Rumplank into the party capital of the Isles. This is the **[Rumplank Renaissance](gm_glossary.md#rumplank-renaissance)** |
| 5862 | Her last students die. With no one to take her place, **Poppy disappears**. People assume she's dead |
| 5992 | Miners move into Shivering Mountain. The oracle **Zellura** starts getting visions from Varagix |
| 5995 | The miners find disturbing things and leave |
| 6001 | Colvi already worships Varagix on his own. He joins Zellura, and the cult is reborn |
| 6020 | The cult sets up a new headquarters in an abandoned mine in the western jungle |
| 6021 | Zellura recruits **Prince Kulupi**. Then she summons the demon **Zaroz** |
| 6022 | Zellura makes Kulupi the official leader |
| 6023 | **Now.** Kulupi finds the map fragment in the castle hall and recruits the PCs |

**Poppy's plan:** Late in life, Poppy made a map to her hideout. She wanted worthy heroes to find it after her death.
- Her wizard, [Arahosko the Red](gm_glossary.md#arahosko-the-red), **cursed the map and split it in two**.
- Arahosko also made the [sapphire scale](gm_glossary.md#sapphire-scale). It's the only key that joins the halves again.
- Poppy hung one half in plain sight in Castle Rumplank. She hid the other half in a Goldcrop ruin.
- She sent the scale away on the *Gasping Gipper*, and that ship wrecked.

> **⚠️ Gotcha:** The book contradicts itself twice and never settles either one. Pick one version of each and stick to it.
> - **Slain high priest:** the cult article says it's **Jakorvo**, a man. Ch 8 says it's an unnamed woman Poppy stabbed beside the Jewel.
> - **Proving grounds:** the intro says Poppy built them from the cult's ruined halls. Ch 4 says she found the caves when she docked the *Chromatic Queen* in the cove.

> **🧩 Sets up:** Poppy is dead. Zellura raises her as **Shaleborn Poppy** in Ch 12. Rumplank can only guess what happened to her. Guesses run from "died of old age" to "found immortality and sails the Isles in disguise" (p. 10).

## The cult

**Patron:** **[Varagix the Glimmering](gm_glossary.md#varagix-the-glimmering)**, a demon lord.
- He was once an earth spirit of [the Eld](gm_glossary.md#the-eld). He loved gems and hated the gods of [the Balance](gm_glossary.md#the-balance).
- He ate a clutch of dragon eggs. Then he left to rule a demon realm.
- He looks like a huge dragon with gemstone scales, behind a jeweled mask.

**What the cult teaches:** the powerful deserve what they want. The weak have only themselves to blame.
- It draws people who are greedy, proud, or bitter.
- Many join through **[Garardi's School for Scamps & Scallywags](gm_glossary.md#garardis-school-for-scamps--scallywags)**. It's a pirate school in Rumplank, run by the cultist Captain Bloodtail.
- New members hear that they worship "an ancient dragon god who brings wealth." Only the upper ranks learn Varagix is a demon.
- Members carry gems instead of holy symbols.

**The behemoth:** a **shale behemoth** that Jakorvo controlled with the Jewel. When the Jewel shattered, the behemoth got confused, and Poppy's team killed it. The cult plans to **bring it back to life** and flatten Rumplank.

**Leaders today** (p. 374):
- **Grand Oracle Zellura:** a priestess covered in bones, half dead. Pirates killed her family. She hates Rumplank's romantic legend of Poppy.
- **[Prismatic Colvi](gm_glossary.md#prismatic-colvi):** a proud priest and researcher. He paid for the new headquarters.
- **[Prince Kulupi](gm_glossary.md#prince-kulupi):** ambitious and ruthless. He's been the official leader since 6022.
- **Zaroz:** Varagix's demon envoy. He advises all three.

Each of the three leaders expects to end up on top. Each plans to leave the worst jobs to the other two.

**Key ally:** **[Falarni Malabria](gm_glossary.md#falarni-malabria)**, an inventor with no morals. Kulupi bought her with research money. She doesn't worship Varagix. She hires the Ch 9 arsonists, and the PCs fight her in Ch 11.

## Act 1: Search for the Missing Map (Chapters 1–4)

**Goal:** follow Poppy's map to her proving grounds.

- **Ch 1: Edges of the Map.** The Redfeathers give the PCs half the map. The PCs track down the other half and the sapphire scale. The trail runs through Rumplank, the Iron Eels' hideout, the [Gasping Gipper](gm_glossary.md#gasping-gipper) wreck, and the [Writhing Swamp](gm_glossary.md#writhing-swamp). When the PCs put the map together, it starts turning to ash. **[Full prep →](gm_chapter_01.md)** · **[Eldamon →](gm_chapter_01_eldamon.md)**
- **Ch 2: "X" Marks the Spot.** The PCs hurry to **[Jakapo Town](gm_glossary.md#jakapo-town)**, a galtzagorri village of mushrooms. Its elder is **[S'matzia](gm_glossary.md#smatzia)**, Arahosko's descendant. Once the PCs win her over, she lifts the curse with a ritual. The toymaker [Batarsi](gm_glossary.md#batarsi) stole a magic harmonica, the [gentle whistler](gm_glossary.md#gentle-whistler). The PCs take it back. Then they play it at [Aksyanis Amphitheater](gm_glossary.md#aksyanis-amphitheater) to calm the mantis swarms of the [Hissing Chasm](gm_glossary.md#hissing-chasm). Along the way, they touch the sapphire scale to Poppy's [waymarkers](gm_glossary.md#waymarkers) to charge it. **[Full prep →](gm_chapter_02.md)** · **[Eldamon →](gm_chapter_02_eldamon.md)**
- **Ch 3: What Makes the Mountain Shiver.** The map leads to **[Shiverskull Cave](gm_glossary.md#shiverskull-cave)** at the base of [Shivering Mountain](gm_glossary.md#shivering-mountain). The PCs get past the abandoned mining camp, shale spitter nests, and cult ruins. They reach the secret entrance to Poppy's hideout. It opens only for a fully charged sapphire scale. The PCs don't level up in this chapter. **[Full prep →](gm_chapter_03.md)** · **[Eldamon →](gm_chapter_03_eldamon.md)**
- **Ch 4: Legacy of the Gilded Pirate.** The PCs explore Poppy's proving grounds: ancient guardians, riddles, and a final test at **G25**. U'oko's trivia from Ch 1 answers that test. The PCs claim the **[Obsidian Star](gm_glossary.md#obsidian-star)** and Poppy's journal. The base starts to collapse. In a secret cove they find Poppy's ship, the **[Chromatic Queen](gm_glossary.md#chromatic-queen)**. The Obsidian Star unlocks its helm, so they escape on it. **[Full prep →](gm_chapter_04.md)** · **[Eldamon →](gm_chapter_04_eldamon.md)**

> **🎯 Why Act 1 exists:** It starts as a treasure hunt and slowly turns into a history lesson. At the end, Poppy's journal reveals **three more fragments**. The stakes rise from "treasure" to "something Poppy feared."

> **🧩 Sets up:** Defense resources for Ch 12 start turning up in Ch 4. See [Campaign-wide systems](#campaign-wide-systems).

**End-of-act party level:** 4.

## Act 2: Voyage to Runaway Reef (Chapters 5–8)

**Goal:** recover the other three fragments of the Jewel.

**The deal:** Fixing up the *Chromatic Queen* costs 2,000 gp.
- The royals pay if the PCs take the royal route and the royal crew on the first voyage.
- If the royals pick the crew, **Prince Kulupi insists on commanding**. If the PCs won't share command, he sails along as an envoy anyway.
- After they return, the PCs own the ship outright (p. 112).

Seaview's Academy of Tastes has the maps that show where the three missing fragments are.

- **Ch 5: Ghosts of Seaview.** The PCs visit **[Bluebell Island](gm_glossary.md#bluebell-island)** and its orpok city, [Seaview](gm_glossary.md#seaview). Kulupi comes along to show off the ship, buy bluebell crops, and get into the Academy of Tastes. Spirits threaten the harvest. **[Councilor Pelborii](gm_glossary.md#pelborii-karnihar)** stirred them up. She's a conspiracy theorist who saw a real omen, and her ritual against it went wrong. The fragment, the **[Aquamarine Wedge](gm_glossary.md#aquamarine-wedge)**, waits in Poppy's cave in the thorny [Briargrove](gm_glossary.md#briargrove). **[Full prep →](gm_chapter_05.md)** · **[Eldamon →](gm_chapter_05_eldamon.md)**
- **Ch 6: Moonshadow Mysteries.** Shadow Plane [sklaggans](gm_glossary.md#sklaggan) have overrun **[Moonshadow Island](gm_glossary.md#moonshadow-island)**, a safe place for outcasts and criminals. The [Gullstone Cave](gm_glossary.md#gullstone-cave) vault is already empty. [Umbrocious](gm_glossary.md#umbrocious), a shadowy fey, serves the [Flesh Stitcher](gm_glossary.md#flesh-stitcher). He slipped in incorporeal and got wounded by Poppy's trap. He stole the **[Bruised Crystal](gm_glossary.md#bruised-crystal)** to power his master's Skinloom. Meeting the swamp witch [Rotsnout](gm_glossary.md#rotsnout) is the level-6 milestone. **[Full prep →](gm_chapter_06.md)** · **[Eldamon →](gm_chapter_06_eldamon.md)**
- **Ch 7: The Flesh Stitcher's Fortress.** The PCs storm the old cult fortress and defeat the Flesh Stitcher. They pull the Bruised Crystal out of the [Skinloom](gm_glossary.md#skinloom). The Skinloom is a gateway made of flesh, built for a Shadow Plane invasion. **[Full prep →](gm_chapter_07.md)** · **[Eldamon →](gm_chapter_07_eldamon.md)**
- **Ch 8: Descent to Razorback Isle.** Razorback Isle no longer exists. Past the chochori town of [Kalacho](gm_glossary.md#kalacho) lies **[Runaway Reef](gm_glossary.md#runaway-reef)** and the sunken cult temple. An air bubble holds the top of the temple. **[Teakni](gm_glossary.md#teakni)** saboteurs, who are water elementals, are flooding it. The PCs get past old defenses and the [skeletal cult dragon](gm_glossary.md#skeletal-cult-dragon). That's the undead high priest inside a suit made of her cultists' bones. The PCs take the last fragment, the **[Tangerine Shard](gm_glossary.md#tangerine-shard)**, and escape as the temple floods. **[Full prep →](gm_chapter_08.md)** · **[Eldamon →](gm_chapter_08_eldamon.md)**

> **🎯 Why Act 2 exists:** The PCs hop between very different islands: a farming island, a haunted outlaw island, and a sunken temple. They come home at the height of their fame, with a legendary ship and a whole legendary gem. That's the moment Act 3 hits them.

> **🔒 GM-only:** Kulupi sails with the PCs on the first voyage and watches the Jewel come together. Play him as a stiff commander who knows his job. He leaves the sailing to the bosun, Garnikor.

**End-of-act party level:** 8.

## Act 3: Battle at Barnacle Bay (Chapters 9–12)

**Goal:** stop the cult and save Rumplank.

- **Ch 9: Party in Rumplank.** The PCs are guests of honor at **[Founder's Day](gm_glossary.md#founders-day)**. It has contests, a parade, and the Rum Run. **[Amon](gm_glossary.md#amon)** and **[Castilli](gm_glossary.md#castilli)**, gods of the Balance, come in person.
  - The four pieces join into the Jewel, an item-20 artifact. It carries a curse through Kulupi.
  - The cult's hired arsonists set fires. A cult ritual confuses the city guard and controls them.
  - The cultists kidnap people to cover their escape. They take sacrifices, plus anyone in their way.
  - The gods being there ruins the cult's planned ritual. Still, the cultists escape **with victims and, most likely, the Jewel**. If the PCs keep it, the book has backup plans for the cult to get it later.
  - The prince and princess are among the missing. **[Full prep →](gm_chapter_09.md)** · **[Eldamon →](gm_chapter_09_eldamon.md)**
- **Ch 10: Where Few Dare to Tread.** The PCs chase the cult from the Crawling Caves through the Okapi Hills and the [Crackbeak Salt Desert](gm_glossary.md#crackbeak-salt-desert). At a ruin, four cultists have barricaded themselves in. The PCs free two captives from them. In the [Drowned Jungle](gm_glossary.md#drowned-jungle), galtzagorris lead the PCs to their hidden village, [Pliticori](gm_glossary.md#pliticori-village). There they meet its founder, the vermilion dragon **[Peerless Ruby](gm_glossary.md#peerless-ruby)**. The PCs leave the rescued victims in the village. **[Full prep →](gm_chapter_10.md)** · **[Eldamon →](gm_chapter_10_eldamon.md)**
- **Ch 11: Cult of the Shivering Behemoth.** The PCs cross the [Embattled Caverns](gm_glossary.md#embattled-caverns). The sapphire drake [Aylushrixa](gm_glossary.md#aylushrixa-the-azure-inferno) rules them. Past the caverns is the cult's base, which is mostly empty. The PCs fight two of the cult's best, one of them Falarni Malabria. They reach the ritual chamber **too late**, and the behemoth wakes. They overhear the plan and learn that **Prince Kulupi is one of the cult's masterminds**. The behemoth's waking causes an earthquake. The PCs flee in a **mine-cart chase**. Then they fly to Rumplank on Peerless Ruby. **[Full prep →](gm_chapter_11.md)** · **[Eldamon →](gm_chapter_11_eldamon.md)**
- **Ch 12: Battle for [Barnacle Bay](gm_glossary.md#barnacle-bay).** The siege uses its own subsystem. The PCs set up defenses in five districts, using resources they gathered all campaign. X'na'zed shows his true form and helps. The PCs fight off the cult's armies in waves. Then they fight three boss battles:
  1. **Prismatic Colvi**.
  2. **Zellura** together with **[Shaleborn Poppy](gm_glossary.md#shaleborn-poppy)**, the undead Poppy von Barnacle.
  3. **Kulupi and his [illusory princes](gm_glossary.md#illusory-princes)**, on the behemoth's back. **[Full prep →](gm_chapter_12.md)** · **[Eldamon →](gm_chapter_12_eldamon.md)**

> **🎯 Why Act 3 exists:** It pays off the whole campaign.
> - The festival shows the PCs the city they'll fight for.
> - The chase takes away the castle and its comforts.
> - The reveal changes how players see everything that came before.
> - The siege spends resources the PCs gathered without knowing why.

> **⚠️ Gotcha:** Kulupi's Ch 9 "kidnapping" is a cover story. He leaves with the cultists. Players learn the truth in Ch 11. Let the king and queen's grief be real.

**End-of-act party level:** 11.

## Campaign-wide systems

- **Monster Parts.** The book is built for the *Battlezoo Bestiary* Monster Parts system, hybrid variant. You can use the standard magic-item economy instead. The book lists replacement treasure where you need it (p. 7). [How to read the numbers →](gm_chapter_01.md#monster-parts-treasure-system)
- **NPC companions.** With four players, allow at most **two** at a time. They fight only when the text says so (p. 8). Act 1 options:
  - D'marti (Ch 1)
  - the scroll mold (Ch 1)
  - Ignatius Barkley (Ch 1)
  - Kuaranda (Ch 2)
  - Apraka Ogari, a kragrak geomancer (Ch 3)
- **Defense special resources.** In Ch 12 the PCs spend 600 Resource Points on defenses. From Ch 4 on, they find rare **special resources**. Each one upgrades a defense to tier 2 or counts as 10 Resource Points. The list is on p. 315. **Write down every one the PCs find.**
- **Background special effects.** Some backgrounds from the book and the free Player's Guide trigger story moments. The book marks these "Background Special Effect." One example is the indigo pirate dueling Melar Kan in Ch 1.
- **Character death.** Each act has easy replacements (p. 9).
  - Act 1: a new PC recruited in Rumplank, or a companion.
  - Act 2: a crew member.
  - Act 3: a festival hero or a rescued kidnapping victim.
- **Eldamon.** These elemental creatures come from *Battlezoo Eldamon*. They live out of phase with the world. Mortal effects can't touch them, and they can't touch the mortal world. The PCs befriend them by talking and never capture them.
  - Each chapter has an eldamon doc. It places a few eldamon into that chapter's scenes, with a mix of elements and rarities. Some are only sightings and never come close.
  - Read the [befriending rules](gm_chapter_01_eldamon.md#befriending-rules) once. The [Eldadex](gm_chapter_01_eldamon.md#eldadex-quick-reference) lists every species and the chapters it shows up in.
  - Highlights: Glacé the chillot, who trades ghost stories (Ch 5). A friendly eldamon bout with Okko Bramblesnout at Founder's Day (Ch 9). A homebrew way to calm the Ancient Water Eldamon in the siege (Ch 12).
- **Eldamon evolution.** Each eldamon has an evolution condition. The docs mark moments that meet it with 🧩. A befriended eldamon can evolve only after a trainer gains the 11th-level Eldamon Evolution feature (Eldamon p. 40). So evolution happens at the very end of the campaign. Keep track of the conditions met along the way.

## Cast Sheet — Names to Know

> **Glossary tip:** each bold name links to its entry in [gm_glossary.md](gm_glossary.md).

**Allies and quest-givers**
- [**King Okeki**](gm_glossary.md#king-okeki-redfeather) & [**Queen Maxelle Redfeather**](gm_glossary.md#queen-maxelle-redfeather): the friendly royals. Okeki is good-natured and easily distracted. Maxelle makes most of the real decisions.
- [**Princess Atriella**](gm_glossary.md#princess-atriella): brave, rushes into danger, and keeps the meeting notes. She hatched in the same clutch as Kulupi. Rumplank expects her to inherit the throne. Kulupi resents that, and the cult used it to recruit him.
- [**D'marti Tixima**](gm_glossary.md#dmarti-tixima): a chatty jungle explorer who is lost as usual. Found the second map half. Possible Ch 1 companion.
- [**Headmistress U'oko**](gm_glossary.md#headmistress-uoko): runs [Quill & Scroll Academy](gm_glossary.md#quill--scroll-academy). Her Poppy trivia answers the Ch 4 test.
- [**Master Olo**](gm_glossary.md#master-olo): an [orpok](gm_glossary.md#orpok) martial-arts master. Teaches the Flying Pig Stance.
- [**Captain Nevergo**](gm_glossary.md#captain-nevergo): a grumpy apothecary who never sailed. A lead to D'marti.
- [**Sagoya Pekipak**](gm_glossary.md#sagoya-pekipak): rat catcher. Invented the Rodential Respirator.
- [**Ignatius Barkley**](gm_glossary.md#ignatius-barkley): the last Embersand leshy who isn't corrupted. Possible Ch 1 companion.
- [**Kuaranda**](gm_glossary.md#kuaranda): a herder from Jakapo Town. Her thyalodon carried the sapphire scale. Possible Ch 2 companion.
- [**Peerless Ruby**](gm_glossary.md#peerless-ruby): vermilion dragon who founded a hidden galtzagorri village. Act 3 ally.

**Friendly but secretly something else**
- 🔒 [**Prince Kulupi**](gm_glossary.md#prince-kulupi): gives the quest. Also a **cult leader**, revealed in Ch 11.
- 🔒 [**Prismatic Colvi**](gm_glossary.md#prismatic-colvi): high priest of the [Temple of Many Colors](gm_glossary.md#temple-of-many-colors). Also a **cult leader**, fought in Ch 12. Track how the PCs treat him.
- 🔒 [**The Indigo Stranger**](gm_glossary.md#indigo-stranger) / [**Zanaya**](gm_glossary.md#zanaya): the [indigo dragon](gm_glossary.md#indigo-dragon) [**X'na'zed**](gm_glossary.md#xnazed) in disguise. A true helper. Use him when players are stuck.

**Eldamon and their trainers**
- [**Glacé**](gm_glossary.md#glacé): chillot eldamon in the Wavewatcher Inn. Trades ghost stories, and scares people only to bond with them (Ch 5).
- [**The Breath Thief**](gm_glossary.md#the-breath-thief): a grima. Students blame it for the Dean de Cuisine's murder, but it only kept his last breath (Ch 5).
- [**Okko Bramblesnout**](gm_glossary.md#okko-bramblesnout): Seaview fishmonger and eldamon trainer. Challenges the PCs to a bout at Founder's Day (Ch 9).
- [**Ancient Water Eldamon**](gm_glossary.md#ancient-water-eldamon): an army that the cult drove into a frenzy. It floods Rosewater in the siege. The PCs can calm it but never befriend it (Ch 12).

**Antagonists**
- [**Melar Kan**](gm_glossary.md#melar-kan): captain of the [**Iron Eels**](gm_glossary.md#iron-eels) and Ch 1 boss. Loves making deals, and cheats at them.
- [**Squotan Malardy**](gm_glossary.md#squotan-malardy): Melar Kan's first mate, who betrayed him. Never seen on screen. Stole the map half, then the sapphire scale.
- [**Cult of the Shivering Behemoth**](gm_glossary.md#cult-of-the-shivering-behemoth): the true enemy, hidden until late. See [The cult](#the-cult).
