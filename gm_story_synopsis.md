## The campaign in one minute

Rumplank's royals hire the PCs to find pirate legend Poppy von Barnacle's treasure. **Act 1:** her map leads to her proving grounds and a gem fragment, the **Obsidian Star**. **Act 2:** on her ship, they sail the Isles gathering the other three fragments of the **[Jewel of the Indigo Isles](gm_glossary.md#jewel-of-the-indigo-isles)**. **Act 3:** they come home as heroes, then a demon cult steals the assembled Jewel, raises a giant undead behemoth, and attacks Rumplank, and the PCs defend it.

**Levels:** 1 to 11 over 12 chapters. **Length:** long for its levels, packed with optional setting detail (p. 7).

## How the twist works

Dramatic irony drives the campaign: for two acts, the PCs' heroic-looking moves are what the villains want.

| What the players believe | What's true | When it's revealed |
|---|---|---|
| Poppy was a swashbuckling pirate | War hero who destroyed a demon cult, then reinvented herself as a fun-loving pirate | Ch 4 (proving grounds, journal) |
| The hunt is for gold and glory | They're reassembling the cult's relic | Ch 4 on, fully clear in Ch 9 |
| Prince Kulupi is a sponsor and ally | One of the cult's three leaders, and its official head | **Ch 11** |
| Prismatic Colvi is a helpful priest | Another cult leader | Act 3, fought in Ch 12 |
| The Indigo Stranger is a mysterious helper | X'na'zed, an indigo dragon who loves Rumplank, unaware he's helping the cult | Ch 12 (true form) |

> **🎯 Why this matters:** A reveal lands hard only if the villain was likable and present all along. In Acts 1–2, make Kulupi and Colvi useful, friendly, and memorable, with no hints, sneers, or suspicious helpfulness.

> **💡 GM tip:** Play the book's planted clues straight, so players can look back and spot them.
> - Ch 1: Kulupi steers the PCs to the Gutted Snake, the best lead in town.
> - Ch 1, Scene 2D: Colvi has the Pazuzu statue hauled off once a PC names it.
> - Act 2: Kulupi insists on commanding Poppy's ship on its first voyage.
> - Act 3: Kulupi goes "missing" with the kidnapping victims.

> **⚠️ Gotcha:** Colvi's Ch 12 dialogue depends on how the PCs treat him from Ch 1 on. Log it in [Chapter 1's tracking table](gm_chapter_1.md#things-to-track-for-later-chapters).

## The real history

Setting calendar; now is **6023**. Timelines: intro p. 10, cult p. 374.

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 5802 | The **[Cult of the Shivering Behemoth](gm_glossary.md#cult-of-the-shivering-behemoth)** starts gathering followers across the Isles |
| 5807 | Cult's main temple built on **Razorback Isle** |
| 5811 | Cult fortress built on **Moonshadow Isle** |
| 5821 | A few cultists settle caves under Goldcrop's tallest peak, naming it **Shivering Mountain** for their god |
| 5822 | **Poppy and her allies defeat the cult at Razorback Isle:** they kill the high priest, shatter the Jewel into four pieces, and slay the shale behemoth it controlled. The temple sinks. Poppy then wipes out the Goldcrop sect, leaving some ruins standing |
| 5823 | Poppy builds her **proving grounds** under Shivering Mountain and starts training would-be successors |
| — | Poppy reinvents herself as the **Gilded Pirate** and turns sleepy Rumplank into the Isles' party capital: the **[Rumplank Renaissance](gm_glossary.md#rumplank-renaissance)** |
| 5862 | Her last students die. With no successor, **Poppy disappears**, presumed dead |
| 5992 | Miners move into Shivering Mountain. The oracle **Zellura** starts receiving visions from Varagix |
| 5995 | Disturbing finds drive the miners out |
| 6001 | Colvi, already a lone Varagix worshipper, joins Zellura. The cult is reborn |
| 6020 | New cult headquarters in an abandoned western-jungle mine |
| 6021 | Zellura recruits **Prince Kulupi**, then summons the demon **Zaroz** |
| 6022 | Zellura makes Kulupi official leader |
| 6023 | **Now.** Kulupi finds the map fragment in the castle hall and recruits the PCs |

**Poppy's plan:** Late in life, Poppy mapped her hideout for worthy heroes to find after her death. Her wizard [Arahosko the Red](gm_glossary.md#arahosko-the-red) **cursed it and split it in two**, and made the [sapphire scale](gm_glossary.md#sapphire-scale), the only key that rejoins the halves. Poppy hung one half in plain sight in Castle Rumplank, hid the other in a Goldcrop ruin, and shipped the scale on the *Gasping Gipper*, which wrecked.

> **⚠️ Gotcha:** Two book contradictions, never reconciled. Pick one version of each and stick to it.
> - **Slain high priest:** **Jakorvo**, a man (cult article), or an unnamed woman Poppy stabbed beside the Jewel (Ch 8).
> - **Proving grounds:** built from the cult's ruined halls (intro), or caves Poppy found when she docked the *Chromatic Queen* in the cove (Ch 4).

> **🧩 Sets up:** Poppy is dead, and Zellura raises her as **Shaleborn Poppy** in Ch 12. Rumplank can only guess her fate, from "died of old age" to "found immortality and sails the Isles in disguise" (p. 10).

## The cult

**Patron:** **[Varagix the Glimmering](gm_glossary.md#varagix-the-glimmering)**, a demon lord. Once a gem-loving earth spirit of [the Eld](gm_glossary.md#the-eld) who hated the gods of [the Balance](gm_glossary.md#the-balance), he devoured a clutch of dragon eggs and left to rule a demonic realm. He appears as a huge gemstone-scaled dragon behind a jeweled mask.

**What it preaches:** the powerful deserve what they want, and the weak have only themselves to blame. It draws the greedy, proud, and resentful, many via **[Garardi's School for Scamps & Scallywags](gm_glossary.md#garardis-school-for-scamps--scallywags)**, a Rumplank pirate school run by the cultist Captain Bloodtail. Initiates are told they worship "an ancient dragon god who brings wealth." Only the upper ranks learn Varagix is a demon. Members carry gems, not symbols.

**The behemoth:** a **shale behemoth** Jakorvo controlled with the Jewel. The shattering confused it, and Poppy's team killed it. The cult means to **reanimate it** and flatten Rumplank.

**Leaders today** (p. 374):
- **Grand Oracle Zellura:** bone-adorned, half-dead priestess. Pirates killed her family, and she hates Rumplank's romantic Poppy legend.
- **[Prismatic Colvi](gm_glossary.md#prismatic-colvi):** proud researcher-priest who bankrolled the new headquarters.
- **[Prince Kulupi](gm_glossary.md#prince-kulupi):** ambitious, ruthless, official leader since 6022.
- **Zaroz:** Varagix's demonic envoy, advising all three.

Each of the three leaders expects to end up on top and plans to leave the worst to the other two.

**Key ally:** **[Falarni Malabria](gm_glossary.md#falarni-malabria)**, an amoral inventor Kulupi bought with research money, not a Varagix worshipper. She hires the Ch 9 arsonists and is fought in Ch 11.

## Act 1: Search for the Missing Map (Chapters 1–4)

**Goal:** follow Poppy's map to her proving grounds.

- **Ch 1 — Edges of the Map.** The Redfeathers give the PCs half the map. The PCs track the other half and the sapphire scale through Rumplank, the Iron Eels' hideout, the [Gasping Gipper](gm_glossary.md#gasping-gipper) wreck, and the [Writhing Swamp](gm_glossary.md#writhing-swamp). When they assemble the map, it starts turning to ash. **[Full prep →](gm_chapter_1.md)** · **[Eldamon →](gm_chapter_1_eldamon.md)**
- **Ch 2 — "X" Marks the Spot.** The PCs race to **[Jakapo Town](gm_glossary.md#jakapo-town)**, a galtzagorri mushroom village, where elder **[S'matzia](gm_glossary.md#smatzia)**, Arahosko's descendant, lifts the curse by ritual once the PCs win her over. They take the stolen magic harmonica, the [gentle whistler](gm_glossary.md#gentle-whistler), back from toymaker [Batarsi](gm_glossary.md#batarsi), then play it at [Aksyanis Amphitheater](gm_glossary.md#aksyanis-amphitheater) to calm the [Hissing Chasm](gm_glossary.md#hissing-chasm)'s mantis swarms. Along the way they touch the sapphire scale to Poppy's [waymarkers](gm_glossary.md#waymarkers) to charge it. **[Full prep →](gm_chapter_2.md)** · **[Eldamon →](gm_chapter_2_eldamon.md)**
- **Ch 3 — What Makes the Mountain Shiver.** The map leads to **[Shiverskull Cave](gm_glossary.md#shiverskull-cave)** at the base of [Shivering Mountain](gm_glossary.md#shivering-mountain). The PCs work past the abandoned mining camp, shale spitter nests, and cult ruins to the secret entrance to Poppy's hideout, which opens only for a fully charged sapphire scale. No level-up this chapter. **[Full prep →](gm_chapter_3.md)** · **[Eldamon →](gm_chapter_3_eldamon.md)**
- **Ch 4 — Legacy of the Gilded Pirate.** Poppy's proving grounds: ancient guardians, riddles, and a final test at **G25** answered by U'oko's Ch 1 trivia. The PCs claim the **[Obsidian Star](gm_glossary.md#obsidian-star)** and Poppy's journal, then flee the collapsing base on her ship, the **[Chromatic Queen](gm_glossary.md#chromatic-queen)**, found in a secret cove. The Obsidian Star unlocks its helm, so they can escape on it. **[Full prep →](gm_chapter_4.md)** · **[Eldamon →](gm_chapter_4_eldamon.md)**

> **🎯 Why Act 1 exists:** A treasure hunt that slowly turns into a history lesson, ending when Poppy's journal reveals **three more fragments** and the stakes rise from "treasure" to "something Poppy feared."

> **🧩 Sets up:** Ch 12 defense resources start turning up in Ch 4. See [Campaign-wide systems](#campaign-wide-systems).

**End-of-act party level:** 4.

## Act 2: Voyage to Runaway Reef (Chapters 5–8)

**Goal:** recover the other three fragments of the Jewel.

**The deal:** Restoring the *Chromatic Queen* costs 2,000 gp. The royals pay if the PCs take the royal route and royal crew on the first voyage. If the royals pick the crew, **Prince Kulupi insists on commanding**. Refused shared authority, he sails as an envoy anyway. After they return, the PCs own the ship outright (p. 112). Seaview's Academy of Tastes holds the maps that locate the three missing fragments.

- **Ch 5 — Ghosts of Seaview.** **[Bluebell Island](gm_glossary.md#bluebell-island)** and its orpok city, [Seaview](gm_glossary.md#seaview). Kulupi comes to show off the ship, buy bluebell crops, and get into the Academy of Tastes. Spirits threaten the harvest, stirred up by **[Councilor Pelborii](gm_glossary.md#pelborii-karnihar)**, a conspiracy theorist whose ritual against a real omen went wrong. The fragment, the **[Aquamarine Wedge](gm_glossary.md#aquamarine-wedge)**, waits in Poppy's cave in the thorny [Briargrove](gm_glossary.md#briargrove). **[Full prep →](gm_chapter_5.md)** · **[Eldamon →](gm_chapter_5_eldamon.md)**
- **Ch 6 — Moonshadow Mysteries.** Shadow Plane [sklaggans](gm_glossary.md#sklaggan) overrun **[Moonshadow Island](gm_glossary.md#moonshadow-island)**, a haven for outcasts and criminals. The [Gullstone Cave](gm_glossary.md#gullstone-cave) vault is already empty: [Umbrocious](gm_glossary.md#umbrocious), a shadowy fey serving the [Flesh Stitcher](gm_glossary.md#flesh-stitcher), slipped in incorporeal, took a wound from Poppy's trap, and stole the **[Bruised Crystal](gm_glossary.md#bruised-crystal)** to power his master's Skinloom. Meeting the swamp witch [Rotsnout](gm_glossary.md#rotsnout) is the level-6 milestone. **[Full prep →](gm_chapter_6.md)** · **[Eldamon →](gm_chapter_6_eldamon.md)**
- **Ch 7 — The Flesh Stitcher's Fortress.** The PCs storm the old cult fortress, defeat the Flesh Stitcher, and pull the Bruised Crystal from the [Skinloom](gm_glossary.md#skinloom), a flesh gateway for a Shadow Plane invasion. **[Full prep →](gm_chapter_7.md)** · **[Eldamon →](gm_chapter_7_eldamon.md)**
- **Ch 8 — Descent to Razorback Isle.** Razorback Isle no longer exists. Past the chochori town of [Kalacho](gm_glossary.md#kalacho) lies **[Runaway Reef](gm_glossary.md#runaway-reef)** and the sunken cult temple, its top held in an air bubble that **[Teakni](gm_glossary.md#teakni)** water-elemental saboteurs are flooding. Through old defenses and the [skeletal cult dragon](gm_glossary.md#skeletal-cult-dragon) (the undead high priest in a suit of her cultists' bones), the PCs take the last fragment, the **[Tangerine Shard](gm_glossary.md#tangerine-shard)**, and escape as the temple floods. **[Full prep →](gm_chapter_8.md)** · **[Eldamon →](gm_chapter_8_eldamon.md)**

> **🎯 Why Act 2 exists:** Island-hopping across the setting's range: farming island, haunted outlaw island, sunken temple. The PCs return at peak fame with a legendary ship and a complete legendary gem, right where Act 3 hits them.

> **🔒 GM-only:** Kulupi travels with the PCs for the first voyage and watches the Jewel come together. Play him as a competent, stiff commander who leaves the sailing to the bosun, Garnikor.

**End-of-act party level:** 8.

## Act 3: Battle at Barnacle Bay (Chapters 9–12)

**Goal:** stop the cult and save Rumplank.

- **Ch 9 — Party in Rumplank.** The PCs are guests of honor at **[Founder's Day](gm_glossary.md#founders-day)**: contests, a parade, and the Rum Run. Gods of the Balance **[Amon](gm_glossary.md#amon)** and **[Castilli](gm_glossary.md#castilli)** attend in person. Joined, the four pieces form an item-20 artifact, cursed through Kulupi. The cult's hired arsonists set fires, and a cult ritual confuses and controls the city guard. Abductions cover the cultists' exit: sacrifices, plus anyone inconvenient. The gods' presence ruins the planned ritual, but the cultists escape **with victims and, most likely, the Jewel** (the book has fallbacks for the cult to get it later). The prince and princess are among the missing. **[Full prep →](gm_chapter_9.md)** · **[Eldamon →](gm_chapter_9_eldamon.md)**
- **Ch 10 — Where Few Dare to Tread.** A chase from the Crawling Caves through the Okapi Hills and the [Crackbeak Salt Desert](gm_glossary.md#crackbeak-salt-desert). At a ruin, the PCs free two captives from four barricaded cultists. In the [Drowned Jungle](gm_glossary.md#drowned-jungle), galtzagorris lead them to their hidden [Pliticori](gm_glossary.md#pliticori-village) village and its founder, the vermilion dragon **[Peerless Ruby](gm_glossary.md#peerless-ruby)**. The PCs leave the rescued victims there. **[Full prep →](gm_chapter_10.md)** · **[Eldamon →](gm_chapter_10_eldamon.md)**
- **Ch 11 — Cult of the Shivering Behemoth.** The PCs cross the [Embattled Caverns](gm_glossary.md#embattled-caverns), ruled by the sapphire drake [Aylushrixa](gm_glossary.md#aylushrixa-the-azure-inferno), into the cult's mostly empty base. After fighting two of the cult's best, including Falarni Malabria, the PCs reach the ritual chamber **too late**: the behemoth wakes. They overhear the plan and learn **Prince Kulupi is one of the cult's masterminds**. The waking causes an earthquake, and the PCs flee in a **mine-cart chase**, then fly to Rumplank on Peerless Ruby. **[Full prep →](gm_chapter_11.md)** · **[Eldamon →](gm_chapter_11_eldamon.md)**
- **Ch 12 — Battle for [Barnacle Bay](gm_glossary.md#barnacle-bay).** In a siege subsystem, the PCs organize five districts' defenses with resources gathered all campaign. X'na'zed shows his true form and helps. They repel the cult's armies in waves, then fight **Prismatic Colvi**, and **Zellura** alongside **[Shaleborn Poppy](gm_glossary.md#shaleborn-poppy)** (the undead Poppy von Barnacle), and finally land on the behemoth's back to fight **Kulupi and his [illusory princes](gm_glossary.md#illusory-princes)**. **[Full prep →](gm_chapter_12.md)** · **[Eldamon →](gm_chapter_12_eldamon.md)**

> **🎯 Why Act 3 exists:** It pays off everything. The festival shows the city the PCs will fight for, the chase strips away the castle and its comforts, the reveal recasts the whole campaign, and the siege spends resources they gathered without knowing why.

> **⚠️ Gotcha:** Kulupi's Ch 9 "kidnapping" is cover: he leaves with the cultists. Players learn it in Ch 11. Let the royal parents' grief be real.

**End-of-act party level:** 11.

## Campaign-wide systems

- **Monster Parts.** Built for the *Battlezoo Bestiary* Monster Parts system, hybrid variant. You can use the standard magic-item economy instead, and the book lists replacement treasure where needed (p. 7). [How to read the numbers →](gm_chapter_1.md#monster-parts-treasure-system)
- **NPC companions.** At most **two** at a time for four players, fighting only when the text says so (p. 8). Act 1 candidates: D'marti (Ch 1), the scroll mold (Ch 1), Ignatius Barkley (Ch 1), Kuaranda (Ch 2), and kragrak geomancer Apraka Ogari (Ch 3).
- **Defense special resources.** In Ch 12 the PCs spend 600 Resource Points on defenses. Rare **special resources** found from Ch 4 on upgrade a defense to tier 2 or count as 10 Resource Points each (list on p. 315). **Record every one the PCs find.**
- **Background special effects.** Backgrounds from the book and the free Player's Guide trigger story moments flagged "Background Special Effect," such as the indigo pirate dueling Melar Kan in Ch 1.
- **Character death.** Each act has easy replacements (p. 9). Act 1: a new PC recruited in Rumplank, or a companion. Act 2: a crew member. Act 3: a festival hero or rescued kidnapping victim.
- **Eldamon.** Elemental creatures from *Battlezoo Eldamon* live out of phase with the world: mortal effects can't touch them, and they can't touch it. They're befriended by talking, never captured. Each chapter has a companion doc placing a few into its scenes, mixing elements and rarities, plus the odd sighting that never approaches. Read the [befriending rules](gm_chapter_1_eldamon.md#befriending-rules) once; the [Eldadex](gm_chapter_1_eldamon.md#eldadex-quick-reference) lists every species and the chapters it appears in. Highlights: Glacé the ghost-story chillot (Ch 5), a friendly eldamon bout with Okko Bramblesnout at Founder's Day (Ch 9), and a homebrew way to calm the Ancient Water Eldamon in the siege (Ch 12).
- **Eldamon evolution.** Each eldamon has an evolution condition, and the docs flag moments that meet it (🧩). A befriended eldamon evolves only once a trainer gains the 11th-level Eldamon Evolution feature (Eldamon p. 40), so evolution lands at the very end of the campaign. Track conditions met along the way.

## Cast Sheet — Names to Know

> **Glossary tip:** every bold name links to its [gm_glossary.md](gm_glossary.md) entry.

**Allies and quest-givers**
- [**King Okeki**](gm_glossary.md#king-okeki-redfeather) & [**Queen Maxelle Redfeather**](gm_glossary.md#queen-maxelle-redfeather): the friendly royals. Okeki is affable and easily distracted. Maxelle makes most real decisions.
- [**Princess Atriella**](gm_glossary.md#princess-atriella): brave, rushes into danger, keeps the meeting notes. Kulupi's clutch-mate and Rumplank's expected heir, and his resentment of that is how the cult recruited him.
- [**D'marti Tixima**](gm_glossary.md#dmarti-tixima): chatty, lost-as-usual jungle explorer who found the second map half. Possible Ch 1 companion.
- [**Headmistress U'oko**](gm_glossary.md#headmistress-uoko): heads [Quill & Scroll Academy](gm_glossary.md#quill--scroll-academy). Her Poppy trivia answers the Ch 4 test.
- [**Master Olo**](gm_glossary.md#master-olo): [orpok](gm_glossary.md#orpok) martial-arts master who teaches the Flying Pig Stance.
- [**Captain Nevergo**](gm_glossary.md#captain-nevergo): surly apothecary who never sailed. A lead to D'marti.
- [**Sagoya Pekipak**](gm_glossary.md#sagoya-pekipak): rat catcher, inventor of the Rodential Respirator.
- [**Ignatius Barkley**](gm_glossary.md#ignatius-barkley): last uncorrupted Embersand leshy. Possible Ch 1 companion.
- [**Kuaranda**](gm_glossary.md#kuaranda): Jakapo Town herder whose thyalodon carried the sapphire scale. Possible Ch 2 companion.
- [**Peerless Ruby**](gm_glossary.md#peerless-ruby): vermilion dragon, founder of a hidden galtzagorri village. Act 3 ally.

**Friendly but secretly something else**
- 🔒 [**Prince Kulupi**](gm_glossary.md#prince-kulupi): quest-giver and **cult leader**, revealed in Ch 11.
- 🔒 [**Prismatic Colvi**](gm_glossary.md#prismatic-colvi): high priest of the [Temple of Many Colors](gm_glossary.md#temple-of-many-colors) and a **cult leader**, fought in Ch 12. Track how the PCs treat him.
- 🔒 [**The Indigo Stranger**](gm_glossary.md#indigo-stranger) / [**Zanaya**](gm_glossary.md#zanaya): the [indigo dragon](gm_glossary.md#indigo-dragon) [**X'na'zed**](gm_glossary.md#xnazed) in disguise. A sincere helper you can use when players are stuck.

**Eldamon and their trainers**
- [**Glacé**](gm_glossary.md#glacé): chillot eldamon in the Wavewatcher Inn who trades ghost stories and scares only to bond (Ch 5).
- [**The Breath Thief**](gm_glossary.md#the-breath-thief): grima blamed for the Dean de Cuisine's murder; it only kept his last breath (Ch 5).
- [**Okko Bramblesnout**](gm_glossary.md#okko-bramblesnout): Seaview fishmonger and eldamon trainer who challenges the PCs to a Founder's Day bout (Ch 9).
- [**Ancient Water Eldamon**](gm_glossary.md#ancient-water-eldamon): cult-frenzied army that floods Rosewater in the siege. Can be calmed, never befriended (Ch 12).

**Antagonists**
- [**Melar Kan**](gm_glossary.md#melar-kan): captain of the [**Iron Eels**](gm_glossary.md#iron-eels) and Ch 1 boss. Loves a deal, and cheats at it.
- [**Squotan Malardy**](gm_glossary.md#squotan-malardy): Melar Kan's traitor first mate, never on screen. Stole the map half, then the sapphire scale.
- [**Cult of the Shivering Behemoth**](gm_glossary.md#cult-of-the-shivering-behemoth): the hidden true enemy. See [The cult](#the-cult).
