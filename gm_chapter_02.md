## The chapter in one minute

The Chapter 1 map is crumbling. [Jakapo Town](gm_glossary.md#jakapo-town) is a [galtzagorri](gm_glossary.md#galtzagorri) village of giant mushrooms. Its cranky elder, [S'matzia](gm_glossary.md#smatzia), descends from [Arahosko the Red](gm_glossary.md#arahosko-the-red). She seals the map after the PCs do three favors:
- Rescue her gardener from a killer rose.
- Fight the ooze her failed experiment summoned.
- Fetch silver dye from the herder [Kuaranda](gm_glossary.md#kuaranda).

The sealed map shows six obelisks on the route to [Shivering Mountain](gm_glossary.md#shivering-mountain). The PCs must touch the [sapphire scale](gm_glossary.md#sapphire-scale) to each one. Breeding mantis swarms block the second obelisk, in the Hissing Chasm. To calm them, the PCs take a stolen magic harmonica from Batarsi, an evil toymaker. Then they guard Kuaranda while she plays it at Aksyanis Amphitheater. Waves of predators attack. Once the chasm is calm, the PCs follow the obelisks to [Shiverskull Cave](gm_glossary.md#shiverskull-cave).

**By the end of the chapter the PCs have:**
1. **A sealed, permanent map** (Event 8).
2. **The gentle whistler**, taken from Batarsi (Event 10).
3. **Calmed the Hissing Chasm** by surviving the amphitheater waves.
4. **Touched the scale to five obelisks** and reached the sixth at Shiverskull Cave.

**Party level:** starts at **2**, ends at **3**.

> **🎯 Why this chapter exists:** It turns a cursed scrap into a working quest map. It teaches the map's rule: touch every obelisk. It introduces Arahosko's people and offers Kuaranda as a companion. The shape is a favor chain. Each favor unlocks the next.

The book has four parts (p. 48–67):

| **Big Show at Aksyanis Amphitheater** (p. 65–67) | Kuaranda plays the whistler through four waves | After Event 10 |

**Play order:**
1. Jakapo Town: Events 5 → 6 → 7 → 8. Event 9's spy follows the PCs the whole time.

> **💡 GM tip (pacing):** The favors are short (Bolfo, S'matzia, Kuaranda, S'matzia). But the map loses HP until Event 8 ends. Keep the clock where the players can see it.

### Rituals

A **ritual** is a long group spell. It doesn't use a spell slot. A caster who knows a ritual can lead it if two things are true:
- They have the skill rank the primary check needs.
- The ritual's level is no more than half their level, rounded up.

So a PC who learns the silver seal (a 2nd-level ritual) can't lead it before level 3.
- The **primary caster** rolls the **primary check**. Its degree of success is the ritual's result.
- **Secondary casters** each roll a **secondary check** first. Successes help the primary caster. Failures hurt (core rulebook ritual rules).
- Each ritual lists **Cast** time, **Cost**, and skills.

**This chapter's example:** the silver seal (Event 8). S'matzia leads with Occultism +12. At least one PC must help with Crafting, Scribing Lore, or Indigo Isles Geography Lore. See [Event 8](#event-8--spellcasting-with-smatzia-ritual).

> **⚠️ Gotcha:** No DC is printed for the seal's checks. See the Event 8 tip.

> **⚠️ Gotcha: is a helper required?** Event 8 says S'matzia "needs one secondary spellcaster," then calls helping "optional" (p. 59). The ritual block requires at least 1. Require it.

### NPC attitudes, Make an Impression, and Request

Attitudes run **hostile → unfriendly → indifferent → friendly → helpful**.
- **Make an Impression:** about a minute of talk, then Diplomacy vs. the NPC's Will DC. Success: one step better. Critical success: two steps better. Critical failure: one step worse.
- **Request:** Diplomacy to ask a favor. Only friendly or helpful NPCs grant one.
- Story moments shift attitudes too. Naming Arahosko raises S'matzia one step. Snapping at Kuaranda drops her from indifferent to unfriendly.

**Start indifferent:** S'matzia (Event 6), Kuaranda (Event 7), Krissia (Landmarks 2 and 3), Golomor (E3).

### Elite and weak creatures

Stat lines marked "weak" or "elite" cite **BB 9**.
- **Elite:** +2 to AC, attack rolls, DCs, saves, Perception, and skills. More damage and HP. Higher level.
- **Weak:** −2 to the same numbers. Less damage and HP. Lower level.

**This chapter's examples:**
- Young sanguine rose (weak sanguine rose, Creature 3).
- Weak blood mice (Creature 0).
- Weak toy spy (Creature 2).
- Mrs. Sloth (elite springjaw doll, Creature 2).

Use the printed adjusted level for XP.

### Concealed, hidden, and undetected

In Event 6, a PC standing in the puddles of eyes can't become any of these to the eyesore.
- **Concealed:** fog or dim light. Attacks need a **DC 5 flat check** or they miss.
- **Hidden:** the attacker knows the square but can't see the creature. The attacker is flat-footed to it. Attacks need a **DC 11 flat check**.
- **Undetected:** the attacker doesn't know the square. It guesses one. The attack also needs a DC 11 flat check.

[Flat checks](gm_chapter_01.md#flat-checks) are in Chapter 1. Golomor (E3) and the toy spy (Event 9) try to stay out of sight.

### Tiny creatures, reach, and mounts

**Batarsi** is **Tiny** and rides a beetle.
- Tiny creatures can **share a space**. Most have **0-foot reach**. They must enter a target's space to attack in melee. The book says Batarsi must do this to use his galtzagorri hook spear.
- His **longspear** has 5 feet of reach. He can attack with it from the next square or from Goosebark's back.
- **Goosebark** has a fly Speed of 25 feet and carries him along. If it flies out of reach, melee PCs need ranged attacks or a way up.

### Swarms

A **swarm** is a mass of Tiny creatures acting as one across several squares. Most swarms take extra damage from **area** and **splash** effects. Most also shrug off much of a single weapon hit. Check each stat block.

**This chapter's swarms:** jungle mantis swarm (Landmark 2), toy swarm (E4), swarm assassin (Wave 1). Area spells and splash weapons like alchemist's fire are the fast answer.

### Rounds, minutes, and the wave timer

**1 round = 6 seconds; 1 minute = 10 rounds.** At the amphitheater:
- Song: **5 minutes** (50 rounds), nonstop.
- First wave: after **3 minutes** (30 rounds).
- Defense: **2 minutes** (20 rounds). **Exactly 2 rounds** of rest between waves.

Two rounds is 12 seconds. That's too short for Treat Wounds or Refocus, which take 10 minutes each. Heal with spells, potions, and other one-turn effects.

### Identify Magic and Learn a Spell

- **Identify Magic:** a skill use to learn what magic does. It usually uses Arcana, Nature, Occultism, or Religion. **DC 15 Arcana** reveals what the map's ashes can do. A DC 15 Arcana check on the map reveals how *mending* works on it.
- **Learn a Spell:** adds a spell or ritual to a caster's repertoire or spellbook. A Sylvan reader can learn the silver seal this way (Event 8).

## The Gilded Treasure Map

> **🎯 Why this section exists:** It sets the chapter's clock and quest shape. The decay pushes the PCs to Jakapo Town. The obelisks lay out the rest of the trip.

Did you hand out **Handout 1: The Gilded Treasure Map** (p. 46–47) at the end of Chapter 1? If not, now is a good time (p. 48).

### Disintegrating magic

Poppy had Arahosko enchant the map to fall apart once assembled. It tests how fast its finder moves (p. 49).

- The assembled map has a maximum of **20 Hit Points** and **loses 1 HP every hour**.
- Below **10 HP**, the DC to decipher the map rises by 2.
- At **0 HP** nobody can read the map. Soon it becomes a handful of ash.

> **⚠️ Gotcha (map HP):** The king's half had 10 HP in Chapter 1 (p. 16). The assembled map has 20 (p. 49). Use 20.

> **⚠️ Gotcha ("the DC to decipher"):** This chapter prints no base DC for reading the map. Pick one if a PC studies it.

### Stalling the decay

- The map **can't be Repaired** with Crafting.
- HP-restoring spells like ***mending*** **don't restore HP**. Instead they **pause the decay for hours equal to the HP they'd restore**. This works for magic items too. A typical *mending* gives **5 hours**.
- A **DC 15 Arcana** check on the map reveals this.

> **⚠️ Gotcha (Chapter 1's mending scroll):** U'oko's *mending* scroll buys 5 hours. It doesn't heal the map or end the curse. Princess Atriella can give two more ([Royal Family Interlude](#royal-family-interlude)).

### Reading the map

- It covers part of Goldcrop Island. It runs from a little west of [Rumplank](gm_glossary.md#rumplank) to a little north of Shivering Mountain.
- It shows no terrain or true distances. It shows landmark icons and **six glowing golden dots** between Jakapo Town and the mountain's base. The dots glow the same color as the scale.
- **Copies don't work.** A copy lacks the map's magic link to the scale. The end of the hunt needs that link (p. 49).

### Follow the dots: the waymarkers

Each landmark has a **5-foot-tall lava-rock obelisk**. It glows as the PCs approach. When a PC touches the **sapphire scale** to it, its golden light pours into the scale. The scale pulses or thrums, and that dot on the map goes dark. S'matzia explains this in Event 8 if needed. All six obelisks together open Poppy's sanctum in Chapter 3.

> **🧩 Sets up:** **Area F27 in Chapter 3** is the secret door to Poppy's hideout. It opens only for a fully charged scale (p. 50). A skipped obelisk means a trip back.

### Make Haste, Not Waste (if the map is destroyed)

The PCs have three ways forward if the map turns to ash (p. 49):
- **Magical ashes:** they glow dim yellow. A **DC 15 Arcana** check to Identify Magic shows they still hold divination magic. Drop a pinch in the air and it drifts toward the nearest landmark, even without wind. The PCs can still reach and charge every waymarker.
- **Unlikely aid:** D'marti Tixima, Prince Kulupi, or Melar Kan has seen obelisks like the one in Kuaranda's yard. Any of them can give directions.
- **Help from a stranger:** the [Indigo Stranger](gm_glossary.md#indigo-stranger) finds the PCs and helps in a mysterious way. He makes odd requests and gives aid as riddles. Any of this chapter's encounters can become a task he sets.

> **💡 GM tip:** The ashes keep the game moving with the least railroading. Use the Stranger last, as in Chapter 1.

## The six landmarks

| 2 | **Hissing Chasm** | Deadly until the song | At the bottom of the ravine | Mantis swarms block it |

### Landmark 1 — Jakapo Town

- **Where:** [Jakapo Town](#jakapo-town).
- **What happens:** the map gets sealed (Events 5–8). The PCs learn how to pass the chasm.
- **The waymarker:** in **Kuaranda's fenced thyalodon yard**. It's the PCs' first scale touch, in [Event 7](#event-7--inking-the-map-no-combat).

The PCs may not know to bring the map here and head somewhere else. If so, repeat the hint or have the Indigo Stranger tell them (p. 50).

### Landmark 2 — Hissing Chasm

- **Travel:** 2 miles of difficult terrain from Jakapo Town, about **2 hours**.
- **On the map:** a gaping hole with a praying mantis hovering over a glowing dot at its center.

At the overlook, read aloud (p. 50):

> *Thousands of bright green mantises swarm about this deep ravine, which stretches for miles in either direction. At the gulch's lowest point, a five-foot-tall obelisk made of carved lava rock glows softly at your approach.*

**Premature visit (before the whistler is played):** a near-deafening squeal grows as the PCs approach. Thousands of angry mantises swarm.
- The chasm is normally a pleasant valley. Goldcrop's jungle mantises return to breed every ten years. A cycle starts this year. For **at least 3 months** they attack anything that enters.
- **DC 20 Nature** or **DC 17 Indigo Isles Geography Lore** (Recall Knowledge) reveals this. Anyone in Jakapo Town or Rumplank can also tell them.
- The PCs must get through or wait out the season in Rumplank.

**Going around:** the chasm runs for miles. The jungle off the path is thick and hostile.
- Best routes: from Aksyanis Amphitheater north and east to Shiverskull Cave, or from the [Writhing Swamp](gm_glossary.md#writhing-swamp) east then north to Never Lonely Hill. Both are many miles of **greater difficult terrain** ([Travel distances](#travel-distances)).
- Expect several wandering monster attacks, perhaps the legendary **Shugiyar**. Each hour, the PCs need a **DC 25 Survival** check to avoid getting lost.
- It's wasted effort. F27 in Chapter 3 needs every obelisk, so they must come back.

**Going through:** walking in triggers a **jungle mantis swarm** (BB 50). The book calls it deadly. Mating mantises **don't chase PCs who flee**.

> **⚠️ Gotcha:** The book gives the swarm no level or threat label. Let the PCs see and hear it first, and let them run.

**Friendly roadster (only if the PCs skipped Jakapo Town):** **Krissia** (LN female [orpok](gm_glossary.md#orpok) roadster 3) rests on a boulder above the chasm.
- She explains the breeding cycle and sends the PCs to Jakapo Town.
- She escorts for **1 sp per hour** to Jakapo Town, Rumplank, or anywhere west of the chasm.
- When she guides, terrain is **one step easier** (greater difficult → difficult, difficult → normal). Travel is never faster than normal speed.
- **Background Special Effect:** **indigo trailblazer** PCs get a **+2 circumstance bonus** to Diplomacy with her. It applies to Make an Impression and Request, here or at Landmark 3.

**The way is clear (after the amphitheater):** the chasm quiets. A few mantises flutter, but the path to the obelisk is open.

**Waymarker:** touch the scale to the obelisk at the bottom. The dot disappears.

> **⚠️ Gotcha (two names):** The book uses both **Hissing Chasm** and **Hissing Gulch** for the same ravine.

### Royal Family Interlude

The PCs may return to Rumplank to report or ask advice (sidebar, p. 51). The king and queen are busy. **Prince Kulupi** or **Princess Atriella** hears the PCs out instead. The royal advises them and urges them on, thrilled with their progress.
- **If the map is still decaying:** Atriella gives **two scrolls of *mending***. She points the PCs to the Jakapo Town elder, said to descend from Arahosko the Red.

> **🔒 GM-only:** [Kulupi](gm_glossary.md#prince-kulupi) leads the cult, and the PCs are collecting what it wants. Play him as an eager, helpful sponsor. Let Atriella hand over the scrolls.

### Landmark 3 — Never Lonely Hill

- **Travel:** 2 miles of difficult terrain from the Hissing Chasm.
- **On the map:** a grassy mound east of the chasm with a shining obelisk. In person: sparse plants, a gentle slope, and a 5-foot glowing lava-rock pillar.

The hill rises a few dozen feet above the treetops. Old Rumplankers named it because someone was nearly always up there already.

**Waymarker:** touch the scale to the obelisk. The dot fades.

**Krissia is here**, whether or not the PCs met her at the chasm. She's eating lunch and gazing over the jungle. If the PCs talk to her:

*"This is the farthest I've ever gone. Beyond here, the entire jungle's a mystery."* Nodding toward the mountain, she adds, *"Only the fiercest roadsters have ever been there. Shivering Mountain. No idea why it's called that. Guess it does give me the shivers, though."* Then she shudders, gets up, and moves on.

- **Escort:** **1 gp** to Jakapo Town or Rumplank. She uses shortcuts that take **half the usual time** from here.
- **Bonus lore:** she starts **indifferent**. At **friendly or helpful** ([attitudes](#npc-attitudes-make-an-impression-and-request)), she shares a legend. Orpok miners of the **Emerald Earth Mining Company** dug a quarry inside Shivering Mountain and abandoned it. *"The place is supposedly riddled with shale spitters... Spitters love eating gems, you know. Their bodies are even made of gems, which you can take for yourself if you manage to kill one of the things. Either way, no doubt them miners left some interesting things if they fled the place in such a hurry."*

**XP:** **10 XP** each for reaching the hill. Give **30 XP** each instead if the PCs improved Krissia's attitude and learned of the mining company.

> **🧩 Sets up:** The miners' abandoned camp and shale spitters fill Shiverskull Cave (**Chapter 3**). Krissia's tip is the PCs' first warning.

> **⚠️ Gotcha (dates):** Krissia says "over ten years ago" (p. 52). Chapter 3 says "a few decades ago" (p. 69). The [synopsis timeline](gm_story_synopsis.md#the-real-history) says 5992 to 5995, about 30 years ago. She's repeating a legend, so her mistake fits.

### Landmark 4 — Ruined Tower *(Moderate 2)*

- **Travel:** 2 miles of difficult terrain from Never Lonely Hill.
- **On the map:** a whimsical stone tower among palms (outdated).

Read aloud (p. 52):

> *The crumbled ruins of a cylindrical stone structure lie in a messy heap at the center of this jungle clearing. Though the building was likely once a multi-story stone tower, the nearby palm trees now dwarf the tower's remains, which amount to a mound of rubble no taller than ten feet at its center. Impossibly, a stone obelisk made of lava rock remains intact and juts out from the rubble, glowing softly at your approach.*

This is the **twin of Batarsi's tower**. A gnome transmuter first lived in Batarsi's tower. Shortly before dying, the gnome moved here. The upper floors have collapsed. The front door opens onto rubble.

**Map:** use the Toymaker's Tower exterior (p. 61). Fill the interior with one layer of impassable rubble that's greater difficult terrain. Or run it as theater of the mind.

**Venomous Snakedead (2):** BB 118, Creature 2. Initiative: Perception +6. Monster Parts 5/12/22 gp. Two mindless [snakedead](gm_glossary.md#venomous-snakedead) wandered in. They attack intruders out of hatred for the living.

**Waymarker:** after the fight, touch the scale. It thrums stronger and the dot disappears.

**Treasure (without Monster Parts only):** a **lensatic compass** in the rubble.

### Landmark 5 — Melon Tree *(Moderate 2)*

- **Travel:** 2 miles of difficult terrain from the Ruined Tower.
- **On the map:** a glowing dot on a squat, gangly tree bearing one big melon unlike any Goldcrop fruit.
- **DC 21 Nature:** the drawing shows a **hook melon**, a monstrous, parasitic plant with a malevolent mind.

Read aloud (p. 52):

> *A softly glowing obelisk of lava rock stands immediately adjacent to a spindly, single-limbed "tree" in the center of this low-lying jungle clearing. Dangling from the tree's sole branch is a torso-sized lump of grotesque, fleshy fruit. Animal bones surround the tree. A small, indiscernible trinket near the base of the tree glints in the sunlight.*

- **The trinket:** a PC must **Seek within 15 feet** of the tree and succeed at a **DC 21 Perception** check. Success spots a silver ring on a mummified hand. Learning more needs a close look.
- **Map:** a plain clearing with the tree and obelisk. Or crop a section of another map, like the Writhing Swamp. Or run it as theater of the mind.

**Hook Melon:** BB 43, Creature 4. Initiative: Perception +11. Monster Parts 12/27/50 gp.
- It waits for prey to come **within 15 feet**, then attacks.
- It latches onto the **strongest-looking PC** and gallops around using its captive.
- **Against ranged PCs:** it has no ranged attacks and it's slow. The best it can do is detach, move behind cover, and block line of effect.

> **⚠️ Gotcha:** It's two levels above the party, so it hits often. Alone it's 80 XP (Moderate). But all its pressure lands on whoever it grabs. Watch that PC's HP.

**Treasure:** the ring is a silver wedding band (**1 gp**). The hand is a ***hand of the mage***.

**Waymarker:** touch the scale to the obelisk. The dot disappears.

### Landmark 6 — Shivering Mountain

**Shiverskull Cave** has an entrance shaped like a snake's open jaws. Reaching it after the other five obelisks ends the chapter. The PCs touch its obelisk at the start of Chapter 3 ([Concluding the chapter](#concluding-the-chapter)).

## Jakapo Town events

If the PCs ask about the map, anyone can point them to S'matzia's house, the biggest mushroom. But first they must handle her problems (p. 54).

> **🎯 Why these events exist:** The favor chain puts the PCs in the town's debt. It also earns S'matzia's grudging respect. Each step leads to the next: Bolfo → S'matzia → Kuaranda → amphitheater.

### Event 5 — Thorny Situation *(Low 2)*

- **Where:** two small gardener's huts west of S'matzia's (map, p. 54).
- **Who:** **Bolfo** (she/her), galtzagorri gardener.

Read aloud (p. 54):

> *Along the path leading up to the enclave's largest mushroom house are two much smaller mushroom huts surrounded by finely manicured grass and garden beds of pretty flowers. Between the huts, a strikingly drab rose bush shakes and mumbles audibly. The source of the noise quickly becomes clear: trapped within the bush—which is plainly moving of its own accord—is a struggling galtzagorri!*

A **sanguine rose** (plant monster) took root between the huts. Bolfo underestimated it, and it caught her. She screams for help when she sees the PCs.

**Young Sanguine Rose:** weak sanguine rose (BB 9, 84), Creature 3. Initiative: Perception +9. Monster Parts 7/18/30 gp.
- It's young and brash. It feeds on Bolfo until the PCs approach. Then it drops her and turns on them.

**Aftermath:** Bolfo thanks the PCs and points to S'matzia's hut to the east:

*"Please, I'm sure my mistress will reward you for your help," Bolfo says. "Just tell her you saved Bolfo's life. But tread carefully—Mistress S'matzia has quite a temper, and she doesn't care for folks interrupting her studies. Indeed, she's been banging around her hut for quite some time now, no doubt wrestling with a particularly frustrating magical challenge."*

**Treasure (without Monster Parts only):** from Bolfo's shed, one dose each of **oil of mending** and **oil of potency**.

> **🧩 Sets up:** Naming Bolfo's rescue earns 10 gp from S'matzia in Event 6. Make sure the players remember her.

### Event 6 — A Sight for Sore Eyes *(Moderate 2)*

- **Where:** S'matzia's hut, east side of the p. 54 map.
- **Who:** **S'matzia** (N female galtzagorri sage 5). Her manners are bad, but she's too brilliant for the town to throw her out.

As the PCs approach, read aloud (p. 56):

> *A massive mushroom stands before you, easily the largest in the grove. A small stone landing leads to a set of double doors, one of which is slightly ajar. You can hear what you believe to be bottles breaking and a woman cursing from within the strange home.*

If the PCs enter or announce themselves, someone yells, *"Whoever you are, get in here and help me!"* When they head toward the noise (east side), read aloud:

> *An elderly galtzagorri in fine robes flits about the hut, half-heartedly thrusting a wooden staff in one hand at an animated green glob of eyeballs. Small orbs of pink light swirl about the elder's free hand, their brightness pulsing as the elder flicks her wrist and fingers about in arcane gestures. Upon noticing you, the elder snaps, "Well, don't just stand there! Take care of this thing while I complete my ritual!"*

**What happened:** S'matzia dabbles in dangerous occult and primal magic. She tried to summon a spider-shaped being of primordial ooze. She got a glob of eyes and sticky ooze instead. Some of it became a slippery hazard. One chunk woke up hungry.
- To do the summoning, she unraveled ley-line threads under her hut.
- Now she's casting an hours-long sealing ritual while she fends off the ooze.
- If she doesn't finish, more violent oozes may pour into town.

**Puddles of Eyes** *(complex magical trap, Hazard 2)*
- **Stealth** +5 (expert).
- Sticky puddles of green goo full of alien eyeballs cover every inch of the hut. **Each space with a puddle is difficult terrain.**
- A creature in a puddle space **can't become concealed, hidden, or undetected** to the eyesore ([what those mean](#concealed-hidden-and-undetected)).
- **Disable:** three **DC 18 Occultism or Thievery** checks (trained). Each check scoops one of the three puddles into the corners. Each success clears the puddles and their difficult terrain in a **5-foot emanation** around the PC.
- **AC** 18. **Fort** +12, **Ref** +5. **Puddle HP** 10 for each of the three puddles. **Immunities:** acid, critical hits, mental, precision, unconscious.
- **Look Upon** (reaction, visual). **Trigger:** a creature enters the hut. **Effect:** the eyeballs fix on the newcomers, and the hazard rolls initiative.
- **Routine (1 action):** eyeballs roll toward anyone in a puddle and climb them. Each creature in an affected square makes a **DC 18 Reflex** save.
  - **Success:** unaffected.
  - **Failure:** **clumsy 1** until it uses an Interact action to brush off the eyeballs.
  - **Critical failure:** as failure, but **clumsy 2**.

**Eyesore:** BB 29, Creature 3. Initiative: Perception +12. Monster Parts 7/18/30 gp. It chases S'matzia until the PCs step in. Then it targets them and anyone stuck in the puddles.

**Ending the encounter:** the fight ends when the eyesore dies or **6 rounds** pass, whichever comes first. At that point S'matzia finishes her ritual and joins in. She likely ends it with a **3rd-level *fireball*** at the eyesore.

> **💡 GM tip (from the book):** If the PCs are about to win on their own, delay S'matzia a round or two. That way she doesn't steal their moment (p. 57).

> **📏 Rules refresher (XP math):** Eyesore (one level up, 60 XP) + party-level complex hazard (40 XP) = 100. That's between Moderate (80) and Severe (120). The book says **Moderate 2**, so go with it. The round-6 fireball caps the fight. See [encounter math](gm_chapter_01.md#encounter-difficulty-and-xp).

**Aftermath:** S'matzia sighs at her ruined experiment and turns to the PCs: *"Yes, well,"* she says sharply. *"What is it?"*
- She's brief and aloof at any attitude. She starts **indifferent**.
- She warms to magic talk. She **improves one step** the moment anyone mentions **Arahosko the Red**.
- She studies the map either way, because the PCs dealt with the eyesore.

After a few minutes she hands the map back:

*"Of course," she says, motioning toward her ruined hut, "I'll need you to gather an ingredient for me. I'm afraid I've misplaced a few things. Don't worry," she continues, "it's not far. Go see the thyalodon herder, Kuaranda, at the other end of town. Ask for a vial of silver dye. It's more expensive than it sounds, so hopefully you've got some coin on you. Well, what are you waiting for? Get to it!"*

**Treasure:** if the PCs mention saving Bolfo, S'matzia grudgingly gives them **10 gp**.

> **🔒 GM-only:** Her annoyance is an act. Bolfo is her dearest friend, but she won't admit it.

**Background Special Effect:** a **Poppy fanatic** PC grew up on Arahosko stories. S'matzia eagerly shares many new ones.

When the PCs are ready to see Kuaranda, go to Event 7.

### Event 7 — Inking the Map *(no combat)*

- **Where:** Kuaranda's hut and ranch, southwest corner of town (D2).
- **Who:** **[Kuaranda](gm_glossary.md#kuaranda)** (NG female galtzagorri bard 2). **Perception +7.**
- **When:** in the favor chain. **Or** right on arrival, if the PCs are returning her lost thyalodon. Either way, the PCs must already have activated the map with the sapphire scale.

Read aloud (p. 57):

> *A squat mushroom hut abuts a rustic but sturdy fence at what can only be described as a ranch. Thyalodons—slow, furry animals with docile demeanors—range around the yard, tended to by a galtzagorri herder in a cotton shirt and thick trousers. Near the yard's western end stands a five-foot-tall stone obelisk made of roughly carved lava rock. The obelisk begins to glow with golden light upon your approach, and the sapphire scale in your possession starts vibrating softly as well.*

Kuaranda chews sugar cane and talks in a slow drawl: *"Hello there,"* she calls with a smile. *"You here for some of my famous thyaldon wool? Just spun a new batch this morning, softest wool this side of Goldcrop and will keep you warm in the fiercest of rainstorms!"*
- **Wool:** many colors and textures, **2 sp to 5 gp per skein**.

**The waymarker (Landmark 1):** the scale brightens near the obelisk. If the PCs approach it, Kuaranda notices the glow. She suggests touching their "fancy blue rock" to it: *"I always thought that thing was just a weird hitchin' post. Looks like it might be important to y'all, though."*
- On contact, the obelisk's light rushes into the scale. The scale thrums softly, and the dot disappears. **Five glowing dots remain.**

> **🧩 Pays off from Ch 1:** This is the scale's first landmark. Every obelisk works this way ([Follow the dots](#follow-the-dots-the-waymarkers)).

**Talking to Kuaranda:** afterward she chats. She tells any non-galtzagorri that Jakapo Town gets few tall folk. *"Y'all from Rumplank?"* She doesn't wait for an answer. *"I been there once. Stayed at the Rumpled Feather, if you know where that is. Really nice folk—even taught me a shanty or two."* Then she sings to a thyalodon while she combs it. She chatters for minutes until someone interrupts.
- She starts **indifferent** and isn't easily hurt. But snapping at her drops her to **unfriendly** until the PCs win her back. She does no business before then.
- **Winning her back:** show love for animals or offer a new tune. It's hard to fake. She sees through most deception (Perception +7) and knows plenty of local songs.

> **💡 GM tip:** The Rumpled Feather is [Clamardinia Wakamarsis](gm_glossary.md#clamardinia-wakamarsis)'s tavern. It's the rival to the Gutted Snake's barkeep. PCs who know Rumplank can chat about it.

**Getting the silver dye:** when the PCs finally explain, she stands and dusts herself off: *"Well why didn't you say so?"*
- The dye is treated **black thyalodon ink**. It comes from animals fed a strict diet of certain beetles and magic herbs.
- **Price:** **10 gp** a vial. If the PCs make her **helpful**, she drops to **8 gp**, her lowest price.
- **Free**, and she's overjoyed, if the PCs returned her missing thyalodon from the Writhing Swamp. It's also free if they can tell her it's safe.

**Kuaranda as a companion:** if the PCs **saved her thyalodon** from the leshy husks, she asks to join.
- If the party has **fewer than two** NPC companions, she joins now. Otherwise she waits in Jakapo Town until there's room.
- **Either way, the PCs must bring her to Aksyanis Amphitheater** once they have the gentle whistler. She plays it.
- **On the road:** she plays music, mends clothes and gear, and tends animal companions.
- **With other companions:** she likes D'marti, the scroll mold, and [Apraka Ogari](gm_glossary.md#apraka-ogari) (Chapter 3). She bristles at [Ignatius Barkley](gm_glossary.md#ignatius-barkley)'s rigid, shortsighted justice.

> **⚠️ Gotcha (dye or ink):** The book says "silver dye" in Events 6–7. It mostly says "silver ink" in Event 8. They are the same thing.

With the dye in hand, the PCs go back to S'matzia: Event 8.

### Event 8 — Spellcasting with S'matzia *(ritual)*

- **Where:** S'matzia's hut, at her divining table.
- **Goal:** seal the map so it stops decaying.

S'matzia clears her table and has the PCs lay the map flat. She needs **one secondary caster**. Others may help. Read aloud (p. 59):

> *The elderly galtzagorri woman flits over to a round table etched with hundreds of curly runes and covered in jars, trays, and papers. In one swift motion, she swipes everything off the table and into a crate on the floor. "Go on, then," S'matzia rasps, "lay out the map." Meanwhile, she collects occult appurtenances from around the room—many-faceted crystals, an enormous black quill, candles of all colors and sizes—and explains the ritual process. "Using the silver ink," she says, "I'll ink over every line and symbol on the map, binding them to the parchment. This will seal the map and stop its deterioration. You may help if you like: pull up a stool and hold the sheet in place, sharpen my nib, help me make sense of these runes—or just stay out of the way.*
>
> *"Now, are you ready?" S'matzia dips her quill into the well of silver ink and waggles her thick eyebrows in anticipation. "Once I touch this quill to the page, I won't be able to stop until the ritual is complete."*

**S'matzia's Silver Seal:** Ritual 2. Rare, divination.
- **Cast** 4 hours. **Cost** rare silver ink worth at least 5 gp. **Secondary casters** at least 1, up to 4.
- **Primary check:** Arcana or Occultism (trained). S'matzia uses **Occultism +12**.
- **Secondary checks:** Crafting, Scribing Lore, or a Lore tied to the document. For the map, that's **Indigo Isles Geography Lore**.
- **Range** 10 feet. **Target** 1 scroll, map, tome, or other portable written material.
- You permanently seal parchment or vellum with magic silver ink. It becomes nearly indestructible and stays readable forever. With **at least two** secondary casters, the secondary check gains a **+1 circumstance bonus**. With **four**, it gains **+2**.
- **Critical success:** as success. The item can also roll saving throws against effects like *disintegrate*. Its save modifier is the ritual's spell DC − 10.
- **Success:** the target regains HP up to its maximum. It gains **Hardness 5** if its Hardness was lower. It's also protected against mundane harm like severe heat, damp, or cold. Spells like *disintegrate* still affect it normally.
- **Failure:** no effect. The ritual fails.
- **Critical failure:** the ritual fails. The target takes damage equal to half its maximum HP and becomes **broken**.

**How it plays out:**
- The ritual uses **half the silver ink**. Meanwhile, S'matzia casts ***mending*** on the map to protect it.
- **On any failure** she tries again. That takes more time and the rest of the ink. If that fails too, the PCs buy more from Kuaranda at **10 gp per inkwell**.
- **On a critical failure** the map loses **half its maximum HP** (10).
- The decay stops if the map has **at least 1 HP** when a successful casting ends.

> **⚠️ Gotcha: how long does it take?** The story text says **1 hour**. The ritual block says **Cast 4 hours** (p. 59). The text also says the map erodes during the ritual. Then it has her protect the map with *mending*. *Mending* usually pauses decay for 5 hours, which covers either length. Run 1 hour of story with no HP lost, unless the map was already near 0.

> **⚠️ Gotcha (a critical failure can destroy the map):** A critical failure costs the map 10 HP. If the map has 10 HP or less, that leaves it at 0, and it turns to ash. Use [Make Haste, Not Waste](#make-haste-not-waste-if-the-map-is-destroyed).

> **💡 GM tip (the DC):** The book prints none. The level-2 DC is DC 16. A 2nd-level spell's DC is DC 18. With Occultism +12, S'matzia succeeds on a 4 or better (DC 16) or a 6 or better (DC 18). Raise the DC if you want the PCs' help to matter more.

When the map is safe, read aloud (p. 59):

> *As S'matzia lays the final strokes of silver ink on the map, the symbols, lines, and words on the parchment begin to glow a faint gold color. With a slight flourish, S'matzia pulls back the quill and watches the map's crumbling edges glow brighter, as if they were limned by magical flame. Yet the flames don't engulf or destroy the page, but rather wash over it like a sheen of oil, encasing the map in a translucent, shiny film. When the glowing stops after a moment, the map remains shiny and smooth, and its edges are lined in protective silver. S'matzia lifts the map from the table and, in an instant, unceremoniously crumples it into a ball in her fist. With a knowing grin, the sage untangles the wad of parchment and hands the flat sheet to you—there's not even a single crease on it. The map's self-destruction has plainly stopped.*

> **🧩 Pays off from Ch 1:** This is the cure the Chapter 1 cliffhanger was building to.

**Background Special Effect:** **indigo trailblazer** and **indigo historian** PCs have reason to want this ritual for maps and old documents. If one gives a good reason, S'matzia shoves a thick vellum book at them: *"It's all in this tome. You can read Sylvan, can't you? I don't have time to teach you myself."*
- A Sylvan reader can **Learn a Spell** to learn the silver seal ([Learn a Spell](#identify-magic-and-learn-a-spell)). They can't be **primary caster** before **level 3**.

**Next steps:** while inking, S'matzia worked out the map. She tells the PCs:
- Charge the scale at each landmark. She says this if they didn't learn it at Kuaranda's. *"At each landmark is a magical waystone. It's clear that you must touch the sapphire scale to these waystones to gain their power. As for what the purpose of empowering the scale—I imagine that will become clear once you find the Gilded Pirate's hideout."*
- **The next landmark is the Hissing Gulch.** *"It's a ravine known for jungle mantises, which, like cicadas, return to the same place every ten years to breed en masse. You won't be able to reach the waypoint in the gulch unless you calm the angry insects first."*
- **The fix:** a sacred harmonica called the **gentle whistler**. Someone must play a certain song on it at nearby **Aksyanis Amphitheater**. That calms every bug in the gulch. **Kuaranda** can play it and knows the song.
- **The catch:** *"Unfortunately, an evil Jakapo outcast named Batarsi stole the gentle whistler when he fled town in rageful shame. Finding him will be easy—he's set up a vile workshop in an old wizard's tower not far from here. Actually defeating the wicked toymaker, however, will be a different matter."*

It's a wooden figurine of a young galtzagorri in an oversized cloak and floppy hat. It looks fine from a distance. Up close, no galtzagorri is fooled.

## Batarsi's Workshop

> **🎯 Why this scene exists:** This is the chapter's dungeon. The PCs explore an empty tower, then ambush the boss at sunset. Golomor (E3) adds a moral choice.

**Batarsi** is an outcast who fled Jakapo Town in shame. He runs his "toy shop" from an abandoned wizard's tower deep in the woods (p. 61).

**How it runs:**

- **Batarsi and the whistler are out** until **sunset**. The PCs can learn this from the toy spy or E5.

### E3 — Back Room *(Trivial 2)*

**Golomor:** shadow thief (BB 90), Creature 2. Initiative: Perception +7 or Stealth +10. Monster Parts 5/12/22 gp.
- Golomor (they/them) is here to avenge their sibling **Holipok**. Batarsi killed Holipok.
- Golomor isn't evil and starts **indifferent**. Improve their attitude and they may team up for now.
- If the PCs show any sign of getting in Golomor's way, Golomor attacks at once.
- **If befriended,** Golomor gives the PCs a piece of **infused shadow**. It's worth Golomor's full Monster Parts value.
- **XP:** **30 XP** story award for befriending or defeating Golomor. The book notes the fight alone isn't worth combat XP.

**Treasure (without Monster Parts only):** a PC can spend **an hour** sifting through the rot. They find a **low-grade cold iron buckler**, a **hat of disguise**, a **feather step stone**, and a **moderate acid flask**.

> **💡 GM tip:** Golomor wants Batarsi dealt with too. Once befriended, they're a natural ally for the ambush. The book says they "might even agree to temporarily team up." Their role in Event 10 is your call.

### E4 — Workshop *(Trivial 2)*

> *Dolls, stuffed animals, wooden figurines, and other tiny toys fill every nook and cranny of this crowded workshop.*

Batarsi keeps most of his finished toys here. He spends most of his time here too.

**Toy Swarm:** BB 113, Creature 2. Initiative: Perception +7. Monster Parts 5/12/22 gp. When the PCs enter, the toys tumble at them as one. They fight until they're destroyed or the intruders leave the tower.

**If the toy spy survived Event 9,** it's here waiting for its maker. It's worn down, and its valuable parts are broken.

**Weak Toy Spy:** BB 9, 112, Creature 2. Initiative: Perception +7. Monster Parts 5/12/22 gp. With it here, E4 is a **Moderate 2** fight.

**Treasure (without Monster Parts only):** an **onyx dog** wondrous figurine, a **levered crowbar**, and a **hat of the magi**.

### E5 — Quarters *(Trivial 2)*

> *A hay-filled bed sits against the northwestern window. South of that, a blackened wood stove fills most of the western wall. In the southeast section, two dressers turned into worktables flank a stained-glass door to a balcony.*

**Mrs. Sloth:** elite springjaw doll (BB 9, 108), Creature 2. Initiative: Perception +9. **Speed** 20 feet, **fly** 20 feet. Monster Parts 5/12/22 gp.
- Batarsi's most cherished construct: a slightly oversized galtzagorri matron with gray curls, a pudgy flushed face, soft edges, and custom palm-leaf wings.

> **🔒 GM-only:** Batarsi's mother died when he was small, and he never knew her. Mrs. Sloth is his guess at what she was like. A PC who works this out gains a lever on him, or a reason for pity.

### E6 — Balcony

> *This rickety wooden balcony juts out from the rest of the tower about 30 feet above the ground below.*

Batarsi lands his beetle here every night at sundown, unless he has a reason not to. Event 10 likely happens here.

### Event 10 — Sunset Showdown *(Moderate 2)*

By day, Batarsi roams the jungle on **Goosebark**, his silk-tailed beetle. He gathers crafting materials, and nobody knows where. At **sundown** he comes home to craft and rest.

**Creatures:** at sunset Batarsi and Goosebark flutter over the treetops. They land on the **balcony (E6)** unless he suspects someone has gotten into the tower. He's a winged sprite and needs no mount. He rides one out of vanity.

**Batarsi:** Creature 2, unique CE Tiny fey sprite. Male galtzagorri hunter (BB 35).
- **Initiative** Perception +7
- **Items:** basic crafter's book, galtzagorri hook spear, **gentle whistler**, longspear
- **Melee** longspear +8, 1d8+4 piercing
- **Tiny:** he must enter a target's space to use his hook spear. His longspear has 5 feet of reach. With it, he can attack from an adjacent square or from Goosebark's back ([Tiny creatures and reach](#tiny-creatures-reach-and-mounts)).

**Goosebark:** silk-tailed beetle (BB 98), Creature 2. Initiative: Perception +7. **Speed** 35 feet, **fly** 25 feet. Monster Parts 5/12/22 gp.

**Tactics:** Batarsi is a coward. At **15 HP or less** he fakes begging for mercy. Then he makes a surprise attack the moment the PCs relax or turn their backs. He'd rather die than submit to "weaklings" like them.

> **⚠️ Gotcha:** Tell the players he's begging, and let them choose. If they accept, he strikes when they're distracted. If they stay watchful, he gets no opening.

**Aftermath:** the PCs take the whistler from Batarsi, dead or unconscious. Almost nobody in Jakapo Town will mourn him. Next, the PCs collect Kuaranda in Jakapo Town and head to the amphitheater.

### Gentle Whistler *(Item 4)*

Uncommon, enchantment, magical. **Price** 90 gp. **Usage** held in 2 hands. **Bulk** negligible.
- A sleek golden harmonica decorated with swirling patterns like insect wings, legs, and bodies.
- **Activate** (2 actions; envision, Interact). **Frequency** once per day. You play a short, quiet melody that soothes the minds of nearby insects and other bugs. It casts ***calm emotions*** at **DC 18**. Affected animals with an Intelligence modifier of −5 take a **−2 circumstance penalty** to the save.

The item appendix says a Jakapo Town folk hero supposedly played the original. The hero didn't know who made it (p. 379).

> **⚠️ Gotcha (the item versus the song):** The daily activation is a short melody. The amphitheater song is a story event that lasts 5 minutes and covers the whole chasm. Use the amphitheater rules for it.

## Big Show at Aksyanis Amphitheater

- **Where:** a historic amphitheater overlooking the Hissing Chasm. Map on p. 65.
- **Who:** Kuaranda, or whoever plays the whistler.
- **Goal:** keep the player safe for 5 minutes of music.

The amphitheater is named for **Aksyanis**, a famed orpok conductor and polymath. People said her performances were unmatched for order and mathematical precision. No living musician could meet her demands. Weary of the Isles, she left to find otherworldly musicians who could play her songs. She supposedly left Alacar too (p. 65).

**Getting there:** any local can point the way ([Travel distances](#travel-distances)).

**The song:**
- Calming the mantises takes **5 minutes** of **nonstop** whistler playing.
- The music does the **opposite to other wildlife**. It enrages some creatures against its source, the party. Kuaranda **warns the PCs** on the way.
- Predators know calm mantises mean easy food, so they come in **waves**.

**Timing** ([rounds and minutes](#rounds-minutes-and-the-wave-timer)):
- The first wave comes at **3 minutes**.
- Then the PCs defend Kuaranda nonstop for **2 minutes**.
- They get **exactly 2 rounds** to regroup between waves.

**If the song is interrupted:** waves stop and attackers calm down. Depending on the moment, the current creature may fight on or retreat. The PCs must **restart the song**. That resets the 5-minute timer and brings more waves, either the same ones or similar substitutes.

> **⚠️ Gotcha (from the book):** Don't let restarts farm extra XP or treasure (p. 66). Track awards.

| Wave | Threat | Creatures | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Angry Assassin** | Low 2 | Swarm assassin | Enraged by the music |
| **2. Blood Suckers** | Low 2 | 3 weak blood mice | Come from every direction |
| **3. Grotesque Nest** | Moderate 2 | Rottskal | Sneaks up disguised as a bird's nest |
| **4. Beak and Fur** | Moderate 2 (book awards 140 XP) | Parrotbear | Survive 4 rounds |

### Wave 1 — Angry Assassin *(Low 2)*

The music soothes mantises, but it enrages some swarms and even pains them. A **swarm assassin** is on its way to Rumplank, to its next client and target. It hears the music and heads straight for the stage to destroy the source.

**Swarm Assassin:** BB 109, Creature 3. Initiative: Perception +10. Monster Parts 7/18/30 gp.

### Wave 2 — Blood Suckers *(Low 2)*

Mantises are the favorite food of [blood mice](gm_glossary.md#blood-mouse). The mice are furious at what the music does to the mantises. They pour in from every direction.

**Weak Blood Mice (3):** BB 9, 14, Creature 0. Initiative: Perception +6. Monster Parts 2.25/5/9 gp each.

> **🧩 Pays off from Ch 1:** These are the jungle blood mice Pekipak warned about. Music stirs them up, just as he said.

### Wave 3 — Grotesque Nest *(Moderate 2)*

A **rottskal** is a meat-eating aberration that loves eggs. This one has grown fat on stray mantises. The mantises draw the birds its food chain depends on. It senses a threat to its meals and comes to look.
- It **sneaks up**, Striding closer over several rounds. Then it sits still to **Impersonate a bird's nest**.
- It hopes curious PCs come close. That gives it the edge in the first round.
- It attacks all-out if it thinks it's been spotted.

**Rottskal:** p. 393, Creature 4. Initiative: Perception +14 or Deception +14. Monster Parts 12/27/50 gp.

> **💡 GM tip:** There are only 2 rounds between waves. A "bird's nest" that appears at the stage's edge is suspicious. Reward a PC who checks it from a distance.

### Wave 4 — Beak and Fur *(Moderate 2)*

The last attacker is a bloodthirsty **parrotbear** (she/her). She's furious at the chaos in her jungle. The book warns this fight is likely hard for 2nd-level PCs. They haven't rested since the waves began.

**Parrotbear:** BB 72, Creature 4. Initiative: Perception +13. Monster Parts 12/27/50 gp.

**Survive, don't win:**
- Kuaranda is nearly done. The PCs must protect her for **4 rounds**. If your tracked time leaves more than 4 rounds, she finishes early.
- The PCs can tell she's close. Roll a **secret DC 15 Performance** check for each PC trained in Performance. Success reveals exactly how many rounds remain.
- When Kuaranda stops playing, the parrotbear calms down, licks her paws, and lumbers off.
- **Even if most or all PCs fall,** they succeed if Kuaranda goes unattacked for 4 rounds. She then stabilizes the survivors.
- **XP:** the full **140 XP** for lasting until she finishes. The book calls this halfway between Severe and Extreme.
- **Monster Parts:** the full award, even if the parrotbear walks away.

> **⚠️ Gotcha (the label and the award disagree):** **Moderate 2** matches one level-4 creature (80 XP). The text awards **140 XP**, Severe-to-Extreme, because the PCs fight unrested after three waves. Use 140 XP.

### Melody's End

The chasm's mantises calm when all waves fall or **5 minutes** pass, whichever comes first. The PCs can climb down to the Landmark 2 obelisk and follow the map ([The six landmarks](#the-six-landmarks)).

**XP:** **30 XP** each for finishing the melody and clearing out the mantises.
