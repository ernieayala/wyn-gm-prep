## The chapter in one minute

The royal family gives the PCs half a magic treasure map. Rumplank's rumors point to the Iron Eels, who had the other half and whose captain reveals the missing half is on a wreck and the keystone that fuses the halves is in a swamp. The PCs recover both, assemble the map, and watch it start crumbling to ash. Cliffhanger.

**By the end the PCs have:**
1. **Both map halves:** the king's (Scene 1) and the Gasping Gipper's (Scene 5).
2. **The [sapphire scale](gm_glossary.md#sapphire-scale)**, the keystone, from a Writhing Swamp thyalodon (Scene 6).
3. **Assembled the map**, triggering its curse (Scene 7).

**Party level:** 1 at the start, **2** at the end.

> **🎯 Why this chapter exists:** It teaches the city, offers the first companions, and plants returning NPCs: Colvi (secret villain), U'oko (Chapter 4 test answers), Kuaranda and the thyalodon (Chapter 2). It sets up the central irony: Rumplank remembers Poppy as a fun-loving pirate; she was a war hero hiding a demon cult's relic.

## How the chapter is built

Five book parts (p. 14–45):

| Part | What it is | Order |
|---|---|---|
| **Getting Started** | Royal audience: quest, Map Half #1 | First |
| **Running Around Rumplank** | Six optional social scenes, each with a clue and bonus reward | Any order, any number |
| **Event Encounters** | Three floating events | Between visits, as pacing needs |
| **Confronting the Iron Eels** | Small dungeon ending with Melar Kan | After enough clues |
| **Missing Pieces** | Two wilderness dungeons: Gasping Gipper, Writhing Swamp | Either order |

The map is assembled at whichever dungeon the PCs finish last, closing the chapter.

> **💡 GM tip — the redundant-clue pattern:** Every road leads to the Iron Eels: D'marti, the Gutted Snake regulars, Nevergo, Master Olo, the Event 1 goons, Gather Information, the Indigo Stranger. That's the **Three Clue Rule**: plant three or more clues per required conclusion, because players miss some. You don't need every location. Once they say "let's go find the Iron Eels," move on.

> **💡 GM tip — pacing:** The book sets no minimum visits. Two or three locations plus Event 1 usually surface the lead. Run more if the table enjoys the city; each visit earns XP.

## PF2 rules you'll use all chapter

Later sections link here instead of repeating these rules.

### Degrees of success

Every check has four outcomes:
- **Critical success:** beat the DC by 10 or more.
- **Success:** meet or beat the DC.
- **Failure:** miss the DC.
- **Critical failure:** miss the DC by 10 or more.

A natural 20 improves the result one step; a natural 1 worsens it one step. Many entries list all four outcomes, so read the whole entry before ruling.

### Encounter difficulty and XP

The book labels every fight with a threat level and the party level they're built for, like **Low 1** or **Severe 1**. XP math for four PCs:

| Threat | XP budget | XP each PC earns |
|---|---|---|
| Trivial | 40 or less | 40 or less |
| Low | 60 | 60 |
| Moderate | 80 | 80 |
| Severe | 120 | 120 |
| Extreme | 160 | 160 |

Creature cost by level relative to the party:

| Creature level vs. party | −2 | −1 | same | +1 | +2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| XP | 20 | 30 | 40 | 60 | 80 |

Hazards: a **complex** hazard costs the same as a creature of its level; a **simple** one costs a fifth (8 XP at party level).

**Worked examples (party level 1):**
- **Event 1:** two goons (level 0, 30 XP each) = 60 = **Low**.
- **C1 Snake Pit:** leshy husk (30) + snake pit, complex level-2 hazard (60) = 90 = **Moderate**.
- **B7 Haunted Hold:** wispy wayfarer (level 2, 60) + three rotten planks (simple level-1, 8 each) = 84 = **Moderate**.

**PCs level up at 1,000 XP**; the counter then resets. The book says the listed encounters give about the right XP for each level-up. Milestone leveling works too (p. 7); this chapter's milestone is assembling the map.

> **⚠️ Gotcha:** Two fights break the math on purpose. **Event 4**, one level-1 duelist, is Trivial on paper; the book rates it Moderate (80 XP) for the duel conditions. **C6** totals past Severe, but the book keeps it Severe because the confused thyalodon may attack the husks. Use the book's label.

> **📏 Party size:** Budgets assume four PCs. Per PC added or removed, adjust by 20 XP for Moderate (10 Trivial, 15 Low, 30 Severe, 40 Extreme). Each PC still earns the four-PC amount for that threat level.

### Basic saving throws

"DC 15 basic Reflex" is shorthand for:
- **Critical success:** no damage.
- **Success:** half damage.
- **Failure:** full damage.
- **Critical failure:** double damage.

### Secret checks and Recall Knowledge

**Recall Knowledge** recalls a fact, usually with Lore or a knowledge skill like Society or Religion. It's a **secret check**: roll behind the screen. The player can't tell failure ("you don't know") from critical failure, where you tell them something false. The book scripts the false answers, like Scene 1's "Redfeathers are demon worshippers."

### Flat checks

A bare d20, no modifiers. **DC 11 = 50%.** Nevergo's flasks use one.

### Conditions you'll see this chapter

| Condition | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Flat-footed** | −2 circumstance penalty to AC. PF2 Remaster name: **off-guard**; same rule. |
| **Prone** | Flat-footed; −2 circumstance penalty to attack rolls. Standing takes an action. |
| **Clumsy X** | −X status penalty to Dexterity-based checks and DCs, including AC and Reflex. |
| **Dazzled** | Everything is concealed to you: DC 5 flat check to target. |
| **Frightened X** | −X status penalty to all checks and DCs. Drops by 1 at the end of each of your turns. |
| **Sickened X** | −X status penalty to all checks and DCs. You can Retch (1 action) for a Fortitude save to reduce it. |
| **Unconscious / dying** | At 0 HP: unconscious, dying 1 (dying 2 on a critical hit). **Nonlethal** damage knocks out at 0 HP without dying. |

### Movement and terrain

- **Difficult terrain:** +5 feet of movement per square.
- **Greater difficult terrain:** +10 feet per square.
- **Swim / Climb:** Athletics vs. the listed DC. Failure: no progress. Climbing critical failure: a fall.
- **Falling:** bludgeoning equal to half the distance; 20 feet deals 10.
- **Grab an Edge:** reaction when falling past an edge; Reflex vs. the listed DC to catch on.
- **Cover:** standard +2 AC, **greater cover** +4.
- **Overland travel:** slowest Speed 20 covers about 2 miles per hour clear, 1 difficult (p. 37). See the [travel distance table](#travel-from-rumplank).

### Hazards

- **Simple hazard:** one triggered reaction, then done (the Gipper's rotten planks).
- **Complex hazard:** rolls initiative and acts each round on a **routine** (the snake pit).
- **Stealth DC 20 (expert):** only expert Perception notices it; a trained-only party steps on it.
- **Disable DC 15 Crafting (trained):** the check to neutralize it, and the minimum rank to try.

### Monster Parts (treasure system)

The campaign uses the *Battlezoo Bestiary*'s **Monster Parts**, **hybrid** variant by default (p. 7). PCs harvest parts from defeated creatures and spend them to **refine** an item (raising its level and item bonuses) or **imbue** magic properties.

- **"Monster Parts 3.5/7/13 gp"**: light/hybrid/full variant values.
- **"+1 dagger (2)"**: refined to item level 2, +1 to attack rolls.
- **"If your group isn't using Monster Parts"**: replacement treasure to even out gold. Use only if you skip the system.

> **⚠️ Gotcha:** Event 1 is likely the first fight. If you use Monster Parts, remind players to harvest the goons.

### NPC companions

Some NPCs can join (p. 8). Book defaults:
- They tag along but **don't fight or join major events** unless the text says so.
- **Two at a time, maximum**, for four players. Extras wait somewhere safe, like Rumplank.
- Mistreated companions leave.

Chapter 1 offers three: [D'marti](gm_glossary.md#dmarti-tixima) (Scene 2A), the [scroll mold](gm_glossary.md#scroll-mold) (Scene 5), and [Ignatius Barkley](gm_glossary.md#ignatius-barkley) (Scene 6).

## Scene 1 — Castle Rumplank (the hook)

- **Where:** the royal hall, [Castle Rumplank](gm_glossary.md#castle-rumplank).
- **Who:** [King Okeki](gm_glossary.md#king-okeki-redfeather), [Queen Maxelle](gm_glossary.md#queen-maxelle-redfeather), [Prince Kulupi](gm_glossary.md#prince-kulupi), [Princess Atriella](gm_glossary.md#princess-atriella).
- **Goal:** the PCs accept the quest and receive **Map Half #1**.

> **🎯 Why this scene exists:** It unites the party and points it at the city. The book frames the PCs as Prince Kulupi's picks from many applicants.

> **💡 GM tip — the hook:** If "the king asked" isn't enough, the book suggests asking each player, "Why did your character answer the call?" so they own the motivation (p. 7). Let them introduce characters before the read-aloud.

### Read aloud (opening)

> *You find yourselves standing atop a wide swath of velvet carpet that cuts through the center of Castle Rumplank's magnificent royal hall. Nearby, the sweet smell of [Barnacle Bay](gm_glossary.md#barnacle-bay) wafts through the open glass windows. Before you stands a family of four beautiful [g'mayuns](gm_glossary.md#gmayun) in spectacular regal garb, flanked all around by countless royal guards. King Okeki — the family's dignified but slightly distracted-looking patriarch — eyes each of you carefully, then clears his throat.*

The king's speech (p. 15), paraphrased:
- A magical map to **Poppy von Barnacle's hideout** is the talk of the town.
- Royal scholars found a piece of it has hung in this hall for generations.
- Find the other half, follow it to Poppy's sanctum, and claim her legacy for Rumplank.

### Likely questions

- **Why?** History and civic pride. A royal envoy will later collect relics the PCs can't carry.
- **How much treasure do we keep?** Queen Maxelle: *"Why, all of it, dears!"* Rumplank wants glory and a better reputation.
- **How does the other half help?** Princess Atriella: court wizards found powerful magic on the parchment that will make sense only once reunited and repaired, perhaps with a keystone or other magic item.
- **Where to start?** Prince Kulupi suggests the [Temple of Many Colors](gm_glossary.md#temple-of-many-colors), [Quill & Scroll Academy](gm_glossary.md#quill--scroll-academy), or [Treasure Beach](gm_glossary.md#treasure-beach); after a pause, the rumor-filled [Gutted Snake](gm_glossary.md#gutted-snake) in the Claw Quarter. Atriella recommends [Prismatic Colvi](gm_glossary.md#prismatic-colvi) at the temple.

> **🔒 GM-only:** Kulupi is a cult mastermind (revealed in Ch 11). He steers the PCs to the Gutted Snake, the best lead in town; his sister steers them to Colvi, a cultist. Play both as earnest. In Act 3, players looking back will spot it.

### Recall Knowledge (secret check)

After the audience, roll a **secret DC 15 Society or Rumplank Lore** check per PC ([how secret checks work](#secret-checks-and-recall-knowledge)).

- **Critical success:** Success, plus: after building Rumplank into a thriving city, Poppy retired from public life. Her fate is unknown; inventing myths about her is a local pastime.
- **Success:** Rumplank, a lively port on Goldcrop Island's sunny side, is known for rowdy citizens and year-round revelry. The pirate Poppy von Barnacle founded it over 200 years ago.
- **Failure:** Nothing specific.
- **Critical failure:** A confident lie: under the parties, Rumplank is a deadly port of pirates, cultists, and ghosts, and the Redfeathers are secret demon worshippers serving a false deity, Poppy.

> **🧩 Sets up:** Funny because it's partly true: a demon cult operates in Rumplank, and part of the royal family serves it.

### Hand over the map

The king hands it over carefully: *"Be careful with that map; you hold the history of Rumplank in your hands!"*

- A **crude sketch of [Goldcrop Island](gm_glossary.md#goldcrop-island)**, frayed enough that only a close look shows the tear.
- **10 HP**; *mending* repairs it.
- A permanent **6th-level *nondetection*** hid it in plain sight.

## Scene 2 — Running around Rumplank

Six locations, any order: short social scenes, each with a **clue** and a **bonus reward** for impressing the NPC or passing a test (p. 17).

**XP per location, per PC:** **10 XP** for talking, **30 XP** with the bonus reward.

**Event timing (book suggestions):**
- **[Event 1 — Iron Fists](#event-1--iron-fists-low-1)** after the first or second location.
- **[Event 2 — Glitterfest Parade](#event-2--glitterfest-parade-trivial-1)** right before or after the Academy.
- **[Event 3 — The Indigo Stranger](#event-3--the-indigo-stranger-no-combat)** only if they're stuck.

> **💡 GM tip:** A Rumplank-native PC (say, a Player's Guide background) may want an unlisted spot. Move a location's key details there (p. 17). The gazetteer (p. 342) has a city map for players.

> **📏 Gather Information:** Any time, a PC can spend **2 hours** on **DC 15 Diplomacy** to Gather Information on the Iron Eels, or Recall Knowledge with **DC 15 Rumplank or Sailing Lore** or **DC 20 Society**. Results: [What the city knows about the Iron Eels](#what-the-city-knows-about-the-iron-eels).

### 2A. The Gutted Snake (worst bar in town)

Sticky floors, sour grog, bad tempers, best gossip. Barkeep: [Molo "Make-Sick" Dogodor](gm_glossary.md#molo-make-sick-dogodor).

**NPC:** [D'marti Tixima](gm_glossary.md#dmarti-tixima) (LN genderfluid g'mayun ranger 1, they/them): cheerful, scattered jungle explorer with a huge, overstuffed backpack.

**Goal:** the PCs learn the **[Iron Eels](gm_glossary.md#iron-eels)** likely stole the other half.

**What D'marti says** (once the PCs mention Poppy or the map):
- Poppy legends: dueling the amber dragon Helexes, steering the *Chromatic Queen* through a hurricane to win the Nevala Regatta, her comrades the [Fortunate Four](gm_glossary.md#fortunate-four).
- About a week ago they found the other half in an **ancient cherry-wood scroll case** poking out of the ground on a jungle expedition. Where? No idea; lost, as usual.
- They showed it off here, far too noisily, and it was stolen. Whispered: *"Between us, I think it was some of Melar Kan's goons."*
- The map is likely cursed. If so, go to **[Jakapo Town](gm_glossary.md#jakapo-town)**, home of Poppy's wizard friend [Arahosko the Red](gm_glossary.md#arahosko-the-red).

**Leaning on the barkeep:** **DC 17 Intimidation**: Dogodor saw high-ranking Iron Eel **[Squotan Malardy](gm_glossary.md#squotan-malardy)** take the map from D'marti's bags. Malardy hasn't been seen since. Another patron gives the same on a **DC 15 or 17** check in a skill you pick.

**Bonus reward — pack D'marti's backpack:** D'marti can't make it all fit.
- **DC 15 Athletics** to force it, or **DC 17 Survival** to repack it.
- Reward: pick **two**: disguise kit, repair kit, climbing kit, firearm cleaning kit, healer's tools, snare kit.

**D'marti as a companion:** pack the bag and make a good impression, and D'marti offers to join, bringing deep knowledge of Goldcrop Island's plants and animals to a party of outsiders.

> **🧩 Sets up:** The cherry scroll case reappears on the Gasping Gipper (Scene 5): scroll mold infested it and it "walked away" from the Iron Eels. Let PCs who compare notes connect it.

### 2B. Nevergo's Antidotes (back-alley apothecary)

**NPC:** [Captain Nevergo](gm_glossary.md#captain-nevergo): surly Claw Quarter g'mayun, pirate drawl near unintelligible. Messy, understocked shop by day; poison out the back door by night.

**Goal:** a backup route to D'marti. Shown the map, he grumbles that D'marti showed him an identical one a few days ago; try the Gutted Snake.

> **💡 GM tip:** If the PCs already met D'marti, he names other gangs who heard D'marti bragging that night: Uta's Hard Girls, the Red-Bellied Three, Yellow Noxious, *"coulda been them Iron Eels for all I know."*

**Bonus reward — sort his flasks:** he mixed up the wormwood and belladonna.
- **DC 17 Crafting** to Identify Alchemy by taste. A **DC 11 flat check** ([flat checks](#flat-checks)) picks the flask; on a failed flat check it's belladonna: **DC 19 Fortitude save** vs. the poison.
- **Safer option:** a PC who watched him recalls each flask's corner with **DC 20 Perception**.
- **Reward:** the **belladonna** flask (CRB poison). *"Ye gotcher spittle on it, 'sides."*

### 2C. Pekipak's Wagon (the [Twin Talons](gm_glossary.md#twin-talons))

**NPC:** [Sagoya Pekipak](gm_glossary.md#sagoya-pekipak): g'mayun rat catcher and aspiring inventor. A tiny leather helmet lets him talk to rodents; he eats the smart ones.

**The Rodential Respirator ("R.R.")**: a vacuum hose on a leather sack holding an air elemental gem and a fan feather token. His left hand is splinted from the last test; he offers a gold coin to anyone who covers the nozzle.
- **DC 15 Fortitude.** Success: hand stays, not pulled in. Failure: **1d6+2 nonlethal bludgeoning**. Critical failure: also **clumsy 1** until a full night's rest.
- He pays the coin regardless.

**What he tells them:** blood mice swarm in the jungle, and he's heard music can stir up vermin: *"bigger the swarm, bigger the ditty."*

> **🧩 Sets up:** Foreshadows Chapter 2's **Hissing Chasm** (Landmark 2, p. 50), where clearing praying-mantis swarms takes a specific magical instrument. Half right: most songs do nothing.

**Bonus reward — improve the device:**
- **DC 15 Crafting** or **DC 13 Engineering Lore** to make it safe.
- Critical success: it needs only the gem, so he gives the PCs the **fan feather token**.

### 2D. [Temple of Many Colors](gm_glossary.md#temple-of-many-colors)

**NPC:** [Prismatic Colvi](gm_glossary.md#prismatic-colvi), high priest: gracious, respected, vain.

**Goal:** the PCs learn the map needs a **keystone** and will **self-destruct** once assembled.

**What Colvi says** after studying the map a few minutes:
- A specific magical key fuses the halves. He doesn't know its look; it's likely with the missing half.
- Once whole, the map carries **disintegrating magic**. Read it fast.

> **🔒 GM-only:** Colvi is a demon worshipper and powerful member of the [Cult of the Shivering Behemoth](gm_glossary.md#cult-of-the-shivering-behemoth), which Poppy nearly destroyed 200 years ago; a major Act 3 villain (p. 218 on). The PCs can't expose him yet, so don't hint. **Note how each PC treats him:** the book suggests tying those moments into his finale fight, and his Chapter 12 dialogue depends on them.

**Bonus reward — name three lava-rock statues:** acolytes haul three rough statues up from the catacombs; which gods are they?
- Any PC **trained in Religion**: **DC 18 Religion** to Recall Knowledge, **once per statue**.
- **Winged** (chochori-sized, sharp-tipped wings): demon lord [Pazuzu](gm_glossary.md#pazuzu).
- **G'mayun on three legs** (one may be a cane): the [Sibling of the Past](gm_glossary.md#sibling-of-the-past).
- **Orpok-sized, big belly:** [Ebrugeses](gm_glossary.md#ebrugeses), god of good food.
- **Name two or more:** **four common bludgeoning weapons** of the PCs' choice plus **one +1 weapon (2)** (any common CRB weapon of their choice). Colvi warns many undead roam the island.

> **🔒 GM-only — the one tell:** Colvi knows the winged statue is Pazuzu and feigns ignorance. If a PC names it, he has "the cursed thing" taken back downstairs: the only crack in his act. Play it as calm piety.

> **🧩 Sets up:** Bludgeoning beats the Gasping Gipper's skeletons, which resist piercing and slashing.

### 2E. Master Olo's Beach Fighting Camp

**NPC:** [Master Olo](gm_glossary.md#master-olo): squat old orpok martial-arts master in white monk robes (*lami*), drilling three bright blue [chochori](gm_glossary.md#chochori) students on any Rumplank beach he likes, sunrise to sunset.

**Goal:** another Iron Eels pointer. A few days ago an Iron Eel clutching a map like the PCs' ran across the beach at Ironsalt Cove; Olo landed *"a warning blow."* A gang patch marked him, and Olo guesses he ran between two points in the Claw Quarter.

> **🔒 GM-only:** The runner was Squotan Malardy, taking the stolen half from the Gutted Snake to the hideout.

**Optional fight — two Flying Pig Disciples:** sparring with two students; declining costs nothing.
- **Stats:** weak chochori wardens (BB 9, p. 386), Creature 1.
- Fists only (**nonlethal**), no tridents, at a disadvantage on land.

**Bonus reward — beat both with no PC knocked unconscious:** Olo offers to teach **Flying Pig Stance** and **Flying Pig Rush** (p. 378). A PC can **retrain** a feat into Flying Pig Stance on the spot.

> **📏 Rules refresher:** Two level-1 foes vs. a level-1 party = 80 XP, Moderate. Tough for a first fight, nonlethal or not.

### 2F. Quill & Scroll Academy

**NPC:** [Headmistress U'oko](gm_glossary.md#headmistress-uoko): busy old g'mayun scholar, door always open, stuck on a petrified-wood puzzle cube, a gift from Professor A'aran. Students and alumni get more of her time.

**Goal:** lore on Poppy's companions, the [Fortunate Four](gm_glossary.md#fortunate-four). She also confirms the map's self-destructive magic, as Colvi did.

**What she says about each companion:**
- **[Arahosko the Red](gm_glossary.md#arahosko-the-red)** ("Shellfire"): matchless evoker, the crew's only Goldcrop native, born in the galtzagorri mushroom village of Jakapo. Poppy's chief cartographer; U'oko is sure she made this map. Joke: the only way to beat her was to **dress her in blue**, her least favorite color.
- **[Jesea Swiftarrow](gm_glossary.md#jesea-swiftarrow)** ("the Rugged Ranger"): unbeatable hardriggan archer with an **X-shaped scar on her jawline, below her left ear**, origin never told.
- **[Pinomeri Elemarte](gm_glossary.md#pinomeri-elemarte)** ("the Drifting Cleric"): Poppy's pious indigo dragon, an Ebrugeses worshipper who rarely refused a drink. *"Rum, wine, ale. He loved them all in equal measure."*
- **[Undayo of Stonegulch](gm_glossary.md#undayo-of-stonegulch)** ("Old Rock"): kragrak barbarian who respected only raw strength; earning his trust meant **beating him one-on-one**.

> **🧩 Sets up:** These four facts answer Poppy's final test at **G25 in Chapter 4** (p. 105). Note them or hand players a note.

**Bonus reward — solve the puzzle cube:**
- A PC **trained in Thievery**: **DC 15 Thievery** to Disable a Device. The cube opens; U'oko happily eats the luluki nut inside.
- **Reward:** three 1st-level scrolls: ***detect poison***, ***jump***, and ***mending***.

> **🧩 Sets up:** Save the *mending* scroll. On the assembled map (Scene 7) it restores no HP; it halts decay for hours equal to the HP it would restore, about 5 hours for a typical cast (Chapter 2, p. 48).

> **Trigger:** Consider running **[Event 2 — Glitterfest Parade](#event-2--glitterfest-parade-trivial-1)** as they leave.

## Scene 3 — Street events

Between locations, in order, wherever pacing wants them (p. 25).

### Event 1 — Iron Fists *(Low 1)*

**When:** after the PCs ask about the map at one or two locations; best in the Claw Quarter.

Melar Kan sends his two lowest-ranking g'mayun pirates, **Crackmaw** and **Litzi**, to learn what the PCs know. If they learn the PCs carry the other half, they fight for it.
- **Iron Eel Goons (2):** BB 34, Creature 0. Initiative: Intimidation +5.
- **Morale:** a goon at **5 HP or less** flees down an alley; when one drops or flees, the other runs.
- **Treasure:** Monster Parts for refining slashing or piercing weapons, 2.25/5/9 gp per goon ([what the numbers mean](#monster-parts-treasure-system)).

**If questioned:** no boss plans without magic, but they admit they're after Poppy's treasure. Crackmaw taunts the PCs and dares them to meet the boss at the **hideout near [Ironsalt Cove](gm_glossary.md#ironsalt-cove)**.

> **🎯 Why this scene exists:** Likely the first fight: a safe Low encounter for learning the action economy, and a backup Iron Eels lead.

> **💡 GM tip:** If the PCs dawdle, the book suggests more goons bully them until they head for the hideout.

### Event 2 — [Glitterfest](gm_glossary.md#glitterfest) Parade *(Trivial 1)*

**When:** right before or after the Academy (parades stay near Barrel Square and Rosewater). For a fight-hungry table, run it before Master Olo or Event 1.

Read aloud:

> *Bits of confetti and glitter waft on the breeze, sticking to your clothes and clouding your vision, before the merrymakers responsible for the mess appear from around a corner. Dozens of Rumplankers dance, sing, and laugh as they march down the street, oblivious to their surroundings. It's impossible not to get swept up in the revelers' good cheer — or their quickening pace.*

**Merry Marchers** — each PC holds ground or keeps up:
- **Hold ground:** DC 17 Fortitude or Athletics.
- **Keep up:** DC 17 Reflex or Performance.
- **DC 15 Party Lore** can replace either.
- **Failure:** 1d4 nonlethal bludgeoning; glitter on their clothes.
- **Critical failure:** also glitter in the face: **dazzled** 1 minute or until an Interact action cleans it.

**Then the toogtoog spill.** The parade upends an elderly monger's cart; dozens of hungry [toogtoogs](gm_glossary.md#toogtoog) hop toward the glitter.
- **Toogtoog Spill:** p. 403, Creature 1. Monster Parts 3.5/7/13 gp.
- They swarm glittered PCs, or an innocent reveler if none.
- **Peaceful option:** calm them with no kills (Wild Empathy, say) and the monger pays the Monster Parts value.

> **📏 Rules refresher — Wild Empathy:** druid and ranger feature: Diplomacy to Make an Impression on animals.

### Event 3 — [The Indigo Stranger](gm_glossary.md#indigo-stranger) *(no combat)*

## Scene 4 — [Iron Eels'](gm_glossary.md#iron-eels) Hideout

> **🎯 Why this scene exists:** First real dungeon, with linked rooms: fleeing goons raise alarms, noise draws the next room. It ends in a social choice (duel or brawl), and either way the PCs learn what sends them into the wilderness.

### What the city knows about the Iron Eels

For **DC 15 Diplomacy** (Gather Information), or **DC 15 Rumplank or Sailing Lore** or **DC 20 Society** (Recall Knowledge) (p. 27).
- **Critical success:** Success, plus: their base is an **abandoned rum distillery** in the Claw Quarter, and the gang is named for Melar Kan's weapon, a giant eel skeleton cast in iron on an iron chain.
- **Success:** One of Rumplank's more successful, more despicable pirate gangs. Melar Kan is cruel and calculating and encourages petty crime ashore. They're often at sea on their white sloop, the ***[Sharpened Bone](gm_glossary.md#sharpened-bone)***.
- **Failure:** Nothing noteworthy.
- **Critical failure:** They're secret assassins for beloved bartender Clamardinia Wakamarsis, hiding in the catacombs under Quill & Scroll Academy, and Melar Kan is a devil in disguise.

### Questioning defeated Iron Eels

Each questioned pirate gives **one**:
- First Mate Squotan Malardy recently vanished in the Writhing Swamp with *"some really important dragon scale."* With another loss at the Gasping Gipper, Melar Kan is furious.
- Melar Kan likes bloodshed but loves gambling and deals more. He rarely plays fair and exploits loopholes.
- Most of the crew wants to sail; settle things soon or the gang may be gone for months.
- With Malardy gone, chef **Clancy** is Melar Kan's favorite.
- Melar Kan heard the Redfeathers gave the other half to adventurers and sent goons to scout them.

### The building

An abandoned distillery off Ironsalt Cove's shore, near Rum Row: 10-foot moat, walls patched with salvaged hulls, a pine mast and crow's nest flying the eel-skull flag.

**Hideout features:** crumbling masonry outside; wood-slat walls and floors inside; ceilings barely 8 feet; **all doors unlocked**; dim light through windows and cracks.

### A5 — Clancy's magical gumbo

Melar Kan's lifelong friend, as handy with a knife on a gangplank as in the kitchen. Caught in the galley, he claims to be a simple cook, then throws the pot.
- **Stats:** LE male orpok olfamancer, p. 390, Creature 1.
- **Hurled gumbo:** 15-foot cone, **1d4 fire**, **DC 15 basic Reflex** ([basic saves](#basic-saving-throws)). Any creature that fails or critically fails is also exposed to the gumbo's magic.
- **Magical gumbo:** **DC 15 Fortitude** (1st-level transmutation). Drinking a lot on purpose is an automatic critical failure.
  - **Failure:** stone fists for **1 hour**: **clumsy 1**; the fist loses nonlethal and rolls **2 damage dice** if it rolled 1.
  - **Critical failure:** same, **24 hours**.
- **Treasure:** the greenish lump on the cutting board: calcite (1 Bulk, near worthless) coated in **yaramite**, **10 gp** to a collector.

> **💡 GM tip:** A monk or unarmed fighter may drink on purpose: two fist dice for 24 hours for clumsy 1. A fair trade; allow it.

### A7 — Riupito and Squints

- **Riupito:** female g'mayun pirate, BB 34, Creature 0.
- **Squints:** blood mouse, BB 14, Creature 1. Monster Parts 3.5/7/13 gp.

### A9 — Malardy's old room and the Living Divination Deck

After Malardy left, the crew took turns in his bed and graffitied the walls: "Duko" everywhere, rude Redfeather caricatures, and stunning murals of Melar Kan fighting a giant squid and a skeleton horde.
- **DC 20 Perception** or **DC 15 Art Lore**: matching paint shows illiterate Duko did the scrawl and the murals; Clancy drew the caricatures.
- **Living Divination Deck** (BB 56, Creature 1): in a fine leather case under the mattress. Poses as cards, then attacks moments later, impatient like its old owner.
- **Optional:** moody, as likely to talk as fight. Befriended, it gives a card worth its normal Monster Parts value.
- **XP:** **30 XP** story award either way (a lone Trivial encounter gives none).

### A11 → A14 — [Melar Kan](gm_glossary.md#melar-kan)

A well-read scoundrel: evenings of philosophy and tactics, keeping only the brutal lessons. Brandy decanter always full, hidden dagger always sharp.

**Melar Kan** — Creature 1, unique LE Small g'mayun humanoid (pirate captain), p. 32
- **Perception** +8; low-light vision, truecolor sight
- **Skills:** Acrobatics +5, Athletics +7, Deception +6, Intimidation +6, Thievery +7
- **AC** 16; **Fort** +10, **Ref** +7, **Will** +4; **HP** 20; **Speed** 25 ft
- **Melee** fist +7 (agile, nonlethal), 1d4+4 bludgeoning
- **Melee** *+1 dagger* +8 (agile, finesse, magical, versatile S), 1d4+4 piercing
- **Melee** spiked chain +7 (disarm, finesse, trip), 1d8+4 slashing
- **Ranged** *+1 dagger* +8 (magical, thrown 10 ft), 1d4+4 piercing
- **Kick 'Em While They're Down** (free action, once per round). **Requirement:** his last action was a successful Trip against an adjacent creature. **Effect:** he makes a fist Strike against the tripped creature. It counts toward his multiple attack penalty.

> **📏 Rules refresher — his combo:** Trip (1 action) with the chain. On a success the target is **prone** and flat-footed ([conditions](#conditions-youll-see-this-chapter)), and Kick 'Em adds a free fist Strike at a −4 multiple attack penalty (Trip has the attack trait; the fist is agile). Two actions remain. Expect a trip-and-stomp.

**How he reacts:**
- **PCs storm in:** he fights and calls his crew; if the lower floors are cleared, only the two A12 goons come.
- **Surrender:** once his minions are down **and** he's at **10 HP or less**, he lays down his weapons.
- **Careful approach, or he hears them:** he spreads his hands: *"Come now. Let's sort this out like sensible people, alright?"* and proposes a duel under "the pirate's code." Refusal or stalling: he tsks and attacks with his goons.

> **🎯 Why he offers a duel:** He wants the PCs alive: with their half, and their labor recovering the rest, Poppy's treasure is his. A party that beat his crew downstairs would win a fair fight, so a rigged duel is his best bet.

### Event 4 — Duel atop the Crow's Nest *(Moderate 1, 80 XP)*

**Terms**, explained at the base of the mast:
- One PC duels Melar Kan **"to the grim"**: until one duelist surrenders and begs mercy. *"If you should fall dead before you're able to surrender, well... you should have spoken up earlier."*
- **Hand-to-hand.** Weapons go in a chest at the base; count to three at the top.
- **No one else may come up** to the nest.
- **PC wins:** he reveals where the missing half and keystone are.
- **Melar Kan wins:** the PCs surrender maps and gear and serve the Iron Eels **two months**.

**The hidden knife:**
- A ***+1 dagger (2)*** (mother-of-pearl handle, serrated blade) is stuck point-first under the crow's nest.
- **DC 18 Perception** to notice, **DC 23 Perception** from the rooftop.
- A climbing PC can palm it with **DC 18 Thievery**; if no PC takes it, **Melar Kan palms it** on his way up.
- Without it, he has only fists.

**All's fair below the nest:** goons hurl insults and rocks at his opponent.
- The nest's **greater cover** (+4 AC) against attacks from below means rocks almost never land.
- Each goon tries to **Demoralize** (Intimidation +5) the duelist.
- Legal, Melar Kan notes: they stay below the nest.
- Up to six goons, depending on earlier losses; two (from the balcony) is likeliest.

> **📏 Rules refresher — Demoralize:** Intimidation vs. Will DC. Success: frightened 1; critical success: frightened 2 ([conditions](#conditions-youll-see-this-chapter)). The target is then immune to that goon's Demoralize for 10 minutes.

> **💡 GM tip — the loophole goes both ways:** The rules don't stop other PCs from attacking the goons below, or Melar Kan, from off the nest. Reward it; it's a pirate's code.

**Running the duel:** normal combat or the *Pathfinder Gamemastery Guide* dueling subsystem. The book says he's much deadlier here than in a normal fight, even without cheating; hence Moderate for one level-1 foe.

> **⚠️ Gotcha — don't kill Melar Kan by accident:** The book says at 0 HP he falls unconscious and starts dying as normal, so he survives to talk and maybe recur as a villain. If the PCs kill him anyway, **his journal in A11** has everything below.

**Background special effect:** an **indigo pirate** PC who takes the challenge and wins fast earns a name in pirate circles; a few surviving goons offer to crew for them if they get a ship. The *Gamemastery Guide*'s Leadership rules can track this.

---

### A Deal's a Deal — what Melar Kan reveals

He keeps his word, duel or brawl. His journal can deliver this instead.

1. **The map half is on the [Gasping Gipper](gm_glossary.md#gasping-gipper)**, a wreck beached on Goldcrop's southern coast. The undead rumors were true; he lost recruits and the map (*"it walked away, believe it or not"*), but found what he came for: the sapphire scale.
2. **The [sapphire scale](gm_glossary.md#sapphire-scale) is the keystone**, somewhere around the [Writhing Swamp](gm_glossary.md#writhing-swamp). Slogging home from the Gipper through *"that nasty bog to the southeast,"* **Squotan Malardy** stole it and has surely sold it; the only buyers out there are the galtzagorris of [Jakapo Town](gm_glossary.md#jakapo-town).
3. **The map is likely cursed.** Poppy always tested her students; the curse likely triggers on reassembly, and *"you'd have to find the map's maker — or someone like her — to undo such a curse."*

**XP:** **30 XP** story award per PC, plus combat XP.

> **🔒 GM-only — what happened:** Poppy shipped the sapphire scale on the Gipper 200 years ago; it wrecked, and the scale sat aboard for centuries. Old shipping ledgers led D'marti to guess it was still there. Malardy overheard, stole D'marti's map half for Melar Kan, and the Eels raided the wreck. They got the scale, but scroll mold infested the case holding their half and it wandered into the ship. Malardy then stole the scale and sold it to a passing galtzagorri herder, leaving Melar Kan with nothing.

### If the PCs lose

They wake next morning in **A8**, bandaged and battered, mopping floors for supper; Melar Kan has no use for them dead. Ways out:
- They overhear Melar Kan planning his return to the Gasping Gipper.
- They win over the unhappy crew and start a mutiny.
- **Last resort:** the Indigo Stranger or Prince Kulupi rescues them.

## Scene 5 — The [Gasping Gipper](gm_glossary.md#gasping-gipper)

- **Travel:** 6 hours from Rumplank.
- **Goal:** get **Map Half #2** from the **scroll mold** in the cargo deck.

A g'mayun pirate caravel a freak storm wrecked on the rocks about 200 years ago. Most crew swam ashore; the captain rode out the crash and now haunts it.

> **🎯 Why this scene exists:** A haunted-ship crawl with a twist: the map half is (sort of) alive and runs. Introduces Poppy's magic leaving pieces of people behind.

### Ship features

- **Doors:** thin rotten wood (Hardness 5, HP 20, BT 10).
- **Lower deck (B4–B7):** 6-foot ceilings; taller creatures duck, unslowed. Dim light through hull cracks.
- **Below the waterline:** **greater difficult terrain** ([terrain rules](#movement-and-terrain)), or a PC with a swim Speed or confidence in water can Swim (**DC 12 Athletics**, for the tides). B5's 15-foot-deep **port side** is swim-only.

### Hazard — Rotten Planks *(Hazard 1, simple)*

Two in B2, three in B7, on the map's dotted squares (p. 36).
- **Stealth DC 20 (expert)**: needs **expert Perception** to spot.
- **Disable:** **DC 15** Crafting or Thievery, **trained**, to brace the plank.
- **AC** 16; **Fort** +5, **Ref** +10; Hardness 3, HP 16 (BT 8); weakness fire 3.
- **Break Plank (reaction).** **Trigger:** a Small or larger creature steps on the square. **Effect:** **2d6+5 slashing**, **DC 17 basic Reflex**. Failure: flat-footed until the start of its next turn. Critical failure: **prone** instead. Either way, the square becomes difficult terrain.

> **⚠️ Gotcha:** Most level-1 parties will step on these; that's the design. Damage is high for level 1, so watch low-HP casters.

### B1 — Rocky Shoreline

No encounter. Skeletons visibly shuffle on deck, and the PCs get a sense of the layout.

### B2 — Upper Deck *(Trivial 1)*

**Skeletal Iron Eels (2)** — Creature −1, NE Small mindless skeleton undead, p. 38. Iron Eels killed by the wispy wayfarer and raised as its minions.
- **Perception** +4; darkvision. **Skills:** Acrobatics +5, Athletics +4.
- **AC** 15; **Fort** +3, **Ref** +8, **Will** +3; **HP** 5 (negative healing)
- **Immunities:** death effects, disease, mental, paralyzed, poison, unconscious. **Resistances:** cold, electricity, fire, piercing, and slashing 5.
- **Speed** 25 ft. **Melee** talon +6 (agile), 1d4+2 slashing plus **bone ticks**.
- **Bone Ticks** (disease), **DC 14 Fortitude**. Stage 1: carrier, no effect (1d4 hours). Stage 2: clumsy 1 (1 day). Stage 3: sickened 1 and clumsy 1 (1 day). Stage 4: sickened 1 and clumsy 2 (1 day). Sickened doesn't fade on its own until the disease is cured.

> **📏 Rules refresher — resistance:** "Resistance slashing 5" subtracts 5 per slashing hit; with piercing resisted too, most level-1 slashing and piercing hits deal a point or two. **Bludgeoning isn't resisted**, so Colvi's maces and the B4 warhammer matter.

- **The grate:** the rusty mid-deck grate lifts off easily; 6-foot drop to **B6**. The glint below: a springjaw doll's glass eye.
- **Captain's door (to B3):** a table is jammed against it. **DC 20 Athletics** to Force Open.

### B3 — Captain's Quarters

- **The clue:** the desk drawer holds the ship's **manifest**. A 200-plus-year-old entry lists a package for Poppy von Barnacle: a **"fine wooden scroll case, cherry,"** bound for **[Moonshadow Island](gm_glossary.md#moonshadow-island)**, crated with small items in the **cargo deck (B5)**.
- **The hole:** collapsed floor opens into the hold, **B7**, where the wispy wayfarer lies on the footlocker posing as a ragged sheet.
  - Spotting it: a **Seek** action, **DC 22 Perception**, and low-light vision, darkvision, or a light source to see into the dim hold.
- **Treasure (without Monster Parts only):** lesser juggernaut mutagen, oil of weightlessness, and full plate in fine condition despite the damp.

> **⚠️ Gotcha — a continuity snag in the book:** The manifest makes the cherry scroll case Moonshadow cargo. Elsewhere the book says Poppy shipped the *sapphire scale* here in a sealed chest and D'marti found a cherry case in the jungle. The book never reconciles these. The clue exists to send PCs to B5; run it as written.

> **🧩 Sets up:** First mention of Moonshadow Island, where Poppy hid another jewel fragment. The PCs visit it in Chapters 6–7.

### B4 — Crew's Quarters

No encounter. A half-flooded cabin, sodden pallets.
- **Treasure under the pallets:** ***+1 warhammer (2)***, 2 lesser bottled lightning, 2 light hammers, all good against the undead and constructs aboard.

### B5 — Cargo Deck *(Trivial 1)* — the scroll case is here

Flooded, mold-covered, an eerie burbling in the water.

**Scroll Mold** — BB 86, Creature 0. Initiative: Perception +6 or Stealth +8. Athletics +0 (+4 to Swim). Monster Parts 2.25/5/9 gp.
- **Finding it:** Seek + **DC 18 Perception** spots the scroll case bobbing in the SE corner.
- **Once spotted**, it casts *longstrider* and swims for the prow.

**How it behaves (p. 40):**
- A harmless prankster giving the PCs a ship tour. It spends **all its actions fleeing**.
- Casts attack spells **only alongside other monsters** (B6 dolls, B7 wayfarer).

- **Getting the map:** take the case and Interact to unstopper it; the parchment is dry. With the scale in hand, they can assemble the map now ([Scene 7](#scene-7--assembling-the-map)).
- **XP:** **30 XP** story award per PC for cornering it. A Trivial creature alone is worth less, but the chase can drag PCs into other fights.

> **🔒 GM-only — who's in the mold:** The map's magic put a fragment of the **map maker's** consciousness, **[Arahosko the Red](gm_glossary.md#arahosko-the-red)**, into the mold. Befriended, it shows hazy **towering mushrooms** and makes a sound of **buzzing wings**. **DC 14 Indigo Isles Geography Lore** or **DC 17 Society** identifies Jakapo Town and flying galtzagorris: where to cure the curse.

**Optional companion:** treated kindly, with fewer than two companions in the party, it may "ask" to join. More trouble than it's worth; a possible mascot.

## Scene 6 — The [Writhing Swamp](gm_glossary.md#writhing-swamp)

- **Travel:** 4 hours from Rumplank.
- **Goal:** get the **sapphire scale**, tied to a thyalodon's forehead at **C6**.

Miles of briny muck an inch underwater even in the dry season, snakes everywhere. Only Jakapo Town's galtzagorris come here, flying over it.

> **🎯 Why this scene exists:** A story told through rooms: a survivor, an empty tree, and a new "god" reveal the Embersand leshies' fate. Also the chapter's hardest fight.

**Finding the ruins:** directed from Jakapo Town or wandering, the PCs reach **C1**, a break in the trees toward suspicious ruins. If players doubt it, their characters hear a distressed **thyalodon bleating** to the north.

### Stopping at Jakapo Town first?

Melar Kan's suspicion about Malardy may send the PCs to Jakapo Town first. Fine.
- Herder [**Kuaranda**](gm_glossary.md#kuaranda) bought a pricey dragon scale from a traveling g'mayun, unaware it was magic or stolen, and made it a head ornament for her favorite thyalodon, now missing.
- Her detailed directions lead to **C1**.
- **Don't run Events 5–8 yet.** They're Chapter 2, for when the PCs return with the finished map. Elder S'matzia is away on an errand.

### The backstory (read before running)

> **🔒 GM-only:** The **Embersand** clan of wildfire leshies lived here. While Ignatius Barkley was off exploring, a **dryad husk named Pyksara** crawled from the C4 gnarled tree and corrupted them all into [leshy husks](gm_glossary.md#leshy-husk). Sick of their adoration, Pyksara went north to corrupt other swamps. The husks adopted a wandering **venomous snakedead** as their new idol, tied it to the C5 statue so it couldn't leave too, and feed it sacrifices. The biggest is underway: a thyalodon at C6.

### C1 — Snake Pit *(Moderate 1)*

High ground winds through trees and boulders toward the ruins; thick foliage keeps the PCs on the path.

**Leshy Husk (1)** — BB 44, Creature 0. Initiative: Perception +3. Monster Parts 2.25/5/9 gp. South-facing lookout, distracted by the prisoner; notices the PCs only within **30 feet** of the pit.

**Snake Pit** *(complex environmental trap, Hazard 2)* — already sprung, easy to find.
- **Stealth** +8 (trained) to hear the snakes, or +0 once the branches are gone.
- A 20-foot pit under branches, full of dozens of frightened, nonvenomous, sharp-fanged snakes.
- **Disable:** **DC 14 Survival or Thievery** clears the branches unbitten. The snakes stop only if killed, or lifted out by some means with **DC 17 Nature** (trained) to keep them calm.
- **AC** 18; **Fort** +10, **Ref** +5.
- **Branches:** Hardness 3, HP 15 (BT 7).
- **Snakes:** HP 20; immune to precision and swarm mind; resistance physical 6 (except bludgeoning); weakness area damage 3, splash damage 3.
- **Pitfall (reaction).** **Trigger:** a creature walks onto the branches. **Effect:** it falls in and takes falling damage (10 bludgeoning for 20 feet). It can Grab an Edge (DC 20). The trap then **rolls initiative**.
- **Routine (1 action):** each creature in the pit takes **1d10+4 piercing**, **DC 20 basic Reflex**. Once the snakes are killed or freed, the routine stops.
- **Reset:** the pit stays open until someone covers it with new branches.

> **📏 Rules refresher — complex hazards:** It acts on its own initiative each round ([hazard rules](#hazards)), so a fallen PC takes 1d10+4 every round until they climb out or the snakes are handled. Swarm weaknesses make area spells and splash weapons (alchemist's fire) the fast answer.

**In the pit — [Ignatius Barkley](gm_glossary.md#ignatius-barkley)** (LG male wildfire leshy fighter 1), stripped of gear. His thorny branches block the snakes and his burning body unsettles them, but he won't last forever. He'd rather talk once out.
- The last Embersand: he came home for a hero's welcome and found his kin turned to husks. *"I'll as soon smash every last one of my ghoulish old 'pals' before I let them turn me into a blighted monstrosity!"*
- **Companion:** once out, offers to join if the PCs have fewer than two companions. Noble and capable; won't tolerate evildoers and leaves at the first sign of corruption. Life goal: find and destroy whatever corrupted his people.
- **Story XP:** **30 XP** per PC for rescuing him.

> **💡 GM tip — going around C1:** If the PCs skip C1 or scout the swamp's edge, two C3 husks on patrol find them and, if the husks escape, warn the rest. After this patrol fight, remove those two from C3.

### C2 — Ruined Columns

No encounter. Barkley's backpack lies among the fallen columns, no check needed. If rescued, he gives the PCs all of it: trail rations, a bedroll, a lesser antidote, a lesser alchemist's fire, and five tindertwigs.

### C3 — Lookout Ledge *(Moderate 1)*

Squat stone ruin a little under 10 feet high, stairs on the east, good view west.
- **Leshy Husks (2)** (BB 44, Creature 0) watch west.
- **Skeletal Iron Eels (2)** (p. 38, Creature −1, Initiative Perception +5) flank the stairs facing east.
- If either pair spots the PCs: an unholy scream, and all four attack.
- Patrol already fought: only the two skeletons remain.

### C4 — Gnarled Tree

No encounter. A dead gum tree, face-like shadows in its bark, its trunk rotted hollow by black fungus and caked in slime.
- **DC 15 Nature:** the fungus is a **dryad husk's** remains.
- Anyone who looks inside sees deep **claw gouges**: something crawled *out*.
- Barkley guesses it corrupted his people. Correct: Pyksara.

> **🧩 Sets up:** Pyksara is still out there, north of the swamp: a target for Barkley's personal quest.

### C5 — Honored One *(Moderate 1)*

Shattered stone statue, thick rope around its base.
- **Wisps** — leshy husk (BB 44, Creature 0), Barkley's *"my main mate, Wisps."* Kneels praying in Sylvan to the snakedead; deaf to nearby fighting, but snaps out of it on seeing anyone within **20 feet**.
- **Venomous Snakedead** — BB 118, Creature 2. Monster Parts 5/12/22 gp. Tied to the statue on a **10-foot** leash.
- **Untying it:** spotting the PCs, Wisps frantically unties it: **4 Interact actions**, about a round and a half.
- Freed, it attacks anything but leshy husks, until destroyed.
- If an escaped C3 lookout warned Wisps, the snakedead **starts untied**.

> **💡 GM tip:** Those 4 actions are a race. Rush Wisps, or stay out of the snakedead's reach while the leash holds, and the fight can get much easier.

### C6 — Sacrificial Animal *(Severe 1)* — the sapphire scale is here

Broad stone platform 5 feet high, blood-stained marble altar, rusty ceremonial dagger. Two husks smear a limp, mewing **thyalodon** with foul herbal paste; an ornament on its forehead glows faint blue through the muck: the sapphire scale.

**Creatures:**
- **Leshy Husks (2)** — BB 44, Creature 0.
- **Thyalodon** — p. 402, Creature 3. Initiative: Perception +8. Monster Parts 7/18/30 gp.

**The confused thyalodon:**
- The scale's stray magic upsets and confuses it; violence worsens it. Unsure who's a friend, it **attacks PCs and husks at random**.
- **Removing the scale** calms it at once:
  - **DC 22 Athletics** to wrestle it off a hostile thyalodon.
  - **No check** if it's unconscious first.
- While it wears the scale, calming it (Nature, or Diplomacy with Wild Empathy) takes a **−2 circumstance penalty**.

> **⚠️ Gotcha — the hardest fight in the chapter:** Two levels above the party, it hits harder and more often than anything yet. The book's Severe rating assumes the PCs kill the husks first or get unlucky and face it alone. Its random targeting is their best friend. Tell players it's lashing out at everything, steering them to the scale instead of the kill.

**The thyalodon is Kuaranda's missing pet,** the herd's runt. She hoped the shiny ornament would earn it better treatment; it made things worse, and the scale's magic sent it wandering into the swamp, where the husks caught it.

> **🧩 Sets up:** Return it alive to Kuaranda in Jakapo Town for a friend for life (**Event 7 in Chapter 2**, p. 57). She's also a potential companion there.

## Scene 7 — Assembling the Map

With **both halves and the sapphire scale**, the PCs hold the halves together and wave the scale over the torn edges. The book's narration (p. 45), paraphrased:

- The parchment glows golden, swelling into a **blinding flash**.
- Two faded scraps become one **seamless, slightly cryptic treasure map**.
- **Six points of light** dot it like a tiny constellation: a path from **Jakapo Town to the base of Shivering Mountain**.
- The **sapphire scale** now glows faint gold, pulsing slowly.

**Then the curse hits:**
- After a moment's celebration, the corners fray.
- Before their eyes, the map is **turning to ash**.
- To reach Poppy's treasure, they must stop its magic fast.

**Cliffhanger.** End the session. The PCs **level up to 2**.

> **💡 GM tip:** Short of 1,000 XP? The book suggests a story award for working out the assembly, or extra social scenes before Chapter 2. The **Gilded Treasure Map handout** (p. 46–47) rewards impatient players.

> **⚠️ Gotcha:** The assembled map has 20 HP and loses 1 per hour. *Mending* pauses decay for a few hours but can't end it. Only Jakapo Town's galtzagorris, led by elder **S'matzia** (Arahosko's descendant), can lift the curse (Chapter 2). The mold's mushroom images and D'marti's advice point there.

### Sapphire Scale *(Item 3)*

Rare, divination, magical. **Price** 50 gp. **Usage** held in 1 hand. **Bulk** negligible.
- A palm-sized, translucent scale, said to be cut from a dragon's hide.
- **Passive:** on a map, sea chart, or atlas it reveals hidden notes. With a suitable map, you get a **+1 item bonus** to Survival checks to Sense Direction.
- **Activate** (1 action, Interact), **once per minute:** hold it to your eye. Your next Seek this turn gets a **+1 item bonus** to Perception to detect invisible creatures. On a critical success against an invisible creature's Stealth DC, it becomes **observed** to you, though it can Sneak to become undetected again.

> **🧩 Sets up:** Chapter 2's map rules hinge on the scale's interaction with each landmark. Keep it in the PCs' hands.

**Next chapter:** the PCs race to **Jakapo Town** to save the crumbling map.
