## Eldamon and the chase

The clock counts **days lost**, each raising the Track DC by 2 and worsening the captives in [W6](gm_chapter_10.md#w6--cultists-sleeping-quarters) ([Charting a course](gm_chapter_10.md#charting-a-course-the-chases-track-check), p. 246–247, 261). Hours inside a day don't count.

> **⚠️ Gotcha:** No eldamon here helps with Track, the post-saltstorm canyon check, the jungle's Sense Direction, or Shelter Points. Only PCs roll for shelter (p. 247).

## Encounter 10.1 — The Pale Horse on the Salt (Klorse)

**Insert after:** [Event 20](gm_chapter_10.md#event-20--fallen-and-abandoned), once the remains are examined · **Where/when:** the trail early Day 2, where salt stalkers caught kidnappers. It lasts until the Area V camp, or until befriended.

**Costs no chase ground:** it runs beside the mounts, so it plays out while the party rides, and a few minutes' halt for a ceremony doesn't cost a day.

> **🎯** Judgment test: the scariest-looking thing in the desert is the friendliest (Eldamon p. 184), so the omen reading is the trap.

**Abilities:** death powers that weaken foes and drain life.

> *Something moves at the edge of the salt haze. A horse stands there, or the frame of one: pale bone under a skin of shadow, a mane and tail of drifting gray cloud, and two dull red lights where its eyes should be. It doesn't come near the remains. It watches you. When your mounts start forward again, it tosses its cloudy head and falls in beside you at an easy trot.*

- **Setup:** Level 8, rare. Run Event 20 first (DC 20 Medicine). The klorse never touched the bodies; it's drawn to death and lonely.
- **Find:** **DC 22 Perception** notices it leaves no hoofprints (out of phase), which with the Medicine result clears it.
- **Recognize:** **Religion** or **Nature DC 29** (L9: 31), [secret](gm_chapter_1.md#secret-checks-and-recall-knowledge). Success: a death eldamon born to run across planes, kind despite plague legends, wanting travel companions it will defend from death itself. Crit success adds the folklore (in some realms, drink and treats for a visiting skeletal horse ward off a year's bad luck) and "Helping klorse experience enough of other realms." Failure: a death eldamon. Crit fail lie: "A pale horse that follows travelers is counting them. It leaves when the first one dies."
- **Befriend:** Request **DC 24** (L9: 26), connection **DC 19** (L9: 21):
  - Race it (Nature on a mount, or Athletics on foot).
  - Share its wanderlust (Society, Indigo Isles Geography Lore, Occultism, or Religion): the strangest place the party has been, or a plane the PC knows.
  - Make the old offering from royal provisions (Society or Cooking Lore). It needs the folklore from a crit success or a background.
- **Leaves if:** the PCs try to drive it off as an omen (the lie leads here; weapons and spells pass through and hurt only its feelings), or a PC says they're done traveling.
- **Outcomes:** crit success joins and that night says it seeks an old friend who walked this road with a sword / success joins / failure keeps its distance, another try at the Area V camp, at no cost / crit failure vanishes into the haze for good.
- **Rolls:** Table 5 Typical (Requian, Common) · 6 No variation · 7 Baleful (sells the omen) · 8 Outgoing (the mounts hate it) · 9a Has a secret · 9b Searching for an old friend
- **XP:** 30 per PC (suggestion), for looking past the omen.
- **💡** Wanted badly? [Skip the Request](gm_chapter_1_eldamon.md#variant-skipping-the-request). Two PCs want it? Add a younger second klorse (Eldamon p. 28). If it's along, it stands untouched in the [saltstorm](gm_chapter_10.md#event-21--saltstorm).
- **🔒 The old friend:** **Aranelle** of W14. It ran her yearly pilgrimages to the well of Mului, kept running her route after she died (klorses visit friends who settle down, Eldamon p. 184), and lost her trail when her blade returned to the ruin. Campaign addition; her stats and her test don't change, and her phantom still attacks. If it was befriended, at [W14](gm_chapter_10.md#w14--warriors-lament-moderate-8) it stamps at the scimitar, circles her phantom instead of attacking if phased in during the fight, and is greeted by her telepathy after the test, which warms her offer to the PC she picks. If the PCs skip the vault, it keeps looking.
- **⚠️ Gotcha:** its refusal to fight Aranelle wastes actions. Warn its trainer before the fight so they can swap it out at the start.
- **Fit:** 40-foot Speed, and its elemental ability moves it when foes let it pass (Eldamon p. 184). It pesters any PC from another plane about other realms.

## Encounter 10.2 — The Iguana on the Warm Orb (Squamatch)

**Insert after:** [W11](gm_chapter_10.md#w11--baths) · **Where/when:** on the warm orb in the dry basin (p. 263), or a sunny ledge outside the doorways at midday if W11 is skipped, Day 3 after the rescue · **Costs no chase ground:** the ruin is the day's destination, and the book sets no Day 3 Track check. Run it only if a player bites.

> *Curled around the half-buried white orb at the bottom of the dry basin, a red-and-orange lizard lies with its eyes shut and its belly pressed to the warm stone. Small flames ripple down its tail in time with its breathing. It opens one eye, looks at you, and doesn't move.*

- **Find / Recognize:** **DC 22 Perception**. **Nature DC 26** (L9: 28): a level 8 fire eldamon that loves heat and lighting fires, but prefers a tidy campfire to a wildfire. Crit success: "Helping squamatch find an excellent location for sunbathing." Crit fail lie: it drinks lamp oil (a fire hazard).
- **Befriend:** Request **DC 24** (L9: 26), connection **DC 19** (L9: 21): find a hotter spot (Survival or Nature) or build a tidy campfire (Crafting or Survival). **Leaves if:** water anywhere near it, a stamped-out fire, or fuel that would spread.
- **Rolls:** Table 5 Typical (Pyric, Common) · 6 No variation · 7 Vibrant coloration · 8 Lazy · 9a No secret · **XP:** none. **💡** A desert cliff ledge might count as its sunbathing spot, but save the payoff for a spot the player picks later.

## The jungle's water eldamon (not befriendable)

> **🎯** A rumor with three versions feeds Pliticori's [Dubious Knowledge](gm_chapter_10.md#dubious-knowledge) and never needs an answer.

One of Ruby's explanations for the rain is "a powerful water eldamon" (p. 276); she isn't sure it's true. The gazetteer's versions (Gazetteer p. 94–95):
- **Cholpekka's tears:** a hardriggan inventor asked water eldamon to move to Crackbeak, but they couldn't help (eldamon follow change, not cause it), so he drowned himself in a ritual and returned as one, bound to the jungle forever. Rain is his tears. He's rumored to rest in **the Draught**, bacoo-held northern marshes; placating him might make the jungle safer.
- **The exodus:** his meddling drove the water eldamon off and the lake dried to salt. A PC who saw the W4 mosaic (p. 260) can link it to this one.
- **Thongfa's refuge:** her clergy say Baoyan dried Crackbeak to trap her, and once freed she made the jungle. **💡** A hook for any PC who follows Thongfa, to chase with Ruby. Leave it a story.

**Using it:** Ruby or a villager answering Gather Information tells it; a failure gives one true and one false detail. The klorse wants to see the Draught.

> **⚠️ Gotcha:** the Draught is off the route to area Y. Save it for after the campaign.

> **🔒 GM-only:** Keep the truth unconfirmed. Neither book settles it.
