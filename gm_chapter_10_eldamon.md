## Eldamon and the chase

The chase clock counts only **days lost**. Hours inside a day don't count. Each lost day raises the Track DC by 2. It also makes things worse for the captives in [W6](gm_chapter_10.md#w6--cultists-sleeping-quarters) ([Charting a course](gm_chapter_10.md#charting-a-course-the-chases-track-check), p. 246–247, 261).

> **⚠️ Gotcha:** No eldamon here helps with these checks:
> - Track.
> - The canyon check after the saltstorm.
> - The jungle's Sense Direction.
> - Shelter Points. Only PCs roll for shelter (p. 247).

## Encounter 10.1 — The Pale Horse on the Salt (Klorse)

**Insert after:** [Event 20](gm_chapter_10.md#event-20--fallen-and-abandoned), once the PCs examine the remains · **Where/when:** on the trail early on Day 2, where salt stalkers caught the kidnappers. It lasts until the Area V camp, or until the PCs befriend it.

**Costs no chase ground:** it runs beside the mounts, so it plays out while the party rides. Stopping a few minutes for a ceremony doesn't cost a day.

> **🎯** A test of judgment. The scariest-looking thing in the desert is the friendliest (Eldamon p. 184). Treating it as a bad omen is the trap.

**Abilities:** death powers that weaken foes and drain life.

> *Something moves at the edge of the salt haze. A horse stands there, or the frame of one: pale bone under a skin of shadow, a mane and tail of drifting gray cloud, and two dull red lights where its eyes should be. It doesn't come near the remains. It watches you. When your mounts start forward again, it tosses its cloudy head and falls in beside you at an easy trot.*

- **Setup:** Level 8, rare. Run Event 20 first (DC 20 Medicine). The klorse never touched the bodies. Death draws it, and it is lonely.
- **Find:** **DC 22 Perception** notices it leaves no hoofprints, because it is out of phase. Together with the Medicine result, this clears it of the killings.
- **Recognize:** **Religion** or **Nature DC 29** (L9: 31), [secret](gm_chapter_01.md#secret-checks-and-recall-knowledge).
  - Success: a death eldamon born to run across planes. It is kind, despite plague legends. It wants travel companions, and it will defend them from death itself.
  - Crit success: the above, plus the folklore. In some realms, people give drink and treats to a visiting skeletal horse to ward off a year's bad luck. The PC also learns "Helping klorse experience enough of other realms."
  - Failure: a death eldamon.
  - Crit fail lie: "A pale horse that follows travelers is counting them. It leaves when the first one dies."
- **Befriend:** Request **DC 24** (L9: 26), connection **DC 19** (L9: 21):
  - Race it (Nature on a mount, or Athletics on foot).
  - Share its love of travel (Society, Indigo Isles Geography Lore, Occultism, or Religion). Tell it the strangest place the party has been, or a plane the PC knows.
  - Make the old offering from the royal supplies (Society or Cooking Lore). This needs the folklore from a crit success or a background.
- **Leaves if:** the PCs try to drive it off as an omen, or a PC says they're done traveling. The crit fail lie leads to driving it off. Weapons and spells pass through it and hurt only its feelings.
- **Outcomes:**
  - Crit success: it joins. That night, it says it seeks an old friend who walked this road with a sword.
  - Success: it joins.
  - Failure: it keeps its distance. The PCs get another try at the Area V camp, at no cost.
  - Crit failure: it vanishes into the haze for good.
- **Rolls:** Table 5 Typical (Requian, Common) · 6 No variation · 7 Baleful (sells the omen) · 8 Outgoing (the mounts hate it) · 9a Has a secret · 9b Searching for an old friend
- **XP:** 30 per PC (suggestion), for looking past the omen.
- **💡** Does a player want it badly? [Skip the Request](gm_chapter_01_eldamon.md#variant-skipping-the-request). Do two PCs want it? Add a second, younger klorse (Eldamon p. 28). If it's with the party, it stands untouched in the [saltstorm](gm_chapter_10.md#event-21--saltstorm).
- **🔒 The old friend:** **Aranelle** of W14.
  - It ran with her on her yearly pilgrimages to the well of Mului. After she died, it kept running her route (klorses visit friends who settle down, Eldamon p. 184). It lost her trail when her blade returned to the ruin.
  - This is a campaign addition. Her stats and her test don't change, and her phantom still attacks.
  - If the PCs befriended it, at [W14](gm_chapter_10.md#w14--warriors-lament-moderate-8) it stamps at the scimitar. If it is phased in during the fight, it circles her phantom instead of attacking. After the test, she greets it by telepathy. This warms her offer to the PC she picks.
  - If the PCs skip the vault, it keeps looking.
- **⚠️ Gotcha:** it refuses to fight Aranelle, which wastes actions. Warn its trainer before the fight, so they can swap it out at the start.
- **Fit:** 40-foot Speed. Its elemental ability moves it when foes let it pass (Eldamon p. 184). It pesters any PC from another plane about other realms.

## Encounter 10.2 — The Iguana on the Warm Orb (Squamatch)

**Insert after:** [W11](gm_chapter_10.md#w11--baths) · **Where/when:** Day 3, after the rescue. It lies on the warm orb in the dry basin (p. 263). If the PCs skip W11, put it on a sunny ledge outside the doorways at midday · **Costs no chase ground:** the ruin is the day's destination, and the book sets no Day 3 Track check. Run it only if a player shows interest.

> *Curled around the half-buried white orb at the bottom of the dry basin, a red-and-orange lizard lies with its eyes shut and its belly pressed to the warm stone. Small flames ripple down its tail in time with its breathing. It opens one eye, looks at you, and doesn't move.*

- **Find:** **DC 22 Perception**.
- **Recognize:** **Nature DC 26** (L9: 28). Success: a level 8 fire eldamon. It loves heat and lighting fires, but it prefers a tidy campfire to a wildfire. Crit success adds "Helping squamatch find an excellent location for sunbathing." Crit fail lie: it drinks lamp oil (a fire hazard).
- **Befriend:** Request **DC 24** (L9: 26), connection **DC 19** (L9: 21). Find it a hotter spot (Survival or Nature), or build a tidy campfire (Crafting or Survival).
- **Leaves if:** there's water anywhere near it, someone stamps out a fire, or someone uses fuel that would spread.
- **Rolls:** Table 5 Typical (Pyric, Common) · 6 No variation · 7 Vibrant coloration · 8 Lazy · 9a No secret · **XP:** none.
- **💡** A desert cliff ledge might count as its sunbathing spot. But save the payoff for a spot the player picks later.

## The jungle's water eldamon (not befriendable)

> **🎯** This rumor has three versions. It feeds Pliticori's [Dubious Knowledge](gm_chapter_10.md#dubious-knowledge), and it never needs an answer.

One of Ruby's explanations for the rain is "a powerful water eldamon" (p. 276). She isn't sure it's true. The gazetteer gives three versions (Gazetteer p. 94–95):
- **Cholpekka's tears:**
  - A hardriggan inventor asked water eldamon to move to Crackbeak. They couldn't help, because eldamon follow change and don't cause it.
  - So he drowned himself in a ritual and returned as a water eldamon, bound to the jungle forever. The rain is his tears.
  - Rumor says he rests in **the Draught**, northern marshes held by bacoo. Calming him might make the jungle safer.
- **The exodus:** his meddling drove the water eldamon away, and the lake dried to salt. A PC who saw the W4 mosaic (p. 260) can link it to this version.
- **Thongfa's refuge:** her clergy say Baoyan dried up Crackbeak to trap her. Once freed, she made the jungle. **💡** This is a hook for any PC who follows Thongfa, to chase with Ruby. Leave it as a story.

**Using it:** Ruby tells it, or a villager does when a PC Gathers Information. A failure gives one true detail and one false one. The klorse wants to see the Draught.

> **⚠️ Gotcha:** the Draught is off the route to area Y. Save it for after the campaign.

> **🔒 GM-only:** Keep the truth unconfirmed. Neither book settles it.
